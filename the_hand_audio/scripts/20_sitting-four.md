The Hand. Episode twenty.

Sitting Four. The Balsley side.

[[PAUSE 1.2]]

Seven questions. This is the sitting that could close the two biggest gaps on the Tasmanian side — question twenty-nine, and question thirty-three.

[[PAUSE 2.0]]

Question twenty-nine.

Which Gordon was the labourer — your dad, your grandfather, or both?

What work did each actually do, and where?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question thirty.

Your grandfather Gordon died in nineteen seventy-two, when you were six.

Do you remember him at all? The house, the voice — anything?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question thirty-one.

What did your dad say about his own growing up?

Turakina, Maxwell, school — leaving school.

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question thirty-two.

Did anyone ever say the Balsleys were Danish?

Any story at all about where the name came from, or about the old country?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question thirty-three.

Who is Mabel?

Who else is buried at Launceston — and can anyone photograph the headstones?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question thirty-four.

Did the New Zealand Balsleys and the Tasmanian ones stay in touch?

Letters, visits — anything?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question thirty-five.

Sydney Balsley died in nineteen sixty-three.

Did your dad know his grandfather? And where did Sydney die?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

That is Sitting Four.
