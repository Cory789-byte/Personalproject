[[PAUSE 1.2]]

Annexure C. This is the audit. It is the least dramatic episode in the series and the one that gives the rest of it whatever standing it has.

[[PAUSE 1.5]]

First — what is documented.

[[PAUSE 1.0]]

The eighteen sixty-four war, and Denmark's loss. Buck's Hamburg recruitment, and the bounty and land-grant policy. Kele Balsley's eighteen seventy-three purchase, and the Mercury's description of the property. The Portmairremener seasonal round.

Handley's Woolshed, the Kai-iwi Cavalry, Bryce, Maxwell, and Ihaka Takarangi. Maxwelltown in eighteen seventy; Maxwell in nineteen twenty-seven; Pākaraka restored in twenty twenty-two.

The Whanganui Tribunal findings, and the nineteen thirty-eight to nineteen sixty-two river litigation.

Tebaga Gap and Point two-oh-nine, in full. Army Form W. three one two one, in full.

The whakapapa sheet: Kimi and Pare; Tame, twenty-seventh of June nineteen twenty to twenty-fifth of December nineteen eighty-nine; Tio; the nineteen twenty-seven brother; Harriet Te Pohe Matiu; and the seven children, including Maria.

Land Court file forty-six thousand and thirty-eight: the Pākaraka, Pukehika, Kaipo, Araukuku and Paratupu interests; the leases; the values; the fourteen dollars and twenty-eight cents. And file thirty-three stroke oh A: the Rangiwaea blocks.

Every Balsley birth and death between Kele and nineteen forty-six. And the sibling relationships.

[[PAUSE 1.2]]

Fourth — inferred, and marked as such.

Kele's marriage into the neighbouring Browns. The nineteen eleven to nineteen thirteen crossing. Kele's route via Buck's campaign. And the patronymic chain above Kimi.

[[PAUSE 1.2]]

Fifth — not established.

Kele's parish. Tamou, the man. Mabel. Tenure at Turakina and Pākaraka on the Balsley side. Tame's company, iwi and hapū. Matiu Tamou's identity. Kiel's place in the line. And which Gordon was the labourer.

[[PAUSE 1.5]]

And then the part most family histories leave out.

[[PAUSE 1.0]]

Corrections logged. Four.

[[PAUSE 1.0]]

One. Sydney was not a maternal surname. It was a man, born in eighteen seventy-eight.

[[PAUSE 0.8]]

Two. This was not a landless family. It held Levendale, and lost it.

[[PAUSE 0.8]]

Three. They did not land in Taranaki. They landed at Turakina — and the Taranaki index entry is downgraded.

[[PAUSE 0.8]]

Four. Launceston was not unsupported. There are graves.

[[PAUSE 1.5]]

Four errors, found and written down, in a book that is still being built.

The reason they are on the record is simple, and it was said at the start: a family history that hides its corrections cannot be trusted about anything else.
