The Hand. Episode eleven.

The Gold Coast.

[[PAUSE 1.2]]

Ground ten. The last one. And it begins by walking back through the other nine, because the walk is the point.

[[PAUSE 1.2]]

Balslev. On Funen. In a country that lost a third of itself in eighteen sixty-four.

[[PAUSE 0.8]]

Levendale. On ground the Portmairremener had been taken off. Bought in eighteen seventy-three, and gone by nineteen twelve.

[[PAUSE 0.8]]

Launceston. Where Mabel is buried.

[[PAUSE 0.8]]

Turakina. On ground the Crown bought, in transactions argued about ever since.

[[PAUSE 0.8]]

Pākaraka. Which was Pākaraka — then Maxwell for a hundred and fifty-two years — then Pākaraka again. With the Tamou title underneath it the whole time, ground to fractions.

[[PAUSE 0.8]]

Whanganui. Where a court converted a river into something saleable, and spent twenty-four years explaining why.

[[PAUSE 0.8]]

Tebaga Gap. Where a twenty-two-year-old from all of that held a section together for two days, and gave a hand for a medal.

[[PAUSE 1.2]]

And now the Gold Coast, Queensland.

Back in the country the family left in nineteen twelve.

[[PAUSE 1.2]]

Three crossings of the Tasman in the documented span. And the line ends up a few hundred kilometres up the coast from where it started.

[[PAUSE 1.2]]

That is what a family with no usable land looks like over a hundred and fifty years.

Landholders stay put, and their grandchildren argue about the estate.

Shearers, knife hands and labourers go where the work is — and take nothing with them, because there is nothing to take.

[[PAUSE 1.5]]

They took the names.

[[PAUSE 1.0]]

Tame died on Christmas Day, nineteen eighty-nine. Aged sixty-nine.

His daughter's son carries his name in English.

[[PAUSE 1.2]]

And somewhere in the youngest generation is Kiel Gordon Balsley.

Kiel — the way an Australian ear hears Kele.

[[PAUSE 1.2]]

Whether that is the Dane's name returning after five generations and two oceans — or coincidence — is a question one living person can answer.

And it is the last question on the tape.

[[PAUSE 1.5]]

That is the end of the ten grounds.

What follows in this series is the apparatus: the lines, the timeline, what is record and what is not, and the searches that would close the gaps.

And then the questions.

Because the middle of this book is not in an archive.
