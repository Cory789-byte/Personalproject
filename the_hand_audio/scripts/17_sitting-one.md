The Hand. Episode seventeen.

Sitting One. Tame.

[[PAUSE 1.2]]

Twelve questions. Start with the frame on the wall, and work outward.

Remember: one question, then silence. Pause the recording, and let him take as long as he takes.

[[PAUSE 2.0]]

Question one.

Start with the frame on the wall — the portrait, and the citation.

Who framed them? Where did they hang? And who has them now?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question two.

What is the very first picture of your grandfather in your head — the earliest thing you remember?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question three.

What did his hands look like?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question four.

Did the war wound ever show? A scar, a stiffness, a way he held things — anything he did differently?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question five.

Did he talk about the war?

If he did — when, to whom, and what did he say?

And if he never did — how did the family come to know what it knows?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question six.

Where is the medal itself?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question seven.

Anzac Day. Reunions. The R.S.A. Battalion hui.

Did he go? Did he march?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question eight.

Which sheds did he shear? Whose farms, which districts, which gangs?

Any names of the men he worked with?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question nine.

Where was the knife-hand work? Which works — and what was his job on the chain actually called?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question ten.

Did he keep tallies? Any numbers the family still quotes about him?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question eleven.

What did he sound like?

Te reo at home, English, both — and who did he speak each to?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question twelve.

Christmas Day, nineteen eighty-nine.

Tell me whatever you are comfortable telling about the day he died — and what the family did.

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

That is Sitting One.

[[PAUSE 1.0]]

Before you stop the recorder: spell every name said today. And say roughly when and where each person was born, if you know it.
