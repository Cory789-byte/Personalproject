Before all of it — and for a very long time before it — the Portmairremener move seasonally, between the mouth of the Prosser and the Eastern Marshes.

[[PAUSE 1.2]]

The first of February, eighteen sixty-four. Prussia and Austria cross the Eider. The Danevirke is abandoned within days.

[[PAUSE 0.8]]

The thirtieth of October, eighteen sixty-four. The Treaty of Vienna. Denmark loses thirty-five per cent of its territory.

[[PAUSE 0.8]]

The twenty-seventh of November, eighteen sixty-eight. Handley's Woolshed. Two children killed. Ihaka Takarangi, nine years old, loses his fingers, and survives.

[[PAUSE 0.8]]

Eighteen seventy. Maxwell dies, and Maxwelltown is named for him. And Buck publishes Tasmania's emigrant handbook in Hamburg.

[[PAUSE 0.8]]

Eighteen seventy-three. Kele Balsley buys land at Levendale.

[[PAUSE 0.8]]

Eighteen seventy-eight to eighteen eighty-eight. Sydney, Helena May and Lilly are born.

[[PAUSE 0.8]]

Nineteen oh nine to nineteen eleven. William John is born at Cutting Grass. Gordon Sydney Thomas is born at Levendale.

[[PAUSE 0.8]]

Around nineteen twelve. The Balsleys land at Turakina.

[[PAUSE 0.8]]

Nineteen thirteen. Maisie Florence is born, in New Zealand.

[[PAUSE 0.8]]

Then — undated — the family moves to Maxwell. Which is Pākaraka.

The twenty-seventh of June, nineteen twenty. Tame Kimi Tamou is born, to Kimi and Pare.

[[PAUSE 0.8]]

Nineteen twenty-one, and nineteen twenty-seven. Tio Kimi, and the third brother, are born.

[[PAUSE 0.8]]

Nineteen twenty-seven. Maxwelltown is shortened to Maxwell.

[[PAUSE 0.8]]

Nineteen thirty-eight. The river litigation begins.

[[PAUSE 0.8]]

May nineteen forty. The Battalion sails.

[[PAUSE 0.8]]

The twenty-fifth and twenty-sixth of March, nineteen forty-three. Tebaga Gap. Hikurangi taken, and held.

[[PAUSE 0.8]]

The twenty-seventh of March, nineteen forty-three. Point two-oh-nine falls. Ngārimu is killed. Tame Tamou is shot through the hand.

[[PAUSE 0.8]]

The third to the seventeenth of April, nineteen forty-three. The Military Medal recommendation passes Brigade, Division, and Army.

[[PAUSE 0.8]]

Nineteen forty-five to nineteen forty-six. The Battalion comes home.

The eighteenth of June, nineteen forty-six. Gordon Sydney Thomas Balsley is born.

The second of February, nineteen forty-eight. Maria Tamou is born.

[[PAUSE 0.8]]

Nineteen sixty-two. The river litigation ends, having achieved nothing.

[[PAUSE 0.8]]

The twenty-second of February, nineteen sixty-six. Thomas Sydney Balsley is born.

The twenty-sixth of August, nineteen seventy-one. Paratupu one-A is sold to the Māori Trustee.

Nineteen seventy-two. Gordon Sydney Thomas Balsley, the one born in nineteen eleven, dies.

February twenty twenty-two. Maxwell officially becomes Pākaraka again.
