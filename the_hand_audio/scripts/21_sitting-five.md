The Hand. Episode twenty-one.

Sitting Five. You, Kiel, now.

[[PAUSE 1.2]]

Five questions. The last one is the most important question in the book, and it is deliberately last.

[[PAUSE 2.0]]

Question thirty-six.

Who is Kiel Gordon Balsley to you?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question thirty-seven.

Does anyone know where the name Kiel came from?

Was anyone thinking of the old Dane — Kele? Or is that this book finding a pattern that isn't there?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question thirty-eight.

When did you leave New Zealand — and why the Gold Coast?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question thirty-nine.

Who is still at Whanganui now?

Who holds the whakapapa sheet, and the land papers?

And who do I ask, before anything from them goes further than the whānau?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question forty. Last one.

What do you want this book to do?

And who has to read it, and be happy with it, before anyone outside the family ever does?

[[PAUSE 5.0]]

Then spell the names.

[[PAUSE 1.5]]

That is the end of Annexure E, and the end of this series.
