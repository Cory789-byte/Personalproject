The Hand. Episode nineteen.

Sitting Three. Harriet, Maria, and the seven.

[[PAUSE 1.2]]

Seven questions. This is the closest-in sitting, and question twenty-six is the one to be careful with. If it lands wrong, skip it and mark it.

[[PAUSE 2.0]]

Question twenty-two.

Tell me about your grandmother, Harriet.

Which name did she actually go by — Te Pohe, Harriet, Phillips, Matiu?

And where was she from?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question twenty-three.

The sheet lists seven children. Agnes El Hama. Linda Rebecca. Maria. Kimi. Leoni Mariana. Mathew. Rosalie.

Take them one at a time. One thing about each.

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question twenty-four.

Tell me about your mum.

Where did Maria sit among the seven — and what was she like, when you were a boy?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question twenty-five.

How did your mum and dad meet?

Where, when — and what were they each doing?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question twenty-six.

You were born in February nineteen sixty-six, and your mum had just turned eighteen.

What did the family ever say about that time?

Who stood by them?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question twenty-seven.

Where were you raised — exactly?

Year by year, house by house, as best you can walk it.

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question twenty-eight.

Did the whānau have a church — Rātana, Catholic, Anglican, something else?

Where were people baptised, and married?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

That is Sitting Three.
