The Hand. Episode fifteen.

The Searches.

[[PAUSE 1.2]]

Annexure D. Twelve searches, named, with what they cost.

Most of them are free. That is not a throwaway detail — it is the argument of this annexure. Almost everything still missing from this book is missing because nobody has looked yet, not because it is locked away.

[[PAUSE 1.5]]

One. The Copenhagen emigration protocols, from the late eighteen sixties on.

Kele's name, age, last residence and destination. It names his parish.

This is the highest-value document in the project. Free.

[[PAUSE 1.0]]

Two. Danish parish registers and censuses, at the Rigsarkivet. Also free.

With a parish, the line runs back into the sixteen hundreds. One record converts four generations into three hundred years.

[[PAUSE 1.2]]

Three. New Zealand Defence Force Personnel Archives — service file thirty-nine thousand six hundred and thirty-nine. Requestable by next of kin. That is Tom.

Date of birth. Enlistment. Occupation on enlistment. Company. Iwi as recorded. Next of kin. The wound. Discharge. And any J Force service.

Free.

[[PAUSE 1.0]]

Four. The London Gazette, mid to late nineteen forty-three. The published Military Medal wording. Free.

[[PAUSE 1.0]]

Five. The LIST — Tasmanian historic deeds, eighteen twenty-seven to nineteen seventy-two. The eighteen seventy-three purchase, and the transaction that ended it. Free account.

[[PAUSE 1.0]]

Six. The Tasmanian Names Index. Kele's arrival, the Brown marriage, and every child.

This one converts Annexure A from UNVERIFIED to RECORDED. Free.

[[PAUSE 1.0]]

Seven. Trove. Kele's obituary — and the Balsley spelling variants: Balsly, Balsely, Bardsley, Baisley. Free.

[[PAUSE 1.0]]

Eight. Mabel's headstone, at Launceston.

A photograph answers whether the family split, or moved. Free.

[[PAUSE 1.0]]

Nine. Turakina and Pākaraka valuation and land records — Archives New Zealand, district records, and the Whanganui Regional Museum. This is the Balsley tenure question.

[[PAUSE 1.0]]

Ten. The Wai nine hundred and three record of inquiry — block histories with named tūpuna, free online. Plus Ngā Rauru Kītahi settlement material, for Handley's Woolshed.

[[PAUSE 1.0]]

Eleven. Māori Land Online, and the Māori Land Court minute books. Succession orders naming Kimi's and Tamou's generations; the Pukehika and Rangiwaea title histories.

And the book adds a condition to that one: approached knowing what those records are, and how they were produced.
