The Hand. Episode eighteen.

Sitting Two. The marae, and the land.

[[PAUSE 1.2]]

Nine questions. And this is the sitting where the book stands back, because most of what is asked here is not the book's to answer, and may not be Tom's either.

Question thirteen is built to hand the question to the right person. That is the point of it.

[[PAUSE 1.2]]

Have the land schedules on the table for this one, if you can. And the block map.

[[PAUSE 2.0]]

Question thirteen.

Which marae is the whānau's? Which hapū — which iwi?

And if that is for someone else to say: who should I ask, and will you take me to them?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question fourteen.

The surname comes from a man — Kimi's father. Tamou.

Has anyone at the marae ever named him?

And who holds the deep whakapapa now?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question fifteen.

What do you know of Kimi and Pare — your great-grandparents?

Where did they live, what did they do, and when did they die?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question sixteen.

Tio Kimi — Joe. What is remembered of him?

And the brother born in nineteen twenty-seven — what was his name?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question seventeen.

Did the whānau talk about the Pākaraka blocks — the leases, the rent?

Did money ever actually arrive? And what did it look like when it did?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question eighteen.

Did anyone in the whānau ever farm any of it — or try to?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question nineteen.

What was said at home about the name Maxwell? And about the woolshed?

Did the old people speak of Handley's Woolshed — of Ihaka Takarangi?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question twenty.

The name went back to Pākaraka in twenty twenty-two.

Was the whānau part of that? How did it land, with everyone?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

Question twenty-one.

On the land files there is a Matiu Tamou, holding the Rangiwaea blocks.

Who is that — Harriet's father? Or her son Mathew? Or someone else?

And where is Rangiwaea?

[[PAUSE 4.0]]

That is Sitting Two.
