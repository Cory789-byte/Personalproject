Annexure A. This is the reference episode — every person in the book, in order, with the evidence grade attached to each one.

It is meant to be listened to more than once, and it is the episode to come back to when a name comes up and you cannot place it.

[[PAUSE 1.5]]

The Balslev line.

[[PAUSE 1.0]]

One. Kele Balsley. From Denmark.

Bought land at Levendale, Tasmania, in eighteen seventy-three, and built a family home. Married Mary Ann Brown. Hosted the St Chad's building-fund fair; property adjoining Leven Banks, six and a half miles from Runnymede.

RECORDED — from the Levendale district history, and the Hobart Mercury.

His Danish parish, his ship, and his arrival: BROKEN.

[[PAUSE 1.0]]

Children indexed: Helena May, eighteen eighty to nineteen thirty-one. Lilly, eighteen eighty-eight to nineteen twenty-two. And Sydney, eighteen seventy-eight to nineteen sixty-three — born at Richmond, an anomaly still unexplained.

All UNVERIFIED.

[[PAUSE 1.2]]

Two. Sydney Balsley. Born the twenty-seventh of October, eighteen seventy-eight. Died nineteen sixty-three. UNVERIFIED.

Probable wife or daughter: Mabel, buried at Launceston. NOT ESTABLISHED.

Probable children: William John, nineteen oh nine, at Cutting Grass. Gordon Sydney Thomas, nineteen eleven, at Levendale. Maisie Florence, nineteen thirteen, in New Zealand. Bryce Peter, nineteen twenty-two, in New Zealand.

All UNVERIFIED. The sibling relationships, INFERRED.

[[PAUSE 1.2]]

Three. Gordon Sydney Thomas Balsley. Born nineteen eleven at Levendale. Died nineteen seventy-two. Carried to New Zealand around nineteen twelve. Turakina, then Pākaraka.

UNVERIFIED as record. The movements are family testimony.

[[PAUSE 1.2]]

Four. Gordon Sydney Thomas Balsley. Born the eighteenth of June, nineteen forty-six. Labourer.

And which Gordon that word attaches to is open — question twenty-nine.

[[PAUSE 1.5]]

The Tamou line.

[[PAUSE 1.0]]

One. Tamou. Kimi's father. The man whose given name became the surname.

Not yet named.

BROKEN — at the marae, not in an archive.

[[PAUSE 1.2]]

Two. Kimi Tamou, with Pare. RECORDED, from the whakapapa sheet.

Pare's earlier union was with Peter Neilson; that son, Peter TeRapa — also Pita Wiwo — nineteen fifteen to nineteen eighty-two.

Their children: Tame Kimi, known as Tom — the twenty-seventh of June, nineteen twenty, to the twenty-fifth of December, nineteen eighty-nine. Tio Kimi, known as Joe — nineteen twenty-one to nineteen sixty-eight. And a third, the twentieth of January, nineteen twenty-seven, to the nineteenth of May, nineteen eighty-one.

[[PAUSE 1.2]]

Three. Tame Kimi Tamou.

Corporal, thirty-nine thousand six hundred and thirty-nine, twenty-eighth Māori Battalion. Military Medal, Point two-oh-nine, aged twenty-two. Wounded in the hand. Shearer, and knife hand.

Married Harriet Te Pohe Matiu — also recorded as Te Pohe Manu, as Phillips, and as Mrs T. K. Tamou — daughter of Matiu.

[[PAUSE 1.0]]

Her estate: Pākaraka one-A-one, one-A-two-A, one-A-two-B, and one-A-two-B-three. Pukehika. Kaipo A thirteen. Araukuku and Paratupu, sold to the Māori Trustee. And shares in Ātihau-Whanganui, and in Parininihi ki Waitōtara.

RECORDED — from Army Form W. three one two one; the whakapapa sheet; and Māori Land Court determination file forty-six thousand and thirty-eight, Aotea District, searched on the twenty-ninth of June, nineteen eighty-two.

[[PAUSE 1.0]]

Their children: Agnes El Hama, Linda Rebecca, Maria, Kimi, Leoni Mariana, Mathew, Rosalie.

[[PAUSE 1.0]]

His Battalion company: NOT ESTABLISHED.

His iwi and hapū: NOT ESTABLISHED — and not for this book to assert. Question thirteen.

[[PAUSE 1.2]]

And separately: Matiu Tamou, on file thirty-three stroke oh A. Holding Rangiwaea four-F-sixteen, four-B-one, four-B-three and four-B-two-A; Kaipo A thirteen; and Pukehika.

Whether that is Harriet under another form of her name, or her father, or her son Mathew: NOT ESTABLISHED. The Pukehika shares differ between the two files.

Question twenty-one.
