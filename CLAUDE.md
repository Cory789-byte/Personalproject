# Working instructions

## Put answers in the repo

**Everything goes in the repo, not just in chat.** If a question is asked and answered, the answer belongs in a file — chat is lost, the repo is not.

- Substantive answers → a document in the folder for that matter (see routing below), or a new section in the relevant existing one.
- Corrections, reassessments, and changes of position → written into the affected document, not just mentioned.
- Anything asked more than once → it was not written down properly the first time. Fix that.
- Commit and push each time.

Chat should summarise what was written and where, not carry the content by itself.

## Accuracy guardrails

These errors are already in circulation in the older documents. Do not reproduce them, and correct them where found.

1. **Do not plead the 25 February 2025 strike-out as an answer to the "order in force" element.** A protection notice generally remains in force until the court decides the application. The conduct alleged is 23 February; the court dealt with it on the 25th. The strike-out matters for many other reasons — it is not an answer to that element.
2. **Do not repeat "Alexia went to prison for 18 months in America."** That is a recollection given on body-worn camera, prefaced with "I think". The documentary record (Miami-Dade M18017586) shows a 2018 misdemeanor battery charge, intake-classified as domestic violence, with a stay-away order, **disposed of by nolle prosequi** about seven weeks after arrest. No custodial sentence. Repeating the claim will damage everything advanced alongside it.
3. **Do not assert criminal history in Spain or Italy.** No documents exist for either.
4. **Do not lead the forensic-linguistic video material** — word-level z-scores, tone-shift analysis, "spatial impossibility". It needs an expert and invites attack. The plain contradictions in Phillips's own account do the same work without the risk.
5. **Keep the criminal and civil answers on the dog separate.** Criminal: honest claim of right under s 22(2) Criminal Code — the belief only has to be honest, not correct. Civil: voidability of the December 2024 transfer, which must be proved and is not a given.
6. **Distinguish instruction from evidence.** Where something rests on Cory's account alone, say so in the document.
