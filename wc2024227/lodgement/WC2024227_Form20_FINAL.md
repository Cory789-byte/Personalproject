## ANNEXED AFFIDAVIT — *I, CORY LEA SHEPHERD, of 15 Edmond Street, Coomera QLD 4209, do solemnly and sincerely affirm and declare:*

**My role**
1. I am the Appellant. I am employed by Metro South Hospital and Health Service ("MSH") as an Administration Officer (AO3) at the Logan Hospital Switchboard. I make this affidavit in support of my application under rule 64G for the Commission to decide MSH's objection of 5 June 2026 to my Notice of Non-Party Disclosure. The facts are within my own knowledge except where I state the source of my information and belief.

2. The Logan Hospital Switchboard is the hospital's communications hub, and I work there as the operator. In a typical shift I personally handle in the order of 250 to 300 incoming and outgoing calls. I coordinate the activation of life-safety emergency clinical codes — Code Blue and MET (Medical Emergency Team) calls — through the SPOK clinical paging system, and I relay urgent clinical communications, including pathology results, between clinicians. Any delay or misdirection in these communications is a matter of patient safety.

3. My duties also include receiving and recording complaints directed to the Switchboard — including complaints about requests that were not actioned, and about pager, telephone and contact-directory entries that lagged behind staffing and roster changes, causing calls and pages to be misdirected; handling calls and paging outside ordinary hours and after hours; and absorbing changes to the roster and to operating procedures. Those changes were issued by the Line Manager and by a second manager in the Switchboard's reporting line; were frequently communicated at short notice and "effective immediately"; and were at times made without any communication to the Switchboard at all. The call volumes, the times and durations of calls, the emergency-code activations, and the records of those complaints, directives, updates and roster changes are all recorded in MSH's systems. The call figures I give are my own estimate from direct experience; the complete and exact records exist only within MSH's systems, which is why I seek them at Items 1, 2, 3(a) and 10.

4. The second manager held a telecommunications management role that performed the same communications function the Switchboard itself performed. Under that arrangement, telephone and contact-directory updates were restricted to, and controlled by, that manager, who to my observation attended the office approximately two days per week and was otherwise at a remove from the day-to-day operating environment. The practical effect was that updates the Switchboard needed in order to direct calls and pages correctly had to await action by that manager, rather than being applied by the Switchboard directly — which is the source of the lag and the misdirected calls and pages I describe at paragraph 3.

**The appeal and the matter in issue**
5. I have appealed the Regulator's rejection of my claim for a psychiatric injury (Major Depressive Disorder with anxious distress), with date of onset 18 June 2024, sustained in the course of that employment. The appeal is before Commissioner Dwyer and proceeds *de novo*.

6. The matter in issue is the Respondent's defence under s 32(5)(a) of the *Workers' Compensation and Rehabilitation Act 2003* — that the injury arose out of reasonable management action taken in a reasonable way. The stressors I rely on are pleaded in my Amended Statement of Facts and Contentions filed 7 April 2026 ("the Amended 9A").

**The Form 29 and the objection**
7. On 22 April 2026 the Industrial Registry sealed my Notice of Non-Party Disclosure (Form 29) ("the Form 29"), which was served on MSH as the affected non-party. The Form 29 sought twenty Items of documents held in MSH's systems, each tied, by a Relevance Anchor Table in its Continuation Schedule, to a pleaded stressor and to an admitted fact in the Respondent's Form 24.

8. By letter dated 5 June 2026 (ref K-LM26/729), signed by the Chief Executive of MSH, MSH objected to production of part of the Form 29, complied with some Items, and stated that documents responsive to other Items did not exist. A true copy of that letter is **Exhibit CS-9**.

9. On 12 June 2026 I made an application under rule 64G(1) for the Commission to decide the objection, within the seven-day period. On 15 June 2026 the Registry declined to accept that application on format grounds. This Form 4 and this affidavit are the corrected re-filing in the approved form.

10.
