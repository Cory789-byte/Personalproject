Good morning,

I am seeking an indicative proposal — scope, approach and fee range — for an independent
psychosocial risk assessment of a 24/7 hospital switchboard room in the Brisbane region:
a small team of operators on a rotating roster across day, evening, night and weekend
shifts, handling a high daily call volume including emergency codes and distressed
callers.

The assessment contemplated would be conducted under the Managing the risk of
psychosocial hazards at work Code of Practice 2022 (Qld) and consistently with ISO 45003,
and would include: review of recorded data (call demand, rosters, leave, incident and
complaint records); the People at Work survey; on-site observation across shift types,
including night and weekend shifts; interviews offered to all operators; risk rating with
a controls report; and a twelve-month review point.

For transparency: I am an employee of the workplace concerned, preparing a contribution
to a consultation process about how such an assessment might be conducted. Any engagement
would be commissioned by the employer, not by me. At this stage I am asking only:

1. whether this is within your service scope;
2. your indicative approach;
3. an indicative fee range for an assessment of this size;
4. your current availability; and
5. one scope question — whether the assessment's deliverables could include a statement
   of the role's psychosocial demands and working conditions in a form suitable for use
   by treating medical practitioners when providing fitness-for-work, restriction and
   adjustment opinions for workers in the room.

I'm happy to take this by email or a short call.
