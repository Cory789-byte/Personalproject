Five questions, so the correct arrangement can be applied

I ask these in good faith. My concern is simply that whatever arrangement applies to me is

1. Your letter refers to "the decision that has prevented you from attending your rostered

   which provision is being relied on?

5. If the arrangement is none of these, then I remain ready, willing and able to work, and
