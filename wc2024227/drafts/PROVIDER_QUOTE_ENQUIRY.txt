WHY THE WORDING IS LOAD-BEARING
1. "I table it as an input; engagement is a matter for the Health Service" — Cory never
   steps into the commissioning role; the assessor's independence stays untainted.
2. s 18 WHS Act: cost is relevant to "reasonably practicable" only after likelihood and
   severity, and only if grossly disproportionate. A real quote converts "expensive and
   hard to arrange" from an argument into a documented untruth.
3. A "no" given against a tabled quote is a no recorded against a known price and
   confirmed availability — documented in the consultation record.
