**Note on the Health Service's position of 13 August 2026**
Cory Shepherd · AO3 Switchboard Services, Logan Hospital · 14 August 2026

**Purpose.** On 13 August 2026 I received two emails from the Health Service. They are the first
substantive response since I was held out of the workplace on 26 June. This note sets out what the
Health Service has now stated, where that sits against its own records, and what I am asking.

---

**1. What the Health Service has stated**

- That the medical evidence provided to date does not support my being fit to return to the
  workplace.
- That my absence "would ordinarily be processed as sick leave", and where no balance is
  available, "automatically… as sick leave no pay".
- That, with my approval, other leave entitlements can be applied "to avoid not being paid".
- That I do have sick leave balances and "they should automatically be applied".
- That it is aware I have an open QSuper claim and may need to advise QSuper of any income I
  receive.
- That a meeting of about two hours is offered for Friday 21 August, to include you, with
  confirmation sought by close of business Wednesday 19 August.

**2. Where that sits against the record**

**(a) A conclusion reached before the Health Service's own deadline.** The Request for Medical
Information of 31 July was made on the stated basis that further medical information is required
before decisions can be made. The extended period for that information closes on 21 August. The
conclusion on my fitness was stated on 13 August, by Human Resources, and no reasons were given.

**(b) It is contrary to the only current medical evidence.** The Employee Capability Checklist of
3 July 2026 certifies me fit for my role with restrictions and records the arrangement as a
"continuation of existing arrangement… worked and tolerated without deterioration".

**(c) The position does not resolve into either outcome.** If I am fit, I should be working and
paid. If the Health Service's view is that I am not fit, I should be supported through paid leave
or an income-replacement pathway. I am neither working nor paid, and have not been since
13 July 2026.

**(d) I did not apply for leave.** I was directed not to attend from 26 June 2026. The absence has
since been coded by the Health Service: a myHR entry of 20 July as "Income Protection – No Pay",
requested by my manager, and my payslip of 12 August recording 76.00 hours of "NP Sick Leave" with
gross pay of $0.00. I requested neither.

**(e) Consent has been applied inconsistently.** My approval is sought before recreation leave can
be applied. No approval was sought before sick leave and income protection were applied.

**(f) My pay appears to have been processed incorrectly on the Health Service's own account.** I am
told sick leave balances exist and should have been applied automatically. My payslip records the
period as sick leave **no pay**, $0.00.

**(g) The leave proposed would be exhausted within weeks.** The balances provided are Recreation
46.82 hours, Sick 10.5 hours and Rostered Day Off 5.28 hours — 62.6 hours in total, or about a
fortnight and a half of my approved hours. Applying them would end my accruals and leave the
underlying question unresolved.

**(h) The hours question remains open.** My working hours were varied by three "Change to Working
Conditions (Temporary)" instruments — 1 to 15 March and 16 March to 26 April 2026 at 56 hours a
fortnight, and 25 May to 28 June 2026 at 40 hours — each recording my employment basis as part
time, and each approved by the authorised delegate, Mr Scott Hughes, on 27 February, 17 April and
9 June 2026. The Request for Medical Information of 31 July, signed by Mr Hughes, describes my
employment as permanent full-time, 76 hours a fortnight. My psychiatrist has asked for
confirmation of which arrangement my capacity is to be assessed against before he can report.

**3. What has not been answered**

The email of 12 August asked four things: that the payroll correction (AVAC) be submitted; that
the basis of the medical inquiry be identified; that the working arrangement be confirmed; and
that I be returned to the roster, or the reason for non-return be stated. None has been answered.
A payroll enquiry lodged in the Health Service's own system on 22 July (log 4438861) remains
"Pending Investigation".

**4. What I am asking**

1. That the sick leave balances that should have been applied be applied and paid.
2. That my wages be paid, or the instrument under which they are withheld be identified.
3. That the basis of the medical inquiry and the working arrangement for assessment be confirmed
   in writing, so the report can proceed.
4. That I be returned to the roster under the arrangement of 1 June 2026, or the reason for
   non-return be stated.
5. That no leave be applied to this period without my written agreement.
6. That the Health Service confirm it will meet the cost of the report it has requested.

**5. Note for your records.** My myHR leave balances were included in the email you were copied
into. I have no objection to you holding them; I mention it only so it is on record how they came
to you.
