⛔ THE THREE-CHANNEL RULE (added 4 Aug — Cory asked whether to voice the contest to the
doctor): The PAPER carries the questions. The ROOM carries the truth. The MSH TRACK
carries the fight. Never mixed.

  it enters the clinical file, the clinical file is what Form 29s produce, and one
  cross-examination question ("your patient told you the request was inappropriate
  before you wrote this, didn't he?") converts the doctor's independent opinion into a
  briefed one. Report A and Report B both bleed.
- The contest is ALREADY filed where it wins: the 3 Aug response, the allocation, the
  dispute, the CE letter. Form A/B ARE the contest in procedural form — that is how the
  objection reaches the doctor: through structure, not adjectives.

  distress, the shutdown, the month unpaid. That is symptomatology, not strategy.
