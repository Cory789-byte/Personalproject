Good afternoon Michelle,

Thank you for your letter of 15 July 2026. Before responding to its substance, I need to correct
the basis on which my situation is being described and managed, because several of the letter's
conclusions rest on that description. I have copied my union, Together Queensland, into this
correspondence, and they will be assisting me from this point.

**1. Correction of the record — I am not "returning to work"**

1.1 Your correspondence describes me as an employee "proposing" or "requesting" a return to work,
and describes my situation to others as "a QSuper Claimant request[ing] to return to duties." That
is not my situation, and I ask that the Health Service's records be corrected.

1.2 The facts are: I have been continuously employed and **working my rostered shifts for the past
year**, up to and including early July 2026. Across that period I adjusted my hours within my
certified capacity, and my income protection benefits ceased **at my own request in March 2026
because I was working**. There was no absence from which to "return."

1.3 On **3 July 2026 at approximately 2:32pm**, I provided the Employee Capability Checklist
completed by my treating practitioner, Dr Ma, certifying me **fit to work with identified
restrictions**. I provided it as a serving employee updating my medical information, in connection
with finalising my superannuation income-protection matters — not as an application for permission
to work. I was rostered to work at that time.

1.4 By **approximately 3:30pm the same day** — less than one hour later — I was advised I would be
held out of the workplace pending a review, with my rostered shifts to be "paid out of [my]
available sick leave." I have been held out of work since, while certified fit, for now twelve
days, with no timeframe.

1.5 Accurately described, then: I am a working employee who has been **directed not to attend work
by my employer**, while holding a current fitness-for-duty certification. Any process applied to me
should proceed on that footing.

**2. Pay and leave**

2.1 I am, and at all times since 3 July have been, **ready, willing and able** to work my rostered
shifts. The decision that I not attend is the Health Service's, not mine.

2.2 No stand-down, suspension, or other power authorising an unpaid direction not to attend has
been identified to me. Unless and until one is identified, I do not accept that my rostered shifts
during this employer-directed period can be treated as unpaid or debited against my accrued leave.

2.3 I note in particular that **sick leave compensates incapacity for work**. I am certified fit.
Debiting sick leave — leave I have already substantially exhausted through the period of my
illness — to cover shifts I was ready to work, and was directed not to attend, is not a purpose
for which that leave exists.

2.4 **Request:** that all leave debited for rostered shifts from 3 July 2026 onward be re-credited,
and that those shifts, and any future rostered shifts during this employer-directed period, be
treated as paid time. If the Health Service's position is otherwise, please identify in writing the
specific power or instrument relied upon.

2.5 For completeness on your letter's final point: my QSuper income protection claim is closed, and
in any event an insurance benefit is not wages. My remuneration for rostered shifts is a matter
between me and my employer under my contract, the Award, and the certified agreement. I will
provide the fund's written confirmation of closure when I receive it, as you have requested.

**3. The applicable framework**

3.1 Your letter states that, absent an accepted workers' compensation claim, my situation "remains
at the discretion of the Employer." I do not understand that to be a complete statement of the
position, and I draw the following to your attention:

(a) **Queensland Health's Workplace Rehabilitation Standard (QH-IMP-401-5)** provides that
workplace rehabilitation, including suitable duties and temporary adjustments, may be provided to
employees **without** a current accepted workers' compensation claim where operationally
reasonable, and that managers are responsible for identifying such duties;

(b) **Queensland Health's Reasonable Adjustment policy (QH-POL-210)** applies to my circumstances;

(c) the **Anti-Discrimination Act 1991 (Qld)**, including the positive duty in force since
1 July 2025, and the **Human Rights Act 2019 (Qld) s 58**, apply to decisions the Health Service
makes about my employment;

(d) the **Work Health and Safety Act 2011 (Qld), ss 47–49**, requires consultation with me about
matters affecting my health and safety — including decisions to exclude me from the workplace and
any assessment said to concern psychosocial risk. To date there has been no consultation: the
decision of 3 July was made and communicated within the hour, and I have not been consulted since.

3.2 Against that framework, the current position — no duties in any capacity, no pay, no
consultation, and no timeframe, for a worker certified fit with restrictions — does not appear to
me to reflect the exercise of discretion those instruments contemplate. My offer to perform
suitable duties within my certified capacity (including drawing on my prior Metro South Health
experience in finance and scanning/records, and my substantive Switchboard role) remains open.

**4. "Health Management processes" — request for particulars**

4.1 Your letter refers to employees "being managed through Health Management processes." So that I
can understand my position and obtain advice, please confirm in writing:

(a) whether I am presently being managed under any such process and, if so, **which** process,
under **what policy or statutory instrument**, commenced **when**, by **whom** as decision-maker;

(b) if reliance is placed on chapter 3, part 8 of the *Public Sector Act 2022*, the basis on which
its preconditions are said to be met, noting that I was working until directed not to attend and
hold a current fitness-for-duty certification;

(c) if I am **not** being managed under any such process, confirmation of that fact.

**5. The Request for Medical Information to Dr Ma**

5.1 I repeat my request that the Request for Medical Information be issued promptly, and that I be
provided a copy when it is sent. I ask that it be confined to my **capacity, restrictions, and
their operational application** — the matters your letter identifies as requiring clarification —
and I confirm my consent extends to information of that kind.

5.2 I again ask that you advise the timeframe within which a decision about my rostered shifts
will be made once Dr Ma's response is received. My restrictions are certified **now**; 28 August
2026 is a review date, not a start date.

**6. Response**

6.1 Given that I remain without work or income while certified fit, I ask for a written response to

I remain certified fit, ready, and available to work my rostered shifts.
