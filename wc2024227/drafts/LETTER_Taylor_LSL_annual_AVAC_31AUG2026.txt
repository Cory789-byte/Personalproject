1. Applies for LSL backdated to 13 July 2026, then annual leave on exhaustion — as instructed.
2. Asks for the NP Sick Leave coding to be REPLACED, not merely supplemented. That matters: leave
   has already been applied to him (unpaid sick), so this is a recoding request, not a fresh
   application onto a blank period.
3. Asks for the AVAC today, with a reference number and a nominated pay run — both are checkable
   later, which is why they are asked for.
4. Builds the protest and the re-credit condition into the OPERATIVE part of the application at 4(d),
   not as a closing remark. If it is ever argued that he elected to take leave, 4(a)-(d) is the
   answer, and it is in the same document as the election.

6. Item 6 converts silence into a dated failure without any accusation.

application expressly conditional and asks for re-credit in terms. ⭐ He has instructed this course
with the protection built in, which is a reasonable trade for someone with no income and no
accommodation. **The decision is his and it is recorded as his.**
