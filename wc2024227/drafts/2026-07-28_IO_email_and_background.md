**CORY SHEPHERD — background for Together**
*Employment dispute: held out of work while certified fit · AO3, Switchboard, Logan Hospital ·
permanent · Together member · IO call 12:00pm, 28 July 2026*

**The short version**
I am a permanent AO3 who has been working my rostered shifts. Since 3 July 2026 my employer has held
me out of the workplace while I am **certified fit to work**, refused me any duties, given no
timeframe, and charged my rostered shifts to my own **sick leave**. No stand-down or suspension power
has been identified to me.

**What happened — 3 July 2026**
- ~2:32pm — I provided the Employee Capability Checklist completed by my GP, Dr Ma: certified **fit
  for my role with restrictions** (including reduced hours; review date 28 August 2026).
- ~3:30pm (same day, under an hour later) — I was advised I would be held out "pending review," with
  my rostered shifts "paid out of [my] available sick leave."
- I have been held out ever since — over three weeks — unpaid beyond my own accrued leave.

**The employer's position (HR/Injury Management letter, 15 July 2026)**
- Interim/alternative duties: refused "in any capacity," pending further medical information from my GP.
- That medical request: "unable to commit to a timeframe."
- Pay: refused — said to sit with my QSuper income protection claim (which is closed).
- My return "remains at the discretion of the Employer" as I have no accepted workers' compensation
  claim; reference to employees "being managed through Health Management processes."
- No consultation with me before or after the 3 July decision.

**Why I think that position may be incomplete (I'd value your view)**
- QH Workplace Rehabilitation Standard (QH-IMP-401-5): suitable duties/adjustments may be provided to
  employees **without** an accepted WC claim where operationally reasonable.
- QH Reasonable Adjustment policy (QH-POL-210).
- Anti-Discrimination Act 1991 (Qld) incl. the positive duty from 1 July 2025; Human Rights Act 2019 s 58.
- WHS Act 2011 ss 47-49 (consultation) — none has occurred.
- No workplace risk assessment has ever been done; my GP certifies me fit on the employer's own form.

**What I'd like your view on**
1. Notifying a dispute under the certified agreement over the exclusion and the leave debits — and
   whether the **status quo clause** applies (I was rostered and working immediately before the
   decision).
2. If I am to be held out at all, whether it should be on **paid special leave** (Directive 12/24)
   rather than my own sick leave, given I am certified fit.
3. Particulars: whether I am being managed under any "Health Management process," under what
   instrument, and by whom.

**Documents I hold**
The 15 July HR letter; the ECC; the minute-stamped 3 July email thread; a 7 July HR letter (which
