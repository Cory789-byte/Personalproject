Thank you for your office's acknowledgment of 3 August 2026, provided at your direction.

I write briefly, on one point only.

Since the disclosure was made, decision-making in the matters it concerns has continued
within the same reporting line. On 4 August 2026 I received correspondence advising that
leave would be processed on my behalf for the period 20 July to 2 August 2026, and
responding to my dispute of 3 August. I have also learned, from my income protection
insurer's letter of 4 August 2026, that a position on my return to work has been
communicated to the insurer by the Health Service — a position I have not been given
directly.

Section 89 of the Public Sector Act 2022 makes the chief executive the person to whom a
conflict is disclosed, and the person who may authorise an employee to continue to act in
a matter to which a disclosed conflict relates. May I therefore ask:

1. Has an authorisation been given under section 89 for officers involved in the matters
   disclosed on 3 August to continue acting in them? If so, to whom was it given, on what
   date, and on what conditions?

2. If no authorisation has been given, may I ask that decisions in these matters —
   including the processing of my leave, the response to my dispute, and any
   communication about my return to work — be made by an officer independent of the
   matters disclosed, until the disclosure is dealt with?

3. Separately, and in the interests of resolving this simply: if the Health Service
   wishes to recall the position communicated to my insurer, I would welcome that — it
   can be corrected with the insurer, and nothing further need be said about it. If the
   position is maintained, then I ask that the officer who made it address these matters
   directly: that the decision be provided to me by its maker, with reasons, and that
   further correspondence concerning my attendance, leave, pay and return to work be
   routed to and from that officer, so that the matters are dealt with by the person
   actually deciding them.

4. The five questions in my letter of 3 August remain open, and I would be grateful for
   the Health Service's response in due course.

I make no allegation against any individual, and I ask for no finding about anyone. My
concern remains, as it was on 3 August, that the ordinary separation of functions be
applied while these matters are resolved.

  conflicted line to keep deciding — she now owns every subsequent act, incl. the leave
  push and the insurer communication) or it DOESN'T (officers acted in a disclosed-conflict
  matter with CE knowledge documented from 12:45 on day one).
- It ties the ART revelation to the conflict question in one sentence, at the only level
  with power over both.
- It is the s 101 produce-or-concede structure, applied one level up.
- It keeps HIS record perfect: no allegation, no names, a request for the ordinary
  separation of functions — the same ask as 3 Aug, now with a dated event showing why.
- Whatever answer comes (or doesn't) feeds: Stage 2 (independent decision-maker ask),
  the s 32(5) manner case, and any later adverse-action defence (knowledge + persistence).
