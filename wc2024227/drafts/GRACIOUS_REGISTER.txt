THE GRACIOUS REGISTER — banked 4 Aug 2026
=========================================
The courteous-contributor voice for any MSH/HR engagement on the assessment or consultation.
Courtesy costs nothing, concedes nothing, and makes their silences louder by contrast.

THE THREE RULES
1. Thank for the PROCESS, never the outcome or the entitlement.
   ✔ "Thank you for the opportunity to contribute input."
   ✘ "Thank you for allowing me to..." — entitlements are not permissions.
2. Frame everything as service to the room and its patients, not to the matter.
   "To assist the service" / "in the interests of the room and the patients it serves."
3. Warm words never touch the machinery. No thank-you extends a deadline, withdraws a
   dispute, or softens a clock (Stage 1/2, 24 Aug deemed refusal, dispute limbs all run
   untouched). The two layers never meet in one sentence.

PHRASE BANK
- "Thank you for the opportunity to provide input — I appreciate the Health Service
  engaging on this."
- "I'm glad to assist with what I see as a genuine operational need."
- "I've tried to make my contribution as practical and ready-to-use as possible."
- "Offered in the hope it's useful to the process."
- "I'm happy to be guided by the process from here."
- "I remain available at any time, with my support person and union representative."
- "I appreciate the time being made for this."

READY REPLY — when HR invites input on the assessment/consultation
------------------------------------------------------------------
Dear [name],

Thank you for the opportunity to contribute — I genuinely appreciate the Health Service
engaging on this, and I'm glad to assist.

My aim throughout is a practical one: to help the service meet a need in the Switchboard
room, in the interests of the operators and the patients the room serves. To make the
discussion as productive as possible, I've prepared an integrated package — the hazard
mapping, proposed controls, a proposed assessment methodology, and information on
independent providers available locally — which I'll bring to table at our discussion.
It's offered as an input for consultation, nothing more; I'm happy to be guided by the
process from here.

I remain available at any time, with my support person and union representative, and I
look forward to it.

Kind regards,
Cory
------------------------------------------------------------------
NOTES
- The reply thanks, offers, and defers — while tabling the whole package with
  "for consultation" stamped on it.
- The record it builds: one side offering and assisting; the other deciding what to do
  about it. That is the exhibit.
- Use the register in every future engagement email unless a document is a formal notice
  (disputes/referrals keep their procedural voice).
