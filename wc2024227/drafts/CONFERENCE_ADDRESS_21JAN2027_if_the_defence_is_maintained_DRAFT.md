## Opening (one minute)

Commissioner, the position since the last conference is this.

On 8 September the Respondent admitted 298 of the 303 facts in my notice and denied none. Thirty-eight of the thirty-nine
documents behind them are confirmed, and the Manager's role description at Tab 32A is confirmed. The Respondent's

So the facts are settled. What is left is whether each circumstance was management action, whether any management
action was reasonable and taken in a reasonable way, and causation. I would like to use today to find out which of
those the Respondent actually contests, and on what evidence.

## The one sentence of law, if invited

The question under section 32(5)(a) is whether the injury as a whole arose out of reasonable management action taken in
a reasonable way: *Langerak* [2020] ICQ 2 at [86]. Reasonable action among the causes does not answer that question:
*Mahaffey* [2016] ICQ 10 at [57]. The review decision counted factors; the Act requires them to be weighed.
