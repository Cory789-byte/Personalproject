STAGE 1 / STAGE 2 MEETING RECORD PROTOCOL — the one proofing gap. Banked 4 Aug 2026.
====================================================================================
IDENTIFIED BY RESPONSE-TREE TEST: every MSH reply branch is covered EXCEPT the branch
where they meet him and bring nothing. An undocumented meeting is the only way the
six questions can be made to disappear.

BEFORE THE MEETING
- Support person + union representative present (already offered in writing).
- Bring: the six questions, the ECC (3 Jul), the dispute notice, the RTW proposal.
- Ask at the start: "may I take notes?" — always yes; write times, names, roles.

IN THE MEETING
- Ask the six questions IN ORDER. If an answer is not given, ask: "is that something
  that can be provided, and by when?" Record the answer to THAT question too.
- Never argue. Never raise motive, the CE letter, the appeal, or the assessment cost.
- If a new characterisation is offered ("special leave", "leave without pay",
  "directed absence"), do not accept or reject it in the room: "thank you — I'll
  consider that and respond in writing."
- If pressed to agree to anything: "I'd like to take that away and respond in writing."

⭐ SAME DAY, IN WRITING (the step that closes the gap)
Send within hours, to the same recipients, subject "Record of Stage 1 discussion —
[date]":

  Dear Chloe,
  Thank you for meeting with me today at [time]. Present were [names].
  My record of the discussion is:
  1. [Question 1 — the answer given, or "no answer was given"].
  2. [ … each question, each answer or non-answer … ]
  6. [ … ]
  [Anything agreed, with dates.] [Anything to be provided, with the date promised.]
  If my record is inaccurate or incomplete in any respect, please let me know and I
  will correct it.
  Kind regards, Cory Shepherd

WHY THIS MATTERS MORE THAN THE MEETING ITSELF
- An unanswered question in an email is a gap. An unanswered question recorded in an
  agreed minute is a FINDING.
- "Please correct me if I am wrong" converts their silence into acceptance of his
  record — the same mechanism that made the 3 August package work.
- It is courteous, standard practice, and impossible to object to.
- Do the same after ANY Stage 2 conference, any phone call, any corridor conversation.
STANDING RULE: nothing that happens orally in this matter stays oral.
