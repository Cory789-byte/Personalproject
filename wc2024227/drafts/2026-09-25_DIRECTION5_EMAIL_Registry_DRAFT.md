(b) agree the documents to be tendered without further proof;

(d) give the Respondent an opportunity to consider its position on the admitted record.

## Why the letter says what it does, and what it leaves out

- **Every document is shown to belong to Metro South Health or the union, with its contents already
  admitted.** That makes the point without arguing it: these are the employer's own records, and
  the only question left is whether the copy is genuine.
- **The "Issue it goes to" column cites only the Respondent's own pleading (SOFC paragraphs) and
  admitted facts.** It argues nothing.
- **Tab 1 is identified as a document MSH itself sent to the Appellant** (RFMI of 31 July 2026,
  Attachment 2). This is more accurate than the 9 September schedule, which said it was held at
  "Metro South Health production of June 2026, Item 6". Item 6 of that production was the role
  description of the **Manager** of Switchboard Services, not the AO3 role description.
- **Left out:** "a public hearing may not be in the public interest", "the documents close the
  matter", and any contradiction between the outlines and the admissions. Reasons are in the earlier
  evaluation: causation is not admitted, a conference is not a settlement forum, and argument belongs
  at the conference or in cross-examination.
