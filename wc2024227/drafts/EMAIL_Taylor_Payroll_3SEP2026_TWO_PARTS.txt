PART 2 — PAYMENT

An ad hoc payment can be made, and it is the process that exists for exactly this situation.

Queensland Health's HR Policy C13, Payment of salaries and wages (QH-POL-188, 23 June 2025), section 8,
describes the ad hoc payment process as one that "enables employees to request a payment that was
missed or requires correction outside of the regular pay day schedule without waiting for the next
pay cycle". The certified agreement requires pay that is not correct to be corrected in a timely
manner (EB12 cl 3.8.1), and the agreed mechanism for that is an ad hoc payment (EB11 cl 3.8.3). One
was processed for me on 11 March 2025 (log 4045255). The statement that a payment cannot be processed
before 9 September describes the pay cycle. It is not the ad hoc process, and I do not accept it.

The pay that was missed. I was paid nothing on 26 August for the fortnight 3 to 16 August, coded
"Sick Leave – No Pay" against my written instruction, and nothing on the three pay days before it. That
fortnight is the "most recent prior pay period" in the policy's definition, and it is the payment I
am asking for by ad hoc payment now. Process 24926366 covers 17 to 30 August only; I ask that it be
confirmed in the 9 September run, but it does not answer the fortnights before it.

What the policy requires of the line manager. Section 2 provides that a line manager, when notified
by an employee of an incorrect wage payment, "must take all steps to rectify the error including …
preparing and submitting further forms". I gave that notice on 3 July and on every fortnight since.
The attendance variation form submitted for 17 to 30 August is the corrective form; I ask that the
same form be submitted today for 3 to 16 August, and for 3 July to 2 August, with the leave type I
have instructed, and that the ad hoc payment pending under enquiry 4471091 be processed against it.
Section 8 also provides that where an employee is unable to access myHR the line manager can lodge
the ad hoc request on the employee's behalf; I could not access myHR until 2 September. The request
was lodged on Wednesday 2 September, within the policy's timeframe for payment before the next pay day.

Under section 9 of the same policy I also ask for a review of my pay entitlements from 3 July 2026,
since my pay has been continuously incorrect from that date, to run with the leave audit Payroll has
opened.
