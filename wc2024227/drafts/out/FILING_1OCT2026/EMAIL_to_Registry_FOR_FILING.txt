3. What the proposed Form 9A changes

   (a) The Appellant accepts that the onus of proof is on him.
   (b) The pleading closes on the facts and documents admitted by the Respondent, save for the few matters identified in the letter:
       - Facts (Form 24): 298 of the 303 facts were admitted on 8 September 2026, to the extent recorded in the Respondent's response.
       - Documents (Form 25): 25 of the 39 documents were admitted on 8 September 2026, and four further documents were confirmed by the Respondent on 24 September 2026, so that 29 of the 39 are now admitted or confirmed. For nine of the remaining ten, the Respondent has requested copies from Metro South Hospital and Health Service; the tenth has not yet been addressed. For those ten, what remains in issue is the authenticity of the Appellant's copies; the facts they record were admitted, except those drawn from one register (Tab 31), as the letter explains.
   (c) It does not enlarge the matter: it adds no new stressor or allegation.

   The Appellant's case is stated on pages 1 and 2 of the proposed Form 9A. The remaining pages set out the particulars against the admitted facts and documents. At the Commission's request, the Appellant can supply the notice to admit facts (Form 24) and the notice to admit documents (Form 25) served on 28 August 2026, the Respondent's responses of 8 September 2026, and the admitted documents.

   The letter explains the structure of the proposed Form 9A and the directions sought.

   Ms Matheson: a copy of the proposed Form 9A is provided with this email for the Respondent's consideration of the leave application. The Appellant seeks the Respondent's consent to leave and asks that the Respondent advise whether consent is given.
