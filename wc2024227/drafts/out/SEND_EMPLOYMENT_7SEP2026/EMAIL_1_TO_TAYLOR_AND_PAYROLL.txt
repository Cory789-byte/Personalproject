Section 94 of the Industrial Relations Act 2016 applies Part 4 of the Act to long service
leave. Section 134(3) provides that "An employee's continuity of service with an employer
is not broken by an absence, including through illness or injury - (a) on paid leave
approved by the employer; or (b) on unpaid leave approved by the employer."

The leave takings report you produced on 4 September records 103 entries of leave without
pay across the whole of my service. Every one of them is marked approved. That includes
all of the days in the three excluded periods.

Where the Act intends a period not to count towards service it says so expressly, as it
does for parental leave at section 90(2). It does not say so for approved unpaid leave.

The Award and the Agreement add nothing further. Clause 22(a) of the Hospital and Health
Service General Employees (Queensland Health) Award - State 2015 points back to the same
Division of the Act. Clause 9.10.1 of the Agreement points to HR Policy C38, which
contains no exclusion either.

Each of those instruments also gives access at seven years. Award clause 22(c): "Employees
who have completed 7 years' continuous service are entitled to take long service leave on
full pay or half pay." Directive 10/24 clause 8.1: "employees are entitled to take pro
rata long service leave after 7 years continuous service." HR Policy C38 section 1:
employees "may apply for pro rata long service leave on full pay, or half pay, after
completing seven years continuous service".

25 March 2026.
