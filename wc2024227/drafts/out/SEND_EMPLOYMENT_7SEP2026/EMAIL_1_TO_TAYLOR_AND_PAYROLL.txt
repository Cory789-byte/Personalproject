The calculation excludes three periods on the footing that they are leave without pay

Section 94 of the Industrial Relations Act 2016 applies Part 4 of the Act to long service
leave. Section 134(3) provides:

    "An employee's continuity of service with an employer is not broken by an absence,
    including through illness or injury - (a) on paid leave approved by the employer;
    or (b) on unpaid leave approved by the employer."

The leave takings report you produced on 4 September 2026 records 103 entries of leave
without pay across the whole of my service, and every one of them is marked approved.
Sixty-seven of those entries fall in the period 21 June to 20 September 2024, each recorded
as leave category "Special", leave type "Leave Without Pay", status "APPROVED". The Health
Service's own record therefore places every excluded day inside section 134(3)(b), not
outside it.

Where the Act intends a period not to count towards service it says so expressly, as it
does for parental leave at section 90(2). Clause 22(a) of the Hospital and Health Service
General Employees (Queensland Health) Award - State 2015 points to the same Division of
the Act and adds no exclusion; clause 9.10.1 of the Agreement points to HR Policy C38, and
C38 adds none either.

Three of those instruments also give access at seven years rather than ten. Award clause
22(c): "Employees who have completed 7 years' continuous service are entitled to take long
service leave on full pay or half pay." Directive 10/24 clause 8.1: "employees are
entitled to take pro rata long service leave after 7 years continuous service." HR Policy
C38 section 1: employees "may apply for pro rata long service leave on full pay, or half
pay, after completing seven years continuous service".
