   2026; the confined reading contradicts the pleading the Commission itself

   qualified ("so far as appears from the material") and asserts only from the
   face of the disclosure. The full asymmetry reply stays in the drawer
   (working-theories §7) for the ~12.5% case where MSH maintains the ground.
4. **Particularity:** Schedule A re-specifies Item 20 by subject and window;
   custodians and windows are defined for every item.
5. **Privacy / effect on persons (Items 8–9):** pre-empted — the application
   itself offers the confidentiality regime (Registry-confidential, parties-only,
   credential/date/timestamp fields, redactions). A privacy objection is hard to
   maintain against field-confined metadata under a protective order.
6. **Collateral-purpose attack:** the danger zone, fully sterilised — Items 8–9
   tied to two pleaded, contested facts and nothing else (D5 closes with "No
   broader use is made of these Items"); the disclosure-response language in
   Item 20/B-20 is institutional and neutral.
7. **Section G overreach:** G4's "cannot stand with" is carried by the annexed
   document itself (an internal MSH email squarely within Item 20's subject and
   window, located and produced). Provenance inferences (who supplied it, why)
   appear nowhere.
8. **Item 19 natural justice (TQ):** handled by the 7-day notice + liberty to
   apply, in both the application and the order.
9. **Order (c) proportionality:** framed as verification of answers MSH has
   already given — "asks nothing of MSH that its response does not already
   assert." The reconciliation engine (Item 14 device date; Item 16 process
   references) is stated as bookkeeping, not accusation.
10. **Tone:** zero adjectives of complaint; concessions lead (C5, I1); every
