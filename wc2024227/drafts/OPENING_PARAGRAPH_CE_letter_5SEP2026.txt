OPENING OF THE LETTER TO THE CHIEF EXECUTIVE — draft, 5 September 2026
Dates and documents only. No motive, no adjectives. Every clause is sourced.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

I am absent from the workplace at the Health Service's direction. I have held an Employee
Capability Checklist since 3 July 2026 certifying me fit for my role with restrictions, and I
have remained available to work under the arrangement the Health Service itself approved on
9 June 2026. The Health Service has said it requires clarification of my medical position. It
has held my signed authority to obtain that clarification directly from my treating psychiatrist
since 24 August 2026. My psychiatrist put four questions to Injury Management on 19 August 2026,
and confirmed to me on 5 September 2026 that he is still waiting for a response before he
provides the report. I have received no wages since 13 July 2026.

Throughout that period I have funded the absence myself. My paid sick leave was exhausted, then
my recreation leave, then half pay, and the absence has since been recorded as leave without pay.
I am now told that I must wait until 29 September 2026 to fund it from seven years of service.

That date is 188 days after seven years of continuous service fell due on 25 March 2026. The
difference is made up of three periods of leave without pay — 8 May to 19 June 2022, 21 June to
20 September 2024, and 13 December 2024 to 23 February 2025 — which have been excluded from my
continuous service. Clause 22(c) of the Award provides that employees who have completed seven
years' continuous service are entitled to take long service leave on full pay or half pay.
Section 1 of HR Policy C38 and clause 8.1 of Directive 10/24 are to the same effect. I have asked
which provision permits the exclusion of those periods and have not been given one. I have read
the Industrial Relations Act 2016, the Award, EB12, HR Policy C38 and Directive 10/24, and I have
not been able to identify it.

  employer. s 90(2) shows Parliament excludes a period from the COUNT expressly when it intends
  to; it did not do so here. Hold this in reserve — do not argue the Act in the opening.
- Strip the 91-day 2024 window (deed cl 6 reserved it; the appeal attacks it) → 30 June 2026.
  Strip both 2024/25 windows → 19 April 2026. Both passed.
- ⛔ Do not write "taking it away from me", "punish", "pressure", "deliberate". The s 32(5)(b)
  perception limb is live in the Regulator's own framing (27 April 2026 notice, matter 4(b)).
  The sequence produces the inference without help.
- Ask, do not assert. "I have not been able to identify it" invites production. "There is no
