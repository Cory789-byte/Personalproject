## WHAT TO ASK ON THE CALL (she'll phone you — let her lead; have these ready)
Priority order for a 15-min call:
1. **Can Together assist with this, and what are the first steps?**
2. **The status quo lever:** "Can we notify a dispute under the certified agreement over the exclusion
   and the leave debits — and does the **status quo clause** apply? Immediately before their decision
   I was rostered and working, so I'd read status quo as: put me back on the roster and stop debiting
   my leave while it's resolved."
3. **The pay fallback:** "Even if they insist on holding me out, shouldn't that be on **paid special
   leave** (Directive 12/24), not my own sick leave — I'm certified fit?"
4. **Should I keep corresponding with HR, or hold?** (defer to her — signals you won't freelance)
5. **Who'll handle it, and roughly what timeframe?** and **what do you need from me?**

## DISCIPLINE
- Lead simple and factual: fit worker, held out, unpaid, leave docked, no basis identified.
- Don't lead with the PID / reprisal / patient-safety / appeal — that's the depth for later, and it
  can make a 15-min triage feel unmanageable. If she opens the door (e.g. "why do you think this is
  happening"), answer briefly and honestly, then return to roster/pay/leave.
- Warm, measured, organised. You're the easy, winnable matter — let that show.
