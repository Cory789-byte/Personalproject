## OUTLINE OF EVIDENCE — CORY LEA SHEPHERD (Appellant)

*Served under direction 2 of the Further Directions Order (3) dated 19 August 2026; not filed.
This outlines the topics on which the witness will give oral evidence. It is not a statement.*

**1. Employment.** Commenced at Logan Hospital Switchboard in [MONTH] 2019; permanent from 3 March
2021; classified a continuous shift worker; full time from 16 October 2023.

**2. The role.** Receiving emergency response notifications and distributing them to the correct
response groups per emergency code procedures, "strictly adhering to protocols and timeframes";
maintaining the contact and paging numbers on which emergency paging depends; keeping "call queues
to minimum at all times". The systems operated concurrently, and the call and emergency-code volume
across a shift.

**3. The database and the contact book.** That from 18 July 2023 operators could no longer correct
entries; that corrections had to go to Ms Stibbard or Ms Taylor; that the Contact & Number Changes
book was removed from the operators' room; and what that meant when a number was wrong, including
overnight, at weekends and on public holidays.

**4. The directives.** The after-hours on-call arrangement of 15 April 2024, the data-entry process
of 19 April 2024 and the call-routing change of 9 May 2024; that he was not consulted before any of
them; and what each required of him.

**5. Ms Taylor's presence and hours.** His observation of when she was and was not present across
2023–2024; that her office hours were not known to the operators; and his request of 15 May 2024
that she state them to the department.

**6. What happened at the console.** What he observed when contact details were wrong — calls for
emergency responses and for medical teams reaching the wrong destination between 2 and 8 May 2024,
and delays to urgent results reaching treating staff — and what he did on each occasion.

**7. The communication book.** The entry he wrote on or about 6 June 2023 about updating on-call
contact numbers; its removal; and what was said to him afterwards, and in front of whom.

**8. August–September 2023.** The concerns raised in writing on 7 August 2023, the meeting that
followed, and the responses of Ms Taylor and Ms Reese.

**9. Special pandemic leave, February 2024.** Being unwell with COVID-19; how many times he
submitted the request and what he attached each time; the reasons given for each decline; and the
approval on the same material.

**10. 13 and 15 May 2024.** That he made a complaint on 13 May 2024, later determined to be a
public interest disclosure *(content not set out)*; that on 15 May 2024 he was directed to retract
the email in which he had asked about office hours; and his knowledge of the email of 9 May 2024 in
which Ms Taylor asked another person to confirm her hours.

**11. Union representation.** When and to whom he expressed interest in becoming the Switchboard
delegate; what followed; and his endorsement on or about 3 November 2025.

**12. Pay.** The public holidays he was not rostered to work between February and April 2024 and
the difference to his pay compared with colleagues; the errors Payroll identified on 3 May 2024;
and when they were corrected.

**13. The rostering pattern.** That rostering errors affecting his line recurred across successive
roster periods, not once; the occasions he identified them and to whom; and the acknowledgement in
Ms Reese's email of 10 May 2024 of "a few rostering errors made by Chloe with regards to Cory's
line in past rosters".

**14. 17 and 18 March 2024.** Where he lived and his travel time each way; finishing at 23:00 on
17 March and commencing at 06:00 on 18 March; the rest actually available after travel; the sleep
he obtained; performing the emergency-dispatch duties at paragraph 2 in that condition on 18 March;
and the sick leave taken on 19 March 2024.

**15. Fatigue — what he raised, and what was never in place.** The fatigue and rostering concerns
he raised and when; sending the fatigue toolkit to Ms Taylor for review and receiving no response;
his request of 8 April 2024 and the refusal given on 1 May 2024; and that at no time before 30 June
2024 was he given fatigue risk management training, or a fatigue or psychosocial risk assessment of
his rostering, or any fatigue management process at the Switchboard.

**16. After 18 June 2024.** Meetings scheduled while he was on certified leave; that his employment
was ended under abandonment provisions while he held current medical certificates; and that he was
subsequently reinstated.

**17. Onset and effect.** The symptoms experienced and when; the effect on his sleep, health and
daily functioning across the period; the onset date of 18 June 2024; attending his general
practitioner and lodging his application on 1 July 2024; and his treatment since. He offers no
clinical or diagnostic opinion.

**18. Documents.** He will identify and produce the rosters and payslips for the relevant periods;
his emails and messages with his line manager and the Director; the leave and payroll records
produced by the employer; the role description; and the letter of Metro South Hospital and Health
Service of 5 June 2026 (ref K-LM26/729).
