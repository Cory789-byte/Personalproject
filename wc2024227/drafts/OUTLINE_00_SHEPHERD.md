QUEENSLAND INDUSTRIAL RELATIONS COMMISSION
Matter No. WC/2024/227 | Cory Lea Shepherd (Appellant) v Workers' Compensation Regulator (Respondent)

# OUTLINE OF EVIDENCE — MR CORY LEA SHEPHERD (APPELLANT)

*Served pursuant to direction 2 of the Further Directions Order (3) dated 19 August 2026.
Not filed in the Industrial Registry.*

I was employed by Metro South Hospital and Health Service as an Administration Officer, Switchboard
Services, Logan Hospital, from [month/year], full time from 16 October 2023. I will give evidence
of the following.

**1. The role.** What the Switchboard did and what I did in it: receiving emergency response
notifications and distributing them to the correct response groups to protocol and timeframe;
maintaining the contact and paging numbers for on-call medical staff; and keeping call queues to a
minimum.

**2. The database and the contact book.** That from 18 July 2023 operators lost the ability to
correct entries themselves; that corrections had to go to Ms Stibbard or Ms Taylor; that the
Contact & Number Changes book was taken from the operators' room; and what that meant in practice
when a number was wrong, including after hours and on weekends.

**3. The directives.** The changes notified to the Switchboard by email — the after-hours on-call
arrangement of 15 April 2024, the data-entry process of 19 April 2024, and the call-routing change
of 9 May 2024 — that I was not consulted before any of them, and what I understood each to require
of me.

**4. Ms Taylor's presence and hours.** My observation of when she was and was not present between
2023 and 2024; that I did not know her office hours; and that on 15 May 2024 I asked her to state
them to the department.

**5. What happened as a result.** What I observed at the console when contact details were wrong —
including calls for emergency responses and for medical teams reaching the wrong destination
between 2 and 8 May 2024, and delays to urgent results reaching treating staff — and what I did
about it each time.

**6. The communication book.** The entry I wrote on or about 6 June 2023 about updating on-call
contact numbers; that it was removed; and what was said to me afterwards, and in front of whom.

**7. The matters raised in August and September 2023.** The email I sent on 7 August 2023, what I
asked for, and the responses I received from Ms Taylor and Ms Reese.

**8. Special pandemic leave, February 2024.** That I was unwell with COVID-19; the number of times
I submitted the request and the reasons I was given for each decline; and what I was told about
what other staff were required to do.

**9. The complaint of 13 May 2024.** That I made a complaint on 13 May 2024, and that on
24 December 2024 it was determined to be a public interest disclosure. *(I do not set out its
content.)*

**10. The direction to retract.** That on 15 May 2024 I was directed to retract the email in which
I had asked about office hours; what I did; and my knowledge of the email Ms Taylor had sent on
9 May 2024 asking another person to confirm her hours.

**11. Union representation.** That I expressed interest in becoming the Switchboard delegate, when
and to whom; what followed; and that I was endorsed on or about 3 November 2025.

**12. Pay.** The public holidays I was not rostered to work between February and April 2024 and the
difference that made to my pay compared with colleagues; the errors Payroll identified on 3 May
2024; and when they were corrected.

**13. The shifts of 17 and 18 March 2024.** Finishing at 23:00 and starting at 06:00; my travel
time each way; the sleep I actually obtained; my condition on 18 March; and the leave I took on
19 March 2024.

**14. Fatigue.** The concerns I raised about rostering and fatigue and when; the material I sent to
Ms Taylor for review; the response I did and did not receive; my request of 8 April 2024 and the
answer given to me on 1 May 2024.

**15. After 18 June 2024.** The meetings scheduled while I was on certified leave; that my
employment was ended under abandonment provisions while I held current medical certificates; and
that I was subsequently reinstated.

**16. The effect on me.** The effect of these matters on my health, sleep and daily functioning
over the period, the point at which I could no longer continue at work on 18 June 2024, and my
treatment since.

*This outline is a summary of the evidence I expect to give. It is not a statement and is not
signed as one.*
