# Examination-in-chief — the treating doctors (INTERNAL, NOT SERVED)
Purpose: convert what the documents already imply into oral evidence. Non-leading. Short.
Rule: every question is anchored to a page they wrote. Nothing asks for a new opinion.

## Dr Ravikumar Bangalore Krishnaiah (report 13 Feb 2025 — Tab 4; email 24 Oct 2024 — Tab 3)
1. Doctor, you first saw Mr Shepherd on 24 October 2024. What diagnosis did you make that day?
2. Your email that morning says "psychological injury of Major Depressive Disorder with anxiety
   state". Is that still your diagnosis?
3. In your report of 13 February 2025 you wrote that he "presented with workplace stress stemming
   from issues with management and rostering at Queensland Health". What did you mean by
   "stemming from"?
4. Is the Major Depressive Disorder you diagnosed the condition that arose from that workplace
   stress? (— the bridge the report leaves implicit)
5. You wrote the issues "began approximately one year ago when a new manager was appointed". From
   your notes, when do you place the onset of his symptoms?
6. Before that onset, did you identify any pre-existing psychiatric condition? Was anything
   aggravated rather than caused?
7. Your report refers to relationship breakdown, job loss and bereavement. Taking each: when did
   it occur relative to the onset you have described, and what role, if any, did it play in the
   onset as opposed to his state in February 2025?
8. Was his employment a significant contributing factor to the Major Depressive Disorder?
   (— the statutory question; ask it last, plainly, once)
9. What did you rely on in forming those views?
   (— consultations; his history; collateral from his mother; the material reviewed)

Do NOT ask: anything about the Regulator's pleading; anything about individual events at
paragraphs 2–7 (the report does not attribute among them — schedule row 4 says so); anything
after 24 Oct 2024 as a cause.

## Dr Peter Hawes (GP note 1 Jul 2024 — Tab 1; certificates 1 Jul, 11 Aug, 8 Sep 2024 — Tab 2)
1. You saw Mr Shepherd on 1 July 2024. What did he tell you he was there for?
2. Your note records "work stress", "they withhold pay at times, no overtime- not processed,
   manipulate his roster- so he works lates then earlies", and "causing anxiety". Are those his
   words or your summary?
3. What did you diagnose that day, and what did you certify?
4. The certificate records the date of injury as 18 June 2024. How was that date arrived at?
5. Your certificate indicated no pre-existing factor or condition. What was that based on?
   (RD p.17 records it; the medication list of 16 May 2024 shows no psychotropic — Tab 1)
6. On 2 September 2024 WorkCover's record has you saying work events were the sole cause. Is
   that what you told them? (RD p.17 — Tab 7)
7. You referred him to a psychiatrist on 8 September 2024. Why?
8. Was his employment a significant contributing factor to the condition you certified?

## What NOT to put in any served document
"Stemming from means significant." That is a submission. It lives in closing, built on the
answers to K3, K4 and K8 above — or, if he does not attend, on the words of the report alone,
with the s 32(1) "a significant" test and the reviewer's own finding at RD p.17.
