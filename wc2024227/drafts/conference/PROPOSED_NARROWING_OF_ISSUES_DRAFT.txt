Yes — but second step, not first. The right slot is 26–30 September, AFTER her letter of the
25th and conditional on its tone; never before. Reasons: (i) the quiet period protects the
clean read of the 25th; (ii) proposing first hands them the pen and lets the 30 Sep outlines
be shaped against it; (iii) a document that ASSUMES the not-admitted matters will be admitted
presumes their concession before they make it — if the 25th does not concede, the premise
collapses and the plan has been shown.
If the 25th is conciliatory, send this to Matheson (cc nobody) with a two-line cover:
  "Before I write to the Registry under direction 5, I attach what I intend to propose at the
   conference, so that any matters the Regulator can agree are recorded with its agreement
   rather than around it. If the Regulator wishes to suggest amendments, I will consider them
   before 1 October."
Wording discipline for that version: everything conditional, nothing assumed —
  ¶2(c) reads "the authenticity of the documents conceded by the Respondent's response of
  25 September 2026"; the register (¶¶228–231) appears only as "any matter resolved by the
  production foreshadowed in that response". ⛔ Never "will be admitted".
Note on authorship: being the author of the notice does not carry the five register facts —
they are MSH's document and stand or fall with its production. The thirteen tabs are different:
his copies, their contents admitted; those can be spoken of as resolved once (and only once)
the 25th says so.
