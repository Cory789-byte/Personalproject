2. WHAT I NOW ASK FOR — A SHORTER REPORT

My letter of 17 August asked you to answer two sets of questions in one report: the matters in

I would now like to separate them.

What I ask you for is a report confined to Matters 1, 2 and 3.1 to 3.3 of my letter — the

and administrative sequence, the other factors placed in time, the interval, and your conclusion

I am no longer asking you, in this report, to answer 3.4 to 3.8 — prognosis, current capacity,
adjustments, the requirements of the position, workplace discussions, and foreseeable risk. Those

Everything else in my letter of 17 August stands unchanged: the attachments, the schedule of
assumed facts, Part B, Part B2, and Part D as to the form of the report.

3. THE REPORT CAN BE SHORT

I want to say this plainly, because I think it may be the most useful thing in this letter.

A report of two or three pages that answers Matters 1, 2 and 3.1 to 3.3, with your reasons, is of
more use to me than a longer one. I do not need narrative history, a treatment or medication
chronology, or any restatement of the workplace facts — those are already recorded in the
attachments and in your report of 13 February 2025, and Part B2 of my letter sets out what you are
not asked to establish.

What the report needs is your reasoning and your conclusions, under the numbering I used, and
nothing that is already on the record elsewhere.

I would be grateful if the practice could tell me:

(a) the fee for the narrowed report described at 2 above — Matters 1, 2 and 3.1 to 3.3;

WHY NARROWING IS THE WHOLE MOVE
1. ⭐ It halves the question set. 3.4-3.8 is roughly half the work and all of the cost above the
   causation core.
2. ⭐⭐ It removes the 2026 material from the appeal report ENTIRELY. That closes the trap: if a
   report attributes the current presentation to the 2026 exclusion and non-payment, the Regulator
   answers that the 2026 events are management action, and s 32(5)(a) is the exclusion the whole
   appeal turns on. Narrowing means nothing from 2026 is in the document at all.

   causation report severs the dependency.
