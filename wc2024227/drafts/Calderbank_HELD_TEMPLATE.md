**Re:  WC/2024/227 — Cory Lea Shepherd v Workers' Compensation Regulator
       Offer to resolve the appeal (Calderbank v Calderbank [1975] 3 All ER 333)**

Dear Ms Matheson,

1.  I make this offer to resolve the whole of the appeal. It is structured to be
    capable of acceptance simply and without admission, and I have set out below
    the mechanism so that resolution requires nothing more of the Respondent than
    its consent.

2.  **The offer.** I offer to resolve the appeal on the basis that, **by consent
    and without any admission of liability**, the Respondent consents to orders
    that:
    (a)  the appeal be allowed and the review decision dated 24 October 2024
         (reference 69983) be set aside;
    (b)  the Appellant's application for compensation be one for acceptance, the
         psychological injury sustained on or about 18 June 2024 being a personal
         injury within section 32 of the *Workers' Compensation and Rehabilitation
         Act 2003* (Qld) and the exclusion in section 32(5) not applying; and
    (c)  the Respondent meet the consequential statutory entitlements — weekly
         compensation from the date of rejection, reasonable medical and
         rehabilitation expenses, and reinstatement of leave taken in connection
         with the injury [confirm against the actual entitlements];
    each party bearing its own costs.

3.  **Scope.** This offer is confined to the compensation claim the subject of
    this appeal. It does not release, and I expressly reserve, all other rights
    and claims — including, without limitation, any claim under sections 40 to 42
    of the *Public Interest Disclosure Act 2010* (Qld) and any claim against Metro
    South Hospital and Health Service. Any deed or terms of settlement are to be
    confined accordingly.

4.  **Mechanism (to make acceptance straightforward).** On acceptance I will,
    within three business days, provide draft consent orders in the terms of
    paragraph 2 and agree the quantum of the entitlements with the Respondent
    promptly. The terms of the resolution (including quantum) are to remain
    confidential between the parties, save as necessary to give effect to the
    consent orders or as required by law.

5.  **Genuine compromise.** In making this offer I give up my claim for costs and
    the determination of the matters in issue, in return for the prompt and
    certain resolution of the appeal.

6.  I do not rehearse the merits, but note only that they rest substantially on
    facts already on the record rather than in contest: the seven-hour break
    between the shifts of 17 and 18 March 2024 is admitted in the Response to the
    Notice to Admit Facts, and fell short of the applicable minimum break on
    either the ten-hour standard or the eight-hour agreement; the Respondent's own
    Reviewing Officer found the rostering of those shifts to have amounted to
    unreasonable management action; and the non-party's correspondence of 5 June 2026 records
    that no fatigue-risk-management framework applied to the Logan Hospital
    Switchboard, that assessment being implemented only after 30 June 2024 —
    after the injury. If the appeal is not resolved, it will require the
    determination of the pending non-party disclosure application and a hearing on
    the merits.

7.  This offer is open for acceptance until **4:00 pm on [the date 14 days after
    the date of this letter]**. If it is not accepted and I obtain a result no
    less favourable than this offer, I will rely on this letter on the question of
    costs.

I make this offer in the expectation that it will be given the consideration the
Respondent's obligations to the Commission require.
