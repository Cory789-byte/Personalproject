## ⚠️ ONE DEPARTURE FROM THE TASK, AND WHY
The task asked for the adverse-action paragraph **in both** documents. ⭐ **It is in Letter B only.**

**Letter A goes to Injury Management and is answered by clinicians.** General protections language in
a medical-information response is the wrong register, and it is the one paragraph most likely to be
forwarded onward out of context. **Letter B is copied to Petering and is where the process question
lives**, so the record is made there — once, carefully, and to the right readers. Letter A carries the
detriment as fact without the rights framing.
