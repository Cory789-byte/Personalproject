--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.  WHAT THIS NOTICE IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This notice informs you, as my immediate supervisor, of the existence of a dispute, in accordance
with clause 1.11.2(a) of the certified agreement.

Clause 1.11.2 provides that where there is "any disagreement between the parties as to the
interpretation, application or implementation of this Agreement", the procedure in that clause
applies. Clause 1.11.1 records that the objectives are "the avoidance and resolution of any
disputes … by measures based on the provision of information and explanation, consultation,
co-operation and negotiation", and that "the emphasis will be on finding a resolution at the
earliest possible stage in the process".

That is what I am seeking. I would prefer this resolved at Stage 1 and I am available to discuss
it at any time.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.  THE SUBJECT MATTER OF THE DISPUTE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The dispute concerns the application and implementation of the certified agreement to my
employment from 26 June 2026, in the following respects.

2.1  ATTENDANCE AND ROSTERED DUTY

I have not been permitted to attend my rostered shifts since 3 July 2026. On 2 July 2026 I was
advised that the Health Service was "unable to facilitate" my return until a completed Employee
Capacity Certificate was received, and asked to "wait for my advice to when your next returning
shift will be". I provided the completed Employee Capabilities Checklist on 3 July 2026 at
2:32pm. At 3:18pm the same day I asked to be told whether I should attend. I have not received an
answer to that question.

The condition on which the advice of 2 July 2026 was expressed has therefore been satisfied. I
have asked which instrument is relied on to maintain the position since, and have not received an
answer.

2.2  LEAVE

Since 3 July 2026 my accrued leave has been debited, and from 13 July 2026 I have been recorded
as absent without pay, in respect of a period in which I have been willing and available to work
and have said so in writing. I dispute the application of the leave provisions of the certified
agreement to that period.

2.3  ROSTER

I dispute the position, so far as it is taken, that my rostered pattern over the preceding twelve
months is inconsistent with the requirements of my role. That pattern was set by the Health
Service and worked without any concern being raised with me.

2.4  WORK HEALTH AND SAFETY MATTERS RAISED

Clause 7.1.11 provides that "the parties commit to ensure that appropriate feedback is provided
to employees who raise workplace health and safety matters". Clause 7.1.5 records the parties'
agreement to address, among other issues, "management of ill or injured employees",
"psychosocial issues and implementation of Managing the risk of psychosocial hazards at work Code
of Practice 2022", and "injured workers to have the opportunity to be re-trained in alternative
areas/departments". I have raised matters within each of those descriptions and have not received
feedback on them.

2.5  PSYCHOSOCIAL RISK ASSESSMENT

Clause 7.2 provides for psychosocial risk assessments conducted using the resources in the
Managing the risk of psychosocial hazards at work Code of Practice 2022, "in consultation with
workers and Health and Safety Representatives". I have asked for a workplace risk assessment and
none has been provided.

2.6  THE PROCESS ITSELF

Since 26 June 2026 I have been asked to provide medical information about myself, held out of my
rostered shifts, had my accrued leave debited and then been recorded as absent without pay, and
told that a decision will be made regarding my ability to perform my role.

I do not know what this process is called.

I have asked which instrument is relied on and I have not been told. I am not raising that here
as a complaint about anyone. I raise it because I cannot follow a process properly, or exercise
whatever rights attach to it, without knowing what it is. If it has a name, a governing
provision and a set of steps, I would like to be told them so that I can take part in it
correctly. If it does not, that is also something I need to know.

I note that the Health Service's Workplace rehabilitation standard (QH-IMP-401-5) requires that
a worker be informed of "the appropriate dispute resolution process to follow in such cases
where the worker does not agree with elements of a proposed workplace rehabilitation plan or
suitable duties program". I have not been informed of any process.

Clause 1.11.1 records that the objectives of this procedure are to be achieved "by measures
based on the provision of information and explanation". This is a request for information and
explanation, and it is the first one I make.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.  THE STATUS QUO WHILE THE PROCEDURE IS FOLLOWED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Clause 1.11.4 provides:

     "Subject to legislation, while the dispute procedure is being followed, normal work is to
     continue except in the case of a genuine safety issue. The status quo existing before the
     emergence of a dispute is to continue whilst the procedure is being followed. No party shall
     be prejudiced as to the final settlement by the continuation of work."

The status quo before the emergence of this dispute was that I was rostered and working. I ask
that I be returned to my rostered shifts while the procedure is followed, in accordance with that
clause. I am not asking for anything to be decided in my favour by doing so; clause 1.11.4
expressly provides that no party is prejudiced as to the final settlement by the continuation of
work.

I anticipate the Health Service may say that the exception for "a genuine safety issue" applies.
If that position is taken, I ask that it be put in writing and that the following be identified,
so that it can be discussed at Stage 1:

  (a)  the hazard identified, and the assessment on which it was identified;
  (b)  the control measures considered, in the order required by section 36 of the Work Health
       and Safety Regulation 2011, and why each was rejected; and
  (c)  why the restrictions certified on the Employee Capabilities Checklist of 3 July 2026 do
       not address it.

I record that I hold a current certificate addressing my capacity, that I have proposed controls
in writing, and that I have asked for the workplace assessment which would resolve the question.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  (a)  That I be told what the process I have been subject to since 26 June 2026 is called, and
       the provision of the Act, directive, certified agreement or policy under which it is
       being conducted.

  (b)  That I be returned to my rostered shifts while this procedure is followed, in accordance

  (c)  That the period from 3 July 2026 be treated as paid, and the leave debited since that date

  (d)  That the roster and payroll record for the twelve months preceding 3 July 2026 be provided
       to me, so that the pattern in fact worked is established from the Health Service's own
       records rather than from my estimate.

  (e)  That feedback be provided on the work health and safety matters I have raised, in
       accordance with clause 7.1.11.

  (f)  That a psychosocial risk assessment of the workplace be conducted in accordance with
       clause 7.2.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Clause 1.11.2(a) requires that I inform my immediate supervisor. I have done so by this notice,
so that there is no question that Stage 1 has been commenced.

I should record, however, that part of the subject matter of this dispute is the advice of
2 July 2026 and the reporting arrangements within Corporate Services. I have separately asked the
Health Service to identify the delegate for decisions concerning my attendance and return to
duty, and whether any conflict of interest has been identified and managed. I make no allegation

For that reason I ask the Health Service to nominate an appropriate officer to conduct the Stage 1
discussion. If it prefers that the discussion proceed with my immediate supervisor, I do not
object and will attend. I raise it so that it is dealt with now rather than later, and so that no
Stage 1 discussion is later said to have been conducted by the wrong person.

I ask to have a support person and my union representative present at any discussion.

6.  TIME
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Clause 1.11.2(a) provides that "Stage 1 discussions should take place within 24 hours, and the
procedure should not extend beyond seven days". Clause 1.11.2(b) provides that if the dispute
remains unresolved, it is referred to the appropriate management representative, who "shall
arrange a conference of the parties", and that this process "should not extend beyond seven days".
Clause 1.11.5 provides that "no party shall act in a manner unreasonably or intentionally delay
the timely resolution of a dispute".

I am available for a Stage 1 discussion at any time, including today.
