Three things you should know before answering anything.

1. ONE STATEMENT IN THEIR LETTER IS INCORRECT.

Question 2 is built on the sentence: "As the Health Service is not aware of any concerns being

That is wrong. I have raised concerns in writing repeatedly, including a public interest

I have asked the Health Service to correct that statement to you in writing. Please do not answer
Question 2 on its current premise.

2. THERE IS A LIVE LEGAL PROCEEDING.

WC/2024/227 concerns whether my psychological injury is work-related. Several of the questions —
in particular the date of first diagnosis, and whether the workplace stressors rest on my
self-report or on your clinical assessment — go to the issues in that proceeding rather than to
any workplace adjustment. I have asked that those be withdrawn.

Anything you write in answer to this request becomes a document in the employer's hands. Please
keep your answers to what is clinically necessary for the stated purpose, which is identifying
workplace controls and adjustments.

3. SOME QUESTIONS ARE NOT PROPERLY YOURS TO ANSWER.

Question 7 asks what "working memory is affected under stress" means in functional terms. A
functional cognitive opinion is properly a psychologist's or psychiatrist's. If you consider a
question exceeds your scope, the correct and complete answer is to say so and to indicate what
specialist input would be required. I have asked the Health Service to confirm it will meet that
cost.

WHAT I AM ASKING OF YOU

Only the four questions that genuinely concern adjustment: Q1(d) foreseeable risk and the controls
you consider medically necessary; Q4 the restrictions or adjustments you recommend, in functional
terms, with duration and review date; Q5 what "complaint handling" means; and Q8 what tasks or
environments may exacerbate my condition.

On Q5, it may assist to know that the Health Service has itself stated, in the same letter, that
complaints received by Switchboard staff are escalated to the Client Liaison Officer and/or the
Manager of Switchboard Services — and the Role Description they attached contains no
complaint-handling duty.

CONTEXT FOR YOUR ASSESSMENT

I have now been held out of the workplace since 3 July, five weeks, without wages, with my accrued
leave debited. I want to return to work. That is the outcome I am seeking, and adjustments that
make it safe and sustainable are what would be most useful to me.
