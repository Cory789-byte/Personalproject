# The admission architecture — Form 24 v3 + Form 25 (internal, never served, never filed)

**27 August 2026.** How the corrected notice suite is engineered so that the Senior Appeals
Officer, working the only way she can work — fact by fact, each checked against the cited
annexure tab — is left with no responsible answer but admission, on all but a small class of
facts whose refusal costs her more than it costs us.

---

## 1. The mechanism, and her working method

Two operative notices go together, with one shared 130-page Annexure A:

| Instrument | Operates on | Deemed consequence after 14 days |
|---|---|---|
| **Form 24** (286 facts, 19 sections) | the facts | each fact admitted, for this proceeding only |
| **Form 25** (36 documents) | authenticity of the documents | each document's authenticity admitted |
| Part B (15 composites) | nothing — expressly a non-r49 **aid** | none; itemised notice governs |

Her method is fixed by her role: she cannot admit anything she has not verified, so she will
open every cited tab. The notice is engineered **for** that method:

1. Every quoting fact carries its inline tab reference. She turns to the tab.
2. The page contains the quoted words, verbatim (machine-checked against the bundle).
3. So the only live question on a quoting fact is *"is this document authentic?"* —
   which is the Form 25 question. And the provenance ledger below answers it for her.
4. Every negative fact is anchored to a **document she controls** — "the pleading, as
   presently constituted, does not allege…", "the amended List of Documents dated
   14 August 2026 does not list…". She verifies it the same way she verifies everything
   else: she opens her own document and looks. A negative she can check is a fact;
   an unanchored negative would have been a submission she could bat away.
5. Every interval is arithmetic on dates stated in documents she has already had to
   accept. Admitting the endpoints admits the interval.

**The cross-reference the user asked for is the core of it:** because she must go to the
annexure for every fact, and because 25 of the 36 tabs are her own documents or documents
produced under compulsion, *the annexure itself does her verification for her*. The role
description and every document in the annexure are on the path to admission not because we
assert them but because the checking she is obliged to do lands on pages she cannot dispute.

**The consequence frame (kept forensic, not threatened):** refusing authenticity of plainly
genuine documents does not make them go away — it converts a 14-day silence into hearing time
spent formally proving switchboard emails, in front of a Commissioner who can see who made
that necessary. The restrained posture (concede what's available; make the rest grantable)
applies to her too, and she knows it.

## 3. The fact classes and her available answers (mock: 249 admit / 37 not-admit / 0 deny)

- **249 facts she must admit:** contents of her own disclosure, her own decision, her own
  pleading and her own List; arithmetic on those documents' dates. The deterministic mock
  classifies them source by source; the Monte Carlo puts the 5th-percentile outcome at 239
  admitted-or-deemed of 286 (median 245), with expected denials ≈ 1.6.
- **37 not-admits:** the appellant-held tabs above plus riders. None damages the case; each
  document remains provable, and the *fact* usually survives through a parallel fact sourced
  to her own documents (the role via the Review Decision; the 15 Apr email via her List).
- **0 denials:** no fact in the notice is contradicted by any document in the record. That is
  not luck — every candidate the external review flagged as deniable or mis-stated was
  reworded to the source's own words or deleted this round.
- **Expected riders (~35):** "the pleading speaks for itself", "de novo", "as at the date of
  this response". All harmless; the deemed-admission value survives every one of them.

## 4. The drafting rules that make admission the path of least resistance

1. **Fact, never conclusion.** Nothing asks her to accept unreasonableness, causation, or
   characterisation — only that a document says what it says and a date is what it is.
2. **Chronology, never motive.** Nothing pleads perception; nothing opens s 32(5)(b).
3. **Verbatim quotes with the lead-in included** (the Reese 8 Aug 2023 "due to a rostering
   error" sentence now carried in full), **[sic] preserving her own pleading's text** ("the Ms
   Taylor [sic]", "Plaintiff [sic]") so she cannot say she was misquoted.
4. **Negatives anchored to her documents** — "as presently constituted" for the pleading,
   the dated List for disclosure — checkable, dated, and closed against amendment arguments.
5. **One admissible fact per sentence; intervals as arithmetic;** Part B severable by
   sentence and expressly non-operative.
6. **s 32(1) discipline throughout:** "a significant contributing factor" — never any other
   formulation.

## 5. What the admissions build (so nothing is asked for that isn't needed)

- **Stressor 3(a)** is the *Mahaffey* spine: the 7-hour break is already admitted on the
  pleadings; Forrest's July 2026 sentence ("this is only applied where staff initiated shift
  swaps have occurred") plus "does not allege the shifts arose from a staff initiated swap"
  closes the only exit. One proven unreasonable stressor suffices.
- **Stressor 1(a)** becomes a single chronological run — database access removed → directives
  without consultation → the two reported accumulations with their intervals → the hours
  finally stated on 17 May — every link from her own documents.
- **The *Delaney* inversion stands ready:** if the Regulator argues globally, the globe now
  contains her own admitted breaches and her own decision's findings (Section N).
- Sections R (18 Feb 2026 admissions) and N (Review Decision) are kept deliberately: they are
  the record of what is already conceded, assembled where the 14-day clock can turn silence
  into a tidy, citable schedule.
