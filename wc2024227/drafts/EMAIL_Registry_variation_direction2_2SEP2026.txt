⭐⭐⭐ WHY THE APPLICATION IS NARROWED THIS WAY
1. It seeks ONE date, for ONE document. The witness list and the lay outlines still go on 9 Sept.
   That converts the application from "I cannot meet the order" into "I will meet all of it except
   one item that depends on a third party." Very different thing to grant.
2. ⭐⭐ IT EXPRESSLY DOES NOT MOVE THE RESPONDENT'S 30 SEPTEMBER DATE. That is deliberate and it
   matters strategically: 30 September is when the Respondent must show its witness list and any
   expert report — and on the amended List of Documents of 14 August it has NO medico-legal report
   and no IME. Do not hand it three extra weeks to fix that. Offering not to oppose an extension IF
   IT ASKS is courteous and costs nothing, because asking would itself be informative.
3. 23 September keeps a week clear before the Respondent's date. It is a date that can be met if
   the gate resolves this week, and para 4 covers it if not.
4. ⭐ Andrews [2020] QIRC 104 is the reason this is being sent at all. Dwyer IC dismissed an appeal
   for non-cooperation with the Commission. Asking a week early is the exact opposite behaviour and
   it will be read that way.
