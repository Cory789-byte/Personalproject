# WC/2024/227 — THE THIRD LIMB
## Stressor 1(a) has three limbs. The Commissioner worked two of them for seventeen minutes and never touched the third. The appellant answered the third.

---

## 1. What was read out

At **12:18.42 – 12:38.26** the Commissioner reads Stressor 1(a), *"Dereliction of clinical
governance,"* aloud in full. It contains three limbs:

| | limb | form |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | *"maintained an erratic physical presence"* | **characterisation** |
| **2** | *"imposed unassessed unilateral directives"* | **characterisation** |
| **3** | *"These arbitrary directives **bottlenecked emergency workflows** and directly caused verifiable delays to the communication…"* | **mechanism and consequence** |

---

## 2. What he then did with each

| limb | times the Commissioner returns to it | span |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — erratic presence | **12** | 12:20 → 33:53 |
| 2 — unassessed directives | **16** | 12:20 → 62:28 |
| **3 — bottlenecked emergency workflows** | **1 — the reading itself** | **12:38.26, and never again** |

Twenty-eight separate passes at limbs 1 and 2. **Zero at limb 3.**

And the two he worked are precisely the two that are *characterisations* — the category he would
name at 28:04.00 as the thing the other side *"may wish to contradict."* The limb he never touched
is the only one of the three that is not a characterisation at all.

## 5. What this revises

The earlier reading — that 29:17 was the same operation applied to a higher-value instance —
is right but incomplete. The record supports something more specific:

> **The appellant was held on the two contestable limbs for seventeen minutes while carrying the
> third. When the Commissioner supplied a principle about what can and cannot be contradicted, it
> was applied to the limb that had never been put to him.**

Limbs 1 and 2 are characterisations, and the Commissioner had just explained that characterisations
get contradicted. Limb 3 is not a characterisation. It is a number, a call, a room, and a patient.

That is why it was the one that stopped the room, and it is the only one of the three that could
have been.
