## 1. What kind of litigation this is

**The appeal.** A s 549 WCRA appeal, heard de novo. The Commission decides afresh whether the injury is
compensable:
- **onus:** the Appellant's;
- **pleadings:** Form 9A and Form 9C;
- **then:** disclosure, witness outlines, conferences under s 552A, and a hearing before a member other than the
  case manager.

It can end by consent orders (s 558), by discontinuance, or by decision.

**In practice:** five listings in 23 months, no hearing, and **no contested application ever decided**. Every
dispute has ended by consent, withdrawal, admission or a procedural direction.

## 5. What the structure implies

1. **The Commission has never had to decide anything contested in this matter.** The second conference is the
   first point where the issues are narrow enough to decide or to resolve.
2. **The case moves on narrowing.** The next narrowing is the 30 Oct answers and the one-page agenda. The next
   widening risk is characterisation, perception or motive at the conference.
3. **Both sides' language has converged** on a procedural, documentary register. A party that re-escalates now
   carries the cost with Dwyer.
