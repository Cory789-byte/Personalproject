## How to read a member's silence

Almost every silence on the merits is **role**: he cannot advise either side, he will not hear the appeal
(Guide 5.1, 7.1), and he must stay neutral. A member's silence means something only when it is one of four
kinds:
1. something members in his seat commonly say, which he has not;
2. something he used to say and has stopped saying;
3. something a party asked him to say, which he declined;
4. something he cannot say because he has not seen it.
