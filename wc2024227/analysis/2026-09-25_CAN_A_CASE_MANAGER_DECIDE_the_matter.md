# Can a case-managing Member decide the appeal? (researched 25 Sep 2026)

Internal. Sources read from the authorised copies in `documents/instruments/` and the Guide.

## 1. The provisions

| Source | Text (verbatim) | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **IR (Tribunals) Rules r 44** | "If the parties agree, the court, commission or registrar may hear and decide a proceeding on an application for directions." | ⭐ The one route by which a Member at the directions stage **can decide the whole proceeding**, but **only by agreement of both parties** |
| **r 45(2)–(3)** | If a party "fails to comply with the order", the commission may "(a) dismiss the proceeding; or (b) make a further directions order; or (c) make another order… including, for example, a **final order**" | A final order is available against **either** party for non-compliance with directions (the Andrews dismissal was of an appellant). Not engaged now: the Respondent complied |
| **r 41(2)(j)** | Directions "requiring the parties (i) to confer to agree on matters that can be agreed on; and (ii) to identify points in issue; and (iii) to report back" | The narrowing tool |
| **r 49(1), (3)** | Admissions are "for the proceeding only"; withdrawal only "with the leave of the court, commission or registrar" | The 298 admissions bind unless the Commission gives leave to withdraw |
| **IR Act s 451(2)** | The commission "in proceedings may (a) give directions about the hearing of a matter; or (b) make a decision it considers appropriate, irrespective of the relief sought by a party; or (c) make an order it considers appropriate" | Wide power, but exercised "in proceedings", subject to procedural fairness |
| **IR Act s 531(2)–(3)** | "not bound by rules of evidence"; "may inform itself in the way it considers appropriate"; "guided… by equity, good conscience and the substantial merits of the case" | Supports deciding on documents where the parties allow it |
| **WCRA s 552A** | "(1) … the industrial commission may, before the hearing of the matter, call a conference of the parties. (2) The parties must attend the conference." | The statute does **not** itself bar the conference Member from hearing |
| **WCRA s 553** | Applies UCPR ch 7 pt 2 and ch 9 pt 4 and the Tribunals Rules | Summary judgment (UCPR ch 9 pt 1) is **not** among the imported parts (verify against the UCPR before relying) |
| **WCRA s 558(1)** | "In deciding an appeal, the appeal body may (a) confirm…; (b) vary…; (c) set aside the decision and substitute another decision; or (d) set aside… and return the matter to the respondent" | The outcomes available on any decision |
| **Guide v2.10, 5.1 and 7.1** | "The Member of the Commission who chairs such a conference will not be the Member who hears the appeal." / "the file will be allocated to a different Member for hearing" | Practice rule (not statute) |

## 2. ⛔ Correction to the chat analysis of 25 Sep 2026

Dwyer C chaired the s 552A conference (13 Mar 2026; `CURRENT.md` line 91). Under Guide 5.1 / 7.1 **he will not
hear and determine the appeal**. Several chat answers on 25 Sep spoke of Dwyer "allowing" the appeal after a short
hearing. That is wrong as to the hearing: **Dwyer is the case manager (directions, conference, mentions); a
different Member hears the appeal.** What was said about his method, the Benjamin parallel and his likely
directions stands; what was said about him deciding the appeal at hearing does not.

## 3. Answer: can the case manager decide the matter?

| Route | Can Dwyer (case manager) do it? | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Decide the whole appeal on the admitted record | **Yes, under r 44** | **Both parties must agree** |
| Narrow the issues by directions (s 451(2)(a), r 41(2)(j), r 43) | **Yes, on his own initiative** | Procedural fairness |
| Final order for non-compliance with directions (r 45(3)(c)) | Yes | Only if a party fails to comply |
| Decide a contested element (injury, SCF, s 32(5)) over objection without a hearing | **No** | Needs a hearing, before a different Member |
| Record admissions as binding | Already so (r 49) | Withdrawal needs leave |

## 4. What this means for the direction 5 election

- The realistic "decided without a full hearing" route is **r 44 by agreement**: the Respondent agrees to the appeal
  being decided on the agreed record (admissions, tendered documents, the medical reports, written submissions).
  If the Respondent will not agree, the path is narrowing directions, then a hearing before another Member, or
  a concession.
- A second s 552A conference stays with Dwyer (or the same conference Member); a hearing goes to someone else.
- Leave the election to the Commission and the Regulator as the held letter already does; do not argue for r 44
  in any served document without deciding that deliberately first.

## 5. Has a case manager ever actually decided a WC appeal? (search, 25 Sep 2026)

| Case | Who | Stage | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Davis v Workers' Compensation Regulator [2021] QIRC 107** | **Dwyer IC** | Case management; no substantive hearing | Appeal **dismissed under r 45** for repeated non-compliance and non-attendance. [11]: "If there is any further non-compliance or if we get any more radio silence from you I will dismiss this appeal without further notice to you." [21]: "has failed repeatedly to comply with directions" |
| Andrews v WCR [2020] QIRC 104 | Dwyer IC | Case management | Dismissed under r 45(3) (summary only; not read in full) |

- **Published examples found: dismissals of appellants under r 45**, including by Dwyer himself.
- **No published example found** of a WC appeal decided **in the appellant's favour** by the case-managing
  Member under r 44 (by agreement) or by consent. Concessions are typically finalised by consent orders
  (s 558(1)(c)) without published reasons, so their absence from the reported cases does not mean they do
  not occur. WorkSafe's appeal guidance (seen only in a search summary, not read directly) describes the
  endings as a decision after a hearing, discontinuance, or "the Regulator seeking orders to concede the appeal".
- Searches run: queenslandjudgments / AustLII / web for "rule 44", "on the papers", "by consent… set aside".
  AustLII and WorkSafe returned 403 to direct fetches; the authorised texts in `documents/instruments/` were used.
