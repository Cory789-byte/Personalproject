## Short answer: what they are trying to do, in their own words

1. **Change position only by their own decision, on "new information".** "the Regulator does not defend review
   decisions at all costs and depending on what new information, if any, is obtained… we may decide to concede
   an appeal" (17 Dec 2024). Every later move fits this rule.
2. **Concede facts without conceding the case.**
   - The concessions have no named actor: "a review indicates that in fact"; "upon further review of the
     appeal file, the Regulator is able to confirm".
   - The 8 Sep letter names exactly what you must not use the admissions for.
3. **Keep options open until Counsel decides.**
   - "at this stage", "in the first instance", "as soon as possible";
   - "reserves its right to amend this list depending on the case presented by the appellant".
4. **Keep the Commission's file clean and orders away.**
   - "in lieu of ordered in directions";
   - their September admissions were served on you, not filed;
   - their emails to the Registry mention no dispute;
   - Dwyer's instruction to them is retold as a direction to "the parties".
5. **Defend by contradicting, not by proving.** Their case depends "on the case presented by the appellant".
   Their language of reasonableness thins exactly where their own review found against them.
6. **Contain you without engaging you.**
   - "note its contents" in every refusal;
   - formal address throughout 2026;
   - no argument in writing;
   - fences on time (after the injury) and forum (other matters).

### 1. The decision rule (17 Dec 2024)

> "Please be aware that the Regulator's role in the appeals process is to defend the decision made by the Review
> Unit – however the Regulator does not defend review decisions at all costs and depending on what new
> information, if any, is obtained during the course of the appeals process, we may decide to concede an appeal."
> Same email: the Commissioner decides "without direct regard to the decisions made by WorkCover Queensland or
> the Regulator's Review Unit".

**What it does:**
- Presents the defence as a duty of office ("role"), not a view of your claim.
- Limits that duty ("not… at all costs").

**What it means:**

- They will not be argued out of the appeal. They can be informed out of it, by a decision of their own.
- The "without direct regard" sentence cuts both ways. Their own review's findings that help you (an injury;
  employment a significant contributing factor; the roster "unreasonable management action") carry no weight
  in their eyes. Nor does the review's exclusion.

**Confidence:** high. It is verbatim, and Guide 5.1 and 23 months of conduct agree with it.

## 9. Limits

   - Some features are the office's habits, not choices about you: the noting formula, the form letter, "Dear
