## 1. The instruments

### Award: Hospital and Health Service General Employees (Queensland Health) Award – State 2015, cl 23 (ATT03, pp 40–41)

| Clause | Who | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 23(a) | Not receiving the additional week's leave (cl 19.2(a)) | Work on a PH: double time and a half, min 4 h |
| 23(b) | Receiving the additional week's leave | Work on a PH: time and a half, **except** the days in (c) to (f) |
| **23(c)** | All employees | **Easter Saturday**: double time and a half, min 4 h |
| **23(d)** | All employees | **Easter Sunday**: double time and a half, min 4 h |
| 23(e) | All employees | Labour Day: a full day's wage regardless; if worked, plus time and a half |
| 23(f) | All employees | Show Day: double time and a half |
| 23(g) | Not receiving the additional week, **other than casuals** | **Rostered off on any PH**: an additional day's wage, a day in lieu, or a day added to annual leave |
| **23(h)** | **Receiving the additional week, other than casuals** | **Rostered off on Easter Saturday, Easter Sunday, Show Day or Labour Day**: an additional day's wage, a day in lieu, or a day added to annual leave |
| 23(i) | | (g) and (h) do not apply to Easter Saturday/Sunday for someone not ordinarily required to work Saturday/Sunday |
| 23(k) | Casuals | Work on a PH: double time and a half, min 4 h. No rostered-off entitlement |
| 23(m) | All | PH hours are counted on a **majority of shift** basis (cl 15.6) |
| 19.2(a), (b) | Continuous shift workers by agreement | The additional week's leave is **in lieu of** PH penalties; requires a full year and having actually worked ordinary hours on PHs |

### Certified agreements (EB11 2022, cl 9.18; EB12 2025, cl 9.21)
- **Show Day is replaced by the 25 December public holiday** for employees under this Award. So in cl 23(h),
  read "Show Day" as **Christmas Day (25 December)**.

### What that means by status
- **Full-time and part-time** continuous shift workers (receiving the additional week) are treated **the same**:
  cl 23(g) and (h) exclude **only casuals**. There is no pro-rata or FTE test in cl 23.
- A continuous shift worker rostered **off** on Easter Saturday, Easter Sunday, Labour Day or **25 December**
  gets an additional day (cl 23(h)). Rostered off on any **other** PH: no extra day, because the additional
  week's leave covers it.
- Majority of shift (cl 23(m)): a 23:00–07:00 or 22:00–06:00 shift counts on the **following** day. The
  Respondent's payroll witness (Ms Earl) applied this rule to the night of 10 April 2023.

## 4. What this shows

1. **Status does not change the entitlement.** Under cl 23, full-time and part-time continuous shift workers are
   treated alike; only casuals are excluded. Ms Taylor's Easter 2023 explanation (0.8 FTE against full-time
   colleagues) goes to how many shifts he had, not to the public holiday rules.
2. **Easter 2023:** the full-time staff had all four days, but so did a part-timer have three (Conaghan). His v1.0
   line had one.
3. **Easter 2024:** as a full-timer he is the **only** full-time employee without Easter Saturday, and he is
   **missing from the published roster** for that fortnight.
4. **Two payslip checks:** 25 December 2023 (cl 23(h) via EB11 cl 9.18) and 26 January 2024 (PH during annual leave).
5. Labour Day, King's Birthday and Anzac Day: worked; no issue.
