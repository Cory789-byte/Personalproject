## How to use it

1. **Keep 21 Jan locked in.** Don't trade the date for partial answers. If written resolution falls short of a
   concession, the conference goes ahead. **The date is the clock they are trying to beat.**
2. **Frame the post-30 Oct report as "for the conference unless resolved".** Every open item stays live, on
   their timetable.
3. **Fix the four flaws first,** so the 9A gives them nothing to aim at.
4. **Make conceding easy:**
   - Say "these documents were not before the review", never "the review was wrong".
   - **If they offer consent orders, check three things:**
     1. **Substitution under s 558(1)(c)**: RD 69983 set aside and "the Appellant's application for compensation
        is accepted" (your 9A relief, p 30). **Not** a return under s 558(1)(d), which only sends it back for
        another decision.
     2. **Costs.** "Each party to bear its own costs" is cheap for you to give: you are self-represented, and scale
        C returns little.
     3. **Nothing else released.** A concession is consent orders in the appeal, not a deed (rule 9: the
        compensation claim only).
5. **If they propose a phone call to "discuss", reply in writing** (rule 4). Anything they want to resolve can be
   resolved on paper.
