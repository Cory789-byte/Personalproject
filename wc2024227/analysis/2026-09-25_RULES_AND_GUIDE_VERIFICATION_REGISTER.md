## ⛔ Corrections

1. **Costs basis.** Rules r 70 applies to orders under **IR Act s 545** (and WHS Act s 229EA), not WC appeals.
   WC appeal costs: **WCRA s 558(3)** plus **WC Regulation 2025 s 191** (from 1 Sep 2025; replaces 2014 Reg
   reg 132, which the Guide v2.10 still cites): discretionary; counsel/solicitor fees at **UCPR sch 2 pt 2
   scale C**; witnesses at UCP (Fees) Reg pt 3; up to **1.5×** scale C if inadequate. Earlier notes citing
   "r 70, Magistrates Court scale" should be read as s 558(3) + Reg 2025 s 191.
2. **s 552A** is in the WCRA 2003, not the IR Act 2016. Fixed in the held letter and paper (commit e39e10c).
3. **WCRA s 553** imports UCPR "chapter 7, part 2 and chapter 9, part 4". In the current UCPR, ch 7 pt 2 is
   "Disclosure generally" and ch 9 pt 4 is "Alternative dispute resolution processes" (offers to settle are now
   pt 5, a numbering that may postdate s 553). **Summary judgment (ch 9 pt 2) is not imported** on either
   reading. Do not cite ch 9 pt 4 for any proposition without resolving the numbering history.
4. **Dwyer does not hear the appeal** (Guide 5.1 / 7.1; he chaired the 13 Mar 2026 conference).
