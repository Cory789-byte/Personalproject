# *DELANEY* IS OURS, AND IT WAS MISAPPLIED IN REVIEW DECISION 69983

**16 September 2026. WC track. For the hearing (s 558(1)(c)) and the closing.**
⛔ Built entirely from Review Decision 69983 itself. **No allegation of bias, dishonesty or improper
purpose is made or implied. None is needed.**

## 1. WHAT *DELANEY* ACTUALLY DOES

***Delaney v Q-COMP* [2005] QIC 11; 178 QGIG 197** is cited **in the Appellant's own Form 4** for:

> **"the composite course of conduct, not isolated shifts, is the unit of assessment."**

⭐ **It is an anti-atomisation doctrine.** Its work is to stop a respondent reducing a course to a
single absorbable incident. ⇒ **It is the Appellant's authority, not the Respondent's.**

**What it answers here.** The Respondent's defence is the **isolation clamp** at SOFC ¶22(a):
*"human error and **not intentional or repeated**."* ⭐ *"Not repeated"* is an otherwise unnecessary
sentence. A single error is absorbable; a pattern is fatal. **Delaney breaks that clamp.**

## 2. ⭐⭐⭐ HOW IT WAS MISAPPLIED

`RD69983-SYNTHESIS.md` item **E**, from the decision itself at **p 26**:

> ***"Delaney threshold recited, never tested.** Factors 2, 3 and 4 differ in subject matter, period
> and person. **The precondition was never examined."***

⇒ The decision used *Delaney* to perform a **global evaluation across factors that differ in subject
matter, period and person** — without ever testing whether they form a composite course at all.

**That inverts the doctrine.** *Delaney* exists to **aggregate** conduct against an atomisation
defence. In RD69983 it was used to **dilute** an adverse finding by averaging it against unrelated
matters. **Aggregation and dilution are not the same operation**, and the precondition that
distinguishes them is the one step that was skipped.

## 3. ⭐⭐⭐ AND THE FINDING THAT SHOULD HAVE RUN THE OTHER WAY

**The Review Officer found factor 4 — the 17–18 March 2024 rostering — UNREASONABLE.** That is a
finding *for* the Appellant, made by the Regulator's own delegate, on the Regulator's own review.

It was then neutralised two ways:

| | |
|---|---|
| **(a) Atomised** | Item **F**: factor 4 treated as an incident — *"only… on one occasion."* ⛔ **The 8 April request, the 9 April HR escalation, the 24 April follow-up and the 1 May refusal are recited elsewhere in the decision and never carried into the analysis** (pp 21–22, 27) |
| **(b) Averaged away** | The untested *Delaney* global approach, applied across factors that do not share subject matter, period or person |

⭐⭐⭐ **The 44-day sequence at (a) IS the composite course *Delaney* contemplates.** The decision
atomised the very thing *Delaney* directs be aggregated — **while citing *Delaney***. Properly
applied, *Delaney* aggregates 8 April → 1 May into a course, and a course is what makes the conduct
unreasonable rather than an isolated error.

Item **G**: **no authority later than 2009** — *Bowers* [2002], *Delaney* [2005], *Prizeman* [2005],
*Rowe* [2009], **all in the insurer-favourable line**. ⛔⛔ ***Mahaffey* [2016] ICQ 10 — the leading
appellate authority on the very subsection — is not cited, not distinguished, not mentioned.**

Item **J**: decided on **the last available day** of the s 545(1) period, with **no power to extend
without consent** under s 545(4).

⇒ **This does not require anyone to have acted improperly.** It is consistent with a decision
produced at speed against an unextendable deadline, in which every step requiring fresh analysis was
skipped. ⛔ **That is how it is to be put, and no further.**

## 5. ⭐⭐ THE TWO HALVES — AND THEY ARE NOT ALTERNATIVES

| Doctrine | What it refuses | Whose |
|---|---|---|
| ***Delaney*** | **Atomisation** — the ¶22(a) *"not repeated"* clamp | **Ours** |
| ***Mahaffey*** | **Flattening** — thirteen mini-trials weighed globally until most look ordinary | **Ours** |

⭐⭐⭐ **The one-mechanism discipline is not a retreat from the course. It is the refusal of the
conversion.** The composite relied on is **the standing fatigue and recovery condition** — a **STATE**,
not an event: no fatigue assessment, no register, nothing implemented until after 30 June 2024, on
the Chief Executive's own certificate. **A state persists across the whole period by definition and
cannot be atomised.** Everything else is context.
