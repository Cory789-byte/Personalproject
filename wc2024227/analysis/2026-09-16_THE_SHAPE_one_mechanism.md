# THE SHAPE — ONE MECHANISM, THIRTEEN TIMES

**16 September 2026. WC track. For the closing and the outline, not for the 9A.**

## The sentence that states it

From the Appellant's own outline of evidence, ¶1, served 9 September 2026:

> *"from the moment I logged onto the board I was **held responsible for what went wrong anywhere in
> that system, while the means of correcting it** — the directory, the on-call roster, my own roster
> and my pay — **were held by others**."*

**That is the case. One mechanism: responsibility imposed, the means to discharge it withheld.**

## Why this framing and not the other

⛔ **A composite course is *Delaney*. *Delaney* is the Respondent's authority** and is how Review
Decision 69983 applied s 32(5) **against him** after finding the 17–18 March rostering unreasonable.
A composite invites the Commission to weigh thirteen items and find most of them ordinary.

✅ ***Mahaffey*: one mechanism, one proven instance** — the seven-hour break, admitted by everybody,
where subtraction gives the answer and his own conduct is not in issue at any point. **It cannot be
diluted.**

## What the Commissioner did with it on 7 August

Recorded because it is on the transcript, not as an assertion about anyone's thinking:

1. **[55:01–55:27]** He put three items — bypass of the safety mandate, administrative detriment,
   admitted unreasonableness. The Appellant answered ***"It's all part of the same thing, yes."***
   **He accepted it and moved on in twenty-six seconds.**
2. He **grouped the fatigue sequence himself** and settled on the admitted break.
3. ⭐ **Of everything in the hour, the item he said had merit was the directives** — *"that particular
   point is the one area I think in all of what we've been through that there might be something to
   look at"* [62:38]. **The directives are the control-removal limb.**
4. He **reserved** patient safety — *"you can raise that"* [27:28] — rather than shutting it down.
