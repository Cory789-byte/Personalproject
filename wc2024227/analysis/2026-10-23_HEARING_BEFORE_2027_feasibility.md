# Can a different member hear the appeal before 2027? (23 Oct 2026)

> Internal. Probabilities are judgement.
>
> **Read from source today:**
> - **Further Directions Order (3), 19 Aug 2026** (rendered; no text layer), direction 5.
> - **QIRC WC Appeal Guide v2.10:** §5.1, §7.1, §4.12.
> - **WCRA:**
>   - s 552A (the conference is discretionary);
>   - s 552B (a lawyer needs the parties' agreement or leave);
>   - s 554 (documents exchanged at least 10 business days before the hearing).

## Short answer

**The point is right.** Dwyer is the case manager, not the hearing member, so his full diary does not hold up a
hearing:
- **Guide §5.1:** "The Member of the Commission who chairs such a conference will not be the Member who hears the
  appeal."
- **Guide §7.1:** "the file will be allocated to a different Member for hearing… you would like to proceed to a
  hearing, you may request that the matter be heard… in writing to the Industrial Registry."

**And the choice was given to you.** FDO(3) direction 5: "Once all directions have been finalised, the Appellant is
to contact the Industrial Registry in order to proceed to a second s552A conference, or to hearing."
- You elected a conference on 1 Oct. The Regulator "agrees with the Appellant's request" (12 Oct).
- **You can elect hearing instead.** The second conference exists only because you asked for it.

**The realistic odds:**

| Event | Chance |
|---|---|
| A hearing actually held before 31 Dec 2026 | about 5%. Too many steps before Christmas |
| A hearing date fixed before Christmas (for Feb–Mar 2027) without a further conference | **about 20%** |
| The conference path (21 Jan, then hearing directions) | A hearing around Apr–Jun 2027 |

**Electing hearing could gain about 2–3 months,** and puts the biggest clock on them. That matters with no income
and the about 18 Jun 2027 common-law limitation.

| **The Regulator will ask for the conference** | "The Appellant elected a second conference; it will assist; Counsel is available 21 Jan" | Your 9:19 email made the hearing conditional on the 30 Oct answer. Keep it that way: elect hearing **after** 30 Oct, on the strength of what is resolved |

## 2. The decision rule after 30 Oct

| Their 30 Oct answer | What to do |
|---|---|
| **s 32(1) agreed (clean or close) and most points resolved** | **Elect hearing under direction 5.** The remaining issue is for evidence; a conference adds little |
| **s 32(1) partial** (injury agreed; roster causation disputed) | Still elect hearing. The dispute is evidential (the doctors), not something a conference resolves |
| **s 32(1) deferred, or most points deferred** | The conference has real value. Dwyer can ask them in the room what evidence contradicts their own review. **Keep 21 Jan**, and ask for hearing directions to be made at the conference |
| **A fight letter** | Elect hearing. Nothing is gained by waiting |

**Why it is framed this way:**
- **Procedural**, so it is the kind of pressure that works.
- **Conditional on their own answer.**
- **It keeps her "resolve" door open** (the last line).
- **It removes their easy objections:** Counsel, the pleading, the length.

## 4. Effect on a concession

- **Without a conference, their decision point becomes "before we prepare for hearing".** If a hearing is listed
  for Feb–Mar 2027, that falls about Jan–Feb: as early as, or earlier than, the conference path.
- **The listing is the clock.** It is the procedural pressure their record says they respond to.
- **Overall concession:** about 60–65%, upper half.
- **If a hearing date is fixed before Christmas,** the chance of a concession before the hearing date is broadly
  unchanged, but it comes sooner.
