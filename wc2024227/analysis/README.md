## THE MODEL
**Five substantive blocks**, each given a blue(high)/red(low) range and moved by a **shared
decision-maker disposition** so correlated issues move together:
| Block | Blue | Red | Why the range |
|---|---|---|---|
| No authority for the exclusion | 0.88 | 0.55 | Nothing identified in five weeks; ss 17/19 are duties; G03 says the opposite; PS Act s 104 never used **vs** implied power to direct + a coherent theory Forrest articulated on 7 July |
| Direction unreasonable | 0.85 | 0.45 | No risk assessment, no consultation, certificate of fitness, unanswered question **vs** the 5 May request was legitimate and TC Alfred is real |
| Leave debiting unlawful | 0.80 | 0.38 | s 105 never invoked; the absence is MSH's own act **vs** he was not attending and QSuper IP is his own claim |
| No psychosocial assessment exists | 0.85 | 0.35 | Five weeks, mandatory under s 55C, and they say they *cannot assess* **vs** a large HHS may produce something generic |
| Consultation breach (s 47/48) | 0.82 | 0.50 | 26 emails, none about the work; no HSR identified **vs** consultation obligations are "so far as reasonably practicable" |

⭐ **The central mediating variable is CREDIBILITY.** Substantive strength is multiplied by
`0.45 + 0.55 × credibility`. **The traps do not lose the argument. They lose the audience — and the
audience is what converts the argument into an outcome.**

## WHAT THE MODEL SAYS
1. ⛔ **Silence is catastrophic and it is not close.** Adverse risk **17% → 76%**; return **62% → 12%**.
   It is four times worse than the worst affirmative mistake.
2. ⭐ **Three traps cost more than they appear to.** Motive theories, conflating the tracks at the
   mention, and a loose Report A each strip **16–26 points off the settlement position** — the single
   most valuable asset — while barely moving the employment outcome. **They are expensive in the
   currency that matters most.**
3. **The psychosocial assessment request is the biggest *upside* lever** (−8.5 pts if omitted) and is
   nearly free on adverse risk (+1.8). **Highest ratio in the model.**
4. **Timing is cheap to get right and expensive to get wrong** — responding late costs +13.4 pts of
   adverse risk for nothing gained.
5. **AGGRESSIVE is worse than MINIMAL on every measure.** Adding the traps to a strong case makes it
   weaker than saying almost nothing.
6. **Book-first vs ask-costs-first barely registers** (−3.4 back@work, −0.1 adverse). ⇒ **Cory's
   judgement to book first is sound and the disagreement was not worth having.**

## BIMODALITY
Credibility 80% interval under DISCIPLINED is **0.57 – 0.98** — wide. The distribution is

narrows sharply to the reasonableness limb; if it cannot, `B4` and `B5` fire together and the
outcome distribution shifts hard to the good tail. **That single answer is the fork.**
