## What MSH's choice of document shows

MSH produced this role description in answer to a request for the **FRMS delegation and governance record**
(Item 6). It contains no fatigue-management delegation. Its only safety content is the general OHS line (row 3) and
"standard rostering protocols" (row 2). That sits with MSH's own statements that FRMS "only applies to health
practitioners and clinical assistants" and that implementation came "after 30 June 2024" (263–271). ⇒ The fatigue
governance for the Switchboard, as MSH itself evidenced it, rested on this one document.
