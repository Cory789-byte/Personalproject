# Self-represented public-sector appellants who won a QIRC WC appeal at hearing

**INTERNAL. 25 Sep 2026.** Each case verified from the published judgment (appearances line quoted
verbatim). Added as found.

## 1. Cockburn v Workers' Compensation Regulator [2018] QIRC 60
- **Member / date:** Deputy President Swan; delivered 15 May 2018 (heard Cairns 22 Feb 2018). WC/2017/169.
- **Appearances:** "Mr John Charles Cockburn, the Appellant, conducting his own case. Mr N. Jarro of Counsel,
  directly instructed by Ms C. Godfrey of the Workers' Compensation Regulator."
- **Employer / role:** Fire Safety and Security Officer, Cairns and Hinterland HHS [6].
- **Injury / issue:** lower back (aggravation while restraining a patient, 29 Mar 2017). Claim accepted, then
  benefits ceased (ss 144A/144B) on an IME's opinion. **Cessation appeal, not s 32(5).**
- **Why won:** treating surgeon preferred over the IME. [67] "Dr Morrey's evidence overall and specifically the
  results of the operation … are, on the balance of probabilities, more than sufficient to uphold the Appeal."
  [72] "I formed the view that the Appellant was a truthful and accurate witness and I have preferred the evidence
  of Dr Morrey over that of Dr Shepherd."
- **How won:** the appellant and his treating surgeon gave evidence (report, oral evidence, operation findings);
  the appellant cross-examined the IME himself [61]. The Regulator's one-visit IME and MRI-based opinion lost to
  the surgical findings. Nothing adverse said about self-representation.
- **Costs:** [74] Regulator to pay the Appellant's costs.
- **Lesson:** a treating specialist giving oral evidence, with concrete findings, beat the Regulator's IME; a
  truthful, direct appellant carries weight.
