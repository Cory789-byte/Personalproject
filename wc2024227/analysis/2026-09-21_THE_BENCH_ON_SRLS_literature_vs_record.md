# WC/2024/227 — What the bench says about self-represented litigants, against the 7 August 2026 record

Sources read in full or extracted with PyMuPDF on 2026-09-21:
- Dewar, Smith & Banks, *Litigants in Person in the Family Court of Australia* (Research Report 20, 2000) — questionnaires, interviews and hearing observations with judges, judicial registrars and registrars.
- R W White J, *Advocacy and Ethics: The Self-Represented Litigant* (Supreme Court of NSW, 18 Oct 2014).
- Richardson & Boughey, *The Impacts of Self-Represented Litigants on Civil and Administrative Justice* (COAT presentation, 2018; summarising the AIJA environmental scan).
- Macfarlane, *National Self-Represented Litigants Project: Final Report* (2013).

## What the bench reports

| the bench's account | source |
|---|---|
| "frustration, stress, annoyance and irritation" at having LIPs appear | Dewar p54 |
| hearings take longer; "more mentions and return dates" | Dewar p55 |
| "the issues are more confused" | Dewar p58 |
| LIPs "are often in the wrong mindset to negotiate"; solicitors act as "buffers, as they are not emotionally involved" | Dewar p58 |
| the LIP "wanted to 'give the goods' right there… with no understanding about jurisdictional difficulties" | Dewar p56 |
| LIPs "feel angry, fearful, anxious and bitter" | Dewar p58 |
| nearly a third of LIPs "not confident at all" | Dewar p59 |
| LIPs take "four or five times more resources… 'clog' up the system" | Dewar p59 |
| SRLs "consigned to one homogenous (largely problematic) group" | White p2 |
| a plaintiff "must frame the facts in a way which includes all legally relevant allegations, and is not obscured by extraneous material… motive will be wholly irrelevant. This is counterintuitive." | White p8, quoting Webb |
| cases with SRLs "are more difficult and require more inter-personal skills of patience" | White p15, Bar Association guideline |
| "many judges have a negative perception of SRLs as problematic and lacking in competence"; SRLs "increase the pressure and stress on judicial officers" | Richardson & Boughey p10 |
| lawyers report "difficulties in negotiating with SRLs" | Richardson & Boughey p11 |
| AAT: SRLs succeeded in 22.5% of applications vs 51.3% represented (ALRC 1995) | Richardson & Boughey p17 |
