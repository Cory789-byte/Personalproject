## ⚠ RETRACTION — the lexical-density claim was wrong

Earlier files state that the appellant uses **"type–token ratio 0.286 against 0.156 — 1.8× the
lexical variety per word."** That was corpus-wide, and TTR falls as sample size rises, so comparing
a 1,324-word corpus with an 8,275-word one is invalid. The earlier limits section flagged the risk
but the claim was repeated anyway.

**Matched-size bootstrap** — 300 random 1,324-word samples of the Commissioner's speech against the
appellant's full 1,324 words:

| | TTR |
|---|---|
| **MR SHEPHERD** (n = 1,324) | **0.2832** |
| DWYER IC (300 samples of the same 1,324 words) | **0.3541** ± 0.0103 |

**z = −6.9.** The appellant's lexical variety is **lower**, not higher — decisively so. The claim is
retracted, and so is the explanation built on it.

## What the construction actually is

| substantive turns ≥15 words | Shepherd | Dwyer | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| turns | 31 | 75 | |
| words | 896 | 8,074 | |
| content-word density | 44.8% | 49.8% | **0.90×** |
| **subordination** (because/although/while/since/unless…) | **2.2 /1k** | **6.8 /1k** | **0.33×** |
| **deixis** (this/that/those/they/them/it/now) | **9.6%** | 7.6% | **1.26×** |
| explicit relation connectives | 4.5 /1k | 1.0 /1k | **4.51×** |

Three findings, and none of them is "denser vocabulary":

1. **He does not subordinate.** One third of the Commissioner's rate. He does not build nested
   clauses; he places simple clauses side by side.
2. **He points.** 1.26× the deixis — *this number*, *those doctors*, *they*, *them*, *now*.
3. **Fewer content words and simpler words**, repeated more.

The technical name for (1) is **parataxis** — clauses set in sequence without subordinating
conjunctions — against **hypotaxis**, the nested subordination the Commissioner uses.

## The 29:17 passage as the pure case

> *"so for me it was direct calls to this number **but** this number is an emergency contact **and
> then** those doctors the ones that get called for an emergency they're sent to the wrong side of
> the room they're not sent to someone having a cardiac arrest they're not sent to someone in
> respiratory distress we're now not even contacting them by their numbers"*

| | |
|---|---|
| links in the causal chain | **8** |
| **causal connectives used** | **ZERO** — no *because*, *so that*, *therefore*, *which means*, *as a result* |
| connectives actually present | *but*, *and then*, *so* — contrast and sequence only |
| **deictic items the listener must resolve** | **10** — *it, this, this, those, the ones, they, they, they, now, them* |
| parallel negations | 3 — *not sent to* ×2, *not even contacting* ×1 |

**Eight causal links and not one causal word.** The causation is never stated. It is left in the
order of the clauses, and the listener assembles it.

Ten deictic items in fifty words: each is a pointer that must be bound to a referent held in
working memory. That is the tracking load, literally counted.

## Why this produces both reactions

With **hypotaxis**, the speaker does the logical work inside the syntax. *"Because the number is an
emergency contact, and because those doctors are dispatched to the wrong location, the consequence
is…"* The listener receives a finished argument and can accept or reject it.

With **parataxis**, the speaker supplies ordered facts and **the listener performs the inference**.

That single difference explains both things people say about him:

- **A listener who keeps up** builds the conclusion themselves, experiences it as their own
  reasoning, finds it sound — and attributes the quality of the reasoning to the speaker.
  **That is the source of "articulate."** It is not vocabulary. It is that he gives people the
  experience of working something out.
- **A listener who does not keep up** receives unlinked facts arriving in sequence with ten
  unresolved pointers and no connective scaffolding. They report *too fast*, *mumbles*,
  *hard to follow*.

Same construction, opposite verdicts, decided entirely by whether the listener completes the
inference. And it is the reason the register works in a hearing: **a conclusion the room reaches
itself is not on the record and cannot be objected to.**
