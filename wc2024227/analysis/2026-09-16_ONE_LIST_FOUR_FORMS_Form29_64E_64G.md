## 1. The proposition

The Form 29, the MSH objection under r 64E(4), and the r 64G application are **the same twenty-item
list in three different forms** — and, because the Form 29 carries a Relevance Anchor Table keying
each Item to a pleaded Stressor and an admitted Form 24 fact, the **Amended Form 9A stressor list
is the fourth**.

| Document | Sealed / dated | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **Form 29** — Notice of non-party disclosure | **22 April 2026** | Items 1–20 + Relevance Anchor Table (Item → Stressor → admitted Form 24 fact) |
| **MSH objection**, ref K-LM26/729, signed Noelle Cridland CE, **addressed to Commissioner Dwyer** | **5 June 2026** | The same Items 1–20, by number and heading, answered individually under r 64E(4) |
| **Form 4 under r 64G** — application to decide the objection | **23 June 2026** | The same Items 1–20 in a disposition table; Schedule A narrowing |
| **Amended Form 9A** — statement of facts and contentions | 7 April 2026 | The stressors the Relevance Anchor Table keys to |

## 2. What the Commissioner read, and what he declined to read

**He had the Form 29 physically open.** He reads its seal date [25:27]:
> *"This is the Form 29 file on the 22nd of April, 2025, 26, yeah."*

He reads Item 10's heading verbatim off the page [23:30]:
> *"Manager, email, filters, A to E."*

**He then set the item list aside** [26:34], [27:32]:
> *"forget all these documents that you want to produce"* … *"let's just pretend none of this exists."*

**But he read the same content twice by other routes:**

- **The stressor list, in full, aloud** [53:51]–[55:59] — Stressor 3, the 17–18 March consecutive
  shifts with a seven-hour break, the fatigue calculation, the 42% pay disparity, the July 2024
  meetings during certified leave, the October 2024 abandonment termination and reinstatement.
- **The objection** [30:20] — *"I see a very comprehensive objection,"* `wide louder`.

⇒ **Inference.** The refusal to work the Form 29 item list was formal, not substantive. Its content
reached the bench twice over: once as the pleaded stressors, once as MSH's item-by-item answer.

## 3. "What does filters mean?" — answered on the face of the document he was holding

Asked four times:
- [23:37] *"What does filters mean?"* `↑+3.6st`
- [26:13] *"I still don't understand what filters means, is this filtered by language, is that what you're saying?"*

The MSH objection answers it three ways:

**(a) MSH uses the word itself, as a heading.**
> *"Item 3(a) — Complaints received - **server side exchange filter**"*

**(b) MSH costed the search — which requires understanding it.** On Item 10, objecting under
**r 64E(4)(a) (expense and inconvenience)**:
> *"Compliance with a request of this date range would require approval from the Health Service
> Chief Executive to engage external resources and assistance from **eHealth Queensland** at the
> Department of Health to review and search individual staff member email accounts."*

and it **counter-offers on scope**:
> *"In circumstances where the Matter in Issue as pleaded in the Notice relates to 'consecutive
> shifts of 17 to 18 March 2024'…"*

**(c) MSH knew how to plead unintelligibility — and did not, on Item 10.**

| Item | Particularity objection pleaded? | MSH's words |
|---|---|---|
| **8** | **Yes** | *"unreasonably broad (lacking particularity)… it is not clear which system is being referred to"* |
| **9** | **Yes** | *"lacks relevance, particularity and could affect a person"* |
| **10** | **No — expense only (r 64E(4)(a))** | *"Item 10 - various… the expense and inconvenience that Metro South Health will incur"* |

⇒ **Inference.** The party holding the documents, obliged to run the search, and motivated to say
the request was incomprehensible, **did not say it was incomprehensible.** It priced the search and
proposed a narrower date range. The term was intelligible to its addressee.

## 4. The objection is an evidentiary document, not just a mirror

Because it answers item by item, MSH stated facts in writing to the Commission:

| Item | MSH's words | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| **1–2** | *"The requested records do not exist. **The system was upgraded in SPOK, and records predating that upgrade were not retained.**"* | MSH confirms an emergency-paging **system change** and **non-retention** of the prior records |
| **4, 7** | *"Mandatory Fatigue Risk Management System training only applies to health practitioners and clinical assistants. **The Logan Hospital Switchboard staff are non-clinical staff**, and therefore there is no mandatory requirement"* | Switchboard — which dispatches MET/emergency codes — sat outside FRMS by classification |
| **5** | *"The implementation of fatigue risk management assessment at Switchboard Logan Hospital occurred **after 30 June 2024** in connection with an organisational change related to the reporting lines for Switchboard"* | FRMS arrived at switchboard **after** the pleaded period, with a reporting-line change |
| **3(c)** | *"there have been **no 'consequential' changes** to operating procedures over the period requested"* | A stated negative, unsworn — the produce-or-swear target |
| **14** | Line Manager had no Queensland Health mobile device until *"in or around the **third quarter of 2024**"* | Manager's directive traffic was on a personal device in the pleaded period |
| **18** | *"the destruction of the record had already been pleaded at Form 9A… (Form 24, Para 14)… **the issue appears to have already been conceded**"* | **MSH relies on the Regulator's own admission to resist disclosure** |
| **20** | *"PID24-ESU-1130 commenced in **November 2024**"* | A dated fact about the PID |

## 5. The link to the 5.7-second silence

At **[29:17]** the appellant described emergency calls routed to a reconfigured contact number —
doctors *"sent to the wrong side of the room… not sent to someone having a cardiac arrest… not
sent to someone in respiratory distress… we're now not even contacting them by their numbers."*
→ ***(5.7 s pause)*** → the costs warning at [29:45].

**Eight weeks earlier, the Chief Executive of Metro South Health had written to that Commissioner
that the SPOK emergency paging system was upgraded and the pre-upgrade records were not retained
(Items 1–2).** That letter was on the file and was described from the bench at [30:20] as
*"a very comprehensive objection."*

Items 4, 5 and 7 add, on MSH's own hand, that switchboard was excluded from fatigue risk management
as non-clinical, and that FRMS was implemented there only after 30 June 2024.

## 6. The narrowing had already been filed

At **[63:00]**: *"it may be that you managed to whittle this list right down to next to nothing if
not nothing."*

The **Form 4 sealed 23 June 2026** — six weeks before the mention, on the file — had already:

- **withdrawn** Items **8**, **14**, **17** and the comparator balance of **12**;
- **confined Item 10** to sub-sets **(a)–(e)** (aggregate metadata-only count; phrase-family and
  bounded-correspondence exports; bounded Payroll→Line-Manager AVAC correspondence);
- reduced the notice to **seven pressed Items — 1, 2, 3(a), 3(b), 9, 10 and 19** — with the balance
  marked *already produced* or *produce-or-swear*;
- narrowed Item **19** to **18 May – 18 June 2024**;
- pressed Item **16** only to the AVAC export, MSH having produced Daily Staffing Variance Forms
  instead.

⇒ **Inference.** The proportionality exercise urged from the bench had been performed and filed
before the mention was called.

## 7. Consequence for the conduct of the appeal

1. **The objection cannot be treated as merely adverse.** It is the only document in which MSH, a
   non-party, states facts about the SPOK upgrade, the FRMS classification gap and the timing of
   FRMS implementation at switchboard. Those statements are **unsworn** — which is precisely what
   the r 64G produce-or-swear relief is directed at.
2. **The "filters" objection is closed.** The holder of the documents understood the term, priced
   the search, and proposed a narrower range, while pleading want of particularity on two other
   Items and not on Item 10.
3. **Item 18 shows the admissions operating as a disclosure bar.** MSH resisted on the footing that
   the Regulator had already conceded the point. With **298 further facts admitted on 8 September
   2026**, that reasoning now runs across the notice — and in the appellant's favour, because an
   admitted fact needs no document.
4. **The four-way identity is the answer to any suggestion the notice was unfocused.** Every Item
   is keyed by the Relevance Anchor Table to a pleaded stressor and an admitted fact on the face of
   the Notice — which is the very point made in Form 4, Part D, answer 1, to MSH's *"we have not
   received the Form 9A"* objection: *"Relevance is demonstrable from the Notice itself; the absence
   of the Form 9A in MSH's hands is not a ground under r 64E(4)."*
