## L1: Responsibility imposed

**The join:** the role description (Tab 1) sets the standard. The admissions confirm it applied to me throughout.

1. **The role.** Facts **1–13**:
   - continuous 24/7 shift work (**2**, **13**);
   - call queues kept to a minimum (**5**);
   - "maintain Omnivista database… accurate" (**6**);
   - emergency response "strictly adhering to protocols and timeframes" (**8**);
   - judgement "where… procedures not defined" (**9**);
   - pressure and high call volume (**10**);
   - limited supervision (**11**);
   - OHS policies (**12**).
2. **Throughout.** Facts **14–16**: the employer's own 2026 movement forms say "Continuous Shift Worker". The SOFC does not allege this ever changed before 18 June 2024.
3. **Critical function, admitted.** Fact **289**: "Maintaining accurate contact details for medical staff is a critical function of the Switchboard to ensure effective clinical handover and patient safety."
   - This one admission links L1 to L2: the function is critical, and the database it depends on was removed (**55**).

## L3: Rest and entitlements withheld or delayed

**The join:** the one admitted instance, the 7-hour break, sits inside a pattern: rest, leave and pay each withheld or delayed, and each fixed only after I chased it.

   - "a break of only 7 hours" (**284**);
   - the minimum is 10 hours, or 8 by written agreement (**285**).
   - The Review Unit found 7 hours (**258**), so "still did not receive a minimum 8-hour break" (**259**), and that it "amounted to unreasonable management action" (**260**).
   - It was a Monday (**227**). No cl 6.2 agreement is alleged (**180**). The June 2020 fatigue policy said not to roster this way (**239–240**). Travel time is at **251**.
2. **The 2020 agreement does not reach it.**
   - It was signed under a different manager (**17, 23–25**).
   - It was made before I went full-time (**18–20**), never reviewed (**21**), and I was not told I could end it before 1 May 2024 (**22**).
   - HR, 7 July 2026: it "is only applied where staff initiated shift swaps have occurred" (**225**). No swap is alleged (**234**).
   - The Review Unit found "uncertainty" about whether it applied (**254**); the employer "did not mention your shift on 18 March" (**253**).
   - Their denials rely on it (**232, 233, 252, 286**). Facts 225 and 234 answer them.
3. **The sick day, 19 March.** Sick leave, 7.60 hours, approved (**235**). The SOFC calls it paid leave, with no fatigue leave because there was no overtime (**236–237**).
