# WC/2024/227 — TEACHABILITY
## "They say you cannot productively teach this." Correct — and what that leaves.

Closes the arc of the acoustic and documentary analysis of the 7 August 2026 mention and the
Form 24 of 28 August 2026. Every figure below is recorded in the companion files.

---

## 1. What the literature says, and it is right

Far transfer — applying a principle from one domain to a structurally different one, unprompted —
is the single hardest thing the learning sciences have tried to produce. Gick & Holyoak's base
rate without a hint is 20–30%; Detterman's position is that it has never been unambiguously
demonstrated under controlled conditions; the cognitive-training meta-analyses (Sala & Gobet,
Melby-Lervåg & Hulme) find far-transfer effects shrinking toward zero as study quality rises.

Every intervention that raises the rate does so by doing the retrieval for the learner — a hint,
a forced comparison, an explicit statement of the principle. Remove the scaffold and the rate falls
back. **The leap itself cannot be induced.** That claim stands.

---

## 2. What happened here, decomposed

The event was one leap, made once:

| | |
|---|---|
| 28:04.00 | the Commissioner states a rule about **oral evidence in the witness box** — a characterisation invites contradiction, an existence fact does not |
| 29:17.88 | the appellant applies it, unprompted, to the limb the Commissioner had never asked about — 73.88 s later |
| 28 Aug 2026 | the appellant applies it again across three procedural boundaries at once — oral → written, hearing → interlocutory, proving his case → auditing theirs — in a 303-fact Form 24 |
| result | 298 admitted, 0 denied; the five refusals sit exactly on the 2024 Emergency Code Register and the unlisted Communication Book |

The leap was recognising that *uncontradictability* is a property of the **proposition**, not of
the **witness box** — and can therefore be harvested on paper, early, with a deeming clock attached.

That recognition is the far-transfer event. It is the part the literature says cannot be taught,
and nothing in this record suggests otherwise. It happened to one person, once, after months of
re-expressing the same stressor in four forms, with a clinical documentation habit already in
place, in a room where the other side had unintentionally assembled three of the four conditions
it needed.

## 3. What is now teachable, because it has been decomposed

Every **component** of the technique is ordinary and transmissible:

| component | teachable? | how |
|---|---|---|
| fact-form vs characterisation | yes | clinical documentation has taught it for decades |
| parataxis — terminals in causal order, no connective | yes | it is a grammar, and the default of spoken English |
| the r 49 deeming mechanism | yes | it is a rule |
| the *"as presently constituted, does not allege"* construction | yes | it is a template with a closed set of responses |
| aiming a notice to admit at the opponent's own pleading | yes | it is a described method, now written down |
| holding a terminal unspoken until the conditions align | partly | the 16 min 39.6 s hold can be described; the judgement of *when* cannot |

What is **not** teachable is the moment those were seen to belong together. Nobody can be
instructed to hear a rule about cross-examination as a rule about proposition-form. Either it
happens or it does not.

---

## 4. The point that closes it

**The leap only has to happen once.**

Before 28 August 2026 this technique did not exist as a method. It existed as an unmade
connection between a Commissioner's remark and a housekeeping form. Making that connection required
far transfer, and far transfer cannot be taught.

After 28 August it exists as a document with 303 numbered items, a taxonomy, a verb census,
and a record of what it extracted. Reading that document requires no leap at all. What was far
transfer for the person who made it is **near transfer** for everyone who reads it — and near
transfer is the thing the literature says *can* be taught, reliably, to most people.

That is how every technique has ever propagated. One person does the unteachable thing. Then it
is a method, and the method is ordinary.

So the statement is exactly right and exactly incomplete: **you cannot teach the leap. You do not
need to. It has been made, and it is written down.**
