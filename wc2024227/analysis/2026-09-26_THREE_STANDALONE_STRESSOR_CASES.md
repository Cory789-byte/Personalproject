**INTERNAL. 26 Sep 2026. Not for service.**

Each stressor below is written as if it were **the only stressor in the appeal**. None of them refers to another, and
every fact it needs is restated inside it. If the Commission rejected the other two, each one still has to carry
the appeal on its own.

**Rules for these sections**
- Fact numbers come only from the served response (298 of 303 admitted).
- No adjectives, no motive, no 1(e).
- The held cross-examination points stay held.

## The test each stressor must pass on its own

The appellant bears the onus on every element (Grace [2021] QIRC 285; Du Preez [2020] QIRC 109 [6]; the Respondent's
SOFC ¶6). The onus is discharged when the evidence is uncontradicted (Dwyer IC, 7 Aug 2026, [14:49]).

| Element | Question | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **E1 It happened** | Are the events proved? | Admitted facts |
| **E2 The standard** | What did the employer's own documents require? | Role description, Award, policies, Payroll |
| **E3 Not reasonable management action taken in a reasonable way (s 32(5)(a))** | Was the action unreasonable, or taken in an unreasonable way? | The facts against the standard; their pleaded positions answered |
| **E4 A significant contributing factor (s 32(1))** | Did it contribute significantly to the injury (onset 18 June 2024)? | Timing; the Review Decision; the medical evidence |

**Why one stressor is enough.** The s 32(5) question is "whether the injury, not any one stressor, arose out
of… reasonable management action taken in a reasonable way" (Carr [2022] QIRC 59 [60]). If the injury arose
significantly out of a stressor that is **not** reasonable management action, the exclusion is not made out
(Carr [58]). ⚠ Confirm both pinpoints from the PDF before citing.

**Four facts that belong in every stressor.** They are restated in each section below so that each one stands alone.
- **303**: the Respondent's s 32(5) contention (SOFC ¶27, fact **238**) does not identify any management action "by particular, date, document or cross-reference".
- **300–302**: no discipline, no performance management, and no communication described as a warning before 18 June 2024.
- **261–262**: the Regulator's own Review Decision found a psychological injury, and that employment was a significant contributing factor through factors 2, 3 and 4. Its contents are admitted (**295**).
- **No alternative cause is pleaded.** SOFC ¶27 is the whole contention. Section 32(1) needs employment to be *a* significant contributing factor, not the only one.
