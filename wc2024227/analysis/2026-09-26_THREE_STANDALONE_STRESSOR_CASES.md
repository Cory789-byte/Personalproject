# The three stressors as pleaded in the Amended 9A, each built to stand alone

**INTERNAL. 26 Sep 2026. Not for service.**

**Scope.** This covers only the three stressors in the Amended Form 9A (filed 7 April 2026). They are taken
particular by particular, in the 9A's own order.
- Each pleaded assertion is set against the served admitted facts that prove it (numbers from the served response,
  298 of 303 admitted).
- Each stressor is complete in itself: its own standard, its own answer to s 32(5), and its own causation. Nothing
  refers across to another stressor.
- Where an assertion in the 9A has **no admitted fact** behind it, that is marked ⚠. What to do about it is stated.
  The recommendation stands: no formal amendment; confine the case in the one-page note.

**Every stressor must prove the same four things.** The appellant bears the onus (Grace [2021] QIRC 285; Du Preez
[2020] QIRC 109 [6]; SOFC ¶6):
- **E1** it happened;
- **E2** the standard;
- **E3** not reasonable management action taken in a reasonable way (s 32(5)(a));
- **E4** a significant contributing factor (s 32(1)).

If one stressor passes all four, the exclusion fails (Carr [2022] QIRC 59 [58], [60]; ⚠ confirm pinpoints from the
PDF).

**Common to each stressor.** These are restated inside each one below.
- **303**: the s 32(5) contention (SOFC ¶27, **238**) identifies no action.
- **300–302**: no discipline, performance process or warning before 18 June 2024.
- **261–262**: the Review Decision found injury, with employment a significant contributing factor through factors 2, 3 and 4. Contents admitted (**295**).
- No alternative cause is pleaded.
