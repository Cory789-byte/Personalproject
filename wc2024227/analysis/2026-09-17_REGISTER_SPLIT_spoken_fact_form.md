## 4. What this revises

The earlier reading — that 28:04 supplied a principle which was transferred at 29:17 — is **not
supported by the record**. Shepherd answers in fact-form from 13:28 onward, without prompting and
without exception.

The correct description: **28:04 named an operation the appellant was already performing.** What
changed at 29:17 is not the method but the **instance** — he moved from the roster and the email
filter to the cardiac arrest and the respiratory distress. Same register, highest-value content.

The measured discontinuity is therefore not in his speech. It is in the **gap between his registers**:
a written pleading in characterisation, a spoken answer in fact. The Form 24 of 28 August 2026 —
303 facts, 298 admitted, 0 denied — closes that gap by moving the writing into the register the
speech was already in.
