## 3. Track B: civil claim in negligence against the employer (common law)

**Gateway:** s 237. Only possible after acceptance and a notice of assessment.
**Breach test:** s 305B.
**Psychiatric injury:** *Koehler v Cerebos* (2005) 222 CLR 44 and *Kozarov v Victoria* [2022] HCA 12.

**Record support:** facts 264, 266, 226, 247, 260 and your written concerns.

**Main defence risk:** *Koehler*, i.e. no evident signs of risk to you specifically. Also expect:
- causation;
- apportionment for other life stressors and pre-existing vulnerability;
- the argument that the hours reduction was a choice.

### 3.1 Future economic loss: the head that decides the value

This rests on the hypothesis that **you cannot return to full time because of the injury**. The
psychiatric report has to say that. The ECC's "worked and tolerated" pattern supports partial
capacity. It does not prove the cause.

**Robinson v State of Queensland [2017] QSC 165:** $1,468,991 plus costs. Queensland Health,
psychiatric injury from managerial mistreatment, with career loss proved. That is the ceiling, and
only applies if total career loss is made out.

### 3.3 What reaches you

- **Settlement.** After risk on liability (*Koehler*), causation and apportionment, a realistic
  negotiated band is **about $250–480k gross**, centred near Scenario B at a litigation discount.
  The 31 Jul memo's $300–650k used a higher full-time figure. This band replaces it.
- **Legal costs.** On no-win-no-fee, Queensland's 50/50 rule (*Legal Profession Act 2007* s 347)
  caps solicitor–client legal fees at 50% of what you receive after refunds. In practice the deduction
  is usually far lower. Plan on keeping roughly 65–80%.
- **Adverse costs.** s 316 limits exposure to costs after final offers.
- **Tax.** Personal injury damages are generally not taxable (capital, ITAA 1997 s 118-37). Wage
  arrears are taxable.

### 3.4 Limitation

- General period to about **18 June 2027**.
- Alternative: Schedule 5 s 2 (6 months after acceptance or after a notice of assessment). See

- ⚠ Verify whether a DPI-assessment request can be made now.

**Recreation leave.** 41.21 hrs = $1,832.20 gross, identified on 4 Aug 2026 and never applied.

**Long service leave.**
- About 9.01 weeks = **$15,229.85 gross** at base.
- Payroll says eligibility arises "only from 04/09/2026". That is 163 days deferred by the
  "NP Sick Leave" coding.
- Every week coded without pay pushes it further out and reduces continuous service.
- Cash-out is not available (10 years required, Award cl 22(d)). Never use the termination route.

**Continuous service.** The NP coding is the mechanism that erodes it. The fix is re-coding to
special leave on full pay (Directive 12/24) or back pay, and **full re-credit since 3 Jul 2026**.

Weekly compensation does **not** cover this period, because you are certified fit with
restrictions. Recovery is through the Award and agreement dispute procedure (cl 1.11), back pay
and re-coding.
