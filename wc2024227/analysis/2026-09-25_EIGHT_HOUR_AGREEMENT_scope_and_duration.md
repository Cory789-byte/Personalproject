## 2. "Indefinitely": whose words

- Nothing in cl 15.1(d) or on the form states a duration. "For more than one break period" is consent to repeated
  application, not a term.
- **Ms Wright's outline** ("no end date, so that it continues to apply indefinitely unless replaced") is her gloss. The
  form provides for **termination** (Part C), not replacement.
- **The employer's own positions differ:** 6 Sep 2024: the change of contract "did not automatically invalidate the
  agreement" (fact 252); HR, 7 Jul 2026: "only applied where staff initiated shift swaps have occurred" (fact 225);
  Review Unit: "uncertainty" (fact 254); payroll "does not itself assess" entitlement "under any change in employment
  status" (Wright outline); Ms Taylor: an unnamed HR officer "confirmed" it continued (outline); Ms Reese: through
  Ms Taylor only (outline).

## 5. Public holidays (correction to the external review)

The external review cited cl 23(f). **Cl 23(f) is Show Day.** The rostered-off entitlement is:
- cl 23(g): employee **not** receiving the additional week's leave (other than casual), rostered off on a public
  holiday: additional day's wage / day in lieu;
- **cl 23(h): employee receiving the additional week's leave (continuous shift worker), rostered off on Easter
  Saturday, Easter Sunday, Show Day or Labour Day: "shall be paid an additional day's wage"**, subject to cl 23(i)
  (Easter Saturday/Sunday do not apply to an employee not ordinarily required to work Saturday/Sunday).
Existing analysis (`2026-09-25_PUBLIC_HOLIDAYS_award_and_rosters.md`) uses 23(h): correct.
