## 4. What that says about how the appellant presents

- **Volume:** 62.2 dB against the Commissioner's 66.0. Quiet, and 3.8 dB is audible.
- **Register:** 0 evaluative terms and 0 mental-state attributions in 1,378 words.
- **Floor time:** 1,331 words against 8,313 — **1 : 6.3**.
- **Turn length:** median 10 words; only 5 turns of 40 words or more in 65 minutes.
- **Uptake:** 28% of substantive turns produce a word the Commissioner then reuses.

The reasoning gets across when it is delivered in a block long enough to carry a chain. The five
turns of 40+ words include 13:28 (the access/log-in reasoning), 23:40 (the filter mechanism),
27:06 (the definition of "unassessed") and 29:17 (the emergency chain) — and **three of those four
are directly echoed or acted on by the bench.**

The short turns are where the reasoning does not travel. At a median of 10 words there is room for
a fact but not for the link between two facts.
