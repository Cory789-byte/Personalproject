sentence was never said.

**What the admissions give:** the payroll thread (**182–210**), the myHR report (**210**), the figure
(**42 = 1.00**), and the period — confirmed on re-decode **by Dwyer's own echo at 1.00**, not by the
Appellant's own token (p = 0.33).

⛔ **What they do not give: a cure for a misunderstanding.** Their ¶39 still denies a *"true
comparator"* and asserts *"the roster was equitable"*.

⭐ **ACTION — one fixed sentence, rehearsed, never improvised:**
> *I was rostered off the public holidays my colleagues were rostered onto, so my fortnightly
> earnings were 42% below theirs.*

**One named comparator, one stated period, one figure.** This is the only item on this map that the
Form 24 did not solve, and it is a **communication** defect, not an evidentiary one.
