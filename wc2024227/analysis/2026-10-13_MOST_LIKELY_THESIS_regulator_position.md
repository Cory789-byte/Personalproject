# Most likely thesis: the Regulator's position after 12–13 October 2026

> Internal analysis. Not for filing or correspondence. Mention-transcript references are internal
> only. All probabilities are judgement, not measurement.

## Thesis

The Regulator is deciding whether to keep defending the appeal, and that decision is waiting on
Counsel.

The 1 Oct package took the file beyond what the appeals team can answer on its own:
- a pleading built on the Regulator's own admissions;
- set against its own witnesses calling the key events errors;
- no medical evidence on its side;
- para 27 admitted to identify no management action (fact 303).

For the first time in the case, the Regulator did not restate that it will defend. Instead it:
- cleared the documents it could not contest;
- agreed to the conference;
- asked to keep everything else out of writing until Counsel advises.

The Commissioner read it the same way. He paused the matter rather than programming it, and put the
clock on the Regulator. **Most likely result: a concession, or a narrowing that leads to one, in
Nov–Dec 2026.**

## The other thesis

Time is being bought for Counsel to build the defence around the Appellant's weakest point:
- he carries the onus;
- he has no medico-legal report;
- causation could be contested by cross-examining the treating practitioners.

This is real, and it caps the concession estimate. But a party building that case asks for
something (medical particulars, time for its own expert, a hearing date), and none was asked for.

### The real intention

On 7 Aug the Commissioner left the Regulator a choice: "They may wish to contradict that. They may
not want to contradict that" [28:12]. The Regulator is keeping that choice open until Counsel decides
it, while doing everything else he asked, so that the delay reads as model-litigant cooperation and
not avoidance.

They cannot contradict:
- the case rests on their admissions;
- their witnesses call the key events errors;
- they have no medical evidence.

They will not concede on paper before Counsel advises. **So the choice has been moved off paper and
into a room.** The intention is a controlled exit (concession or a narrow remainder) on their terms,
without an order recording that they were made to.

**Pattern.** Every expectation of conduct from 7 Aug was met (cooperate, disclose, talk, be
efficient). The one expectation of substance was avoided: say what you contradict, and with what.

**The Commissioner's intention.** He will ask the question in the room. He chairs the conference and
does not hear the appeal, so he can put his own test directly. The 25 Sep read predicted the question:
"what do you contradict, and with what?". His reply gets them there promptly, with Counsel, the only
person who can answer it.

**What follows.**
- "Para 27 not maintained", or "the break was not reasonable management action": the exit, and a
  concession follows.
- Para 27 particularised and reasonableness argued particular by particular: a contest.
