## 3. The arithmetic (the admitted times, plus travel)

- The shift ended at **23:00 on Sunday 17 March**, and the next started at **06:00 on Monday 18 March**.
  That is a 7-hour break (fact 284).
- Drive home: about 45 minutes, so home at about **23:45 at the earliest**.
- Drive back: about 45 minutes, so leaving home at about **05:15 at the latest**.
- **That leaves at most about 5 hours 30 minutes at home**, before allowing for handover, walking to and
  from the car, parking at the building and at the hospital, and getting ready.
- This is consistent with the Appellant's account recorded by the Review Unit: with travel, the break
  "would have been less than 5 hours" (fact 251).
