## 1. The matter in three sentences
Cory Lea Shepherd, an Administration Officer on the Logan Hospital Switchboard (the Code
Blue / MET emergency-code coordination node), sustained a psychiatric injury (MDD, onset
18 June 2024) from a June 2023–June 2024 course of management conduct. WorkCover rejected

## 3. The case theory (the spine)
**A life-safety function run inside two vacuums — no fatigue framework and no present
supervisor — into which fell an admitted, already-found-unreasonable roster, and a
sustained failure to respond when the worker raised it through every channel.**
- Reframed (per the sent Calderbank) as a **course of INACTION**: s 32(5)(a) is a defence
  to positive management *action*; a course of *omissions* measured against positive duties
  is not "management action taken in a reasonable way" (*Read* [2017] QIRC 72; *Allwood*
  [2017] QIRC 88). Either it isn't management action (nothing to exclude), or it is but can't
  be shown reasonable (whoever bears the onus).
- **Single unreasonable stressor suffices:** *Q-COMP v Hochen*, adopted *Mahaffey* [2016]
  ICQ 10 [51],[54]–[57]; not arithmetical, not restored by outnumbering (*Adams* [2015]
  ICQ 1 [23]); pleaded stressors define the enquiry ([19]–[20]); global evaluation *Delaney*;
  *Carr* [2022] QIRC 59.

### 17.1 ⛔ THE STRUCTURAL CORRECTION — the dominant pathway lost its engine

The v3 model's single largest pathway was **post-production renewed offer (~36–38%, late
Sept/early Oct)**. It required production to happen.

At the mention the Commission raised that the appellant could **give sworn evidence and be
cross-examined**, and worked through the case on that footing. Whatever else that signalled,
it is also the **r 64B(2) answer against his own Form 29**: if the facts can be established
by the appellant's own evidence, there *is* "another reasonably simple and inexpensive way",
and the necessity threshold is not met. The 64G application is therefore materially more
likely to be refused or narrowed than it was on 6 August.

**Consequence: the settlement engine changes from *documents* to *the witness plus the
medical report*.** Every plan premised on "the record gets fatter, then I re-offer" must be
rewritten. Nothing in this matter now moves the Regulator except the 12 August report.

⚠ Do not read this as the 64G being worthless. Two things survive a refusal:
1. **Party disclosure is untouched.** r 64B(2) governs non-party notices only. The Regulator
   holds the source material behind Review Decision 69983 (employer's response, the 17–18 Mar
   2024 roster = Ex C2, the Award extract "included by the employer", the communications
   report recording Hawes 2 Sep 2024). That is obtained by request, not by notice — which is
   what Matheson's 20 March offer ("email me a request… without the need for a Notice to
   Produce") was always about.
2. **A refusal on necessity grounds is a finding that his evidence carries the case.** That is
   worth more at a hearing than the documents were.

### 17.5 ⚠ SERVING REPORT B TRIGGERS THEIR EXPERT — sequence accordingly

The **7 April 2026 Further Directions Order contains no medical/expert report direction.**
Expert evidence is not timetabled. The moment a favourable Report B is *filed into the
proceeding*, the realistic response is the Regulator seeking a direction for its own
examination — which (a) pushes the hearing out, (b) puts a competing opinion in the field, and
(c) hands them a second bite at s 32(1) and at the untouched s 32(5)(b) perception limb.

**Therefore: serve Report B inside a without-prejudice offer first, not into the file.** A
report served under cover of a Calderbank is an inducement to settle; the same report filed is
an invitation to be examined. If the offer is refused, it goes into the file afterwards and
nothing is lost but a fortnight.
