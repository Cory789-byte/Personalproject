# Face-page verification — every judgment PDF in this bundle

Checked 10 September 2026 by reading page 1 (and the order line) of each file.  
QJ path `icq/{year}/{n}` is **not** the same as medium-neutral `[year] QIC n` or `[year] ICQ n` for pre-2014 Gazette extracts. That is how Delaney and Bowers were first pulled wrong.

## Trap that produced the wrong Delaney

| What was requested | What QJ `icq/2005/11` actually is | Correct QJ path |
|---|---|---|
| *Delaney v Q-COMP Review Unit* (2005) 178 QGIG 197; also cited `[2005] QIC 11` | *Wesche AND Vancrete Pty Ltd (No. 2)* (C72 of 2004), Hall P, 10 March 2005 — WHS *costs* after a failed inspector appeal. Not workers’ compensation. Not Delaney. | **`icq/2005/7`** |

Bowers had the same trap: QJ `icq/2002/18` is *Murrays Australia Ltd AND Training Recognition Council*. Real Bowers is QJ **`icq/2002/16`**.

Those two wrong files have been deleted from the bundle.

## Gazette-era search rule

Cite and search Hall P decisions by **(year) vol QGIG page**, then by Industrial Court file number. Do not search `[2005] ICQ 11` for Delaney. That path is *Wesche v Vancrete (No 2)*.

Delaney is (2005) 178 QGIG 197 (Gazette extract dated 11 March 2005, Vol. 178 No. 10, pages 197–199). QJ’s later database key is `icq/2005/7`.
