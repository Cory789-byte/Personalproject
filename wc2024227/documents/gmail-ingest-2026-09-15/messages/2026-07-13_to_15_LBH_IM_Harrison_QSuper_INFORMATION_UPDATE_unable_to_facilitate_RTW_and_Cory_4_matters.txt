I will also clarify that the obligation to provide rehabilitation and suitable duties under the Workers' Compensation and Rehabilitation Act 2003 (Qld) applies to workers with accepted workers' compensation claims. It does not extend to employees being managed through Health Management processes or those receiving QSuper (Australian Retirement Trust) Income Protection benefits.
As such, any return to work under a Health Management or QSuper Income Protection arrangement is subject to the employer being able to safely accommodate the medical restrictions within the operational requirements of the workplace and remains at the discretion of the Employer if they are safely able to accommodate this.
