Dear Mr Sheperd

Thank you for your contact with the Office of Industrial Relations' (OIR) Ethical Standards concerning Ms Renee Matheson, Senior Appeals Officer, Workers Compensation Regulatory Services, OIR. We have reviewed your complaint emails, referred to relevant legislation and completed preliminary enquiries. We have assessed the information and decided Ethical Standards will not be taking any further action. I understand that WCRS will be addressing you separately about the concerns you have raised.

I understand you alleged that Ms Matheson inappropriately obtained and disclosed confidential medical records to your former legal representation. I understand that you allege that Ms Matheson's action is considered reprisal under the Public Interest Disclosure Act 2010 as you have previously been assessed as a Public Interest Discloser (PID) by the Crime and Corruption Commission and Metro South Hospital and Health Service.

I understand you alleged that Ms Matheson failed to issue you with a Form 29 – Notice of non-party disclosure in relation to obtaining and disclosing your medical records.

I have considered your initial complaint in relation to public officers within Metro Hospital and Health Service. I have considered the actions of Ms Matheson and the requirement of Ms Matheson's role to obtain and consider all relevant information in preparation for an appeal in relation to WorkCover cover claim that has exhausted the Workers Compensation Regulatory Services process. It is my view that Ms Matheson's conduct does not amount to reprisal under the Public Interest Disclosure 2010. It is also my view that the information obtained and disclosed by Ms Matheson was relevant to your matter and in accordance with legislation and OIR policy.

If you would like to discuss further, you can contact me on 0466 524 864 or respond to this email.

Regards, Bethany Skinner, Manager, Ethical Standards, Office of Industrial Relations
