Thanks for taking the time to speak with me recently. As discussed, the key issue at the moment is understanding exactly where your employer's consideration of your return to work arrangements is up to.
Clarifying the Current Position — Based on our discussion, you have already provided your Employee Capacity Certificate (ECC) and that your employer has indicated they require further information. Can you please give me an update on where this is at. When you receive the additional information from your doctor, please send it through to me.
Medical Information and Return to Work — As we discussed, your employer should not be making medical decisions about what you are fit to do. The relevant medical advice should come from your treating doctor. Where an employer is considering reasonable adjustments or a return to work arrangement, they will generally need sufficiently clear information about any restrictions and the practical adjustments required. The more specific the medical advice is about your current capacity, the easier it is for your employer to assess what can reasonably be accommodated. Where there is uncertainty about restrictions, it is not uncommon for employers to seek clarification from treating practitioners.
WorkCover Appeal — As we discussed, the matters relating to your WorkCover appeal are separate to the issue of your current capacity for work. The focus of any return to work discussion should be on what you are presently able to do, what restrictions apply, and whether reasonable adjustments can be made. If requests are being made that appear to go beyond your current capacity for work, it is important to identify exactly what information is being sought before providing any additional material.
Communication with Your Doctor — We also discussed consent for communication between your employer and your treating doctor. As a general proposition, I would encourage you to carefully consider whether requests for medical information should be provided directly to you first, rather than permitting unrestricted direct communication. This allows you to understand exactly what information is being requested and, if necessary, seek advice about whether those requests are reasonable before any information is provided.
Alternative Duties — You also raised the possibility of working in other areas where you have previous experience. Generally speaking, the focus of a return to work process will be on returning an employee to their substantive role where possible. Alternative duties or redeployment options are more commonly considered where a return to the substantive position cannot be achieved. That is why obtaining clear medical information about your current capacity remains the most important step at this stage.
