Thank you for your email dated 28 July 2026. We acknowledge receipt of your correspondence and the attached documentation.



As previously advised, the Injury Management Team requires further information from your treating medical practitioner before we are able to determine whether Metro South Health can safely facilitate your return to duties.



The Employee Capabilities Checklist (ECC) provided has been reviewed; however, further clarification is required regarding your functional capacity and the medical restrictions identified to enable an informed assessment of whether suitable duties can be accommodated safely and operationally.



This request is consistent with Metro South Health's obligations under the Work Health and Safety Act 2011 (Qld) to ensure, so far as is reasonably practicable, the health and safety of workers and others in the workplace. While your treating medical practitioner's advice is an important consideration, Queensland Health has an independent obligation to assess the medical information provided alongside the inherent requirements of the role and determine whether duties can be safely facilitated.



To assist in progressing this matter, please confirm whether you would prefer to take our request for further medical information to your treating general practitioner yourself, or alternatively, complete and return the attached Employee Authority Form (EAF) authorising the Injury Management Team to write directly to your treating practitioner. Should you choose the latter option, you will be copied into our correspondence with your treating practitioner for your records.



Once the requested medical clarification has been received, we will review the information and advise you of the outcome regarding your capacity to return to duties.
