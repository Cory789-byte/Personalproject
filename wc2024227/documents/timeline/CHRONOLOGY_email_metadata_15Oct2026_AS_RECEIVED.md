## 4. Gaps / limits
- Attachment metadata was extracted only for Matheson emails from June to October 2026. Earlier attachment-bearing Matheson emails (Feb–May 2026) were listed but not downloaded. Two 250704 NNPD PDFs (Mind & Memory, Our Medical Ashmore) were not downloaded.
- The Outlook search was capped at 100 per query, and the `from:health.qld.gov.au` search has further pages that were not fetched. Health and Taylor rows come from the combined keyword searches. No MSH-origin email after 11 Sep was found in Hotmail apart from forwards, which matches the repo silence map. The QH mailbox (Cory.Shepherd@health.qld.gov.au) is not covered.

- Some sends have no from field (drafts or items sent via other clients). They are shown as "(no from)".
- The repo event logs R33 and E38 were not found by those names on the branch. The repo cross-check used CURRENT.md, index/EVENT_LEDGER.tsv, MSH-SILENCE-MAP-14OCT2026.md and documents/myhr-2026-10-14/README.md.
