# HR Policy C38 — Long Service Leave (QH-POL-163), December 2021 — VERIFIED EXTRACTS
Obtained 5 September 2026. This is the instrument EB12 cl 9.10.1 makes operative for long
service leave, and it had never been read. Quotations are exact.

## ⭐⭐⭐ §1 Policy Application (p 2) — THE FINDING
> *"Queensland Health employees **may apply for pro rata long service leave on full pay, or half
> pay, after completing seven years continuous service**, and are entitled to a cash equivalent of
> long service leave on termination after 10 calendar years continuous service, or a lesser period
> as outlined in section 6.1."*

⇒ Access to pro rata LSL **on full pay** does NOT require ten years. It requires **seven**.
⇒ Commencement was 25 March 2019 (deed recital B). Seven years fell on **25 MARCH 2026**.

## §2 Minimum access periods (p 2)
> *"For leave on full pay - the minimum leave taken at any one time is one day, or one rostered
> shift. For leave on half pay - the minimum leave taken at any one time is one week."*
⇒ One day minimum. There is no minimum block that would prevent a bridging application.

## §7.1 Cash equivalent on termination (p 5)
> *"Cash equivalent payment on termination normally occurs at or after 10 calendar years continuous
> service. Payment can be made for lesser periods … Upon ill health retirement – five years …
> Termination except where termination is due to dismissals or career advancement – seven years."*

## Attachment One — procedural (p 1 of attachment)
> *"Granting of long service leave, including on a half pay basis, is subject to organisational
> convenience **however requests for leave are not to be unreasonably refused**."*
> *"The employee is to be given **timely advice** as to whether or not leave is approved."*
> *"Leave may be taken up to the total amount of leave due as at the date of commencement of the
> leave, calculated by: — determining the total period of the employee's continuous service having
> regard to the provisions of this policy in respect of leave credited for service …"*

## ⚠ WHAT C38 DOES **NOT** SAY
It contains **no provision excluding periods of approved leave without pay** from continuous
service. The only "break in service" definition is confined to a permanent-to-fixed-term
re-engagement and is irrelevant here:
> *"Break in service — For the purposes of this policy, means any break greater than two days …
> between ceasing as a permanent employee of Queensland Health and being re-engaged as a fixed
> term temporary employee of Queensland Health."*

⇒ The exclusions Payroll has applied are not sourced to anything on the face of C38.

## ⭐⭐ WHAT THIS DOES TO THE DATE
Seven years fell on 25 March 2026. Payroll's date is 29 September 2026 — **188 days later**.
The audit excludes exactly three windows: a 2022 block, 21 Jun–20 Sep 2024 (91 days), and
13 Dec 2024–23 Feb 2025 (72 days). 91 + 72 = 163, plus the 2022 block ≈ 188.
⇒ **The three excluded windows ARE the whole of the gap.**
⇒ Restore the 91-day window alone → eligibility 30 June 2026, already passed.
⇒ Restore both 2024/25 windows → eligibility 19 April 2026, already passed.

⚠ And the unpaid period since 3 July 2026 is NOT excluded by the same calculator (final accrual
period runs 23/02/2025–04/09/2026 continuously). Unpaid absence is therefore being counted in
2026 and excluded in 2024. That inconsistency is the question to put.
