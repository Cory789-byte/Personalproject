# The long service leave chain, traced end to end — 5 September 2026
Every instrument in the chain has now been obtained and read. Quotations exact. INTERNAL.

## THE CHAIN
| Instrument | What it says about access at 7 years | Exclusion for leave without pay? |
|---|---|---|
| **IR Act s 134(3)(b)** (applied to LSL by s 94) | — | **NO.** Continuity "is not broken by an absence … on unpaid leave approved by the employer" |
| **IR Act s 90(2)** (parental leave) | — | Shows the drafting: where Parliament excludes a period from the COUNT it says so expressly. It did not here |
| **HHS General Employees Award 2015 cl 22(c)** | *"Employees who have completed 7 years' continuous service **are entitled to take** long service leave on full pay or half pay."* | **NO.** cl 22(a) points to the QES |
| **EB12 cl 9.10.1** | points to C38 | — |
| **HR Policy C38 §1** (Dec 2021) | *"may apply for pro rata long service leave on full pay, or half pay, after completing seven years continuous service"* | **NO.** ⚠ And it cites Directive **11/18**, superseded |
| **Directive 10/24 cl 8.1** (current, eff 30 Sep 2024) | *"employees **are entitled** to take pro rata long service leave after 7 years continuous service"* | **NO** |
| **Directive 11/18** (superseded) | no equivalent clause — its 7-year provision (cl 21) is a payment **on termination** only | **NO** |

## ⭐ THE FINDING
**Not one of the five instruments excludes a period of approved leave without pay from continuous
service for long service leave purposes.** The 188 days excluded by Payroll's calculator — 8 May–
19 Jun 2022, 21 Jun–20 Sep 2024, 13 Dec 2024–23 Feb 2025 — have no identified source.

## ⚠ THE ONE PLACE IT COULD HIDE
Directive 10/24 cl 25: *"**Continuous service** includes service and periods of leave that have been
**recognised for long service leave purposes under the relevant directive**."* That is circular and
undefined. If a recognition rule exists it is there, in a payroll standard, or in the HR
Delegations Manual — none of which is in the chain the Award and EB12 actually point to.
⇒ Do not assert there is no rule. Ask them to **identify** it.

## OTHER OPERATIVE PROVISIONS
- **Directive 10/24 cl 7.1**: "The taking of long service leave is subject to entity convenience."
- **Directive 10/24 cl 7.4**: *"The employer **must respond** to a request to take long service leave
  **in a timely manner** indicating whether the leave applied for has been approved or not."*
- **C38 Attachment One**: *"requests for leave are **not to be unreasonably refused**"*; *"the employee
  is to be given **timely advice** as to whether or not leave is approved."*
- **C38 §2**: minimum period for leave on full pay is **one day**.
- **Award cl 22(b)**: accrual at **1.3 weeks per year** of continuous service — more generous than the
  Act's 8.6667 weeks at ten years.
- **Directive 10/24 cl 12.2**: the chief executive may grant LSL accruing to the end date of the
  leave period, but *"nothing in this clause affects a qualifying period"* — helps quantum once
  qualified, not the qualifying date.
