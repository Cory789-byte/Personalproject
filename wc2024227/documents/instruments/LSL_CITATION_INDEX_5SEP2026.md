## B · ⭐ SEVEN-YEAR ACCESS — THE LOAD-BEARING PROVISIONS
Three separate instruments give access to long service leave at **seven** years, not ten.
Seven years of continuous service fell on **25 March 2026** (commencement 25 March 2019, deed recital B).

**Award cl 22(c)** —
> *"Employees who have completed 7 years' continuous service **are entitled to take** long service
> leave on full pay or half pay."*

**Directive 10/24 cl 8.1** —
> *"Subject to clause 7.1, employees **are entitled to take** pro rata long service leave after
> 7 years continuous service. See clause 20 for payment on termination after 7 years continuous
> service."*

**HR Policy C38 §1** —
> *"Queensland Health employees **may apply for pro rata long service leave on full pay, or half
> pay, after completing seven years continuous service**, and are entitled to a cash equivalent of
> long service leave on termination after 10 years continuous service, or a lesser period as
> outlined in section 6.1."*

⚠ The superseded **Directive 11/18** had **no** equivalent. Its seven-year provision (cl 21) was a
proportionate payment **on termination** only. The right to *take* pro rata leave while still
employed is express in 10/24 and dates from 30 September 2024.

**EB12 cl 9.10.1** routes to the policy —
> *"Long service leave entitlements and conditions are outlined in HR Policy C38 Long Service Leave."*

## C · ⭐ NO INSTRUMENT EXCLUDES APPROVED LEAVE WITHOUT PAY
Read for an exclusion rule; there is none in the chain the Award and EB12 point to.

**IR Act s 94** — Part 4 applies to LSL:
> *"To remove any doubt, it is declared that the provisions of part 4 apply for working out an
> employee's rights and entitlements to long service leave under this division…"*

**IR Act s 134(3)** — Part 4, and it says the opposite of exclusion:
> *"An employee's continuity of service with an employer is **not broken by an absence, including
> through illness or injury** — (a) on paid leave approved by the employer; or **(b) on unpaid leave
> approved by the employer**."*

**IR Act s 90(2)** — the drafting contrast. For parental leave Parliament excluded the period from
the *count* expressly:
> *"Parental leave and flexible parental leave **are not to be taken into account in working out the
> employee's period of service**…"*
⇒ Where Parliament intends a period not to count, it says so. It did not say so for approved
unpaid leave.

**Award cl 22(a)** — defines nothing itself:
> *"Long service leave, including for casual employees, is provided for in Division 9 of the QES."*
(QES = Part 3 of Chapter 2 of the IR Act.)

**C38** — no exclusion provision. Its only "break in service" definition is confined to a
permanent-to-fixed-term re-engagement:
> *"**Break in service** — For the purposes of this policy, means any break greater than two days …
> between ceasing as a permanent employee of Queensland Health and being re-engaged as a fixed
> term temporary employee of Queensland Health."*

**⚠ THE ONE PLACE A RULE COULD HIDE — Directive 10/24 cl 25**, which is circular:
> *"**Continuous service** includes service and periods of leave that have been **recognised for long
> service leave purposes under the relevant directive**."*
⇒ Ask them to **identify** the provision. Do not assert none exists.

## D · QUANTUM AND RATE

**Award cl 22(b)** — more generous than the Act:
> *"In lieu of the provisions of sections 95(2)(a) and (b) of the Act, all employees who complete 10
> years' continuous service are entitled to long service leave at the rate of **1.3 weeks on full pay
> for each year of continuous service** and a proportionate amount for an incomplete year of service."*

**Directive 10/24 cl 6.1** — 6.5 working days, or 1.3 calendar weeks, per year of continuous service.

**IR Act s 95(2)(a)** — the statutory baseline the Award and Directive exceed: 8.6667 weeks after
10 years continuous service.

**Award cl 22(d) / Directive 10/24 cl 20.1** — cash equivalent on termination: retrenchment 1 year;
ill health retirement 5; retirement at 55+ 5; death 5; **termination except dismissal or career
advancement 7**; all other circumstances 10.

**IR Act s 95(3)–(4)** — proportionate payment after 7 years on termination; before 10 years only on
death, or where the employee terminates because of illness or *"a domestic or other pressing
necessity"*, or the employer dismisses because of illness / a reason other than conduct, capacity or
performance / unfairly.

## E · HOW LEAVE IS TAKEN, AND THE DUTY TO ANSWER

**Directive 10/24 cl 7.1** — *"The taking of long service leave is subject to entity convenience."*

**Directive 10/24 cl 7.4** —
> *"The employer **must respond** to a request to take long service leave **in a timely manner**
> indicating whether the leave applied for has been approved or not."*

**C38 Attachment One** —
> *"Granting of long service leave, including on a half pay basis, is subject to organisational
> convenience **however requests for leave are not to be unreasonably refused**."*
> *"The employee is to be given **timely advice** as to whether or not leave is approved."*

**C38 §2** — minimum period for leave on full pay is **one day, or one rostered shift**.

**Directive 10/24 cl 12.2** — a chief executive may grant LSL accruing to the end date of the leave
period, but *"nothing in this clause affects a qualifying period for long service leave."*
⇒ Helps quantum once qualified. Does not bring the qualifying date forward.

**IR Act s 97(2)** — *"An employee and employer may agree when the employee will take long service
leave."*

## G · THE THREE QUESTIONS

1. **Identify the provision** relied on to exclude periods of approved leave without pay from
   continuous service. C38, the Award, EB12, Directive 10/24 and the IR Act do not contain one.
2. Award cl 22(c), C38 §1 and Directive 10/24 cl 8.1 each give access at seven years, which fell on
   **25 March 2026**. On what basis is that access refused?
