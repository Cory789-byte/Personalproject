## What follows from the dates

1. **The material promised "this week" on 3 August arrived on 14 August** — eleven days
   after the promise and **seven days after the mention**. 21 days from the request.
2. **The amended List of Documents is dated 14 August 2026.** The items identifying the three
   directive emails (items 25, 26 and 27) did not exist on 7 August. An answer given at the
   mention about what the disclosure contained cannot be measured against it.
3. ⭐ **The step the bench directed had already been taken.** At 18:31 Dwyer said the starting
   point was to ask the Regulator. The request was made **on 24 July 2026, fourteen days before
   the mention**, and was outstanding on the day. That was not said on the record.
4. **Point 2 of the request was answered by cross-reference, not production.** The Regulator
   supplied the notices and stated the documents produced under them "were already disclosed on
   14 July 2025, 22 July 2025 and 11 June 2026." ⚠ **Open item:** the produced documents have not
   been matched, notice by notice, to what was disclosed on those three dates.
5. **The pattern repeats.** 24 Jul request → "this week" → 21 days. 9 Sep request → 10 Sep reply
   seeking until **25 Sep** → 16 days.

## ⚠ Conflict to resolve

The Appellant's recollection is that the Regulator handed over material **the day before the
mention**, which he used at it. **Nothing in this repository supports a delivery between 3 and
7 August 2026.** Either the recollection is a week out, or a separate delivery exists that has not
been filed. If a covering email dated on or about 6 August 2026 exists, file it here.
