# Cases cited in the 1 October 2026 filing set — verified against source

Checked 1 October 2026 against the judgments in this folder (Supreme Court Library archive,
archive.sclqld.org.au/qjudgment) and, for Prizeman, the jade.io copy. Every proposition below is
the one the proposed Second Amended Form 9A relies on, Part C.

| Case | Cited in 9A as | Verified | Source passage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prizeman v Q-COMP | [2005] QIC 53; (2005) 180 QGIG 481 (C.1) | ✅ citation and proposition | "it is the reality of the employer's conduct and not the employee's perception of it which must be taken into account". Report citation 180 QGIG 481 confirmed by Carr, fn 55 ("180 QGIG 481, 481"). File: Prizeman_v_Q-COMP_2005_QIC_53_(jade).pdf |
| Delaney v Q-COMP | [2005] QIC 11; (2005) 178 QGIG 197 (C.6) | ⚠ citation and holding confirmed **secondarily** | Review Decision 69983, fn 5: "[2005] QIC 11; 178 QGIG 197"; holding as stated there: management actions "joined by subject matter, time and personality" are evaluated on a global basis. **Full text NOT in the repo**: not on the SCL archive under QIC05-011; AustLII blocks automated download. Download it from jade.io (as was done for Prizeman) and save here. |
| Workers' Compensation Regulator v Langerak | [2020] ICQ 2 (C.7) | ✅ | At **[86]**: "The ultimate question under s 32(5)(a) is whether the injury, not any one stressor, arose out of, or in the course of, reasonable management action taken in a reasonable way … The determination … is one made by weighing reasonable management action against other employment factors in light of the whole of the evidence." Martin J, President, 5 Mar 2020. |
| Carr v Workers' Compensation Regulator | [2022] QIRC 59 at [60] (C.7) | ✅ | At [60] quotes Langerak [86] verbatim. Note [59]: "simply because a large number of stressors are nominated does not mean a consideration of the impact of the stressors on a global basis is justified" — consistent with the 9A's cautious use of Delaney (C.6: "only where the Commission considers the connection close enough"). |
| Simon Blackwood (WCR) v Mahaffey | [2016] ICQ 10 (C.7) | ✅ | At [53]–[57]: dominant-cause enquiry rejected; [57] "If … the Commission forms the conclusion that any of the conduct referred to in s 32(5) does not, on balance, displace the evidence in favour of the worker then a finding in the worker's favour must follow." 9A says only that Mahaffey "is consistent with" its proposition — accurate. |
