## THE PATTERN THE TABLE SHOWS
1. **Every condition met produced a new condition.** ECC required → provided → excluded → further medical required → not sent.
2. **Conclusions preceded the evidence said to justify them** (item 10 is the clearest).
3. **No decision has an identified maker or power** (items 6, 11, 20).
4. **No process the instruments require was performed**: no task match, no risk assessment, no consultation, no feedback (items 5, 7, 9, 17).
5. **The financial consequence ran continuously** while none of the above occurred (items 3, 11, 12).
