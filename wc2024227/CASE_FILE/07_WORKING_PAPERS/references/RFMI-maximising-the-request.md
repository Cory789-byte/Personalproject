## B. THE MEDICAL EVIDENCE

### B1. Their questions legitimise the specialist
They asked about **the psychiatrist's own report of 13 Feb 2025** (Q7), the **clinical basis** for the
stressors (Q1(b)), **foreseeable risk and necessary controls** (Q1(d)), and **exacerbating tasks**
(Q8). **A GP cannot answer those.** Referring is compliance, not opportunism.

### B2. ⭐⭐ Q1(d) is the gift — it asks for a prescription
> *"…what **specific workplace controls or adjustments you consider medically necessary** to manage
> that risk."*

⇒ **They have invited a specialist to prescribe controls.** Once those controls are in writing:
- **WHS s 19** — they must eliminate or minimise the risk so far as reasonably practicable. **They
  are now on notice of the specific controls a specialist says are necessary.**
- **G03 cl 2** — the onus is on **Queensland Health** to prove an adjustment unreasonable, tested
  against **the whole organisation**.
- Refusing a specified control becomes a **decision they must justify**, not a silence.

### B3. The current-exacerbation finding
A clinician may properly find that **prolonged exclusion, uncertainty as to return, and unresolved
line-management matters are recognised psychosocial hazards** currently exacerbating his condition —
because **Q1(d) asks for exactly that**, and the **Psychosocial Code 2022** lists poor organisational
justice, poor procedural fairness and poor support as hazards.
⭐ **Effect: continuing the exclusion after receiving that report becomes a knowing failure to
control a notified hazard.**
❌ **The clinician must NOT opine on lawfulness, breach or conflict.** Outside the field; it
discredits the whole report. **Cory argues the law; the doctor describes the harm.**

### B4. Scope discipline
| **Report A — MSH-funded** | **Report B — his own** |
|---|---|
| Current capacity · restrictions · **controls required** · **current exacerbating factors** | **Causation of the June 2024 injury** · chronology · severity over time |
| Goes to **Injury Management only** | His file |

⚠️ **Never instruct the conclusion.** The referral letter is disclosable. Provide the questions, the
ECC, the role description and a **neutral dated chronology**. Nothing else. An independently reached
opinion is unanswerable; a coached one is worthless.
