## 1. WHAT THIS MODEL IS FOR
To answer, at any moment: **which future is actually running, how confident am I, what would tell
me I am wrong, and what should I do this week?**
It holds **competing hypotheses** rather than a single theory, and updates them on evidence. The
discipline is: *predictions must be falsifiable, and the falsifying observation must be named in
advance.*

## 7. ⭐ THE UPDATE PROTOCOL — run this on every new item
**Trigger:** any email, document, payment, payslip, phone call, or a deadline maturing in silence.

1. **INTAKE** — Log: date, time, from whom, which mailbox/channel, who was copied, what was
   attached, what thread. *(Channel and copy-list are evidence; capture them.)*
2. **CLASSIFY** — Which actor (§3)? Which track (appeal / employment / fund / union)? Does it
   match a signal (§4)?
3. **EXTRACT** — Three columns: **what it concedes**, **what it demands**, **what it is silent
   on**. Silence on a specific prior ask is itself a finding.
4. **TEST THE HYPOTHESES** — Does it confirm or refute H1–H4? Did a discriminating test fire?
   Revise the confidence column. **If nothing moved, say so** — false updating is worse than none.
5. **RE-PRICE** — Update the scenario probabilities (§5) and the appeal numbers. State what drove
   the change.
6. **RE-DERIVE STRATEGY** — What lever opened? What lever closed? What is now urgent that was not?
   Does the intersection set (§6) change?
7. **DEADLINE SWEEP** — Any new clock started? Any existing clock affected? Anything now at risk
   of lapsing in silence?
8. **ACT** — Produce the resulting action list with owners and dates.
9. **BANK** — File the source document in `documents/`; append findings to `working-notes.md`;
   update this file in place and bump the version.

## 10. STANDING DISCIPLINES (do not re-derive these each cycle)
1. Income first; everything else is downstream of survival.
2. Knowledge and chronology, never motive.
3. The relationship is real; it is not a substitute for the instrument.
4. Tracks stay separate — 2026 employment material never enters the appeal's causation case
   (causation stops 24 Oct 2024); hardship never enters appeal correspondence.
5. Every deadline is met or consciously adjusted in writing — never allowed to lapse in silence.
6. Proven / arguable / speculative — label everything.
7. Hold competing hypotheses; act on the intersection.
