## MATTER 2 — DID THE INJURY ARISE OUT OF, OR IN THE COURSE OF, THE EMPLOYMENT?

⭐⭐⭐ **This is where the admitted facts become a mechanism.** A psychiatrist cannot ordinarily
attribute causation to contested facts. Here they are admitted, so the opinion rests on agreed
ground. **This is what the Form 24 was built for.**

*From a clinical perspective, whether and how conditions of that kind — the level of demand, the
degree of control, and the adequacy of support — can contribute to a condition of this kind.*

| Reasons from | What it establishes |
|---|---|
| **Attachment 3** — Role Description, AO3 Switchboard Services | Continuous shift work across the full 24-hour period, 7 days; Emergency Response participation; *"strictly adhering to protocols and timeframes"*; *"maintaining call queues to minimum at all times"*; *"high volume call traffic"*; judgement *"where precedence have not been set and procedures not defined"*; *"limited supervision"* |

### ⭐⭐ 2.2 Fatigue, and the rostering of 17–18 March 2024
*What clinical significance attaches to that sequence; does fatigue from a shortened break resolve

| Reasons from | What it establishes |

### ⭐⭐ 3.3 Causation — the conclusion
*On the assumed facts, and having regard to 3.1 and 3.2, was the employment a significant
contributing factor to the injury with onset on 18 June 2024?*

Asks, in addition: the clinical reasoning; the distinction between **the cause of onset** and factors
bearing only on **the subsequent course**; and — if yes — **which** of the matters at 2.1 to 2.3
contributed, and **their relative weight**, *"so that the opinion identifies a mechanism rather than
the employment at large."*

⭐⭐⭐ **And the sentence that does the most work of any in the letter:**
> *"I do not ask you to find that employment was the only cause, or the main one — only whether, on
> the material, it was **a significant contributing factor**."*

⇒ **That is the statutory test stated correctly** (s 32(1) — *a* significant contributing factor;
the 2013 *"major significant"* test was repealed in 2019, long before this injury). ⛔ **It must
never drift to "major".**
