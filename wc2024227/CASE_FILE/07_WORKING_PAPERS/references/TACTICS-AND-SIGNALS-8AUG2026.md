# PART 1 — THE ORGANISING PRINCIPLE

⭐⭐⭐ **He is not trying to win the appeal in the next two months. He is trying to make the
Regulator's minute writable.**

Reconsideration is an act: a carriage officer writes to a delegate that prospects have materially
reduced and costs exposure is real (§20). **Every step from here is measured against one question:**

> **Does this make that minute easier or harder to write?**

That test resolves almost every tactical choice below without further argument.

# PART 2 — THE SEVEN TACTICS

## T1 ⭐⭐ REDUCE THE CASE TO ONE ISSUE, VISIBLY

The mention stripped it to **causation**. Everything else is admitted, conceded by MSH in writing,
or provable from his own evidence.

**Acts that make the reduction visible:** not pressing the r 64G · listing the witnesses ·
requesting a hearing date.

⭐ **Why it works: a defending party can hold a broad, messy case indefinitely at no cost. A narrow
case with a date forces a decision.** Narrowing is not retreat — it is how the pressure is applied.

## T2 ⛔ NEVER SUPPLY A REASON TO HOLD

The things that let a delegate write *"continue to defend"* are cheap and avoidable:
unresolved interlocutory disputes · non-compliance with directions · overstatement · an appellant
who reads as unready.

⛔ **Therefore, in every external document:** no rhetoric · no characterisation of any person · no
complaint about the mention or the Commission · **no mention of hardship, money or urgency** · no
*punishment / hostile / targeted / reprisal* (s 32(5)(b)) · no adverbs of assertion.
**Procedural, short, correct.** Every time.

## T3 ⛔⛔ MAKE THE COSTS CONSEQUENCE REAL AND UN-ARGUABLE

**s 558(3) costs are his only coercive instrument.** It must not have a defect in it.

1. **Fix the ¶37 error in all five documents before Calderbank #3** — including **Form 20 ¶41, on
   oath**. A filed affidavit cannot be unfiled; **correct it in writing, volunteered, before anyone
   finds it** (ledger §10.4).
2. **Correct it on the face of Calderbank #3.** Volunteered, it is a reliability asset.
3. **#3 tighter than #2.** The bare no-reasons rejection of #2 on 16 July is already banked.

## T4 ⭐⭐ SEQUENCE SO THE REVALUATION FIRES ONCE, ON A COMPLETE FILE

**Report → offer → date.** Not date → report (§21). Worth ~12 points of settlement probability and
about four months. **A hearing date is the most reliable revaluation trigger there is — do not
spend it on a file with no causation evidence in it.**

## T5 ⛔ SOLVE THE MONEY ON THE OTHER TRACKS, SILENTLY

24 August deemed refusal (EB12 cl 10.3.6 / IR Act s 29) · PS Act s 89 · the possible fresh claim
for the 2026 incapacity (⚠ only after the report, and only framed as continuation/aggravation).

⛔ **None of it ever appears in the appeal or in correspondence with the Regulator.** Visible time
pressure is priced time pressure.

## T6 ⭐ CONVERT RECALL INTO RECORD, CHEAPLY

**Audio for all five listings** (it already exists; no typing) → **self-transcribe** → **order only
the portion** that turns out to matter. Ask the Registry about cost and the hardship waiver first.
⛔ Never quote his own transcription to anyone.

## T7 ⭐⭐ START SOLVING THE WITNESS-COST PROBLEM NOW — it is on the critical path

Krishnaiah must attend and be cross-examined or the report *"cannot be considered"* (Guide 7.6.5),
and Taylor's **r 62** expenses must be paid in time or she has **a lawful excuse not to appear**.

**Four moves, all cheap, all available now:**
1. **Ask Dr Krishnaiah's rooms for a fee estimate** for attendance at a hearing, and **whether he
   would give evidence by telephone or video** — the Guide expressly permits it for experts, and it
   is far cheaper.
2. ⭐⭐ **Find out whether the Regulator intends to call Ms Taylor.** Their witness list is directed
   to follow his (AFDO: Appellant's list, then the Respondent's three weeks later). **If Taylor is
   on their list, he cross-examines her at no cost to himself.** That single fact could remove the
   largest witness expense in the matter.
3. **Ask the union** (Together — Petering / Moran) whether it will assist with hearing costs. They
   are engaged on roster, pay and leave, not the appeal — but the question costs nothing.
4. **Apply for the transcript hardship waiver** — its outcome is also a useful indicator of how the
   system treats his position.

  to the eventual bench.
