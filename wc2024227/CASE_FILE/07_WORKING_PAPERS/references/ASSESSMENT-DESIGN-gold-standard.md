# HOW A COMPREHENSIVE PSYCHOSOCIAL + WHS ASSESSMENT WOULD ACTUALLY BE RUN HERE
> ⛔ Strategy layer / ready position. The gold-standard methodology to hold against whatever
> MSH eventually proposes — and the additional improvements a real assessment would surface
> beyond the current proposals. Use at scoping consultation. Banked 4 Aug 2026.

## THE EIGHT-PHASE METHODOLOGY
1. GOVERNANCE. Independent assessor (outside Corporate Services; conflict-screened);
   published terms of reference; steering group incl. operator representative + HSR + union;
   Code + ISO 45003 as the stated methods frame.
2. DATA (work-as-recorded). Demand curves (call volumes by hour/day; code activations);
   rosters audited against FRMS rules (count the sub-10h breaks); ⭐ leave × demand
   correlation (the team-wide leave-as-fatigue-control test); turnover/vacancy/agency fill;
   complaint corpus analysis (the recurring vocabulary IS a dataset); EAP usage; incident +
   workers' comp history; overtime; SPOK error/paging-failure data.
3. SURVEY (work-as-experienced). ⭐ "People at Work" — Queensland's own free, validated
   psychosocial risk survey (WorkSafe QLD co-developed, benchmarked). MSH cannot refuse its
   own regulator's instrument. All operators, anonymity protected.
4. OBSERVATION (work-as-done). Assessor sits in the room across shift types INCLUDING nights
   and weekends; structured task-concurrency observation. (The assessment itself applying
   the integrated-presence principle.)
5. INTERVIEWS. All operators offered, structured prompts; EXIT interviews of recent leavers
   — turnover carries the story the survey misses.
6. CONVERGENCE + RISK RATING. Triangulate 2–4–5; rate per Code (duration × frequency ×
   severity); publish the risk register to the room.
7. CONTROLS CO-DESIGN. Workshop WITH the room (the consult principle); hierarchy-ordered;
   owners + dates per control; the existing Proposals P1–P8 tabled as the starting set.
8. REVIEW LOOP. Leading-indicator dashboard (FRMS breach count → zero; complaint response
   times; leave-on-peak correlation trending down; survey re-run at 12 months).

## IMPROVEMENTS A REAL ASSESSMENT WOULD SURFACE BEYOND P1–P8
A. CRITICAL-INCIDENT DEBRIEF PROTOCOL. Operators handle codes, deaths, abusive and
   distressing calls with NO defusing/peer-support step. A brief structured debrief +
   opt-in peer support after defined trigger events. (Standard in QAS/000 rooms; absent here.)
B. POST-CALL RECOVERY MICRO-BREAKS + SURGE PROTOCOL. Defined short off-queue recovery after
   distressing calls; a surge protocol for when the queue exceeds capacity, instead of
   silent endurance.
C. ACOUSTIC + ERGONOMIC WHS (the physical assessment). Headset acoustic-shock limits, room
   noise, console ergonomics, screen/DSE breaks, lighting — the recognised physical hazard
   set for call rooms, never assessed here.
D. QA FRAMEWORK WITH CALL-RECORDING REVIEW (per the APCO/NENA QA standard). Protective for
   operators: the recording resolves accusations — directly answers the blame-absorption
   baseline (¶23). Framed as protection, introduced in consultation.
E. OCCUPANCY CAP. Call-centre science: sustained occupancy above ~80% predicts burnout;
   staffing set so occupancy stays within evidence-based bounds (feeds P7).
F. UPSTREAM DATA SLAs. The wrong-number/stale-roster problem ORIGINATES in departments not
   feeding updates. Service standards on DEPARTMENTS for roster/contact updates into the
   Switchboard — fixes the cause, not the symptom (pairs with P1).
G. TRAINING + COMPETENCY MATRIX. Documented competencies per function (incl. the supervisor
   pathway's training layer) — role clarity made structural.
H. PSYCHOLOGICAL SAFETY MEASURE. A standing, anonymous route for operators to raise hazards
   with feedback guaranteed (cl 7.1.11 made systematic) — measured in the 12-month re-survey.

## THE SCOPING-MEETING POSTURE
Table the mapping + proposals as inputs; ask for the eight phases by name; nominate People
at Work as the survey instrument (their regulator's own tool); ask that observation include
nights/weekends; ask for the leave × demand correlation explicitly; and ask which of A–H
the assessment will scope in — every "no" is recorded in a consultation minute.
