## 3. ⭐⭐ WHAT THE OBJECTION ADMITS (its own words — the most valuable material in the file)
(a) **FATIGUE MANAGEMENT POST-DATES THE INJURY.** Item 5: "The implementation of fatigue
    risk management assessment at Switchboard Logan Hospital occurred **after 30 June 2024**
    in connection with an organisational change related to the reporting lines for
    Switchboard." ⇒ At the 7-hour break (17–18 Mar 2024) and at the injury (18 Jun 2024)
    there was NO fatigue risk management at Switchboard. CE-signed.
(b) **SWITCHBOARD WAS EXCLUDED FROM THE FRMS.** Items 4 and 7: "Mandatory Fatigue Risk
    Management System training only applies to health practitioners and clinical
    assistants. The Logan Hospital Switchboard staff are non-clinical staff, and therefore
    there is no mandatory requirement for them to complete Fatigue Risk Management System
    training." ⇒ A 24/7 room that activates emergency codes was outside the fatigue system
    because it is "non-clinical".
(c) **THERE IS NO COMPLAINTS SYSTEM.** Item 3(a): "All employee complaints relating to
    Logan Hospital Switchboard operational errors are made directly to the Line Manager of
    Switch Board and managed **solely via email or verbally** with the complainant."
    ⇒ No register, no tracking, no formal process. The organisational-justice hazard,
    admitted.
(d) **NOTHING CHANGED AFTER THE COMPLAINTS.** Item 3(c): "there have been no
    'consequential' changes to operating procedures over the period requested."
(e) **EMERGENCY-CODE RECORDS WERE NOT RETAINED.** Items 1–2: "The system was upgraded in
    SPOK, and records predating that upgrade were not retained." ⇒ Paging/activation records
    for a patient-safety system, destroyed in an upgrade.
(f) **THE TEAM IS SMALL.** Item 17: "the Logan Hospital Switchboard team is comprised of a
    small team" and members "can be easily re-identified even if redacted."
(g) **MANAGEMENT USED A PERSONAL PHONE.** Item 14: the line manager "was not issued with a
    Queensland Health mobile device"; leave-request SMS were on a personal device;
    "The Queensland Health retention policy does not apply to employee's personal mobile."
    A QH device issued only "in or around the third quarter of 2024."
(h) **A NARROWING IS OFFERED ON ITEM 19** — "the date range ought to be narrowed to
    17-18 March 2024, limited to emails received from Together Queensland by the Switchboard
    line manager and their Director, or by members of the Logan Hospital Human Resources
    team" ⇒ a concession in principle that the search is possible when bounded.

## 4. ⭐⭐⭐ THE SELF-REFUTING BURDEN OBJECTION (Item 3(a)/3(b))
In one paragraph MSH says BOTH:
  · complaints "are made **directly to the Line Manager** of Switch Board"; and
  · compliance "would require... assistance from eHealth Queensland... to review and search
    **individual staff member email accounts**" with "significant administrative and
    operational burden."
⇒ If every complaint goes to ONE named custodian, the search is ONE mailbox. The burden
   objection is answered by MSH's own sentence, two lines earlier. This is the single
   strongest point available on the objection, it requires no evidence, and it is neutral
   in tone: *"the objection identifies a single custodian; the burden asserted assumes many."*

## 7. ASSESSMENT
The production is six administrative documents about leave. The declines cover every item
that would show how the room was run. And the objection, in the course of declining, admits:
no fatigue system before the injury, exclusion of the room from the fatigue system, no
complaints system, no consequential change, destroyed emergency-code records, a small team,
and management conducting leave decisions on a personal phone outside retention policy.
⇒ **The objection is a better document for the appellant than the production would have
been.** It is CE-signed, it is on the Commission file, and it cannot be withdrawn.
DISCIPLINE: none of this is argued at a mention. It is submissions material, deployed as
quotes and item numbers, without adjectives.
