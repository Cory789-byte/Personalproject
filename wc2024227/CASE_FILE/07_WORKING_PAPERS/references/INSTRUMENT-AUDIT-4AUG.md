## ⚠ UNUSED — GENUINELY AVAILABLE, NOT YET CONSIDERED
1. **Workplace Health and Safety Queensland notification / request for an inspector.**
   A worker may raise an unmanaged psychosocial risk with the regulator; an inspector can
   attend and issue an improvement notice. An improvement notice would (a) compel the
   assessment and (b) be devastating evidence in both tracks. It is a protected act.
   ⚠ Cost: it is escalation to an external regulator while a consultative process is on
   foot; it hands MSH a "he escalates everything" narrative and cannot be withdrawn.
   POSTURE: hold until Stage 2 has been given its run, or until an adverse act occurs.
2. **HSR election (WHS Act ss 50–72).** If the work group has no Health and Safety
   Representative, workers may request that one be elected and the PCBU must facilitate it.
   Non-adversarial, structural, and it creates a standing consultation channel with
   statutory powers (including PINs) that does not depend on him personally.
   POSTURE: raise at the Stage 1/2 discussion as a constructive proposal. Low cost, high
   structural value, entirely consistent with the tabled package.
3. **⭐ AD Act 1991 (Qld) — impairment discrimination / failure to make reasonable
   adjustments. LIMITATION ~8 SEPTEMBER 2026.** Flagged repeatedly; still not dated with
   Petering. This is the only instrument in the matter with a running limitation clock
   that has not been secured. It must be decided (lodge or consciously let lapse) BEFORE
   early September — not drifted past.
4. **Human Rights Act 2019 ss 25, 26(2), 58 / QHRC complaint** — public-entity obligations;
   pairs with the AD Act complaint rather than standing alone.

## THE ONE-LINE ANSWER
The instruments are close to fully deployed. The gaps are not tactical — they are
(a) the AD Act limitation, (b) the two WHS instruments (regulator, HSR) deliberately
unspent, and (c) runway, which is not an instrument at all but decides whether the
instruments can be held long enough to work.
