## HOW TO READ THIS

Three things run at once through the hour, and they do not move together:
**(A) what the Appellant does** · **(B) what the bench makes of him** · **(C) what accrues against
us.** The Appellant's stock falls for the first twenty minutes, turns at twenty-two, and rises
steadily to the end. **Our exposure runs on a different clock entirely, and it peaks at a moment
when he is doing badly.**
