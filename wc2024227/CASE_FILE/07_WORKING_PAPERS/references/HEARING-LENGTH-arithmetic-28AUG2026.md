## 1. WHY THE APPELLANT'S CASE IS SHORT

### 1.1 The Form 24 removes the whole documentary layer
308 facts across 19 sections, 39 annexure tabs, plus a **Form 25** notice to admit documents.

⇒ On the r 49 mechanics, **facts not answered within 14 days are deemed admitted**. That means:
- **no tender fight** — authenticity is dealt with on the papers by the Form 25;
- **no chronology led orally** — the dates, the emails, the roster, the payroll, the AVAC delay,
  the FRMS breach, the delegate suppression are all on the page before anyone is sworn;
- **no witness needed to prove a document into evidence.**

⭐ In an ordinary self-represented psychological-injury appeal, **that layer is a day to a day and a
half of court time.** Here it is zero.

### 1.2 The outlines are one page each, by direction
Direction 2 caps each outline at **one A4 page**. The four built are Shepherd (20 topics, first
person), Jeffrey, Harrison-Jones, Conaghan.

⇒ Evidence in chief for the three lay witnesses is confined to what is on their page. **Ten to
fifteen minutes each in chief.** None of them touches causation or medicine, so cross has very
little surface: they say what the roster was, what the emails were, what the culture was, and that
they observed no psychological difficulty before 2024.

### 1.3 The Appellant's own evidence in chief is short *because the facts are admitted*
His outline is 20 topics. With the Form 24 standing, most of those topics are **adopted, not
proved**. ⇒ **Twenty to forty minutes in chief.**

⚠ **Cross-examination is the one uncapped block in the entire hearing** — see §4.

## 4. ⚠ WHAT "SHORT" ACTUALLY COSTS

⭐ **A short hearing is a hearing where the Appellant gives evidence, is cross-examined, and almost
nothing else happens.**

⇒ The shorter the hearing, the more of the case rests on **one block of cross-examination of one
person**. There is no documentary contest to hide in, no long medical debate to dilute it, no
parade of witnesses to spread the risk.

**The Form 24 has converted a long documentary hearing into a short credibility hearing.** That is
the right trade — but it should be made with eyes open, because:
- the **37 not-admits** from the mock are the topics cross will go to first;
- everything not admitted must be proved **out of his own mouth**;
- and the 7 August transcript shows the tempo he answers at, which is his strength — but sustained
  cross is a different exercise from a sixty-five minute mention.

⇒ **Preparation should be weighted to cross-examination survival, not to more documents.**

## 5. ⛔⛔ THE TWO THINGS THAT WOULD MAKE IT LONG — BOTH ARE FIXABLE NOW

### 5.1 The 26 October 2022 Anxiety/ADHD record
Tab 27 row 32: *"denies untrue the fact because the records show **a past medical history of Anxiety
and ADHD from 26 October 2022** which is **missing from Exhibit A5**."*

⇒ **The only specific evidentiary assertion the Regulator has made anywhere in the proceeding.**
If it surfaces first at the hearing, it produces: an application to adjourn, a supplementary
psychiatric report, and a second hearing day. If it is obtained **now** and put to Dr Krishnaiah
**now**, it is dealt with in a paragraph.

⚠ It is **not** fatal — s 32(1) requires employment to be ***a*** significant contributing factor,
not the only one, and aggravation of a pre-existing condition is compensable on its own terms. But
it must be met in the report, not in the room.

### 5.2 The five false "Admitted Fact" tags in the Form 9A
See `FORM9A-ADMITTED-FACT-TAGS-ERROR.md`. ¶¶1.2, 1.3 (×2), 1.4 and 3(d) assert as **admitted** five
facts that are **not admitted in the form pleaded** — including the *"clean psychiatric baseline"*,
which is pleaded **against a document the Respondent says exists**.

⇒ If those tags stand into the hearing, the medical spine of the case has to be **proved orally**
instead of read. **That is the difference between a half-day case and a two-day case, and it is
entirely self-inflicted.**

## 7. WHY THIS *IS* LIKELY ONE OF THE SHORTEST WC APPEALS HE WILL LIST

1. ⭐ **The facts are admitted.** Most WC appeals spend day one proving the chronology.
2. ⭐ **The Respondent's own review decision found both limbs in the Appellant's favour**, and its
   contents are admitted.
3. ⭐ **The exclusion case is one unparticularised sentence** with no witness, no act and no document
   behind it.
4. ⭐ **The outlines are capped at one page** by direction.
5. ⭐ **Only one witness on the other side is structurally necessary.**

⇒ **The hearing is short because the Respondent's case is short.** That is not the same as the
appeal being easy — it means the whole appeal will be decided in a small number of hours, most of
them spent on the Appellant in the box and Ms Taylor under cross.
