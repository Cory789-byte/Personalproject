## 2. ⛔⛔ IT MUST NOT BE RAISED IN THE APPEAL — two reasons, both serious

**1. On its face an EAP referral is neutral to positive.** Offering support is what a reasonable
employer does. **Attacking it reads as resenting an offer of help, and a tribunal will not like
it.**

**2. ⛔⛔ It walks straight into s 32(5)(b).** *"They offered support and he experienced it as
hostile"* **is the perception limb in one sentence** — and the Regulator has **not** pleaded it.
**This is among the most dangerous topics he could open.**

**3. And it is out of time anyway.** The strongest instances — the **31 July 2026** RFMI and the
**4 August 2026** Stage 1 acknowledgment — are **2026 employment-track documents**. For the appeal
they are **post-injury**, and **SOFC ¶25 pleads post-injury matters are not relevant.** ⇒ Raising
them forfeits a shield he currently gets for free (same logic as the 2025 fatigue refusal —
`THE-FATIGUE-SEQUENCE.md` Part 6).

## 3. ⭐ WHERE IT IS GENUINELY USEFUL — the employment track

**The legitimate point is not about the EAP. It is about knowledge.**

⭐⭐ **An employer that attaches a mental-health support line to its own correspondence has, in that
act, recognised that the communication is capable of causing psychological harm.** On the day each
letter went out, **the organisation was on notice of the foreseeability of psychological impact.**

That bears on the employment-track questions, where the conduct is in time:
- **WHS Act ss 17 and 19** — psychosocial hazard management;
- the **AD Act positive duty** (window running to 2027);
- **HR Policy G3** and the reasonable-adjustment obligation.

⚠ **But keep it in proportion.** ⭐⭐⭐ **The G3 asymmetry already recorded in
`confirmed-record.md` is the far sharper version of the same argument:** the same Director treated
the **8 September 2025** attendance matter as **conduct** — G3 never invoked, no medical inquiry,
the psychological injury never mentioned — and the **31 July 2026** fit-with-adjustments
certificate as **medical**, with G3 expressly invoked and nine questions in seven days.

⇒ **That asymmetry is the argument. The EAP boilerplate is texture on it, not a point of its own.**

`msh-document-quality-comparison.md`.

⭐ **What it proves:** the block was pasted, not composed. **That supports "template," not

⛔ **Never use it to mock.** Sneering at a spelling error costs more credibility than the point is
worth, and it invites the same scrutiny of his own documents.

## 5. ⚠⚠ THE RISK THAT RUNS THE OTHER WAY — and it is real

**Two edges:**

1. ⛔ **If the employer can show it repeatedly offered support**, that is a mitigation point for
   them on reasonableness, and potentially a contributory point on the medical side.
2. ⛔⛔ **He has told them, in writing, that he was *"accessing Telus counselling services"* over a
   two-month period.** ⭐ That is helpful in itself — it shows he engaged. ⚠ **But it puts his EAP
   engagement on their record, and it is a doorway to a request for EAP counselling notes** —
   material with the same privacy sensitivities as the rest of his medical information, and the
   subject of a live complaint about medical information being obtained without a Form 29.

⇒ ⛔⛔ **Do not open a topic that invites a request for EAP records.**

## 6. THE RULE

**Interesting. Real. Small. And routed:**

- ⭐ **Available in the employment track**, as evidence of knowledge — **and only behind the G3

- ⛔ **Never as a complaint about being offered support.**
