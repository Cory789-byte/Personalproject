## 0. THE COMMERCIAL REALITY — why this matters for how he pitches it
A QIRC **statutory appeal** (s 550 WCRA) is, on its own, a poor commercial proposition for a
plaintiff firm: modest statutory entitlements, costs generally lie where they fall, and the work
is heavy. What makes a file like this attractive is the **common-law damages claim sitting behind
it** — scheme-insured (WorkCover's money, not the hospital's), and in his case **expressly
preserved** by deed cl 8 (unqualified carve-out).
⭐ **THEREFORE THE PITCH IS NOT "please run my appeal". IT IS: "there is a preserved common-law
claim; the appeal is the gateway to it, and the gateway is already 80% built."**

## 1. THRESHOLD RESEARCH — the common-law pathway (VERIFY before relying)
- The **5% DPI threshold** introduced in 2013 was **repealed in 2015**; for injuries after
  31 Jan 2015 a common-law claim may be pursued regardless of DPI, subject to proving negligence.
- **20% DPI** is the line at which a worker may accept the statutory lump sum **and** pursue
  damages without electing; below that an election is required.
- **Limitation: 3 years from injury.** Injury pleaded **18 June 2024 ⇒ approximately 18 June
  2027.** ⚠ Notice-of-claim steps under WCRA Ch 5 must be commenced well before that. **This is a
  hard date and it is now under two years away.** → add to the clocks register.
- ⛔ **Several public sources still state the psychiatric test as "the major significant
  contributing factor".** That is the pre-2019 test. This file's own verification against ATT26
  (authorised consolidation, current 1 Jan 2025) establishes the test as **"a significant
  contributing factor"**. Do not adopt the websites over the verified source — but expect
  opponents and even some practitioners to quote the old test.
- ⚠ A common-law claim requires **negligence/breach of duty**, which is a different and harder
  question than s 32(1) compensability. Do not assume the appeal's strength transfers directly.

whether the firm's agreement leaves him liable for disbursements if the matter fails. He cannot
carry a costs risk in his present position. **Get the answer in writing before signing anything.**
