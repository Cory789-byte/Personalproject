## 1. ⭐⭐⭐ WHAT THE PRIOR DOES TO THE AI COMMENT

> *"when I read stuff and I start to see terms and language and **complexities of language that go

⇒ ⭐⭐ **Read against a man he had already assessed, that sentence means: *this does not sound like
you*.** It is a complaint about **register**, not about honesty.

## 6. ⚠ THE HONEST COUNTERWEIGHTS

1. ⚠ **A high opinion raises the bar, it does not lower it.** Look at how he handled him:
   *"Yes or no"* **three times** in eighteen seconds [40:16–40:33]; *"answer my question first"*;
   cutting him off mid-sentence at [40:21] while he was giving the real answer. ⇒ **That is not
   indulgence. That is a judge who thinks the person can take it.**
2. ⛔ **It cuts the other way on compliance.** If he rates the Appellant as capable, he will be
   **less** forgiving of a missed direction, not more. **Andrews [2020] QIRC 104 is the case where
   he dismissed an appeal for non-cooperation with the Commission.** ⇒ **Never let 9 September pass
   without either the report or an extension application.**
3. ⛔ **And it cuts against the filings.** A judge who thinks you are bright and then reads an
   AI-register document does not conclude you are dishonest — he concludes **you are not showing
   him your own thinking.** ⇒ **That is the real indictment in [09:02], and the only cure is the
   plainer register.**
4. ⛔⛔ **The March remark can never be used.** It was said in a **confidential conference**, it is
   a personal observation, it has **no evidentiary value whatever**, and referring to it in any
   filing, letter or hearing would be improper and would damage him. ⚠ **It is context for how to
   pitch the work. It is not a card.**
