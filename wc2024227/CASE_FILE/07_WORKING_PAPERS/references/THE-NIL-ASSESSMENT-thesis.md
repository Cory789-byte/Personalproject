## 1. WHAT IT MEANS STRUCTURALLY
The WHS Regulation runs identify → assess → control → review. If no assessment was ever done,
the sequence never started. Consequences:
- There are no controls to review, because none were ever selected against identified hazards.
  Reg 38 review obligations have nothing to operate on.
- "EXISTING CONTROLS IDENTIFIED: None identified" (the matrix column) stops being one operator's
  vantage point and becomes structurally necessary — you cannot have controls for hazards you
  never identified.
- The breach is not an inference from poor outcomes. It is an ABSENCE, provable by a nil record.

## 2. WHAT IT DOES TO EACH MSH DEFENCE
| Their line | What the nil assessment does to it |
|---|---|
| "No concerns were raised" (RFMI Q2) | They never installed the mechanism for concerns to surface — no survey, no register, no assessment. Silence is not evidence of safety where nobody asked. |
| "This is one employee's perception" | Neither side has data — but only ONE side had the legal duty to obtain it. The absence of data is their breach, not his weakness. |
| "Reasonable management action, reasonably taken" (s 32(5)) | Every management action — rostering, substitutions, exclusion, complaint handling — was taken **without any assessment of the psychosocial risk of the work being managed**. Action taken blind is hard to characterise as reasonable IN THE MANNER, which is the contested centre. |
| "The role is unchanged / he cannot perform it" | The role's psychological demands have never been assessed, so the employer's own position on what the role demands rests on nothing but the position description. |

## 3. WHY IT ALSO EXPLAINS THE INJURY
No assessment → no demand data → no evidence-based rostering → within-shift fatigue absorbed by
personal leave → no complaint-response standard → organisational-justice failure → injury. The
absence is not a technicality alongside the harm; it is the mechanism of the harm.

## 4. IT EXTENDS ABOVE THE ROOM
If Corporate Services as a LINE has never been assessed, the unassessed unit is not a room but a
management structure. That reframes the matter from "one worker's complaint" to "a division
operating without the first step of the WHS duty" — and it is the honest frame for the CE letter's
five questions and any later systemic pathway.

## 6. THEIR PREDICTABLE COUNTER-MOVE
Once they realise the gap, the cheap fix is a rapid desktop "assessment" — no survey, no
observation, business-hours walkthrough, findings low. Pre-empted by the tabled methodology:
the eight phases, the People at Work nomination, the multi-shift observation requirement, and
scope (a)-(h) are already on the record as what an adequate assessment looks like. Any thinner
product is measurable against a document they were given in advance, in consultation.
⚠ Also note: an assessment done in Aug 2026 cannot retrospectively supply controls for the
period in which the injury occurred. A new assessment answers the future, never the past.

## 7. EFFECT ON THE NUMBERS (conditional)
Unproven, it changes nothing. On a nil return, the s 32(5) manner limb strengthens materially
(the whole course of management action was taken without the assessment the Regulation requires):
appeal-win band moves from ~35% toward the high 30s/low 40s, and the settlement propensity rises
more than the hearing number does — a nil return is exactly the document a respondent does not
want read aloud.
