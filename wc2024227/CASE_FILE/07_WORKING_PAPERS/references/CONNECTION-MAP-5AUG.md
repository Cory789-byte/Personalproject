## WHAT TO DO WITH THIS

Items 1, 2 and 3 are the ones that matter, and they matter for a single reason:
**they establish knowledge in the decision-making unit.** Under s 306 IR Act and
s 113(2) WHS Act the onus shifts to the employer once the protected act, the detriment
and knowledge are established. Knowledge is normally the hard part. Here it is on the
Commission's own service list and in the Regulator's own disclosure.

**Discipline rule 1 applies without exception: none of this reaches paper as an
allegation of coordination or collusion.** It is held, it is evidence of knowledge, and
it is deployed — if ever — through process, never through accusation.
