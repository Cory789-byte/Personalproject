PART 2 — PAYMENT

An ad hoc payment can be made, and it is the process that exists for exactly this situation.

Queensland Health's HR Policy C13, Payment of salaries and wages (QH-POL-188, 23 June 2025), section 8,
describes the ad hoc payment process as one that "enables employees to request a payment that was
missed or requires correction outside of the regular pay day schedule without waiting for the next
pay cycle". The certified agreement requires pay that is not correct to be corrected in a timely
manner (EB12 cl 3.8.1), and the agreed mechanism for that is an ad hoc payment (EB11 cl 3.8.3). One
was processed for me on 11 March 2025 (log 4045255). The statement that a payment cannot be processed
before 9 September describes the pay cycle. It is not the ad hoc process, and I do not accept it.

The pay that was missed. I was paid nothing on 26 August for the fortnight 3 to 16 August, coded

am asking for by ad hoc payment now. Process 24926366 covers 17 to 30 August only; I ask that it be
confirmed in the 9 September run, but it does not answer the fortnights before it.
