**3. The legal question, stated accurately**

The question in this appeal is whether my employment was *a significant* contributing factor to the injury (s 32(1) of the Act). I set out the features of that test below only so that your report is directed to the correct legal standard, not to any particular answer:

- Employment need only be *a significant* contributing factor. It need not be the only, nor the main, cause. (The word "major" is not the test.)
- Other, non-employment contributors may coexist; they need not be outweighed by employment for the test to be met (*Adams v Q-COMP* [2015] ICQ 1 at [23], adopting *Hochen*).
- An aggravation of a pre-existing condition is itself an injury where employment is a significant contributing factor to the aggravation (s 32(3)(b)), to the extent of the effects of the aggravation (s 32(4)).

I ask only that your report answer the question according to your own clinical judgment. **I do not ask you to reach any particular conclusion, and I would not wish you to.** A report that reflected instruction rather than your independent opinion would be of no use to me. Please answer each question below neutrally and on its clinical merits, including where the answer is adverse to my case.
