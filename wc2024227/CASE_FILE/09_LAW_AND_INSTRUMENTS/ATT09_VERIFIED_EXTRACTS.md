## PAGE 4 of 28 — §2.2 (line manager duties, continued) and §2.3 (RRTWC)

### §2.2 — the line manager shall:
> *"The immediate line manager is best placed to be the primary contact for the worker wherever
> possible and facilitate the injured workers on-going connection with the workplace."*

> *"Where alternate work duties are required, identify and actively seek, with support from the
> RRTWC if needed, meaningful suitable duties, and ensure suitable duties are made available
> wherever reasonably practicable."*

> *"Ensure suitable duties considerations are documented for **all accepted workers compensation
> cases**. In rare instances, if it is determined that it is not practicable to provide suitable
> duties to the worker, the evidence relied on to support that opinion must be provided to the
> insurer, as per s228(2) of the act."*
> ⚠️ **Limited to accepted WC cases. Cory has none. Do not use.**

> *"Monitor implementation of the suitable duties program, to ensure duties provided comply with the
> injured/ill workers current medical capacity, ensuring the health and safety of the worker and
> others in the workplace."*

> ⭐ *"**Inform the worker of the appropriate dispute resolution process to follow** in such cases
> where the worker does not agree with elements of a proposed workplace rehabilitation plan or
> suitable duties program."*

### §2.3 — The Rehabilitation and Return to Work Coordinator (RRTWC) shall:
> ⭐⭐ *"Ensure that an injured/ill worker who requires workplace rehabilitation for **either a work
> or non-work-related injury/illness**, is contacted for workplace rehabilitation support, as soon
> as practicable after notification of the injury/illness or request for support."*
> ⇒ **This is the "applies without an accepted claim" point. It sits at §2.3, the RRTWC duty — not
> at §2.1.**

> *"Support the rehabilitation of injured/ill workers by ensuring this Workplace rehabilitation
> standard and local workplace rehabilitation procedures are followed."*

> *"Request the injured/ill workers signed authorisation to communicate with, and obtain information
> from the workers treating doctor and/or other health practitioners, for the purposes of planning
> and implementing workplace rehabilitation."*
> ⚠️ **Note: the standard contemplates a signed authorisation sought by the RRTWC — not nine
> questions sent to the worker's own GP by a Director. Relevant to the option 1 / option 2 election.**

> *"Provide coordination of an injured/ill workers workplace rehabilitation and maintain effective
> communication with key stakeholders in the workplace rehabilitation process."*
