## §1 Principles (p 1)
> *"Timeliness: Salaries and wages must be processed within the timeframes prescribed by the Industrial
> Relations Act 2016, Queensland Government Directives … legislation, and certified agreements."*
> *"Compliance: All payments, deductions, and recovery processes must comply with legislation, Health
> Employment Directives, Queensland government Directives, policies, and certified agreements."*

## §2 Payment of salary and wages (pp 1–2)
> *"The Queensland Health fortnightly pay period commences on a Monday and concludes on the Sunday at the
> end of the fortnight. Payments to employees are made in arrears …"*
> *"The PSC Directive 17/13 Pay Date for Employees of Queensland Health prescribes that the pay date …
> will be no later than ten calendar days after the completion of the fortnightly pay period."*
> *"Should an employee identify they have been paid incorrectly, the employee is to raise the error with
> their line manager or through myHR as soon as possible."*
> ⭐ *"**Line managers, when notified by an employee of an incorrect wage payment, must take all steps to
> rectify the error** including but not limited to: assisting the employee; preparing and submitting
> further forms if required; liaising with Payroll to correct the error, where appropriate."*

## §4 Form management (p 2)
> *"Where a payroll issue cannot be resolved between the employee and line manager, either the employee
> or line manager can escalate the issue to Payroll Services."*

### §4.1 Employees (pp 2–3)
> *"… within 24 hours or as soon as reasonably practicable as it relates to time and attendance …
> Should this delay result in incorrect payment for an employee, the line manager must discuss with the
> employee corrective action to be undertaken (e.g. submission of a leave form)."*
> ⚠ *"Employees must submit or approve all other applications (e.g. long service leave applications …)
> prior to commencement of the relevant planned leave …"*

### §4.2 Line managers (p 3)
> *"Line managers, upon receipt of a myHR form or application, must action this form within two days or
> prior to 5pm Monday following the roster period end date, whichever is sooner. All roster variations
> must be submitted at the end of the affected shift through a daily staffing variance (DSVF) or
> attendance variation and allowance claim (AVAC) form …"*
> *"For those employees on long-term leave without pay or employees on WorkCover or Income Protection,
> line managers are required to ensure compliance with **HR Policy G4 Absence management** …"*

## §5 Submission timeframes — table (p 4)
| Form type | Submission deadline | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Leave forms | within two days or prior to 5pm Monday following the roster period end date | supporting documentation attached |
| Roster variations | end of affected shift or next business day | DSVF or AVAC |
| **Ad hoc requests** | **5:00 PM on pay day Wednesday** | include relevant Process Reference Number (PRN) |

## §6 Line managers signing on behalf of employees (p 5)
> *"… line managers may submit leave requests on behalf of employees provided: The employee's request
> for leave is documented in writing (e.g. email, text message, or hard copy); and any required
> supporting documents (e.g. employee's written request for leave, medical certificates for sick
> leave) are sourced and retained."*
> ⭐ *"For leave types requiring mutual agreement (e.g. annual or long service leave), line managers must
> not unilaterally place employees on leave, unless permitted to do so under an industrial instrument
> or the Industrial Relations Act 2016. An employee's written consent must be retained by the line
> manager."*

## §8 Ad hoc payment process (p 6) — VERBATIM
> *"The ad hoc payments process **enables employees to request a payment that was missed or requires
> correction outside of the regular pay day schedule without waiting for the next pay cycle.** The ad
> hoc payment process requires:*
> *1. Employees should review their payslip … via myHR to ensure their pay is accurate.*
> *2. If any pay discrepancies are identified, employees must promptly inform their line manager and
> submit a corrective payroll form, if this has not already occurred.*
> *3. Line managers and employees are responsible for submitting payroll forms as soon as possible.*
> *4. The employee can request an ad hoc payment through: lodgement of the ad hoc payment request in
> myHR; contacting Payroll Services; **in the event an employee is unable to access myHR or contact
> Payroll Services, the line manager can request an ad hoc payment on their behalf.***
> *5. To enable prompt receipt of the payment, ad hoc payment requests should be lodged by 5pm payday
> Wednesday to ensure payment is received before the next regular pay day schedule.*
> *6. … at end of financial year, ad hoc payments may be delayed …"*

## §9 Employee requested review of pay entitlements (p 6)
> *"When an employee identifies that a potential underpayment (or overpayment) occurred prior to their
> most recent pay, or considers their pay may be continuously incorrect, the employee is to promptly
> advise their line manager regarding incorrect wage payments in the first instance. Additional support
> … is available through the local Payroll and Human Resources teams."*

## Definition — "Ad hoc payments" (p 7) — ⚠ THE LIMIT
> *"An ad hoc payment is a special payment option available for Queensland Health employees if they
> identify they are **missing pay in the most recent prior pay period, and seek the payment prior to the
> next pay day. Ad hoc payment process is not to be used for payment of missed pay for extended
> periods.** Refer to section 9 …"*
