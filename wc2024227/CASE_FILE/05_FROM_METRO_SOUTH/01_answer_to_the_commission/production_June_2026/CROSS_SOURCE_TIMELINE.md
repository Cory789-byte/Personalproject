## The cross-stream finding — the same record, captured twice, weeks apart

Cory's leave record 15480560 appears in **both** streams, but captured
differently and at different times:

- **Stream A, 6 May:** captured as **live screenshots of an open administrative
  session with edit rights**, dropped straight into the Regulator's WCRS
  container inside the Form 29 compliance window.
- **Stream B, 25 May–2 June:** re-created as **formal PDF prints** (Renee Dawson
  25 May; Ruttan's AVAC History 2 June) for the Cory-facing production, only
  after the 22 May direction.

**This is the two-stream model made concrete at the document level:** the
Regulator received the record immediately and informally (live screen-walk, 6
May); the Appellant received it formally packaged and over two weeks later
(25 May onward). Same custodial system, same leave record, two postures by
recipient — the asymmetry, now datable to the hour.

A second alignment: the **McGinley email (16 May 2024)** appended to Stream A on
11 June was never addressed to Cory — an MSH-side custodian supplied it through
the conferencing channel. Stream B confirms MSH was actively printing
Cory-related records for production in the same window. The two streams meet at
the conferencing channel.
