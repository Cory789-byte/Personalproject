## 2. ⭐⭐⭐ Half one — the events. Converted, and upgraded.

His mechanism was **proof by non-contradiction**: give the evidence, and if they don't contradict it
*"there's a likelihood that it'll be accepted."*

**What that route actually depended on:**
- you getting into the box and giving the evidence
- surviving cross-examination on it
- the Regulator **choosing** not to contradict
- and the hearing running before any of it was worth anything

**What the notice did instead:**

| His route | The route taken |
|---|---|
| a **likelihood** of acceptance | ⭐ a **concession**, certain for the proceeding (r 49(1)) |
| resting on **your credit** in the box | ⭐ **independent of your credit entirely** |
| secured by their **inaction** | ⭐ secured by their **act** — a signed response |
| available **at hearing** | ⭐ established **on 8 September**, on paper |
| *"a problem for them"* | ⭐ **no longer a problem at all — it is agreed** |

⇒ ⭐⭐ **Yes: the documentary half of the recommendation became admitted fact, and it is a stronger
form of the same thing.** He told you the emails' existence probably wasn't in dispute. **You made
sure of it** — Stressor 1(a) came back **75 of 75**.

And §I — the union delegate — admits the **consultation and ballot instruments** and the
Consultation Paper's *"more equitable roster"*. ⛔ **It does not admit that the appointment was
"actively suppressed for 13 months."**

⇒ ⭐⭐ **Every characterisation stayed out, by design.** The notice was built so that every fact is
one the Respondent can and should admit — a fact about a document, no characterisation, no inference.
**A notice to admit cannot capture "she was hostile." Nothing can, except evidence.**

⇒ ⛔ **So half two of his recommendation is still live, and still runs exactly as he set it out.**
That is precisely why **Harrison-Jones ¶ 6** exists — *"what Ms Taylor said… the sequence in which
each of them spoke, and **the manner and volume in which it was said**"* — and why the covering
letter of 9 September puts the burden of calling Ms Taylor on the Respondent.

## 4. ⭐⭐⭐ And one thing that was never his recommendation at all

Nothing Dwyer said suggested asking the Respondent to admit **what it does not allege**, that
**documents do not exist**, or that its own pleading **identifies nothing**. That is not in his
method. It is an extension of it — and it is where the strongest material came from:

- §P — no fatigue assessment, no training, no implementation, **no procedure changed by any complaint
  in seven months**, **no consultation document**
- §O — the Chief Executive: *"**the requested documents do not exist**"*
- §S — never disciplined, never performance-managed, never warned
- ⭐⭐⭐ **303 — paragraph 27 *"does not identify, by particular, date, document or cross-reference,
  the management action relied upon"***

⇒ **Fact 303 bites on the only live issue in the appeal, and he never pointed at it.**

## 5. The answer in one line

⭐⭐ **His recommendation was "you probably won't be contradicted." You converted that into "they have
confirmed it" for every event — and then took it somewhere he did not go, into what they cannot
allege.** ⛔ **What you could not convert, and what remains, is exactly what he said would need the
witness box: the manner.** That has not moved, and it should not be described as though it has.
