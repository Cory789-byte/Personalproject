## PART 7 — WHAT THE TIMING REVEALS
1. **Urgency is available to them at will.** Five hours to demand a retraction; twenty-five days
   to submit a form their own payroll called immediate. Speed is not a capacity problem.
2. **Their fast responses are administrative; their slow responses are substantive.** They move
   quickly to *manage* and slowly to *resolve* — and on the four asks of 12 August, not at all.
3. **His reliability is a documented fact, not a claim.** Same-day answers to WorkCover while
   injured; a three-day RFMI turnaround inside a seven-day window; the only slow item on his side
   is the **ART letter, still unsent — and it is the one that produces income.**
4. **Delay improves his record and destroys his position.** The documentation gap widens by
   itself; the financial loop tightens by itself. Same clock, opposite effects.
5. **He litigated blind for thirteen months** on the third-party notices. Fixed now; the lesson is
   procedural and permanent.
