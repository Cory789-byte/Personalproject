## 3 · The 10 Sep email — five mechanisms
1. **Done, not decided.** HR's whole 2026 posture is that no decision is attributable. The email
   removes the shield by asking only for execution of MSH's own instruction. Refusal therefore
   requires the thing HR avoids: an owned decision — and (b) demands exactly that: *"who has
   decided it will not be done and the written basis."*
2. **The bottleneck pre-empted.** 1.3 offers to lodge the forms himself and asks for the approving
   delegate if the line manager is unavailable. ⭐ That is the case's central mechanism — one
   unreachable person controlling correction — being prevented from recurring in real time, on the
   payroll file, in 2026. The structural echo will not be lost on anyone who has read the 9A.
3. **The reservation paragraph.** Leave taken under protest, drawn from his own accruals, "not
   paid as wages", rights reserved to seek the leave back. This converts every ad hoc "help" into
   documented self-funding and preserves the wages claim without litigating it. It also
   neutralises, on the record, the kindness register of the 4 Sep ad hoc.
4. **The RFMI trilemma (2.1–2.3).** Same shape as the Regulator request's (a)/(b): every branch
   concedes something. Still required → why unanswered since 19 Aug? Sent → produce it (it was
   not). Withdrawn → **the stated basis for withholding work collapses** — the "further medical
   clarification" chain dies with it. 2.3 is the live target. ⚠ And 2.2's condition (questions to
   him first) protects the appeal medical case: anything sent to Krishnaiah becomes NNPD-able by
   the Regulator, so nothing goes to him unseen.
5. **The delegate line.** "I will ask Together Queensland to take the matter up… and will write as
   the formal office as delegate." He was endorsed as workplace delegate on/about 3 Nov 2025 —
   admitted (¶¶178–179). Acting against a delegate in his industrial capacity is a separate
