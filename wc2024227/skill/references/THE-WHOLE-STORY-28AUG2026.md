⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **A man coordinating cardiac arrests on four hours' sleep, in a role with no fatigue system
at all.** This is the only strand in fourteen months where the employer's own rule produces the
answer by subtraction, and the only one where his conduct is not in issue at any point.

## PART 10 — WHERE IT STANDS, AND WHAT DECIDES IT

### The shape of the hearing
**Short — because the Respondent's case is short.** The facts are admitted, so the documentary layer
is gone; the outlines are capped at one page; and to run s 32(5)(a) at all the Regulator must call
Ms Taylor. **Ask for two days; expect to finish inside one.**

### ⭐⭐ Why it may not be heard at all
The Regulator's **amended List of Documents of 14 August 2026** contains **no medico-legal report and
no IME**. Every medical document it holds is his own treating evidence. Direction 4 gives it until
**30 September** — twenty-one days after his report lands — and a psychiatric medico-legal takes six
to ten weeks.

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **30 September answers everything.** Is Taylor on their list? Is Reese? **Is there an expert
report?** If not, they cannot contest causation at all.

### The three things that decide it
1. ⭐⭐ **The psychiatric report**, narrowed to causation, walking the **admitted facts** and
   attributing weight. A psychiatrist cannot normally attribute causation to contested facts —
   **the Form 24 is what makes that report possible.**
2. ⛔ **The 26 October 2022 record**, met expressly. Their only cheap route to a medical case is
   cross-examining the psychiatrist on a history he was never shown.
3. ⛔ **The five false tags in the Form 9A**, fixed before 9 September. As drafted they are the best
   argument the other side has, and they are self-inflicted.

### ⭐ And the sentence the whole appeal rests on
**s 32(1) requires employment to be *a* significant contributing factor — not the only one, not the
main one.** Competing causes need not be outweighed. They need only leave employment *a* significant
contributor.

⇒ **On the Respondent's own review decision, it already is.**
