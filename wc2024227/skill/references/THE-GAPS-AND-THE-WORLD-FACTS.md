## C · What the two classes do together
The world-facts admit **presence**: the misdirected emergency calls occurred, during the
arrangement; the intervals are true; the reply came after the stated hours ended; the
leave was approved in thirteen minutes when the manager chose to, and in ten days when
she did not. The gaps admit **absence**: no consultation, no ballot, no restoration, no
stated hours, no response, no assessment, no training, no warning, no process, no
consequential change, no particulars. **Together they are the whole case in two
sentences: everything that happened to him is admitted as having happened, and
everything that should have stood behind it is admitted as never alleged.** Neither
sentence cites a document. Neither can be objected to at tender, because there is
nothing to tender — facts admitted under r 49 are simply taken as admitted. The
authenticity dispute, whatever happens to it on the 25th, never touched this layer at
all: the fourteen tabs could be burned and these 56 admissions would still carry the
conditions, the events, the clock and the silence.
