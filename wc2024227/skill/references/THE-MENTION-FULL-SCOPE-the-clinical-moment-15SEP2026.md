## 5. ⛔ WHERE THE STANDING RULE COMES FROM — and exactly where the line sits

The disciplines *"never allege or hint patient harm"* and *"'patient safety' as a PID descriptor stays
off WC paper"* have an empirical origin, and it is **[1786]**. The cost of crossing that line was
measured once, in real time, and it was the costs-and-hearing warning.

| ✅ SAFE — admitted, and already served | ⛔ NEVER |
|---|---|
| Calls were transferred to the wrong medical teams | That any patient was harmed, or might have been |
| Switchboard could not locate a MET call and MASPER had to redirect | "dereliction of clinical governance" |
| A service said it *"can not help patients or other clinical staff"* with those issues | "arbitrary" directives |
| The manager acknowledged *"many ongoing issues"* | "directly caused verifiable delays" |
| Five days with no alleged response; the second email at 11:03 am | Any outcome, actual or hypothetical, for any patient |

⭐ **The admitted facts describe a system that did not work. They assert no outcome.** That is the
line, and the served instrument holds it exactly.
