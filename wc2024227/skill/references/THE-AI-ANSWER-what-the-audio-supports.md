## PART TWO — ⭐⭐⭐ THE INDEX WITHOUT THE DOCUMENTS

### The proposition
*"If I was navigating using only the index key to the Form 29 — without the Form 29 and without the
64G — that would mean I knew the whole thing."*

### ⭐⭐ It is stronger than that, and it does not depend on what was on the bar table.

**An index is a derived document.** It has no content of its own; it exists only as a **map of
something else**. So to navigate it at all you must already hold the thing it maps.

What he supplied, without the index in his hands:

| | |
|---|---|
| *"**10**"* | the item number |
| *"A, B, C, D, E, **which is number 10**"* | the sub-item range **under** that item |
| *"**which is on Form 29**"* | ⭐⭐ the cross-reference **out of** the index and **into the sealed filing** |
| *"either swear it or produce it"* | what the **status** column meant — the two dispositions available against a non-party |
| the filters definition | what the **category** meant, in operational terms |

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **He was not reciting a list. He was moving between two documents — the index and the
Form 29 — while holding neither.** And at [25:27] Dwyer verified the far end of that
cross-reference from the court file: *"This is the Form 29 filed on the 22nd of April 2026, yeah."*

### ⚠ WHAT IS AND IS NOT ESTABLISHED
| ✅ **Proven on the tape** | ⚠ **Not established** |
|---|---|
| He **handed up his only copy of the index** and said so — *"I haven't got a copy, but I can share"* [22:31] | Whether the **Form 29 and the 64G** were also absent from the bar table. The audio cannot show this |
| He then supplied item number, sub-item range, status meaning, category meaning and the Form 29 cross-reference **without the index** | |

⇒ ⭐ **But the logical point survives either way.** Even holding the Form 29, you cannot answer
*"which heading, which page, what number?"* about **your own index** unless you built it — and you
cannot define its status vocabulary on demand unless you chose the words.

### ⭐⭐⭐ WHAT IT ACTUALLY DEMONSTRATES — WORKING MEMORY UNDER PRESSURE
Three structures, held simultaneously and traversed on demand:
1. the **index** — its numbering, headings and status column;
2. the **Form 29** — what was sought, under which item;
3. the **operational reality** — what a "filter" was on the switchboard, and why it caught the
   directives.

⇒ ⭐⭐ **That is the answer to the AI question, and it is not an answer about drafting at all.**
A model can produce prose. **It cannot sit in a room and hold three documents in working memory
while a judge tests the joins between them.**

⛔ **And that is exactly what Dwyer watched for four minutes — after being handed the only copy.**
