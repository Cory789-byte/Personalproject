# Medical causation framework — the Krishnaiah report brief + the pre-existing-anxiety rebuttal

> PURPOSE: structure the UPDATED psychiatrist's report so it (a) states a genuine, DSM-5-grounded
> diagnosis, (b) articulates the causal MECHANISM to the legal test, and (c) defeats the Regulator's
> "past medical history of anxiety" displacement (9C ¶8) HEAD-ON. Critical path for the appeal;
> Willson (WHS expert) probes causation hardest (comparable-cases §7).
> BOUNDARY (non-negotiable): the DIAGNOSIS and the CAUSAL OPINION are Dr Krishnaiah's independent
> clinical judgment. This frame supplies the QUESTIONS, the FACTUAL FOUNDATION, and the LEGAL TESTS
> the report must engage — it does NOT script a conclusion. A report that reads as built-to-order is
> worthless; a genuine report that ADDRESSES the right test and ENGAGES the contrary evidence is what
> survives cross-examination. Robustness = genuineness + completeness + engagement, not manufacture.

## 1. The legal tests the report must engage (⚠️ verify operative s 32 wording as at the mid-2024 claim)
- **s 32(1) WCRA** — injury where employment is **A significant contributing factor** (threshold is
  "significant," NOT "major significant" — the lower post-amendment test; confirm the version in force
  at date of injury/claim). Employment need not be the ONLY or the MAIN cause — only significant.
- **Aggravation limb** — the Act includes **aggravation of a psychiatric/psychological disorder**
  where employment is a significant contributing factor **to the aggravation** (injury only "to the
  extent of the effects of the aggravation"). Confirm exact subsection (s 32(3)/(4) family).
- **s 32(5)(a) exclusion** — NOT reasonable management action taken reasonably. The report addresses
  this MEDICALLY by tying the injury to the pleaded COURSE (the stressors), not to any legitimate
  action; the reasonableness fight is legal, but the causal attribution to the identified stressors
  is the doctor's.
- **"Take the worker as you find them" (eggshell-skull)** — a pre-existing susceptibility does not
  defeat the claim; a defendant/insurer takes the worker with their vulnerabilities.

## 2. THE PRE-EXISTING-ANXIETY DISPLACEMENT — four independent answers (any one suffices)
The 9C ¶8 does not admit the clean baseline "because medical records identify a PAST MEDICAL HISTORY
OF ANXIETY." Defeated on FOUR independent tracks — build all four; they are cumulative:

**(a) FACTUAL — their own admitted clean baseline.** The 16 Nov 2023 record ("no psychological
illness such as depression/psychosis") is ADMITTED as listed (Form 24 ¶34) — a clean contemporaneous
baseline ~7 months before onset. A prior NOTE or mention of "anxiety" in records is not a diagnosed
anxiety DISORDER. Distinguish: symptom-mention vs formal DSM diagnosis. The report should identify
what the "history of anxiety" actually WAS (situational? a single GP note? never treated/diagnosed?)
and whether it ever met diagnostic criteria for a disorder. Often it evaporates on inspection.

**(b) NEW DISCRETE INJURY.** Even if some anxious trait pre-existed, the report can find a NEW,
discrete Major Depressive Disorder with Anxious Distress (DSM-5) with onset 18 June 2024 — a
different condition, not a continuation. The clean 16 Nov 2023 record supports a new onset.

**(c) AGGRAVATION (the fallback that still wins).** Even if the Commission finds a pre-existing
condition, employment aggravating it into the current disorder is COMPENSABLE where employment is a
significant contributing factor to the aggravation. This route concedes nothing fatal — it wins on
the aggravation limb. Belt-and-braces: the report can address BOTH new-injury (primary) and
aggravation (alternative).

**(d) EGGSHELL-SKULL.** A constitutional vulnerability to anxiety does not break the causal chain;
the workplace course is still a significant contributing factor to the injury as it actually
occurred in this worker.

WHY ADDRESS IT HEAD-ON (not hide it): an expert report that IGNORES the anxiety history is
vulnerable — Willson raises it and the report looks evasive/incomplete. A report that NAMES it,
characterises it accurately, and explains why it does not displace causation (routes a-d) is
FORTIFIED by having dealt with the strongest contrary point. Engagement = credibility.

## 3. THE MECHANISM — the three-dimensional course (clinical framing for the report)
The report should articulate causation as a CUMULATIVE COURSE (global evaluation / Delaney), not one
event — this is what makes it clinically an MDD and legally durable:
1. **ACUTE / powerlessness** — raising serious patient-safety & WHS concerns (fatigue; misrouting;
   on-call/directory failures) through proper channels and being met with dismissal, non-
   investigation, and no change, WHILE responsible for a life-safety function (affidavit ¶¶6, 23-25;
   Regulator admits the safety-criticality, Form 24 ¶8). Mechanism: responsibility without authority;
   moral weight of ignored foreseeable-harm warnings.
2. **CHRONIC / no-recovery** — the undischargeable complaints load (affidavit ¶¶19-22): terminal
   accountability sink, no offload point, complaints about the manager returning to him. Mechanism:
   absence of psychological detachment/recovery; sustained allostatic load.
3. **TEMPORAL / prolongation** — the whole ~June 2023-June 2024 course, unresolved throughout
   (grievance Aug-Nov 2023; >9-month delegate suppression; pay Feb-May 2024; MSH admits no change
   until after 30 June 2024). Mechanism: chronicity is the injury variable — sustained non-resolution
   converts stress into disorder.
Corroboration already in hand: Hawes recorded the mechanism (Form 24 ¶36 admits the certificate
states "ongoing breaking of workplace rules by bosses, victimizing him").
