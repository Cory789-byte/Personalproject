# PART 5 — HOW TO DEPLOY THIS

1. **The Part 1 contrast is the strongest single item** — 15 minutes on leave, 4 weeks on attendance,
   10 months on a complaint. It is computed, checkable, and needs no adjective.
2. **The Part 2 register is the union brief**, not the letter to MSH. Sixteen obligations in one
   table reads as an indictment. **In correspondence, raise them one at a time, as requests.**
3. ⛔ **Never use the word "unlawfully" in correspondence to MSH.** It is an accusation, it invites a
   defence, and the model puts accusation framings at −16 to −26 points of settlement position.
   ⭐ *"I have asked which instrument is relied on and have not received an answer"* says the same
   thing and cannot be argued with.
4. **The measured response pattern belongs in the union brief and, if it comes to it, in evidence.**
   Not in a letter.
