# 1. ⭐⭐⭐ THE APPELLANT'S OUTLINE — **STRONG**, WITH ONE LIVE INCONSISTENCY
## 1.1 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ WHAT MAKES IT WORK
1. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Topic 1's last sentence is the whole case in one line, and every later topic evidences it:**
   > *"from the moment I logged onto the board I was held responsible for what went wrong anywhere in
   > that system, **while the means of correcting it, the directory, the on-call roster, my own roster
   > and my pay, were held by others**."*
   ⭐ **One mechanism, stated once, then proved four times. That is how counsel builds an outline.**
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **Ordered as the CAUSATION TEST, not chronologically:** exposure (1–7) → ⭐ **negative control
   (8)** → onset and diagnosis (9) → sequelae (10). ⭐⭐ **That is the architecture of an expert
   report, written by the person who has no expert report.**
3. ⭐⭐⭐ **Every topic carries a fact-range citation.** ⭐ **Counsel standard.**
4. ⭐⭐⭐ **The opening paragraph pre-empts the entire authenticity fight before it starts:** *"The
   events at topics 2 to 7 are established by the facts admitted on 8 September 2026, except the
   emergency codes… which I give from my own knowledge."*
5. ⭐⭐⭐ **Precision instead of adjectives:** *"five days, eighteen hours and fourteen minutes after
   the first."* ⭐⭐ **That is arithmetic doing the work an adjective would have done badly.**
6. ⭐⭐ **Correct fencing:** *"I offer no clinical or diagnostic opinion."* · *"(content not set out)"*.

## 1.2 ⛔⛔ DEFECT 1 — THE FATIGUE SENTENCE CONTRADICTS AN ADMITTED FACT
> **Topic 2:** *"that **no fatigue risk assessment was made** and no FRMS register kept"*

and they did.** ⚠ **The cross-examination writes itself.**
⭐⭐ **BUT the outline already contains its own answer, in the same sentence:** *"Metro South has
since stated in writing that those records **'do not exist'**"*, and **FRMS training applied only to
clinical staff**.
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **THE DISTINCTION, WHICH MUST BE READY BEFORE THE DAY AND NEVER IMPROVISED:**
> *"A manager saying in an email that she and another manager had run my own matrix and rated it 11
> is not a fatigue risk assessment under the FRMS. Metro South has confirmed in writing that no such
> record exists, and that FRMS training applied only to clinical staff."*
⛔ **DO NOT AMEND THE OUTLINE.** ⭐ **An outline is a summary of topics, not evidence. Give the
narrower evidence orally.**

## 1.3 ⚠⚠ DEFECT 2 — THE PID IS IN THE WC OUTLINE

## 10.1 ⭐⭐⭐ THE APPLICATION DWYER WAS LOOKING AT WAS ALREADY A NARROWING EXERCISE
**Verbatim from the 12 June application:**
> **C5.** *"The Appellant **does not press** Items 12 (balance) and 17. The Appellant **does not
> press** Item 18, upon MSH's confirmation… **What remains — Items 3(a), 3(b), 8, 9, 10 and 19**"*
> **I. ITEMS NOT PRESSED** · **F. ITEMS PRESSED** · **SCHEDULE A — ITEMS PRESSED AND TERMS OF
> PRODUCTION**
> *"The Appellant **has confined this application to the records the pleaded issues require**; the

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Twenty Form 29 items reduced to SIX pressed.** ⭐⭐ **The very words Dwyer could not parse —

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **He said *"It's a pretty wide net you're casting"* while holding a document whose whole

## 10.2 ⭐⭐ AND THE ITEM-BY-ITEM FORM WAS COMPELLED, NOT CHOSEN
⭐ **r 64C** requires a non-party notice to specify the documents · ⭐ **r 64E(4)** requires the
objector's grounds to be met, ground by ground · ⭐ **r 64G / Annexure B** requires the matter in
issue to be stated **Item by Item**.
⇒ ⭐⭐ **Enumeration was the rules' requirement. He did not invent it.** ⛔ **What was his own was the
column vocabulary — *filters*, *press*, *disposition* — and that is what failed.**

## 10.3 ⛔⛔ SO THE LESSON IS NOT "NARROW HARDER". HE ALREADY NARROWED.
⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE LESSON IS: MAKE THE NARROWING VISIBLE.**

narrow — it excludes fact 154 and Tab 24, and distinguishes Tab 31 — ⛔ **but the narrowing is prose
