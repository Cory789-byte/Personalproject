# 1. ⭐⭐⭐ THE APPELLANT'S OUTLINE — **STRONG**, WITH ONE LIVE INCONSISTENCY
## 1.1 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ WHAT MAKES IT WORK
1. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Topic 1's last sentence is the whole case in one line, and every later topic evidences it:**
   > *"from the moment I logged onto the board I was held responsible for what went wrong anywhere in
   > that system, **while the means of correcting it, the directory, the on-call roster, my own roster
   > and my pay, were held by others**."*
   ⭐ **One mechanism, stated once, then proved four times. That is how counsel builds an outline.**
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **Ordered as the CAUSATION TEST, not chronologically:** exposure (1–7) → ⭐ **negative control
   (8)** → onset and diagnosis (9) → sequelae (10). ⭐⭐ **That is the architecture of an expert
   report, written by the person who has no expert report.**
3. ⭐⭐⭐ **Every topic carries a fact-range citation.** ⭐ **Counsel standard.**
4. ⭐⭐⭐ **The opening paragraph pre-empts the entire authenticity fight before it starts:** *"The
   events at topics 2 to 7 are established by the facts admitted on 8 September 2026, except the
   emergency codes… which I give from my own knowledge."*
5. ⭐⭐⭐ **Precision instead of adjectives:** *"five days, eighteen hours and fourteen minutes after
   the first."* ⭐⭐ **That is arithmetic doing the work an adjective would have done badly.**
6. ⭐⭐ **Correct fencing:** *"I offer no clinical or diagnostic opinion."* · *"(content not set out)"*.

## 1.2 ⛔⛔ DEFECT 1 — THE FATIGUE SENTENCE CONTRADICTS AN ADMITTED FACT
> **Topic 2:** *"that **no fatigue risk assessment was made** and no FRMS register kept"*
> **Fact 219, ADMITTED:** *"Chloe and I have run thought this and we say at best there would be a
> rating of **11 which is moderate**"*

⛔ **This is the single worst thing in the set, and it is self-inflicted: he asked them to admit it,
and they did.** ⚠ **The cross-examination writes itself.**
⭐⭐ **BUT the outline already contains its own answer, in the same sentence:** *"Metro South has
since stated in writing that those records **'do not exist'**"*, and **FRMS training applied only to
clinical staff**.
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **THE DISTINCTION, WHICH MUST BE READY BEFORE THE DAY AND NEVER IMPROVISED:**
> *"A manager saying in an email that she and another manager had run my own matrix and rated it 11
> is not a fatigue risk assessment under the FRMS. Metro South has confirmed in writing that no such
> record exists, and that FRMS training applied only to clinical staff."*
⛔ **DO NOT AMEND THE OUTLINE.** ⭐ **An outline is a summary of topics, not evidence. Give the
narrower evidence orally.**

## 1.3 ⚠⚠ DEFECT 2 — THE PID IS IN THE WC OUTLINE
> **Topic 7:** *"my complaint of 13 May 2024, **later determined to be a public interest disclosure**
> (content not set out)"*

⚠ **This is the highest-risk sentence in the outline.** ⭐ **It is minimally stated and correctly
fenced, and it has a legitimate function — it resists "he was merely complaining".**
⛔⛔ **RULE FROM HERE: never expand it, never say reprisal, detriment, punishment or retaliation, and
if asked what it concerned:** *"The content of that disclosure is not part of this appeal."*
⭐ **Then stop.** ⛔ **It is the one door in the outline that opens onto s 32(5)(b) and the parallel
track.**

## 1.4 ⚠ DEFECT 3 — TOPIC 9 IS UNDER-BUILT RELATIVE TO EVERYTHING AROUND IT
⭐ **His evidence of EFFECT is one clause:** *"My symptoms from about 18 June 2024 and their effect
on sleep, concentration, self-care and relationships, and on my capacity for work."*
⚠⚠ **That is the most exposed part of the case — the *Kerr* surface — carried in one line, in a
document where every other topic is dense with dates.**
⛔ **Not a drafting fault (an outline states topics). ⭐⭐ It is a map of where the preparation must
go, and it is the least-prepared thing in the set.**

# 4. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE RESPONSE LETTER OF 9 SEPTEMBER — **THE BEST DOCUMENT IN THE SET AS ADVOCACY**
1. ⭐⭐⭐ **§1 concedes everything that costs nothing** — *"for this proceeding only"*, de novo,
   *"relevance, admissibility and weight are for the Commission"*, ⭐ *"draws no characterisation,
   conclusion or inference from any admitted fact beyond the fact admitted."*
   ⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **That is restraint as posture, and it is what makes §2 grantable.**
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **§2 states USE, not meaning, category by category with fact ranges.** ⭐ **Precise and
   difficult to answer.**
3. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The quiet sentence:** *"Several of the documents in those ranges were sent by Ms Taylor
   or Ms Reese, **whom the Appellant expects the Respondent to call**."*
   ⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **Written 21 days before their witness list is due. If they call neither, that sentence is
   already on the record.** ⭐⭐ **Best line in the set.**
4. ⭐⭐⭐ **§3 answers heading 3 the day after it was written, and concedes the RD is not binding** —
   ⭐ **the concession that makes the rest safe.**
5. ⭐⭐ **§5 is procedurally immaculate: it invites objection to the filing course, with a date.**
⚠ **Only fault: §2 is dense.** ⭐ **Acceptable — it is written to a lawyer, not to the Commission.**

# 5. ⚠⚠ THE REQUEST LETTER — **GOOD, BUT OVER-BROAD. THE ONE THING TO HAVE DONE DIFFERENTLY.**

the Regulator.** *(already a pending task)*
