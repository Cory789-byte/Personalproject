# 6. ⛔⛔ THE CREDIBILITY POINT — AND THE CORRECTION
⛔⛔ **"They would not be credible at all" is the wrong target, and aiming at it is a mistake.**
⭐⭐ **An attack on credit invites an attack on his, and the 8 September 2025 attendance letter is
sitting there for exactly that purpose.**

⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE RIGHT TARGET IS RELIABILITY, NOT HONESTY — AND IT IS EASIER TO WIN.**
Where a contemporaneous document says one thing and a recollection two years later says another,
**the tribunal prefers the document.** ⇒ **That requires no finding that anyone lied.** ⭐⭐⭐ **It
is the ordinary approach, it is far more likely to succeed, and it costs him nothing.**

⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AND THE ARITHMETIC DOES THE REST WITHOUT BEING SAID.** A witness explaining one of their
own documents is ordinary. A witness explaining **five** of them, each written for a different
purpose on a different date, is doing the Commission's work unaided.
⛔⛔ **He never says so. He puts the five documents, stops, and sits down.**
