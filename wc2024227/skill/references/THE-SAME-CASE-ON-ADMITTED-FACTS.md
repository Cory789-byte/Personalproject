# The same case, returned on admitted facts — what changes for the Member who walked it

**WC/2024/227 · 17 September 2026 · ⛔ INTERNAL ONLY.**

## 1. What he was doing on 7 August: a possession audit

He walked the amended 9A stressor by stressor and asked one question of each: **do you hold the
proof?**

> *"But that's all, I have all that. You've got all that? **Okay, tick.**"* [38:38]
> *"So there you go. **You've got the documents that you need for those stressors.**"* [43:03]
> *"You've got that document… Yes, I got that. And you've got that? Yeah. **Done. You've got your
> documents for that stressor.**"* [37:16]

⇒ **Possession was the whole enquiry.** Every "tick" meant: *you can prove this yourself, so you do
not need the disclosure.*

## 2. And he set the standard before closing

> *"you need to focus on the stressors that you rely on to support the contention that you've
> sustained an injury within the meaning of the act. **You don't need to prove it was an overall
> toxic place to work** and Miss Taylor… was a terrible manager, et cetera. You don't need to prove
> all of that. **You need to prove the matters that you've identified in the stressors as being
> causative of your injury.** And as I said, that's a much shorter scope… **Fewer documents means
> simpler case, means easier for someone like me to understand it and not get it wrong.**"* [60:48–61:22]

⭐⭐ **Then, immediately, he turned to the Regulator:**
> *"anything arising out of all of that, Ms Matheson? **Have I misrepresented your role in the matter
> or placed a burden on you that you're not prepared to accept?**"*
> **MS MATHESON: "No."** [61:31–61:37]

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **She accepted, on the record and in his presence, the burden he had just described** — to
contradict what is said, to produce the emails, to be *"all over that"*, and to call Ms Taylor.

---

## 3. ⭐⭐⭐ What changes when the same case comes back on admitted facts

**The audit is obsolete.** *Do you hold the proof?* is the wrong question where the fact is conceded.
Possession does not matter. **Every "tick" became "Admitted".**

**The unit of the case changed.** On 7 August each stressor was **a proposition the appellant would
have to establish**. It is now **a fact the respondent has agreed**. That is not the same case proved
— it is a different kind of object.

**And each instruction he gave was answered in its own terms:**

| What he asked for | What came back |
|---|---|
| *"focus on the stressors… not that it was an overall toxic place to work"* | ⭐ the notice contains **no characterisation at all** — every fact is about a document |
| *"a much shorter scope"* | 303 atomic propositions replacing a twenty-item disclosure notice |
| ⭐⭐ *"fewer documents… easier for someone like me to understand it **and not get it wrong**"* | **a document read one line at a time with the answer printed beside each line** |
| *"the regulator does have to contradict things that you say"* | **nothing was contradicted. 0 denied.** |
| *"I would have thought the regulator would be all over that"* | **no witnesses named, no expert, 298 admitted** |

⇒ ⭐⭐ **The burden Ms Matheson accepted in his presence was not discharged. It was conceded.**

---

## 4. ⭐⭐ And the symmetry he would notice: his probe was right in both directions

He did not tick everything. **He probed exactly three items**, and slowed on each:

| What he probed | Where it stands now |
|---|---|
| **the management directives** | the documentary base is **admitted** (§D, 75/75) — ⛔ but the characterisation *"unassessed unilateral directives"* is **not**, exactly as he predicted at 28:28: *"the argument might be about whether or not they're **correctly categorised** by you"* |
| **erratic presence** | ⛔ **not admitted.** He said prove it by cross-examining Ms Taylor, not by the adjective. Unchanged |
| **the union delegate** | ⛔ **not admitted.** *"So actively means they deliberately. Am I right about that?"* → *"I don't know if I can prove it as much."* Unchanged |

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **Everything he ticked is now admitted. Everything he probed is still open.** The audit he
performed in one hour identified precisely the three items that eleven days of admissions could not
close — because all three are characterisations, and characterisations cannot be admitted into
existence.

⚠ **And the same is true of the reprimand** (§F): the removal, the *"indirect dig"* and the *"burn
book"* are admitted; **the *"hostile and verbally aggressive reprimand"* is not.** He told you that
one needed the witness box, and it still does.

## 5. What he would be looking at, stated plainly

- **the facts he audited: closed** — and closed by the other side, not by disclosure
- **the scope he prescribed: adopted** — no toxic-workplace case, no characterisation on the page
- **the legibility he asked for: delivered in the strongest available form**
- **the burden he described and the Regulator accepted: undischarged**
- ⛔ **the three items he probed: unchanged, and unchangeable by admission**
- ⛔ **causation: still to be proved, on treating evidence, with no expert report from either side**

⇒ **The case he allowed to proceed was one that needed a hearing to establish what happened. What
would return to him needs a hearing for two evaluative questions and nothing else.**

⚠ ⛔ **And none of this is in front of him.** The Form 24 and the response are served, not filed.
