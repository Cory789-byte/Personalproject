# What the admissions actually confirm — the events, not the count

**WC/2024/227 · 17 September 2026 · ⛔ INTERNAL ONLY.**

## ⛔⛔ First, a correction to yesterday's answer

I wrote that **element (d) — employment as a significant contributing factor — was "untouched" by
the admissions**. ⛔ **That is wrong, and it was wrong because I counted the facts instead of reading
them.** Facts **261 and 262** are directly on it. The position is not that causation is untouched.
It is that **the Regulator's own delegate decided it in his favour, and the Respondent has now
admitted that the decision says so.**

---

## 1. ⭐⭐⭐ What their own delegate confirmed — §N, all six admitted

| Fact | What Review Decision 69983 states, **admitted** |
|---|---|
| 258 | *"The break between the shift on 17 March 2024 and 18 March 2024 **equated to 7 hours**."* |
| 259 | *"Even if you were allowed to leave early… you left a maximum of 30 minutes early, which meant **you still did not receive a minimum 8-hour break**."* |
| ⭐⭐ **260** | *"Based on this, **I find the rostering of these two shifts amounted to unreasonable management action** given that it was in direct contradiction to the award and the 8-hour agreement."* |
| ⭐⭐ **261** | Conclusion: *"**you sustained a personal injury of a psychological nature**"* |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **262** | Conclusion: *"**your injury arose out of employment**, to the extent that it arose out of factors 2, 3 and 4, **where employment was a significant contributing factor**"* |

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **Two of the four elements of s 32 were found in his favour by the Regulator's own
decision-maker, and the finding is now on the record as an admitted fact.** The delegate refused the
claim on the **s 32(5) global evaluation under *Delaney***, not on injury and not on causation.

⇒ **So the appeal is a s 32(5) appeal.** That is what the admissions confirm about the shape of the
case — and it means the Respondent's pre-existing-condition theory, raised for the first time in
February 2026, **argues against its own delegate's conclusion**, on a record where they have admitted
the delegate reached it.

## 2. ⭐⭐⭐ What the absences confirm — and absence is normally unprovable

These are the admissions that cannot be obtained any other way. **Each is a confirmation that
something did not happen.**

**From the employer's own letter of 5 June 2026, signed by the Chief Executive (§O):**
- *"**the requested documents do not exist.** Mandatory Fatigue Risk Management System training only
  applies to health practitioners and clinical assistants. **The Logan Hospital Switchboard staff are
  non-clinical staff**, and therefore there is no mandatory requirement for them to complete Fatigue
  Risk Management System training."* (263, 265)
- *"The implementation of fatigue risk management assessment at Switchboard Logan Hospital occurred
  **after 30 June 2024**."* (264)
- *"there have been **no 'consequential' changes to operating procedures** over the period
  requested."* (266)

**From their own pleading's silence (§P):**
- no fatigue **risk assessment** alleged before 30 June 2024 (269)
- no fatigue **training** alleged (270)
- no FRMS **assessment implemented** at Switchboard before 30 June 2024 (271)
- ⭐ **no change to operating procedures as a consequence of *any* employee complaint, 1 December
  2023 – 30 June 2024** (272)
- ⭐ their amended List of Documents lists **no document recording consultation** with operators
  before the 15 April 2024 change (273)

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **Confirmed: the fatigue system did not cover his position, and was not implemented until
after he had gone. No complaint by anyone changed any procedure in the seven months to 30 June 2024.
And no document of consultation exists for the change that put the after-hours contact on the
manager.**

⚠ **That is not the absence of evidence. It is admitted evidence of absence** — from both directions
at once: the employer says the documents do not exist, and the Respondent's pleading never asserts
they do.

## 3. ⭐⭐⭐ The closed loop, confirmed in the employer's own description of its own system

**Fact 267, admitted** — the Chief Executive's letter:

> *"**All employee complaints relating to Logan Hospital Switchboard operational errors are made
> directly to the Line Manager of Switch Board and managed solely via email or verbally with the
> complainant.**"*

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **This is the mechanism confirmed by the employer describing its own process.** Complaints
about the console went to the one manager whose conduct was in question, and were *"managed solely
via email or verbally"* — **so by design there is no record, no escalation and no independent
review.** It explains every absence at §2 without alleging anything about anyone.

⇒ Read with fact **272** — no procedure changed as a consequence of any complaint in seven months —
**the loop is closed and admitted at both ends: where complaints went, and what followed them.**

## 5. The events, as now confirmed — every line admitted

1. His role **required** him to maintain the Omnivista database accurately and to participate in the
   emergency response *"strictly adhering to protocols and timeframes"* (§A).
2. In **July 2023** the employer **removed everyone's access** to that database and told staff to send
   changes to Ms Stibbard or Ms Taylor — *"while she fixed it"* (§D).
3. **The duty stayed where it was.** Nothing in their material alters it.
4. Complaints about console errors went **to the line manager alone**, managed *"solely via email or
   verbally"* — **no record by design** (267).
5. Clinicians reported the consequences in writing: a **MET call team that could not be told where
   the MET call was**; a clinical service saying *"we can not help patients or other clinical
   staff"* (§D).
6. **No fatigue system applied to his position**, and none was implemented until **after 30 June
   2024** (§O, §P).
7. **No consultation document exists** for the April 2024 change (273).
8. **No procedure changed as a result of any complaint** in the seven months to 30 June 2024 (272).
9. He was rostered a **7-hour break**, and their own delegate found that rostering **unreasonable
   management action** (258–260).
10. That delegate found he **sustained a psychological injury** and that **employment was a
    significant contributing factor** (261–262).
11. He was **never disciplined, never performance-managed, never warned** (300–302).
12. And the exclusion now relied on **identifies no management action** (303).

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **That is not a set of admitted documents. It is a confirmed sequence in which the only thing
still in contest is a legal characterisation — and the paragraph carrying that characterisation has
been admitted to be unparticularised.**

## 6. ⚠ What the admissions still do not do — held, because it matters

1. ⛔ **Admitting that a document *states* something is not admitting the statement is true.** Facts
   257–262 confirm what the decision says, not that the Commission must agree.
2. ⛔ **The hearing is *de novo*.** The delegate's findings **do not bind** the Commission and are
   **not an estoppel**. ⭐ They are **inconsistency material** — the Respondent must explain why the
   Commission should depart from its own delegate — and that is how they are used, never as a bar.
3. ⛔ **Causation still has to be proved by him**, on the treating records and the oral evidence of
   Dr Krishnaiah and Dr Hawes. **Krishnaiah declined to report on 5 September; no expert report has
   been served by either side.** ⭐ But it is proved **into a record where the Regulator's own
   delegate already concluded it**, not into a vacuum — and that is the correction at the top of this
   file.
4. ⚠ **r 49(1)** — admitted *"for the proceeding only"*. **r 49(3)** — withdrawable **with leave**.
5. ⚠ The two substantive refusals stand: the **Emergency Code Register** for 17–19 March (228–231),
   curable from admitted fact **268**, and the **Communication Book** absence from their List of
   Documents (154).
