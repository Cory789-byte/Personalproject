## The anchors, all verified against the instruments in `documents/instruments/`

**QH-POL-210, Reasonable Adjustment** (ATT10). Costs may include "training, or temporary or ongoing changes to work
practices or roles, in a workplace rehabilitation context". The examples include:
- "Training and career advancement: … making adjustments to allow opportunities to act in other positions";
- "Deployment/redeployment: relocating an employee from one work environment to another";
- duties "meaningful, commensurate with their classification, experience and abilities".

**WCRA** (ATT26, current 1 Jan 2025):
- **s 40(3)(b):** if return to pre-injury duties is not feasible, return the worker, "temporarily or permanently, to
  other suitable duties with the worker's pre-injury employer";
- **s 40(2)(a)(iii):** rehabilitation includes services approved by an insurer;
- **s 42(e):** suitable duties have regard to "the worker's age, education, skills and work experience";
- **s 228:** the employer must take all reasonable steps to assist or provide rehabilitation (500 penalty units), and
  must notify the insurer with evidence if a suitable duties program is not practicable;
- **s 220:** the insurer must take all reasonable steps to secure rehabilitation and an early return to suitable
  duties.

**QH-IMP-401-5** (ATT09 extracts): "meaningful suitable duties"; suitable duties considerations documented "for all
accepted workers compensation" claims.
