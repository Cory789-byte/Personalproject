> *"308 facts, 39 tabs, plus the Form 25 — these come back and are assessed against the annexures;

**The read is sound. The directions order makes it sharper than stated. But the mechanism is not

---

## 1. ⛔ WHAT DOES *NOT* CAUSE A REGULATOR TO FOLD

⚠ **Volume does not fold a statutory respondent.** 308 facts and 39 tabs do not frighten an appeals
officer; the Regulator was always going to admit most of the documentary chronology. The mock ran
**249 admit / 37 not-admit / 0 deny** — and that ratio is the point. **The admissions were never the
threat. The 37 not-admits are the case.**

⇒ ⭐ **What folds them is not what the Form 24 proves. It is what the Form 24 leaves them unable to
run.**

## 3. ⭐⭐⭐ THE MEDICAL DOOR — VERIFIED, AND IT IS THE HINGE

### 3.1 They have no medical evidence of their own. None.
Their **amended List of Documents of 14 August 2026** — served *after* the mention, *after* the
amended NNPDs — lists under "Medical" exactly three items:

| # | Document | Author |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | Email noting injury and medication, 24/10/2024 | **Dr Krishnaiah** (his treating psychiatrist) |
| 10 | Report of Mind & Memory Service, 13/02/2025 | **Dr Krishnaiah** |
| 11 | Practice records, various dates | **Our Medical Ashmore** (his GP) |

plus three workers' compensation certificates from **Dr Hawes** and **Dr Ki Pang** — again, his.

⇒ ⛔⛔ **Two years after the injury, and four months out from the hearing directions, every piece of
medical material the Regulator holds is the Appellant's own treating evidence. There is no
independent medical examination, no medico-legal report, and no expert of any kind.**

### 3.2 And direction 4 gives them 21 days to fix that
> **FDO (3), direction 2:** the Appellant serves outlines *"as well as any **expert reports** that
> will be relied upon"* by **4pm 9 September 2026**.
> **Direction 4:** the Respondent serves its outlines *"as well as any **expert reports**"* by
> **4pm 30 September 2026**.

⇒ ⭐⭐ **From the moment they read the causation report on 9 September, they have twenty-one days to
obtain a psychiatric IME and a written opinion answering it.** In Queensland, instruction → booking
→ attendance → report on a psychiatric medico-legal is routinely **six to ten weeks**. **Twenty-one
days is not enough.**

Their options are therefore:
1. **Serve nothing** ⇒ the only expert opinion in the proceeding is the Appellant's, unanswered;
2. **Seek an extension** ⇒ they must explain to Dwyer IC why they have no medical evidence two years
   in, on a claim their own Review Unit allowed on both limbs;
3. ⚠ **Already have one in train that is not on the list** — possible, but nothing in the SOFC, the
   amended LOD or the NNPDs suggests it.

### 3.3 Why "tying it to the evidence" is the right instinct
A treating report that says *"work stress caused MDD"* is answerable. A report that walks the
**admitted facts** — the 7-hour break of 17–18 March, the destroyed communication book entry, the
7 August 2023 grievance dismissed the same day, the twice-declined COVID leave against the
11:41:27 server log, the 25-day AVAC delay, the reprisal email within 48 hours of the PID — **and
attributes weight to each** is not answerable without an expert of their own.

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **The Form 24 is what makes that report possible.** A psychiatrist cannot ordinarily attribute
causation to contested facts. Here the facts are admitted, so the opinion rests on agreed ground.
**That is the whole design, and it is why the report is the hinge and not the documents.**

## 5. ⭐⭐ DIRECTION 5 — THE FOLD DOOR, AND IT IS HIS TO OPEN

> **Direction 5:** *"Once all directions have been finalised, **the Appellant is to contact the
> Industrial Registry in order to proceed to a second s 552A conference, or to hearing.**"*

⭐⭐⭐ **Regulators do not fold at hearings. They fold at conferences.** A concession made in a
conference is a resolution; a concession made at hearing is a public loss on a decision the
Regulator itself made. Institutionally, the second is very much harder to authorise.

⇒ **Direction 5 puts the choice of forum in the Appellant's hands, at the exact moment he knows
what they have.** The correct sequence is therefore:

1. **9 Sept** — serve list, outlines, expert report.
2. **9 Sept** — **Calderbank #3**, 21 days, expiring **~30 Sept** — *timed to expire on the day their
   list and reports fall due.* ⭐ The offer is live across precisely the window in which they
   discover they cannot answer the report.
3. **30 Sept** — read their list. Count the witnesses. Look for an expert.
4. **Early Oct** — **elect**. If they have no expert and one witness, elect the **second s 552A
   conference** and give them the door. If they have both, elect **hearing** and take the short
   hearing.

⚠ **Direction 5 also carries the sting:** *"Should no action be taken by the Appellant… the matter
may be placed into abeyance and subject to lapse pursuant to rule 230."* **The election is not
optional and it is not open-ended.** Non-cooperation with the Commission's timetable is the one
behaviour Dwyer IC's own decisions show he terminates proceedings for.

## 6. ⛔ THE THREE THINGS THAT WOULD STOP THEM FOLDING

### 6.1 ⛔⛔ The 26 October 2022 Anxiety/ADHD record
Tab 27 row 32. **This is the only route by which they get a medical case cheaply** — not by
obtaining an IME, but by cross-examining Dr Krishnaiah on a history he was never shown.

⇒ **If that record reaches them before it reaches Krishnaiah, the fold does not happen.** Obtain it
and put it in the report. This single step is worth more than any further document.

### 6.2 ⛔ The five false "Admitted Fact" tags in the Form 9A
See `FORM9A-ADMITTED-FACT-TAGS-ERROR.md`. ¶¶1.2, 1.3 (×2), 1.4 and 3(d) plead as **admitted** five
facts that are **not admitted in the form pleaded** — with the *"clean psychiatric baseline"* pleaded
directly against a record the Respondent says exists.

⇒ ⭐ **An appeals officer assessing hearing risk reads the Form 9A first.** Five overstated
admissions and a plea contradicted by their own asserted document is exactly the material that
converts *"we should resolve this"* into *"he has overreached; run it."* **The tags are currently
the strongest argument available to the other side, and they are self-inflicted.** Fix them before
9 September.

### 6.3 ⚠ Institutional inertia, and the MSH/Regulator gap
- Statutory bodies run losing cases. Conceding requires an internal authorisation that no one is
  rewarded for seeking.
- ⚠ **MSH is a non-party.** MSH not wanting its managers in the box does not bind the Regulator —
  **but it starves it.** The Regulator cannot compel a cooperative proof from a witness whose
  employer would rather the whole thing went away. That is pressure, not control.
