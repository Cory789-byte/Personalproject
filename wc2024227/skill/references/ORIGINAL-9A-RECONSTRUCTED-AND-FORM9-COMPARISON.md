## PART 3 — HIS OWN FIRST FORM 9 (signed 21 Nov 2024, filed 26 Nov 2024)

Self-drafted, unrepresented, five months after injury. Seven grounds:
1. **Unreasonable management action — withholding of pay** (*Blagojevic v AGL Macquarie* [2018] FWC 2906)
2. **Failure to follow policies — rostering**, the **10-hour break** under the fatigue risk management policy
3. **Holistic evaluation** — *Delaney v Q-COMP* [2005] QIC 178 **but** *Carr v WCR* [2022] QIRC 059: one unreasonable stressor out of nine sufficed
4. **Psychological injury and employment** — cumulative effect, hostile environment (*Hansen v WorkCover Qld*)
5. **Union delegate** — Union Encouragement Policy **F4, QH-POL-248**, quoted; passive acceptance insufficient
6. **ESU complaint and Reese's post-injury contact** — meetings scheduled **12 and 16 July 2024** while certified unfit
7. **Misinterpretation of precedent and human rights** — selective application of authority
Plus *Ms SB* [2014] FWC 2104 (management action must not be irrational, absurd or ridiculous).

⚠ **One date error: he wrote the review decision as 22 October 2024. It is 24 October 2024.**
Never taken against him — the 9C admits ¶2 on the correct date — but know it.

## PART 4 — ⭐⭐⭐ THE COMPARISON

### Where his Form 9 was BETTER than the Saines 9A
1. ⭐⭐ **He had the correct legal architecture in November 2024.** *Carr* (one unreasonable act is
   enough) + *Delaney* (global evaluation) + *Hansen* (sound and based on reason) + *Ms SB* (lawful,
   not absurd) **is** the s 32(5)(a) framework. **The Saines 9A carries no visible authority at
   all** — the 9C answers 43 factual paragraphs and never once responds to a case. A pleading with
   no legal architecture can be answered *"reasonable in all respects"* — and it was, **eleven
   times**.
2. ⭐⭐ **He aimed at the breach; Saines aimed at the entitlement — and Saines lost on it.** His
   ground 2 is the **10-hour break under the FRMP** — a policy breach. Saines' ¶43 asked for
   **fatigue leave**, and the Regulator killed it in one line with Award cl 18.10 ("not performing
   overtime"). ⭐ **The 2026 amended 9A reverts to his framing** — "Admitted Breaches of Statutory
   Fatigue Management", the keystone. **His instinct in 2024 was right and the paid pleading went
   the wrong way on it.**
3. ⭐ **Two grounds Saines abandoned entirely.** Nothing in the 9C answers the **union delegate**
   ground or the **post-injury contact** ground (Reese, 12 & 16 July 2024). They were in his Form 9
   with the instrument named and quoted (F4, QH-POL-248) and **they are not in the 9A**.
4. ⭐ **He attacked the review decision itself** (ground 7 — selective application of authority).
   The 9A does not. On a de novo appeal that is strictly unnecessary — but it is the ground that,
   two years later, became the HopgoodGanim question.
5. **One date error against four.**

### Where the Saines 9A was BETTER
1. ⭐⭐⭐ **Form produced admissions. Prose cannot.** 43 numbered facts forced a paragraph-by-
   paragraph answer, and that answer is where **every admission now relied on came from**: the
   2020 agreement (¶34), *"at no stage did the appellant seek to change or alter the terms"* (¶36),
   **the human-error admission (¶40)**, the Communication Book removal (¶8), s 11 worker status
   (¶¶45–46), and ¶¶16, 18, 19, 33, 35 outright. **His Form 9 is continuous prose. Nothing in it
   is answerable, so nothing in it could be admitted.** That is the whole case for pleading form.
2. **Shape.** Facts → contentions → decision sought is the shape the Commission works in.

### ⛔ Where the Saines 9A was WORSE THAN ANYTHING IN HIS FORM 9
> **"The respondent does not admit the allegations in paragraph 5 of the appellant's statement
> because there are no particulars or details to respond to."** — Form 9C ¶8

**The lead paragraph of Stressor 1 in a paid pleading attracted the worst sentence a pleading can
attract.** And within the same stressor: February 2024 answered as June 2023; an incident date
answered as 14 January 2024; an April 2024 event *"not admitted … or at all"*; 1 May 2024 answered
as 13–15 May 2024. **Four date errors and one no-particulars finding, in one stressor.**

## PART 5 — ⭐⭐ WHAT THIS MEANS NOW
1. ⭐⭐⭐ **THE SAME OBJECTION, MADE TWICE, ELEVEN MONTHS APART — AND NOW CLOSED.**
   | When | Where | Against |
   |---|---|---|
   | 22 Jul 2025 | **Form 9C ¶8** — *"no particulars or details to respond to"* | the **Saines** 9A, ¶5 (lead of Stressor 1) |
   | 13 May 2026 | ⭐ **SOFC ¶11** — the no-particulars point, **re-made** | the **amended** 9A, **Stressor 1(a)** |
   | 12 Aug 2026 | **The 28-page particulars bundle, served** | ⇒ **the objection is answered** |

   ⭐⭐ **The Regulator published the defect twice, in writing, in its own pleadings. He read it and
   closed it.** That is not foresight — it is **responsiveness to a signal the other side put on the
   record itself**, which is the strongest and least assailable kind.
   ⭐⭐⭐ **And ¶11 was the one criticism he could extinguish unilaterally.** Every other limb of the
   defence ("reasonable in all respects" ×6) turns on evidence and witnesses. **The particulars
   objection turned only on his own diligence — and particulars, once supplied, cannot be
   un-supplied.** It was the single closable item on the board, and it is now closed.
   ⚠ At the 7 Aug mention the tribunal read the 9A aloud; per the 10 Aug register, almost everything
   voiced was already admitted, and the genuinely contested content was **the adjectives + 1(a)
   particulars + the six causation words**. Four days later the particulars half of that was gone.
2. ⭐ **The amended 9A is his Form 9 in Saines' clothing.** Hostile course / remuneration /
   **fatigue as breach** — his 2024 grounds, in the numbered form that produces admissions.
3. ⭐ **Every admission in the case came from the Saines pleading's *form*, not its content.** Keep
   using the form. The content was replaced in April 2026 for good reason.
4. ⚠ **Two grounds are still parked**: the union delegate policy (F4, QH-POL-248) and the
   post-injury contact of 12 & 16 July 2024. Neither is in the operative pleading. **Decide
   deliberately whether they stay out** — do not let them lapse by inattention.
5. ⚠ **¶23(d) is a live trap and an opportunity**: *"the amended pay slip for this period 24 April
   2024 shows the correct entitlement."* That is why the 4 Jul 2025 NNPD asked Queensland Health
   about **amended payslips and when they were amended**. Get that production before the pay
   stressor is run at hearing.

### The honest verdict on his question
**As a notice of appeal, his Form 9 was correct and it was accepted.** As a *pleading* it was not
one — it was prose, and prose cannot be admitted to. **But on legal architecture, on choosing the
fatigue *breach* over the fatigue *entitlement*, on ground coverage, and on factual accuracy, the
unrepresented November 2024 document was better than the represented one.** The Saines 9A's value
was its skeleton. He has since taken the skeleton and put his own case back inside it.
