## THE CONSULTATION ARCHITECTURE (standing, all items)
- I attend any discussion at any time, with a support person and my union representative.
- Meetings occur ALONGSIDE, never instead of, written responses (his existing line — keep).
- Everything agreed is confirmed in writing before it is treated as resolved; nothing is
  signed in the room.
- Participation waives nothing: cl 1.11.4 (no prejudice by continuation), and every clock
  continues unless a written agreement says otherwise.
- Tone standing order: every proposal is offered as the ORDINARY way — "this requires no
  restructuring and no findings about anyone" — the same register as the CE letter.

## WHY THIS DOCUMENT EXISTS
When any door opens (Stage 1/2, scoping, an s 89 reallocation, a settlement conference), he
is never the party who has to be asked "well, what do you want?" — the constructive,
instrument-anchored, immediately-implementable answer is already in hand. The party with
the plan runs the meeting.
