# BUILDING AN UNANSWERABLE NOTICE TO ADMIT — the ladder, the binaries, and the medical residue
> 14 Aug 2026. Design architecture for a second r 49 / Form 24 notice, built so that every item is
> either admitted or its denial collides with something already on the record — and so that when it
> is finished, **the only live question left is medical.**
> ⛔ **DESIGN ONLY. NOT FOR SERVICE NOW** — see `RULE-49-NOTICE-TO-ADMIT.md` Part 5 for why.

## PART 1 — ⭐⭐⭐ THE GOVERNING PRINCIPLE
**A fact is unanswerable when denying it requires the Respondent to contradict a document it
authored, a pleading it filed, or an admission it has already made.**

⇒ **Never plead a conclusion. Plead the components, one per paragraph, each with its own
independent source.** A conclusion invites *"a matter for the Commission."* A component invites
nothing.

### The six classes of item that cannot be safely denied
| Class | Form | Why denial is closed |
|---|---|---|
| **1 · Authenticity** | *"The document at Annexure X is a true copy of…"* | It is **their own** document, or the Registry's |
| **2 · Their own words** | *"On [date] Ms Taylor wrote to Ms Reese in the terms appearing at Annexure X"* | The document says what it says. ⚠ Plead **the communication and its terms**, never its meaning |
| **3 · Prior admissions, re-put** | Facts already admitted at SOFC ¶¶12(a), 14(e)–(f), 15, 21(a)–(c), 22(a)/(c)/(e), 26 | Denial contradicts their own pleading — **and it converts a pleading admission into an admission for the proceeding**, which is cleaner evidentially |
| **4 · ⭐⭐ Negatives** | *"The Respondent's List of Documents dated 14 August 2026 contains no document recording…"* | **They certified the list.** Denial requires **production** — and production is the better outcome |
| **5 · Arithmetic** | *"The interval between 1:15pm and 6:23pm on 15 May 2024 is 5 hours and 8 minutes"* | Not deniable by anyone |
| **6 · Instrument text** | *"Clause X of the FRMP provides [quote]"* | The instrument says what it says |

⭐⭐ **Class 4 is the sharpest, and it only became available on 14 August 2026.** The amended LOD
(52 items) plus the four sealed Form 29s and their productions define, by the Respondent's own
certification, the outer boundary of what exists. **Every absence inside that boundary is now
pleadable as a fact.** That is the concrete payoff of the disclosure he just received.

## PART 2 — ⭐⭐⭐ THE LADDER (why sequence, not content, is what makes it impossible)
Order the notice so that **each admitted item makes the next harder to deny.**

| Rung | Content | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Authenticity** — 12–18 items, every annexure | Costless to them; they admit; **the annexures are locked** |
| **2** | **Their prior admissions, re-put verbatim** | Must be admitted; denial contradicts their filed pleading |
| **3** | **Dated events**, each sourced to a document whose authenticity was admitted at rung 1 | ⭐ Denial now contradicts a document they authenticated **in this same notice** |
| **4** | **Intervals and arithmetic**, computed from rung-3 dates | Mechanical; no discretion available |
| **5** | **The negatives**, last | ⭐⭐ By here the entire factual frame is admitted. A denial of *"no document exists"* is conspicuous and forces production |

⭐⭐⭐ **That is the mechanism he is asking about.** By item 40 they cannot deny item 41 without
contradicting items 1–40 they have just admitted. **The impossibility is built by ordering, not by
cleverness in any single paragraph.**

## PART 3 — ⭐⭐ THE BINARIES (pairs where either answer helps)
A binary is two items where **denying one entails admitting the other, or entails production.**

### B1 · The office hours — the cleanest in the file
- (a) On 15 May 2024 at 1:15pm the Appellant asked, in the terms at Annexure __, for the manager's
  office hours. *(their SOFC ¶16(b)(iii))*
- (b) On 17 May 2024 at 9:30am Ms Taylor stated the manager's on-call hours to the Logan Switch
  team, in the terms at Annexure __. *(their LOD item 38)*
- (c) **Between 15 and 17 May 2024 no document was sent to the Appellant stating those hours.**
⇒ Deny (c) → produce it. Admit (c) → **the question was answered to everyone except the person who
asked.** ⭐ A fact, carrying its own force, with no adjective anywhere near it.

### B2 · The asymmetry — put the two intervals adjacent, nothing else
- (a) 8 Apr 2024, 3:56pm — the roster/minimum-break request, marked High importance
- (b) 24 Apr 2024, 3:15pm — the follow-up, marked High importance
- (c) The first substantive response was sent on 1 May 2024
- (d) **The interval between (a) and (c) is 23 days**
- (e) 15 May 2024 — the direction to retract was sent **5 hours and 8 minutes** after the email it
  concerned *(their 9C ¶15(b)–(e))*
⇒ ⭐⭐ **Nothing is argued. The asymmetry is admitted arithmetic sitting on one page.**

### B3 · The keystone
- (a) The FRMP provides a minimum break of 10 hours *(admitted, Form 24 ¶3)*
- (b) The break between the shifts of 17 and 18 March 2024 was 7 hours *(admitted ¶1; SOFC ¶22(a))*
- (c) The Respondent has described that shortfall as **"human error"** *(their words, SOFC ¶22(a))*
- (d) **No document records any assessment of fatigue risk before that roster was set**
- (e) ⚠ *(verify first)* No fatigue risk management system applied to Switchboard Services before
  30 June 2024
⇒ Deny (d) or (e) → produce. Admit → **the rule, the breach, their own characterisation, and the
absence of any process — all admitted, none of it argued.**

### B4 · The 15 May sequence
- (a) 1:15pm — the Appellant's email, in the terms at Annexure __ *(admitted)*
- (b) 1:20pm — Ms Taylor raised it with Ms Reese *(their 9C ¶15(d))*
- (c) 6:23pm — Ms Reese, **having sought advice from Human Resources**, emailed the Appellant
  *(their 9C ¶15(e) — ⭐ their own words, and it establishes HR involvement)*
- (d) The email was removed from the server *(their 9C ¶15(g))*
- (e) **No document records any assessment or consultation preceding the direction at (c)**
⇒ Only (e) is deniable, and denial means production.

### B5 · The protected act — ⛔ AND WHERE IT STOPS
- (a) On 13 May 2024 the Appellant made a complaint regarding clinical risks *(SOFC ¶15, **admitted
  in full**)*
- (b) The Ethical Standards Unit determined it constituted a Public Interest Disclosure *(admitted)*
- (c) That determination was made on 24 December 2024
⛔⛔ **NOTHING FURTHER. Not one item connecting (a) to any later conduct.** The dates sit where they
sit and the tribunal draws its own line. Any connective item is the conspiracy framing, it is barred
by Discipline Rule 1, and it would open s 32(5)(b) from his own side.

## PART 4 — ⭐⭐⭐ WHY THIS LEAVES ONLY THE MEDICAL
| s 32 element | Can a r 49 notice reach it? |
|---|---|
| Worker (s 11) | ✅ **Already admitted** |
| The **events** — every act, communication, date and interval | ✅ Fully |
| The **absence of process** — no risk assessment, no consultation, no written basis, no instrument identified | ✅ Via class-4 negatives against their certified list |
| Their own **characterisations** — *"human error"*, the June 2020 agreement | ✅ Their words, admitted |
| **Authenticity** of every document | ✅ Free |
| ⛔ **Personal injury exists** | ❌ **Medical** |
| ⛔ **Employment a significant contributing factor** | ❌ **Medical** |
| ⛔ Whether the action was **reasonable, and reasonably taken** | ❌ **Argument.** Form 24 ¶¶2 and 16 prove they will always answer *"a matter for the Commission"* — **never put it; it wastes an item and dilutes the notice** |

⭐⭐⭐ **THE DESIGN RULE, IN ONE LINE:**
> **The notice establishes the NOUNS and the NEGATIVES. It never touches the ADJECTIVES or the
> CONCLUSIONS.**

Executed properly, at the close of it the record contains an admitted chronology, an admitted
absence of process, and their own admitted characterisations — and exactly two things remain live:
**whether the admitted conduct was reasonable (argument, on an agreed factual base), and whether
employment contributed (Report B).** ⭐ **That is the residue he described, and it is achievable.**

## PART 5 — ⛔⛔ THE SEVEN TRAPS
1. ⛔⛔ **Never a fact whose denial supplies them a positive case.** *The June 2020 agreement entered
   this proceeding through his own Notice ¶4.* Write the worst possible denial of every item **before**
   including it; if the denial helps them, cut it.
2. ⛔ **Never a characterisation** — *hostile, victimisation, unreasonable, suppression, reprisal.*
   Reserved on answer, wasted as an item, and it opens **s 32(5)(b)**, which they have **not pleaded**.
3. ⛔ **Never motive, and never a connective item** between the PID and anything later.
4. ⛔ **Never a 2026 employment fact.** Causation in this track stops **24 October 2024**.
5. ⚠ **Never plead a document's meaning — only that it was sent, when, and in what terms.**
6. ⚠ **Volume is a risk.** Fifty was proportionate. A notice that reads as oppressive invites a
   complaint about the notice itself. **Cut every item that does not close something.**
7. ⚠ **They will not sleep on it.** The February notice was answered in **8 days**. The 14-day deemed
   admission will probably never operate. ⇒ ⭐ **The value is not in their silence. It is in forcing
   them to commit, in writing, before the hearing** — and every denial they plead is advance notice
   of their case theory.
