supplies the frame — and the frame is stated, never argued.**

# 3. ⛔⛔ CORRECTION — *"STRETTON SEVERAL TIMES OVER"* OVERSTATED THE MECHANISM

## 3.1 ⭐⭐⭐ WHAT *STRETTON* ACTUALLY IS, AND YES — THE CLAIM SUCCEEDED
> ***State of Queensland (Queensland Police Service) v Workers' Compensation Regulator & Neville
> Stretton* [2023] ICQ 015.**
⭐⭐⭐ **The EMPLOYER appealed. THE APPEAL WAS DISMISSED.** ⭐⭐ **The Commission below had found the
investigation into a complaint was NOT conducted in a reasonable way and that the injury was NOT
excluded by s 32(5). That stood.**
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **So the worker's claim succeeded, and survived the employer's appeal.**
⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AND IT SUCCEEDED ON THE HARDER, REPEALED TEST — *"the MAJOR significant contributing
factor"*.** ⭐⭐ **Cory's 18 June 2024 injury is governed by *"A significant contributing factor"*,
which is easier.**

**[84], the paragraph:** *"…There may be **ONE** significant aspect of the management action taken
which cannot be described as reasonable or reasonably taken or both. If that event was a significant
contributor to the worker's suffering a psychological or psychiatric injury and arose out of or in
the course of the worker's employment, then unless there was something else…"*
⚠ **The reference note truncates it. Read the full judgment in `documents/authorities/` before it is
quoted anywhere.**

## 3.2 ⛔ SO THE CORRECTION
