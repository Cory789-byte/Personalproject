## PART 1 — ⛔ THERE IS NO "AGREED FACTS NOTICE" IN A QIRC WC APPEAL
| Instrument | What it is | Applies here? |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐⭐ **r 49 — Notice to admit facts or documents** (**Form 24**) | **Unilateral.** Does not need the other side's agreement | ✅ **YES** — Part 2, general to proceedings before the commission |
| r 80(1)(d) — *"a statement, signed by all parties… of the agreed facts"* | A genuine agreed-facts statement | ⛔ **NO** — only for applications to **interpret an industrial instrument** (s 467) |
| r 123W(6) — memorandum of a settlement conference "including any admissions made by the parties" | Consent record | ⛔ **NO** — **Part 3B**, fair work claims and unpaid amount claims |

⇒ ⭐ **The QIRC instrument is r 49, and its power is that it needs no agreement at all.**
⚠ Note: the **Workers' Compensation Appeal Guide does not mention it.** Its forms list stops at
Forms 9/9A/23/27/29/32. **It is in the Rules, not the guide.** Do not conclude from the guide's
silence that it is unavailable.

## PART 2 — ⭐⭐ THE MECHANICS (r 49, verbatim effect)
1. **(1)** A party may, **by notice in the approved form served on another party**, ask that party
   to admit — **for the proceeding only** — the **facts or documents** stated in the notice.
2. **(2)** If the other party does **not**, **within 14 days** of receiving it, serve a notice
   **disputing the facts or the authenticity of the documents**, that party **is taken to admit**
   them.
3. **(3)** A deemed admission may be withdrawn **only with the leave** of the court, commission or
   registrar.

⭐⭐⭐ **Silence is an admission, and undoing it requires permission.** That is the same architecture
as the categorical disclosure request and the particulars bundle: **an instrument that produces a
result out of the other side's inaction.**
⭐ **"or documents" is half the value and is routinely wasted** — authenticity can be locked in so
no hearing time is spent proving that a document is what it appears to be.

## PART 4 — ⛔⛔ THE LESSON THE FIRST ONE TAUGHT, WHICH MUST GOVERN ANY SECOND
**They did not stay silent. They answered in eight days — and they used the answer to ASSERT.**
- **Notice ¶4** (no written agreement) → **DENIED**, and the denial asserts a **June 2020 8-hour
  agreement**. ⭐⭐ **That assertion entered the proceeding through his own notice.** It is now
  pleaded against him at SOFC ¶22(e) and it was the basis of the 2024 roster refusal.
- **Notice ¶22** (the comparator email) → denied: *"circumstances were different."*
- **Notice ¶19** (delegate facilitation) → denied on three grounds.

⇒ ⛔⛔ **A notice to admit is not free. Every fact put is an invitation to plead a denial, and a
pleaded denial becomes their case.** The 2020 agreement is the standing proof.

### ⭐ THE RULE FOR ANY FUTURE NOTICE
**Put only facts that are either (a) admitted, or (b) harmless if denied.**
Never put a fact whose denial would supply the Respondent with a positive case it does not yet have.
Test every item by writing the worst denial first.
