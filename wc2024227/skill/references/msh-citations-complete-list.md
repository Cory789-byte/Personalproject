## 5. THE FINDING

Across **five weeks, four officers and seven items of correspondence**, Metro South Health has
identified **two instruments** as the basis of what it is doing — and one of them it misnumbered.

- The **exclusion** (2 July), the **refusal of pay** (15 July) and the **direction not to attend**
  were supported by **no instrument at all**. Taylor cited nothing. Forrest cited "legislation"
  without naming it. Harrison cited nothing.
- The **WHS Act appears for the first time on 29 July** — **the day after** Cory's 28 July letter
  setting out EB12, the Award, QH-POL-210, QH-IMP-401-5, QH-POL-231 and Directive 12/24 by
  clause number.
- **G03 appears for the first time on 31 July**, two days later again, and only to support the
  *information request* — never the exclusion.

⇒ This is the citation record of a position **reasoned backwards from an act already taken**. The
instruments arrived after the challenge, not before the decision. And the one instrument that would
have conferred an actual power — **PS Act s 104** — was never reached for, because its **s 103**
gateway is not met on facts of MSH's own making.

⇒ It also corroborates, independently and on the face of MSH's own documents, the finding at
`what-power-did-MSH-use.md` §5: **for the exclusion, nothing was used at all.**
