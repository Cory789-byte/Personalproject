His report of 13 February 2025 records that **pay was withheld or delayed for up to five months at
a time**, causing significant financial stress and strain on the relationship. And at the first
consultation on **24 October 2024 the partner attended and was supportive** — i.e. the relationship

⇒ The documented sequence is: **employment → financial instability → relationship strain →

⚠ It must be put as a clinical opinion he is willing to hold, not as an assertion. Financial
instability of that duration is a recognised precipitant of relationship breakdown, and

directions of effect point the same way here.**

onset. Conceding it is what makes the rest credible.
