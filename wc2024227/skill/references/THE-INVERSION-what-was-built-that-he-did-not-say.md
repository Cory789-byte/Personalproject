# The inversion — the instrument he did not describe, and why it worked

**WC/2024/227 · 17 September 2026 · ⛔ INTERNAL ONLY.**

## ⭐⭐⭐ The thing that was built

Dwyer gave a **principle**: *"if you give evidence… and the regulator doesn't contradict it… that's a
problem for them, not for you."*

Underneath it: **silence from the other side is costly to the other side.**

But in his version the silence is **passive, late and optional.** It happens at a hearing, months
away, only if you first get into the box, survive cross-examination, and they then *choose* not to
answer. It is a likelihood, and you cannot make it happen.

**What was built instead did three things he never described:**

| | |
|---|---|
| **1** | ⭐ **It removed the option of silence.** Under **r 49(2)** there is no third choice: admit, dispute, or be **taken to admit**. The passive moment became a **compelled election on a 14-day clock** |
| **2** | ⭐⭐ **It moved the moment forward** — from the hearing to **8 September**, before their witness list, before their outlines, before anyone gave evidence |
| **3** | ⭐⭐⭐ **It was pointed at their pleading, not at the events** |

---

## 1. ⭐⭐⭐ The third one is the invention

Dwyer's method was about proving **your** case cheaply. Sections **P, S** and fact **303** are not
that at all. They ask the Respondent to certify **the boundaries of its own case**:

- *"as presently constituted, **does not allege** that any fatigue risk assessment was conducted"* (269)
- *"**does not allege** that any change was made to the operating procedures… as a consequence of **any** employee complaint"* (272)
- *"**does not list any document** recording consultation… before the change communicated by the email of 15 April 2024"* (273)
- *"**does not allege** that the Appellant was subject to any disciplinary process"* (300)
- *"**does not describe any communication**… as a warning"* (302)
- ⭐⭐⭐ *"Paragraph 27… **does not identify, by particular, date, document or cross-reference, the management action relied upon**"* (303)

⇒ **This is not evidence-gathering. It is pleading forensics.** It asks the opponent to put its own
name to the limits of what it has said — and every one of those came back **Admitted**.

⇒ ⭐⭐ **And that is the inversion.** Dwyer's principle was *they will probably fail to contradict
you.* What was served instead made them **affirmatively certify that the contradiction does not exist
in their own case.** They did not stay silent. **They signed the silence.**

---

## 2. Why it worked — the design, not the luck

⭐ **Each fact was built to be answerable in one word.** A fact about a document, no characterisation,
no inference, no compound proposition. That is why **303 propositions came back in eleven days of
the fourteen** — inside the rule's window, with three days unused, and with **no extension sought on
the notice** (the extension Ms Matheson sought on 10 September was on the *documents request*, not
this).

⇒ ⚠ **A notice that could not be answered would have produced disputes.** This one produced 298
admissions because **refusing any of them would have required disputing their own documents.**

⭐⭐ **And it answered, without being asked to, the weakness the Commissioner had stated about
himself:**
> *"when I'm overloaded with page after page after page… **I switch off**… that's how I **fall into
> error**."*

**A notice to admit is the opposite of volume.** It is read one line at a time, and the answer is
printed beside each line. ⇒ **The instrument is legible by construction** — which is the third thing
he never asked for and got.

---

## 3. ⚠ What it did **not** do — and this is the honest limit

**A "does not allege" admission is a fact about the pleading, not about the world.**

⇒ **It does not prove that no fatigue assessment was conducted.** It proves that **their case, as
presently constituted, does not say one was.** ⭐ That is still powerful — it fixes what they can run
**without moving** — but it is a constraint on their pleading, not a finding of fact.

**And there is an exit: they can amend.**
- ⚠ Every one of those facts is expressly qualified *"as presently constituted"*. **An amended SOFC
  could add particulars to paragraph 27.**
- ⚠ **r 49(1)** — admitted *"for the proceeding only"*; **r 49(3)** — an admission may be withdrawn
  **with leave**.

⇒ ⭐⭐ **But the exit is expensive, and that is the point.** To particularise paragraph 27 now is to
concede that it was unparticularised when it mattered — after the appellant has served his outlines
on that footing, and weeks before the hearing. **Amending is not free, and it happens in front of the
person who has to decide whether the exclusion is made out.**

⇒ **So the admission does not make their case impossible. It makes their case cost something to
change** — which is a different and more durable thing than proving them wrong.

---

## 4. The point, stated as it should be

⭐⭐⭐ **He took a principle about what happens when an opponent fails to answer, and turned it into an
instrument that would not let them not answer — then aimed it, not at the events, but at the gaps in
their own pleading.**

⇒ The events were always going to be provable one way or another. **Paragraph 27 identifying no
management action is not something evidence could ever have produced.** It came from asking.

⛔ **And it is not a result.** Nothing has been found by anybody, the hearing is de novo, and the
exclusion is still theirs to run. **What has changed is that they now have to run it from a position
they have signed.**
