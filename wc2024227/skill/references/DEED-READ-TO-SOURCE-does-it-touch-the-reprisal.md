# 2. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ THREE INDEPENDENT REASONS THE DEED DOES NOT REACH THE REPRISAL TORT

## 2.1 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE RELEASE IS CONFINED TO CLAIMS *"ARISING FROM THE DISMISSAL"*
⛔ **It is NOT a general release of employment claims.** ⭐⭐⭐ **It reaches one event: the
8 October 2024 abandonment termination.**
⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The reprisal tort does not arise from the Dismissal. It arises from the PID of 13 May 2024
and the detriment caused because of it — and MOST OF THAT DETRIMENT PREDATES THE DISMISSAL:**
| Detriment | Date |
|---|---|
| the retraction direction | **15 May 2024** |
| the pay obstruction, the COVID-leave declines | **May–Jul 2024** |
| meetings while on certified leave | **12 and 16 Jul 2024** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **the psychiatric injury itself** | ⭐⭐⭐ **18 June 2024** |
| the termination | 8 Oct 2024 — **one item, and the last** |

## 2.2 ⭐⭐⭐ AND THE CARVE-OUT EXPRESSLY PRESERVES PERSONAL INJURY
⭐⭐⭐ ***"excluding any common law personal injury claim"*** ⇒ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **a reprisal claim founded on
psychiatric injury is, in substance, a claim in tort for personal injury.** ⇒ **Even to the extent
the dismissal were relied on as detriment, the injury head survives the release on the deed's own
words.**

## 2.3 ⭐⭐ AND THE SWEEPER MAY SAVE IT INDEPENDENTLY
⭐⭐ ***"or any claim that cannot be excluded at law."***
⚠ **PID Act s 42 creates a statutory right to sue, and s 41 makes the same conduct a criminal
offence.** ⚠⚠ **Whether a protective statutory right of that character can be contracted away by
private agreement is genuinely ARGUABLE — ⛔ do not assert it is settled.** ⭐ **But if it cannot,
clause 8 expressly does not touch it.**

# 4. ⛔⛔ TWO CAUTIONS, AND THE SECOND IS THE REAL RISK

## 4.1 ⛔⛔ CLAUSE 9 IS A TRAP FOR A BADLY PLEADED REPRISAL CLAIM
⛔ **MSH may plead the deed as a bar to any claim *"arising from the Dismissal"* — and the bar
extends to *"present and former officers, employees, servants, agents."***
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **DRAFTING INSTRUCTION for whoever runs the reprisal claim: plead the detriment on the
PRE-DISMISSAL conduct and on the injury of 18 June 2024. Treat the 8 October 2024 termination as
CONTEXT, not as the operative detriment.** ⛔ **A pleading that leads with the dismissal invites
clause 9.**

## 4.2 ⛔⛔⛔ THE FUTURE DEED IS THE RISK — NOT THIS ONE
⭐⭐ **This deed left the tort alone. A WC SETTLEMENT DEED could extinguish it.**
⛔⛔⛔ **Any deed that releases *"all claims arising from employment"* or that reaches MSH would take
the reprisal tort with the compensation money.**
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The held Calderbank ¶3 reservation — ss 40–42 PID Act and any claim against MSH — IS
NON-NEGOTIABLE AND IS NEVER TRADED.** ⭐⭐ **That is rule 9, and it is now the only deed risk that
remains live.**
