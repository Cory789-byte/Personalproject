# The medical schedule of 9 September — how it is organised, and what that does
> 13 September 2026. Read from the served original,
> `documents/served-2026-09-09/Schedule_of_Medical_Documents_Tabs_M1_to_M9_AS_SERVED.pdf` (43pp).

## The conclusion, first

⭐⭐⭐ **It is built as a pre-emptive concession document.** Every item carries a *"Relied upon for"*
column **and** a *"Not relied upon for"* column. **The limits of the Appellant's own medical case are
stated in the served document, before anyone could put them to him.**

It is the same principle as the Form 24 and the footing letter of 9 September: **concede the
boundary yourself, and what remains inside it becomes very hard to attack.**

## The five devices it uses

### 1 · ⭐⭐ It discloses its own weaknesses

| Item | What he surfaced himself | And answered with |
|---|---|---|
| **3** — psychiatrist's email of 24 Oct 2024 | ⭐ *"The reference to **preoccupation with the claim as a cause of the injury**"* | *"The injury relied upon is that of 18 June 2024; the email records its state four months later."* |
| **4** — the 13 Feb 2025 report | ⭐ the report is marked **"not for medico-legal use"** | the author's own emails of 5 and 8 September 2026 — *"You can use them according to the need to support your legal issues"* |
| **2** — the certificates | *"The recorded mechanism as a finding of fact"* | the events are proved by the admitted facts, not the certificates |
| **6** — the Capability Checklist | *"Causation. It post-dates 1 July 2024"* | relied on for effect and capacity only |
| **7** — Review Decision 69983 | *"As a binding determination"* | de novo; relied on as an **admitted document** |

⇒ **Item 3 is the important one.** A passage in his own psychiatrist's email, suggesting
preoccupation with the claim contributed to the injury, is the Respondent's best causation point in
the treating material — and **he put it on the table himself**, with the answer attached. Found by
them later, it would have been damaging. Declared by him, it is neutralised.

### 2 · ⭐⭐ Every document relied upon is one the Respondent already holds

> *"Each document is already held by the Respondent at the item of its amended List of Documents
> dated 14 August 2026 stated, except item 5… and item 6, served herewith."*

⇒ The medical case is assembled **from her own disclosure** — exactly as the documentary case was
assembled from her own admissions. Nothing is sprung. The schedule tells her which of her own
documents he relies on, and for what.

### 3 · ⭐⭐⭐ The redaction protocol — and it answers the private-material worry outright

Tab M1:

> *"on source pages 5, 6 and 10, entries concerning private medical matters unrelated to the injury
> have been blacked out by the Appellant and are marked as such on the page… That entry is a
> medication for a private condition unrelated to the injury; **it is not an antidepressant, an
> anxiolytic or any other psychotropic medication, and the Respondent can confirm that from its own
> copy.** Nothing else relied upon has been redacted. **The Respondent holds the unredacted record at
> item 11.**"*

Four moves in one paragraph: **declare** the redaction, **locate** it exactly, **characterise** what
it is not — the only thing that matters to the case — and **point to her own unredacted copy** so it
is verifiable.

⇒ **That is the correct handling of sensitive personal material: no concealment, no exposure, and
checkable.** And it was done on 9 September, before the question was ever raised.

### 4 · The chronological spine

> *"No psychological illness before June 2024 (item 1); first presentation attributing symptoms to
> work, 28 June 2024 (item 1); certification of no capacity from 1 July 2024 (item 2); diagnosis of
> Major Depressive Disorder with anxiety state, 24 October 2024 (item 3); the treating
> psychiatrist's account of origin, 13 February 2025 (item 4); continuing effect, 3 July 2026
> (item 6)."*

Six steps, each anchored to a document she holds: **baseline → presentation → certification →
diagnosis → origin → continuing effect.**

### 5 · Guide 7.6.5 satisfied on the page

> *"Items 3, 4 and 5 contain clinical opinion; the Appellant does not rely upon that opinion
> otherwise than through the oral evidence of its author. Dr Ravikumar Bangalore Krishnaiah and
> Dr Peter Hawes… will be called."*

## ⚠ The one live thread — item 5

> *"Clinical records of Dr Krishnaiah from 24 October 2024 (offered by the practice on 5 September
> 2026 and requested; **not yet received**)… **Nothing until received.** … If any direction is
> required for reliance on them, the Appellant will apply for it."*

⭐ **Any correspondence between the Appellant and his psychiatrist sits in item 5**, not in anything
served or listed. It has been requested, it has not arrived, and its status is already declared on
the record.
