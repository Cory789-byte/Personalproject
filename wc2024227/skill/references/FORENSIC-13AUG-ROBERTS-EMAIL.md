### THE CAPACITY PARAGRAPH — the core

**"Whilst I understand your belief you are fit to return to the workplace; the medical evidence
provided to date does not support that."**
1. **"your belief"** — a signed medical certificate is recharacterised as the employee's opinion.
   The employer's own unstated, unparticularised concern is by contrast dignified as "evidence".
2. **A determination made before the employer's own deadline.** The RFMI (31 Jul) exists because
   MSH says it needs further information; the extended window closes **21 August**. The conclusion
   is announced on **13 August**.
3. **Made while the basis of the inquiry remains unidentified.**
4. **Contrary to the only current evidence.** ECC 3 July 2026: fit for the role with restrictions;
   the arrangement recorded as *"continuation of existing arrangement… worked and tolerated
   without deterioration."*
5. **No reasons given** for departing from that certificate.
6. ⭐⭐ **Assessed by Human Resources, not by a clinician.** An HR officer has weighed medical
   evidence and reached a conclusion about fitness. That is not an HR function.
7. ⭐⭐⭐ **THE INCOHERENCE — the strongest single finding.** If the medical evidence does not
   support fitness, the employer's own position is that he is **unfit**. An unfit employee is
   supported through paid sick leave or an income-replacement pathway. A fit employee works and is
   paid. He is **neither working nor paid**. The employer's position therefore produces a state
   that **neither of its own limbs justifies**: too unfit to be given work, not unfit enough to be
   paid anything.

**"Therefore, this leave would ordinarily be processed as sick leave"**
- ⭐ **"this leave"** — a presupposition doing enormous work. He applied for no leave. He was
  **directed not to attend** from 26 June. A direction not to attend is not leave; it is a
  direction. The word converts the employer's own act into the employee's absence.
- *"would ordinarily be processed"* — an administrative default, not a decision and not an
  instrument.

**"if no sick leave balances are available, it will automatically be processed as sick leave no pay"**
- ⭐ **"automatically"** — the mechanism of the non-payment is admitted to be a **system default**.
  No decision-maker, no instrument, no consideration. This is the answer to "on what basis was the
  absence coded" — the answer is *none; the system did it.*
