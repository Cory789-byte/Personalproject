⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The pattern is not hostility. It is the avoidance of a recorded position.** Processing,
forwarding, relaying, correcting, referring onward — **and, where a position would have to be

⚠⚠ **BE FAIR, AND KEEP IT FAIR ON PAPER.** A manager may not answer because she is busy, because
she has been told not to, or because she has been referred to HR herself. ⛔⛔ **He does not know
which, cannot know, and never needs to.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The chronology is the evidence. The reason is
not his to assert.**

# 3. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ WHY THIS IS GOOD FOR HIM

1. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE CONCESSION IS PAYROLL'S, NOT HERS.** Because she never answered, Payroll formed
   and stated its own view. ⇒ **She cannot withdraw it, and he does not need her to adopt it.**

2. ⭐⭐⭐ **THE RECALLS PROVE THE APPARATUS EXISTS AND WORKS.** ⇒ **Which is exactly why its

3. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **SILENCE ON A DIRECT QUESTION IS THE CLEANEST FORM OF EVIDENCE THERE IS.** It needs no
   interpretation, no motive and no witness. **It is a timestamp and an absence.**
4. ⭐⭐ **"Please feel free to contact your local HR team"** is a referral, on 8 September, by MSH,
   **in writing** — and it answers in advance any suggestion that he went outside the proper
