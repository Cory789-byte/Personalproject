# WC/2024/227 benchmarked against the won and lost QIRC psychiatric appeals (10 Aug 2026)

Research pass 10 Aug 2026 (web, secondary sources — ⚠ read any case to its ratio before citing
in a filing; nothing here is a pinpoint authority).

## WON (worker side)
- **Carr v WCR [2022] QIRC 059** — 9 stressors pleaded; 8 found reasonable; ONE unreasonable
  ("imposition of an unattainable level of workload and work intensity" without adequate
  personnel support) → injury compensable, Regulator paid costs. ⭐ The closest template to
  WC/2024/227: many strands fall, the workload/fatigue keystone wins alone.
- **Mahaffey [2016] ICQ 10** — the ICQ authority Carr applies: one unreasonable stressor
  suffices; s 32(5)(a) fails.
- **State of Qld (DAF) v WCR [2024] QIRC 53** — employer's appeal dismissed; workload-stress
  psychological injury compensable; RMA defence failed.
- **Austin v WCR [2025] QIRC 110** — worker succeeded on appeal (Littles note; read before use).
- **Adams [2015] ICQ 1 [23]** — no dominant-cause test; competing causes need not be outweighed.
- (King & Co note on councils: reasonable action taken in an UNREASONABLE WAY is compensable —
  the second limb.)

## LOST (worker side)
- **Anderson v WCR [2025] QIRC 361** — single upsetting interaction with a junior doctor;
  distress REAL but the action was reasonable enough; "reasonable ≠ perfect". ⚠ The shape of
  Stressor 1(a) if led as an interpersonal case.
- **Stable v WCR [2024] QIRC 274** — teacher; every pleaded stressor resolved into RMA; global
  sprawl, nothing survived. ⚠ The Delaney/global-evaluation shape — the same mechanism that
  lost Review 69983 despite the UMA finding.
- **Neale v WCR [2023] QIRC 009** — rejection upheld, same family.
- **Rogers/Dorman (costs, Dwyer C)** — conduct case: out of time, template submissions, no
  prospects; $9,062 against the lawyer personally + LSC referral. Conduct fingerprint, not
  merits — the anti-model WC/2024/227 does not resemble.

## The decisive variable across every case
Does at least ONE stressor survive as (a) not management action, (b) management action that was
unreasonable, or (c) reasonable action taken in an unreasonable way — with medical evidence
connecting THAT stressor to the injury. Everything else is noise the losing cases drowned in.

## Where WC/2024/227 sits
Structurally a **Carr/DAF case** with two advantages neither had:
1. The keystone stressor's unreasonableness was already found by the RESPONDENT'S OWN DELEGATE
   (factor 4, Review 69983), and the 7-hour break is admitted by every party (Form 24 ¶1;
   SOFC ¶22(a)).
2. The employer conceded in writing there was no fatigue framework at the Switchboard until
   after the injury, and "no consequential changes to operating procedures".
And two risks the won cases did not carry:
1. Self-representation across a multi-day hearing (the discipline block).
2. Enough contested strands (16, of which ~5 fall) to invite the Delaney global read that
   already defeated the claim once at review. ⭐ The hearing must be run as Carr — the keystone
   carried, the rest as context/severity — not as Stable.

## Costs spectrum (from the 10 Aug costs research)
WC appeals: costs follow the event, Magistrates scale (UCPR Sch 3 Scale E; UQ v WCR (No 2)
[2022] QIRC 245); both directions (Wicks (No 2) [2021] QIRC 112 — Regulator paid). Punitive /
personal orders attach to CONDUCT (Rogers), not to genuine unsuccessful cases.
