# 2. ⭐⭐⭐ CONTEST ONE — TAB 31, AND WHY IT IS THE ONE THEY HAD TO STOP

| Fact | Admitted |

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **The breach is conceded. Their whole remaining position on this stressor is the SIZE of it:
"human error, not intentional or repeated." That holds only if the shifts were unremarkable.**

## 2.2 ⭐⭐⭐ WHAT TAB 31 DOES TO THAT
⭐⭐⭐ **A CODE BLUE at 19:20 on the 17th. A CODE GREY at 08:48 and two NEONATAL MET CALLS on the 18th.
Six emergency events between 06:00 and 14:00 — on the shift that began after the seven-hour break.**
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **It converts a technical award breach into a fatigue exposure carried through emergency
traffic**, and it lands on his **admitted patient-safety function (fact 289)**.
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **Tab 31 is the bridge from "the roster was wrong" to "the roster was dangerous, and he was
the one carrying it." ⛔ That is why it is the ONLY document they fought on BOTH fronts —
authenticity disputed AND contents refused.**

## 2.3 ⭐⭐⭐ AND THE LEVER IS ALREADY ADMITTED — FACT 268

> K-LM26/729 **to Commissioner Dwyer**, stated: *"**a spreadsheet of recorded MET calls is available
> for the period 17-18 March 2024.**"*

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **The custodian has stated in writing, to the Commission, that the source document exists and
is available — and the Regulator has ADMITTED that she said it.**
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **A one-document notice resting on the employer's own written statement is exactly the
application Dwyer said he could decide quickly.** ⭐⭐ **Tab 31 is the only tab worth an application,
and this is why.**
