# PART 1 — ⛔⛔ THE DECISIVE REASON: IT CONTRADICTS HIS OWN LIVE CLAIM

**A wages claim and a compensation claim are opposite assertions.**

| | Says |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **The claim he is running** (Stage 1 dispute, 3 Aug 2026 — the RWA pay claim) | ***I was ready, willing and able. You excluded me. Pay me.*** |
| **A WorkCover claim** | ***I am incapacitated for work.*** |

⭐⭐⭐ **He cannot run both at full strength at the same time.** And the wages claim is:
- **consistent with the 3 July ECC**, which certifies him **fit with adjustments**;
- **consistent with everything he has said since 3 July**;
- ⭐⭐ **the only income route that requires him to prove nothing medical at all** — if an employer
  excludes a fit employee, the employer wears the cost;
- **already lodged, already running, with the 24 August deemed refusal about to crystallise.**

⇒ ⛔⛔ **Lodging now would hand MSH a written inconsistency authored by him, five days after he
asserted the opposite.** That is not a risk to be managed. **It is a wound he would inflict
himself.**

⚠ **The repo already flagged this tension for the fund lane** (`working-notes`, the Zappia
sequencing point): *the employment record says certified FIT; an IP or compensation claim asserts
incapacity.* **The same tension, only sharper, because both would be live simultaneously.**

# PART 2 — THE CASE FOR LODGING (it is not weak)

1. ⭐⭐⭐ **Income, on a statutory timetable.** WorkCover must decide within a fixed period. That is
   **weeks**, against an appeal whose median resolution is now **December 2026 – February 2027**
   (FULL-PICTURE §21). **No income since 13 July 2026.**
2. ⭐⭐ **It is a separate entitlement.** It does not depend on winning WC/2024/227.
3. ⭐⭐ **The 2026 factual base is fresher and better documented than the 2024 one** — the exclusion,
   the non-payment, the ECC and the RFMI are all dated, all recent, and **all employer-authored.**
4. ⭐ **Certification exists or is imminent** — the 3 July ECC, and 12 August.
5. ⭐⭐ **It converts the standoff.** An accepted claim moves the cost off him, and brings statutory
   rehabilitation and return-to-work obligations onto the employer.
6. **s 131** — six months from the entitlement arising. On a 26 June 2026 onset that runs to about
   **26 December 2026.** ⚠ A clock, but **not a pressing one.**

# PART 3 — THE CASE AGAINST (four, and two are serious)

## ⛔⛔ 1. It can hand the Regulator its best argument in the appeal — but ONLY if framed wrong

| Framing | Effect on the appeal |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **Aggravation / continuation of the 18 June 2024 injury** | **Supports it** — it asserts the very continuity that makes acceptance worth money |
| ⛔ **A new injury from 2026 events** | **Attacks it** — *the 2024 injury resolved; this is something else* |

⭐⭐⭐ **So the risk is not lodging. It is framing — and the framing is only his to control if the
medical evidence supports continuity.** That is **gate (ii) of the 12 August report**
(FULL-PICTURE §18.4).

⇒ ⭐⭐ **"After the report" is not caution. It is the only sequence in which he controls the
framing.**

## ⛔⛔ 2. It puts a second psychiatric opinion in the field — obtained by an insurer

A new claim invites **WorkCover's own examination**. That produces a second opinion **about the same
person and the same underlying condition, before Report B has even been served** in the appeal.

⇒ ⛔ **An unfavourable insurer's report would damage both matters at once — and he would have
commissioned the process that produced it.**

⚠ It also collides with `PROCEDURAL-ROADMAP` §1.2: expert evidence in the appeal is **not yet
timetabled**, and the plan is to **serve Report B under cover of an offer, not file it**. **A
WorkCover examination bypasses that control entirely.**

## ⛔ 3. The 2026 claim is a HARDER s 32(5)(a) case than the 2024 one

The 2026 conduct is: **an exclusion following a fit-with-adjustments certificate · a request for
medical information · leave and pay decisions.** ⚠ **All of that is textbook management action.**

And unlike 2024, there is **no admitted 7-hour break, no delegate's finding of unreasonable
management action, and no conceded absence of a fatigue framework.**

⇒ ⭐⭐ **Rejection is a real possibility — and a rejection is itself a document recording that a
delegate found the 2026 management action reasonable.** ⛔ **That document would land in the middle
of both the appeal and the employment track.**

## ⚠ 4. Bandwidth

Live already: the appeal · the Stage 2 referral (10 Aug) · the deemed refusal (24 Aug) · the RFMI ·
the psychiatrist · the union · the PID/reprisal track. **A fifth front carries its own timetable,
its own examination and its own decision-maker.**

1. ⭐⭐⭐ **The RWA pay claim** — certified fit with adjustments, excluded by the employer, wages

⇒ **These are consistent with each other, consistent with the ECC, and consistent with everything
