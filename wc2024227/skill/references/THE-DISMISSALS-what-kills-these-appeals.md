# 1. ⚠⚠⚠ *KERR* [2025] QIRC 190 — THE MOST INSTRUCTIVE LOSS, AND THE ONE CORY CANNOT BE
**Seven-day hearing. Fifteen nominated stressors. The Regulator CONCEDED worker, injury, and that
employment was the significant contributing factor. She still lost, on the exclusion. Costs ordered.**

> **[308]** *"I am in no doubt that she is affected by some form of psychiatric condition… But I am
> equally confident that **the condition she displays is entirely unrelated (in any compensable way)
> to her employment**."*
> ⭐⭐⭐ **[310]** *"Determining how or why **Ms Kerr's evidence was so consistently at odds with the
> facts** goes beyond what I am required to do… I do not consider her to be a deliberately dishonest
> witness. On the contrary, **I accept that she truly perceived the nominated events to be
> distressing**… **I accept that was her firmly held, but subjective perception of those events.**"*
> **[311]** *"…some underlying psychiatric illness has the effect of **distorting Ms Kerr's
> perceptions** of the words and actions of others, including her managers… she has felt set upon and
> unreasonably treated when, **on the facts of this matter, the reality was very much to the
> contrary**."*
> **[312]** the management action was *"in every respect reasonable and was reasonably implemented"*.

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE DOMINANT FAILURE MODE IN THESE APPEALS IS THAT THE WORKER'S ACCOUNT DOES NOT SURVIVE
CONTACT WITH THE EVIDENCE.** Not dishonesty — **a sincere account that the facts do not bear out.**
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The Form 24 has structurally eliminated that failure mode for Cory. 298 facts about what
happened are ADMITTED. He cannot be found to be at odds with facts the Respondent has conceded.**
⭐⭐ **And this is why his own correction was right: "perception" is fatal only where the events are
disputed and the worker's version fails. Where the events are admitted, it has nothing to attach to.**

⚠⚠ **Kerr also supplies the Respondent's best authority**, at [306]: ***Bowers v WorkCover
Queensland* [2002] QIC 18; 170 QGIG 1 — "Reasonable schemes, reasonably implemented, can miscarry."**
⇒ ⚠ **That is the authority behind their *"human error"* answer at ¶22(a) and ¶14(f).**
⭐ **The counter is *Delaney*: an award-based obligation is not a scheme that miscarried — "there is
no room for selective observance" — and the reviewer already found the rostering "in direct
contradiction to the award."**

# 3. ⚠⚠⚠ *CANNING* [2026] QIRC 51 — THE MEDICAL WARNING, AND IT IS PRECISE
Lost on **the FIRST limb** — no injury established.
> **[149]** the treating psychologist *"could best be described as **an advocate** for Mr Canning. So
> much is demonstrated by her **directing a 'letter of support' to WorkCover seeking that his appeals
> be expedited**."*
> **[152]–[153]** she would not give opinions, was equivocal, could not recall the context of her own
> notes, and *"appeared to be **committed to a version of events**"* inconsistent with those notes.
> ⭐⭐⭐ **[154]** *"In light of various authorities of this Commission with respect to **expert
> witnesses who assume the role of an advocate**, I am of the view that her evidence should be given
> **minimal weight**."*
> ⭐⭐⭐ **[156]–[157]** the remaining doctors' evidence did *"no more than establish… that it is
> **possible**"* — and could not show a new injury was **more probable** than a temporary
> exacerbation of a **pre-existing anxiety disorder**.

## 3.1 THREE DIRECT WARNINGS
1. ⛔⛔ **A treating clinician who advocates gets minimal weight.** ⭐ **The K1–K10 questions are
   correctly neutral — keep them that way, and never ask either doctor to support the appeal.**
   ⭐⭐ **Dr Krishnaiah's reluctance to write a medico-legal report is, on *Canning*, an ASSET: it is
   the opposite of advocacy.**
2. ⛔⛔ **"Possible" loses.** ⭐ **K8 must be asked plainly and answered in the statutory language —
   "a significant contributing factor" — not "may have contributed."**
3. ⚠⚠ **A pre-existing condition can swallow the claim.** ⭐ **Cory has ADHD and anxiety noted
   26/10/2022 — the *Canning* risk exactly.** ⭐⭐⭐ **But his answer is far stronger: the GP record of
   16 November 2023 (no psychological illness such as depression or psychosis, mood good), no
   antidepressant or anxiolytic among his medications, and RD 69983 at p 17 recording that the
   certificate of 1 July 2024 indicated "there was no pre-existing factor or condition", maintained
   in all later certificates. *Canning* had no equivalent.**
