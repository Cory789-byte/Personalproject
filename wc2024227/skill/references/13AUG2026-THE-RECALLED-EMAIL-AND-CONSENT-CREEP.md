the employee's opinion. Note the asymmetry: their unstated, unparticularised concern is treated as

leave balances, so they should automatically be applied."* The payslip of 12 Aug 2026 codes the

Roberts is wrong. ⭐ Must be put in writing and answered.

nothing left; (d) erase the hardship evidence; (e) convert the employer's withholding of work into
the employee's own consumption of entitlements. ⛔ **DO NOT CONSENT.**
