It says she monitors and audits emergency code responses, develops and monitors rostering and leave systems, and trains staff (`TWO-ROLE-DESCRIPTIONS-side-by-side.md`). Dwyer said on 7 Aug, about disclosure: "I'm inclined to take a broad approach in terms of things of relevance" (segs 140–144); that shows disposition, not a ruling on tender.

- **Use:** "without admission" is cheap for Cory to give (a consent order makes no findings, and the civil claim runs on MSH's own documents). Trade it for the substantive terms in E4. Lawyer to confirm before any offer is answered.
