# 1. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE GOVERNING RULE: A PUBLIC RESPONDENT NEVER SAYS IT WANTS TO SETTLE
⛔⛔ **Do not wait for words. There will not be a letter saying *"we would like to resolve this."***
⭐⭐⭐ **A statutory office cannot author a concession casually — someone has to be able to justify it
afterwards.**

does not name.** ⭐⭐ **The tells are things they DO, and one of them is a thing they FAIL to do.**

## C3. ⛔⛔ WITHDRAWN — "TAYLOR ABSENT ⇒ THE DEFENCE IS IMPOSSIBLE"
⛔⛔⛔ **The onus is the APPELLANT'S** (*Davis v Blackwood* [2014] ICQ 9 — he must establish the
injury **and** exclude s 32(5)(a)). ⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **The Respondent does not have to PROVE reasonable
management action. It can succeed if he fails to exclude it — on the admitted documents, the
pleaded explanations at C1, and cross-examination of HIM alone.**

⭐⭐⭐ **The replacement test, and it is better:**
> ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **FOR EACH MATERIAL STRESSOR, WHAT EVIDENCE WILL THE RESPONDENT USE TO EXPLAIN THE CONDUCT
> AND TO CHALLENGE ITS CONNECTION TO THE INJURY?**

⭐⭐ **Ms Taylor's name remains the single highest-INFORMATION observable — the Respondent employs
nobody who was present, and ¶¶12–16 are entirely about what she and Ms Reese did — but it is ONE
INPUT, not the answer.** ⛔ **And naming her preserves an ability to defend; it does not foreclose a
later concession.**

## C4. ⛔⛔ WITHDRAWN — "ONLY A CONTRARY EXPERT BEATS THE MEDICAL"
⭐⭐⭐ **Cheaper and more usual: attack the FACTUAL ASSUMPTIONS underlying the treating opinions in
cross-examination.** ⭐⭐ Or accept an employment contribution and run the exclusion.
⭐⭐ ***Mahaffey* [50]–[57]** — no formula; the assessment is of the accepted evidence and the causal
significance of the conduct. ⇒ ⭐ **An expert report is neither necessary to them nor, if obtained,
decisive.**
