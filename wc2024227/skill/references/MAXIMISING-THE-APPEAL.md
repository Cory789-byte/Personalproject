# PART 3 — ⭐⭐⭐ TIER 2: THE MEDICAL GATE. The only place large points live

## 3.1 The letter of instruction — four days away, and the single biggest lever in the matter

- ⛔ **Ask the s 32(1) question in terms** — *a **significant** contributing factor*, never "major".
- ⛔ **Ask it neutrally.** *"What, if anything, do you make of the reports of fatigue recorded in
  April and May 2024, in the context of the diagnosis you made in October 2024?"* **A report that
  reads as instructed is worth less than no report.**
- ⛔ **Confirm in writing that the practice will NOT apply the *"not for medico-legal use"* footer.**
- **Ask both gates:** the 2024 injury and its cause; **and** whether the 2026 incapacity is a
  continuation or aggravation.

## 3.2 ⭐⭐⭐ GIVE HIM THE CONTEMPORANEOUS RECORD — this is the difference between two kinds of report

Right now the opinion rests on a **history taken in October 2024, four months after onset.**

**Give him the dated trail:** 8 April · 9 April (escalated to HR) · 24 April · 1 May (refused) ·
18 June (onset).

⇒ ⭐⭐ **An opinion anchored to documents is one a cross-examiner cannot erode. An opinion anchored
to recollection is one they can.**

## 3.3 ⭐⭐⭐ THE STRUCTURAL RULE — already in the skill, and it is worth real probability

**The report must say: *"I am instructed to assume the facts set out in Mr Shepherd's statement
dated X."***

⛔ **The load, the break, the channels must be proved by HIM. If the factual description lives only
inside the psychiatrist's report, it is hearsay and it will be attacked there.** ⭐ **He proves the
facts; the doctor opines on assumed facts. Two witnesses, two jobs.**

## 3.4 ⛔⛔ THE UNADDRESSED THREAT — the 26 October 2022 entries

**Form 24 ¶32: the Regulator asserts an anxiety / ADHD history from 26 October 2022, said to be
missing from Exhibit A5.** ⭐ That is their attack on the **clean baseline** — which is otherwise
strong (16 Nov 2023, *"no psychological illness"*, admitted at ¶34).

⛔⛔ **This is the largest unworked threat to Gate A. If Dr Krishnaiah meets it for the first time
in cross-examination, the report is damaged in the box.**
⇒ **Brief him on it. A prior anxiety history is not fatal — s 32(3)(b) makes an *aggravation* an
injury — but it must be addressed in the report, not discovered at the hearing.**

## 3.5 ⚠ VERIFY BEFORE WEDNESDAY

- **Exhibit A5 (Our Medical Ashmore) for April–June 2024.** Attendances in that window would lift
  Gate A materially. **Their absence must be handled, not discovered.**
- **The 24 October 2024 clinical note *with consultation time*, the referral document and its
  date.** ⭐ These establish the **independent clinical pathway** — GP referral in September 2024,
  before any legal process — which is the answer to *"this is a litigation reconstruction."*
