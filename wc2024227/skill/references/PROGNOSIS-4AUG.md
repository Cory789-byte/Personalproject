## THE PROGNOSIS IN ONE LINE
Guardedly favourable: ~85% of paths end with something material (restoration, payment,
settlement or a defeated dismissal); ~35% end with him back at work; 4% end badly.
The dominant risk is not defeat — it is **attrition**: 19% of runs have him accepting
terms he would otherwise refuse because there is no income.

## THE THREE FORKS THAT DECIDE IT
1. **Runway (his, immediate).** The only variable that degrades every other number
   simultaneously. Centrelink/hardship = buying the right to say no.
2. **Report B (his, ~2 weeks).** Gates causation; without it the appeal drops ~10 points.
3. **MSH posture by 17 Aug (theirs).** Sets restoration, assessment, adverse risk together.
Secondary but real: the RTI nil return (69% likely, worth ~+3 on the appeal and more on
settlement propensity).

## WHAT THE MODEL CANNOT PRICE
Dwyer's view of the parties; a genuinely adverse IME; his own health under sustained
pressure; and any decision by MSH to treat this as a systemic problem rather than a
personnel one (which is the only branch that produces a good outcome for everyone).
