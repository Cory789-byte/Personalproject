# 1. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE FIVE FENCES — VERBATIM

## 1.1 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE DE NOVO FENCE (the master move)
> *"does not admit the fact contained therein **because the appeal is a hearing de novo and it is
> for the Commission to determine if this fact amounted to unreasonable management action**."*

⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Deployed wherever the proposition contains a characterisation.** It converts a factual
sentence into a legal conclusion and then hands the conclusion to the Commission. ⇒ **Nothing is
conceded about what the conduct *was*.**

## 1.2 ⭐⭐⭐ THE CONTEXT FENCE
> *"Further the Respondent says **the email as a whole, needs to be read to determine the meaning
> of the phrase and context**."*

⭐⭐ Admits the words, contests the meaning. ⚠⚠ **AND IT CUTS BOTH WAYS: if the whole email must be
read, the whole email is in evidence.** ⭐⭐⭐ **A party that fences on context has opened the
context.**

## 1.3 ⭐⭐ THE KNOWLEDGE FENCE
> *"does not admit the facts **because these are matters within the Appellant's knowledge**."*

⭐ Non-admission without denial. **Puts him to proof and asserts nothing** — so it cannot be
cross-examined on.

## 1.4 ⭐⭐ THE RELEVANCE FENCE
> *"**admits the contents** of the Respondent's Review Unit decision **and says that this fact is
> not relevant** because the appeal is a hearing de novo."*

⭐⭐ Admits the document, denies it matters.

## 1.5 ⭐⭐⭐ THE "SAYS FURTHER" RIDER
> *"**admits** that the Respondent sent an email directly to the Appellant on 14 July 2025 **and
> says that** the email addressed to the Appellant on that date **was in error**."*
> *"**admits** the fact that the Respondent served the medical records … on 22 July 2025 **and says
> further** the Respondent was not notified that the Appellant's solicitors no longer acted …"*

⭐⭐⭐ **Admit the event, then add a fact that reframes it.** ⚠ **Each rider is itself a positive
assertion by the Respondent — and a positive assertion is a thing a witness must support.**

## 1.6 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ AND THE MASTER FENCE, 8 SEPTEMBER
The cover letter reserves **truth, relevance, admissibility, weight, characterisation and
inference**, and limits the document admissions to ⭐⭐⭐ ***"existence and wording."***

# 2. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ WHY THEY ARE ENTITLED TO TELL A DIFFERENT STORY — AND WHERE IT STOPS

⭐⭐⭐ **s 550 WCRA makes the appeal a hearing de novo.** The Commission decides afresh on the
evidence before it. ⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **The Regulator is not bound by its own Review Decision of 24 October
2024 and may run a case inconsistent with it.** That is not misconduct; it is the nature of the
appeal, and the QIRC Appeal Guide anticipates it.

⭐⭐⭐⭐ **BUT THE DE NOVO FREEDOM OPERATES ONLY OVER WHAT IS NOT ADMITTED.** Under r 49 an admitted
fact is admitted **for this proceeding** and the Commission proceeds on it; evidence contradicting
it needs leave.

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Read the reservation and the admissions together and the shape of the hearing is
already fixed:**
| | |
|---|---|
| **What happened** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **settled — 298 facts admitted** |
| **Characterisation and inference** | ⭐⭐⭐ **expressly reserved — the whole of the live case** |

⭐⭐⭐⭐ **They have conceded the events and kept the argument. That is the only case they can now
run, and they have told him what it is.**

# 3. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ WHAT A "STATEMENT OF FACT" CAN BE — THE THREE KINDS

⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Three different things get called "facts", and the Regulator's response sorts along the
line perfectly.**

| Kind | Example from the notice | What happened to it |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **(a) Primary / operative fact** — an actor, a date, an act | *"On 15 May 2024, Director Tammy Reese sent an email directing the Appellant to 'retract' an email he had sent inquiring about office hours"* | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **ADMITTED** |
| ⭐⭐ **(b) Evaluative proposition** — a characterisation | *"the statement was false or misleading"* | ⛔ *"denies as untrue the fact, because the statement was not false or misleading"* |
| ⭐⭐ **(c) Inferential proposition** — a conclusion drawn from facts | *"demonstrates a lack of due diligence or transparency"* | ⛔ *"Ms Taylor's conduct does not lead to a conclusion that there was a lack of due diligence or transparency"* |

⭐⭐⭐⭐ **A notice to admit is for category (a) only.** ⛔ **Every (b) and (c) proposition put into a
notice is a free refusal handed to the other side.** ⭐⭐ **The 298 admissions are what they are
because the notice was built almost entirely out of (a).**

# 4. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE CENTRAL POINT — THE EMAILS ARE NOT EVIDENCE OF THE ACT, THEY **ARE** THE ACT

## 4.1 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ TWO WAYS A DOCUMENT ENTERS EVIDENCE
| Purpose | What must be proved | Hearsay? |
|---|---|---|
| **To prove the truth of what it asserts** — "the roster was equitable" tendered to show the roster was equitable | the **truth** of the assertion | ⚠ **yes** — needs an exception or a witness |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Because the making of the statement is itself the legally operative event** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **that it was made, and in what words** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **NO — it is not tendered for its truth** |

## 4.2 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ A DIRECTION IS A THING DONE IN WORDS
⭐⭐⭐⭐ **A direction, an instruction, a refusal, an approval, a demand, a reprimand — these are
performed *in and by* language.** ⇒ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The email does not report the direction. The email
IS the direction.** There is no separate act behind it to prove. **A direction has no truth
value — only content and effect.**

⭐⭐⭐⭐ **So when the Respondent admits the existence and wording of Ms Reese's email of 15 May
2024, it has admitted the management action itself — not evidence of it. The act.**

## 4.3 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ WHICH IS WHY *"LIMITED TO EXISTENCE AND WORDING"* GIVES THEM ALMOST NOTHING
⭐⭐⭐⭐ **For an operative document, existence and wording are the whole of it.**
⚠ The reservation bites only on **assertive** documents — a document tendered to prove that what it
says about the world is true.
⭐⭐⭐ **And even there it often fails, because where the case is about *the making of the
statement* rather than its truth, the making is again an act.** ⇒ **Whether Ms Taylor's statement
about the AVAC was accurate is not proved by calling a witness to its truth; it is proved by
setting the admitted statement beside the admitted instruction from Payroll and the admitted date
of submission. Three admitted documents, no witness, no hearsay.**

## 4.4 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ AND THE MANNER IS THE WORDS
⭐⭐⭐⭐ **s 32(5)(a) asks whether management action was reasonable *and taken in a reasonable way*.**
⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Where the action is done in words, the *manner* of the action IS the words** — the terms
chosen, the tone, the copy list, the hour it was sent, what it demands and what it does not offer.
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **An email is simultaneously the act and the best evidence of the manner of the act.**
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **No witness is needed to prove that it happened or how it was expressed. A witness is
needed only to explain WHY — and that is exactly where cross-examination lives, and where the
s 32(5)(a) defence holds or fails.**
⛔⛔ **Never open s 32(5)(b).**
