# Report B — letter of instruction: adversarial verification and fixes (9 Aug 2026)

The letter of instruction, the source-package guide and the "why it is the ceiling"
explanation were built by a workflow (3 draft agents + 3 adversarial verify lenses:
leading/instructed-answer; s 32(5)(b)+premorbid; ECC/sources/assumed-facts). All three
lenses ran on the **first** draft of the letter. This file records every defect they
confirmed and how the **finalised** letter (`drafts/out/CEILING_SET/01_Letter_of_Instruction_Report_B.md/.pdf`)
resolves it. Strong convergence across lenses; I agreed with essentially all of it.

## CRITICAL — resolved

1. **The delegate's conclusions were in the assumed-facts basket.** The first draft's
   Enclosure D narrated *"the Reviewing Officer's finding that the 17–18 March 2024
   rostering amounted to unreasonable management action, and that my employment was a
   significant contributing factor"* — and para 4 asked the doctor to *assume* Enclosure D.
   Two of three lenses rated this **critical**: it plants the exact s 32(1) answer sought
   at Q6.5, imports the s 32(5) framing that is not the doctor's question, and makes his
   causation opinion read as instructed. The de novo appeal (s 558(1)(c)) also strips the
   finding of binding status — the decision under appeal **rejected** the claim.
   **FIX:** Review Decision 69983 removed from the enclosures entirely. Enclosure D is now
   the **Form 24 admitted facts only** (7-hour break; criticality; delay). Para 4 adds an
   express direction: *"do not adopt, or treat as established, any conclusion,
   characterisation or finding of any other decision-maker."* The delegate's findings stay
   in Cory's **hearing/settlement** toolkit (submissions, Calderbank), never the report
   bundle. The ceiling doc §2 independently reaches the same place ("a tailwind, not the
   gate"). The package-guide item 4 was rewritten to match.

## MAJOR — resolved

2. **Q6.8 presupposed causation.** "…a diagnosed condition *to which employment was a
   significant contributing factor*" assumed the open question at 6.5. **FIX:** reframed to
   "consistent with, or instead displace, **the diagnosis you have made**" — causation left
   to 6.5.

3. **Q6.8 quoted A4's premorbid descriptors verbatim** ("detail-oriented, perfectionistic
   and conscientious, with high standards") — the **opening of the exact A4 sentence** whose
   next clause is *"can become consumed by issues he perceives as unjust"* = s 32(5)(b) in
   the report's own words. Putting it in a **disclosable** instruction invites the doctor to
   reproduce/expand the passage and memorialises the perception-limb link. Per
   REPORT-B-ARCHITECTURE Part 9 the "perceives as unjust" line is handled **orally on 12 Aug**,
   never written. **FIX:** Q6.8 now asks him to address premorbid personality **"from your own
   clinical assessment"** with no quote and no cross-reference to A4.

4. **Q6.10 instructed the capacity conclusion** — "express it *consistently with* my
   certificate of capacity dated 3 July 2026." Also incoherent: the 3 July 2026 ECC is
   **Dr Ma's** certificate, so telling Krishnaiah to match another clinician's document.
   **FIX:** enclose the ECC (Enclosure G), ask him to opine on capacity **on his own
   judgment**; consistency should emerge from independence, not instruction.

5. **Q6.5 / Q6.9 embedded loaded characterisations** ("sustained high-volume and
   complaint-exposed", "absorbing the cost"; "high demand, low control, absent/inadequate
   support"). **FIX:** 6.5 now points to "the conditions of the Switchboard role **as
   described in the assumed facts**" and "my **consequent use** of my own leave"; 6.9 asks
   **"whether, and if so how,"** those conditions can contribute — the causal profile is no
   longer asserted.

6. **Sources gap** — the letter demanded a "complete list" (6.4) but the enclosures omitted
   the very documents the questions turn on. **FIX:** enclosures now include the **GP / Our
   Medical Ashmore records** (26 Oct 2022 anxiety/ADHD + Vyvanse; 16 Nov 2023 baseline),
   the **Hawes WCC**, the **3 July 2026 ECC**, and **the 13 Feb 2025 report** — E, F, G, H.

7. **Wrong presentation date (also a factual correction that HELPS causation).** First draft
   said "first medical presentation… recorded from about September 2024." Per
   `confirmed-record.md`: the Hawes certificate records **first seen for this injury
   01/07/2024**; 8 Sep 2024 is the **signing** date. The true onset→presentation interval is
   **~2 weeks (18 Jun → 1 Jul)**, not ~3 months. Overstating it conceded the Regulator's
   best point for free. **FIX:** letter no longer asserts the date — it points to the Hawes
   certificate (Enclosure F, "first attendance … 1 July 2024") and asks the doctor to take
   presentation dates from the records. The ceiling doc's two "three-month gap" references
   were corrected: the ~3-month interval is **March roster → June onset** (the clinical
   bridge Report B builds), and onset→presentation is short and favourable.

8. **Same-clinician capacity contradiction unmet.** Krishnaiah authored A4 (13 Feb 2025 =
   "complete incapacity"); Report B will say fit-with-adjustments. Nothing invited him to
   explain the trajectory. **FIX:** added **Q6.11** — "how has my capacity evolved between
   that assessment and the present, and the reasons for any change" — neutral, no suggested
   answer. Lets the temporal explanation come from him, not from cross-examination.

## MINOR — resolved

9. **Q6.7 coached the temporal inference** ("a factor arising *after* 18 June 2024 could
   have…"). **FIX:** timing supplied as assumed facts; the inference asked neutrally. The
   contested cessation date changed to **"about October 2024"** (deed 20 Sep vs Form 20 ¶43
   8 Oct — nothing turns on it; both post-date onset).

10. **Q6.3 presupposed aggravation** ("whether and *how*… aggravated it"). **FIX:** "whether
    the employment matters… aggravated any such condition and, **if so, to what extent**."
    Para 3 tightened (test stated once, without heavy bold; "need not be outweighed" not
    re-cited inside the questions).

11. **Q6.10 "handling of that exclusion"** invited a s 32(5)(a)/(b) perception inquiry into
    the 2026 exclusion. **FIX:** "the **consequences** of my exclusion" — the fact, not its
    handling.

12. **Enclosure C (Amended 9A) as assumed facts** would import pleading characterisations.
    **FIX:** 9A is now provided "for **scope and context** … **not as facts you are asked to
    assume**"; assumed facts are the signed statement + admitted facts.

## Net
Every confirmed defect is closed in the finalised letter. The two factual corrections
(#7, #9) also **improve** the causation position. Companion docs (guide, ceiling) were
edited to stay consistent — chiefly the guide's item 4 (keep the delegate's conclusions
out of the doctor's bundle) and the gap references.
