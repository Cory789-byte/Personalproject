## PART 1 — THE PATHWAY EXISTS AND HAS A KNOWN SHAPE
A Queensland public sector **capacity / ill-health** route runs, generically:
1. a medical concern is identified → 2. the employee is directed to provide medical information or
attend an examination → 3. the employer forms a view on capacity → 4. if incapacity is found **and
no reasonable adjustment is available**, separation on ill-health grounds → and throughout, the
employee is **held out**, with pay depending on leave balances.

⭐⭐ **Its defining feature: no one has to allege misconduct.** No show-cause, no allegations to
answer, no conduct-style natural justice. It is a **capacity** route, not a **conduct** route — which
is precisely why it can run without anything ever being put to the employee to rebut.
⚠ **VERIFY the governing provision before it is named in any document.** The repo's working note
puts the hold-out argument on **PS Act s 101** — **read it to source first.** This matter has already
been damaged twice by provisions cited from memory.

### ⭐⭐⭐ Q9 IS THE STRUCTURAL POINT — AND IT IS NOT EVIDENCE OF A PLAN

the medical finding on which an ill-health separation would rest.**

## PART 3 — ⭐⭐⭐ THE HONEST POSITION
**The steps are consistent with a capacity pathway. They are equally consistent with a slow,
poorly-run return-to-work process with no one driving it.** Nothing on the record distinguishes them.
⇒ ⭐⭐ **But the practical effect is identical either way — excluded, unpaid, leave debited, no end
date, no instrument ever identified — so the defence is identical too.**
**Do not allege the plan. Close the exits.**

## PART 4 — ⭐⭐⭐ HOW TO CLOSE EVERY EXIT (in priority order)
### 1 ⭐⭐⭐ Defeat Q9 on the facts, before it is answered
**The duties exist and are being performed by someone else** — on his own account, five days a
fortnight for weeks. **An employer cannot say there is no work available while another employee is
doing it.** That is a **fact** question, not a medical one, and it removes the premise of Q9 entirely.
⚠ **Evidence it properly**: who, which shifts, from when — and in writing.

### 2 ⭐⭐⭐ A COMPLETE Report A. Never a thin one.
A clinician who specifies **achievable adjustments** removes *"cannot accommodate"* as an available
finding. ⛔⛔ **A thin report leaves exactly the gap the pathway needs.** This is the concrete reason
the "minimal report to MSH" idea had to be abandoned.

### 3 ⭐⭐ Force the instrument question — either answer helps
Asked six times since 26 June; never answered. **A capacity process has a statutory basis and
procedural steps.**
- If they **cannot name** the instrument, they are not on the pathway — and the exclusion has no
  stated legal basis at all.
- If they **do name** it, his procedural rights under it are triggered, in writing, dated.

### 4 ⭐⭐ Separate the pay from the medical question
He is **certified fit with restrictions**. Being held out is the **employer's decision**, not a
medical state. **Pay follows the direction, not the certificate.** The AVAC and payment argument
stands independently of anything the doctor writes.

### 5 ⭐⭐ The Anti-Discrimination Act window is the real deterrent
Exclusion and failure to provide work connected to an impairment engages the AD Act; the conduct is
continuing under **s 15(2)** and the window runs into **2027** (⛔ **not** ~8 Sep 2026 — that error is
already corrected in `CLAUDE.md`). ⭐ **A medical-grounds separation of an employee certified fit,
whose duties are being performed by others, with no reintegration plan and no psychosocial
assessment, is a very exposed decision.** It does not need to be threatened. It needs to be
*documented as available*.

### 6 ⭐ Keep the levers separate
Union (Petering) on the employment track · the appeal independent · the WHS/psychosocial package on
foreseeability. **Three levers, never merged into one letter.**

## PART 5 — ⭐⭐ WHAT PROTECTS HIM MOST

## 6.2 ⛔ BUT "THEY COULD NOT GET ME OUT" IS TOO STRONG
**Reasonable adjustment is not a job guarantee.** The obligation is to make adjustments that are
*reasonable*; the answers to it are **unjustifiable hardship** and **inability to perform the
inherent requirements** even with adjustment.

⛔⛔ **RFMI Q6 and Q9 ARE THE EXIT ROUTE FROM THE OBLIGATION:**
- **Q6** — *"able to fulfil the **full inherent requirements** of his Role … without restrictions or
  modifications to duties?"*
- **Q9** — *"**If we are not able to accommodate** the restrictions you have recommended, is
  Mr Shepherd able to safely return to the workplace?"*
⇒ Answered adversely, the obligation is **discharged** and a capacity separation becomes available.

## 6.3 ⚠⚠ THE ROLE DESCRIPTION CUTS BOTH WAYS — THIS HAS NOT BEEN FLAGGED BEFORE
It supplies the **demands** (the two-column put). ⛔ **It also defines the INHERENT REQUIREMENTS
against which Q6 is answered** — and under *"Mandatory qualifications … and other requirements"* it
says:
> *"The position is a **continuous shift working role**. You **must** be able to work a roster which
> covers multiple shifts over a **24/7 period**."*

⚠ **If continuous 24/7 shift work is mandatory, and his restrictions include unpredictable
rostering, MSH has a written basis for saying the inherent requirements cannot be met.**
**Do not deploy the role description without holding this in view.**

## 6.5 ⭐ AND SEPARATION WOULD CREATE FRESH RIGHTS
He is a **current employee**, so he retains the internal grievance and dispute machinery an
ex-employee loses — and **any separation would be a fresh dismissal**, opening reinstatement under
the IR Act 2016 in the QIRC, the AD Act, and the general protections/reprisal file.
⛔ **That is a deterrent to be understood, never a threat to be made.**
