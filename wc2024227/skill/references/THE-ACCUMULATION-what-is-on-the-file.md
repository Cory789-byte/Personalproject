## 4. ⚠⚠ THE LIMIT — and it is the whole reason the Form 24 exists

**Material exhibited to an interlocutory application is on the Commission's file. It is not evidence
in the appeal.** The hearing is **de novo**; evidence must be led and tendered. An exhibit to a
February affidavit supporting an application that was recalled in March proves that the Commissioner
has seen the document. It does not prove the document, and it does not bind the Respondent.

⭐⭐ **That gap is exactly what the Form 24 closes.** Under IR(T) r 49 an admission is made **for the
proceeding**, and r 49(3)'s leave-to-withdraw reaches only admissions "taken to have been made under
subrule (2)" — deemed ones. These 298 are express. So:

| | The accumulation (Feb–Jun 2026) | The Form 24 (Aug–Sep 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Effect | the bench **has seen** it | the Respondent **has admitted** it |
| Binds the Respondent? | no | **yes, for the proceeding** |
| Needs tender at hearing? | yes | no — the fact is admitted |
| Needs a witness? | yes | **no** — an act admitted is an act proved |

⇒ **The accumulation built the picture. The Form 24 converted it into evidence.** Three years of
filing put the documents in front of the Commission; eleven days in September made the other side
own them.
