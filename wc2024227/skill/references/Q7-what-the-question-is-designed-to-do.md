# RFMI QUESTION 7 — WHAT IT IS DESIGNED TO DO, AND WHY NARROWING THE REPORT DEFERS IT
> 28 August 2026. ⛔ Internal. ⚠ Analysis of the question's function. **No intention is asserted** —
> this is a defensive reading of an instrument on its own words.

## THE QUESTION
> **RFMI Q7 (Hughes, 31 July 2026):** *"What does **'working memory is affected under stress'** mean
> in functional terms — how it may affect capacity to perform the substantive duties **under normal
> workplace demands**, and the circumstances likely to cause the difficulty."*

## 1. ⭐⭐⭐ WHERE THE PHRASE CAME FROM — IT IS HIS OWN
*"Working memory affected under stress"* is **Dr Krishnaiah's, from the report of 13 February 2025**
— seventeen months before the ECC picked it up.

⇒ ⭐⭐ **The employer is not introducing new material. It is taking a phrase out of the Appellant's
own treating report and asking that it be converted into a functional finding.**

## 2. ⛔⛔ WHAT THE WORDING DOES — THREE MOVES IN ONE SENTENCE

| The move | Effect |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐ **"under normal workplace demands"** | The original phrase is **stress-conditional**. Q7 re-frames it against the **ordinary** baseline. ⇒ **A contextual, conditional difficulty becomes a general one** |
| **"capacity to perform the substantive duties"** | Shifts from **symptom** to **capacity for the role** — the statutory language of fitness |
| **"the circumstances likely to cause the difficulty"** | Invites a **list of triggers**, which in a 24/7 emergency-coordination role is a list of **the ordinary duties** |

## 3. ⭐⭐ AND WHERE IT SITS IN THE SEQUENCE
| Q | What it establishes |
|---|---|
| **Q7** | that working memory is impaired in performing the substantive duties |
| **Q6** | *"Is he able to fulfil the **full inherent requirements** of his role **without** restrictions?"* |
| ⛔ **Q9** | *"**If we are not able to accommodate** the restrictions you have recommended, is Mr Shepherd able to safely return to the workplace?"* |

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **Q7 is the evidential predicate; Q6 is the framing; Q9 is the conclusion.** Read together the
set builds, from the Appellant's own psychiatrist, the elements of an inherent-requirements case.

⚠ **That is what the questions are structurally capable of doing. It is not an allegation that it is
intended** — see `IF-THEY-TRY-MEDICAL-SEPARATION.md`.

## 4. ⭐⭐⭐ THE DEFENCE IS ONE DISTINCTION — CAPACITY v CONDITIONS
- **Capacity** is what he can do. The record says it is intact: **~29% FTE, six shifts a fortnight,
  worked and tolerated for twelve months "without deterioration"** (ECC, 3 July 2026), usual
  switchboard operational duties **suitable**.
- **Conditions** are what determine whether the capacity is usable — predictable rostering, adequate
  recovery between shifts, limits on sustained concurrent emergency load.

⇒ ⭐⭐ **Q7 is designed to blur those two. The entire answer is to keep them sharp.** A restriction is
a statement about **conditions**, not a deficit in the person.

## 5. ⭐⭐ THE NARROWING ALREADY DEFERS IT — AND THAT IS PROTECTIVE
Q7 is answered at **3.5(c)** of the letter of instruction. **3.4–3.8 have been cut from the report.**

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **A benefit of the narrowing that was not articulated when it was recommended: it removes Q7
from the document entirely for now.** The causation report says nothing about functional working
memory, and nothing that could be read across into a capacity case.

⚠ **The employer's request remains outstanding and will have to be dealt with separately** — but on
its own timetable, with its own funding, and after the appeal report is safely served.

## 6. ⛔ WHAT TO DO BEFORE Q7 IS EVER ANSWERED
1. ⭐⭐⭐ **Obtain formal cognitive testing.** The February 2025 report records cognitive difficulty
   *"particularly with concentration and working memory"* **but expressly records that "no formal
   cognitive assessment was conducted."**
   ⇒ **Q7 currently asks a clinician to give functional detail he has never measured.** Testing
   converts Q7 from an invitation to speculate into a measurement — and a measurement can be
   normal, or normal-with-conditions, in a way an impression cannot.
2. ⭐ **Any answer must be anchored to the twelve months actually worked**, not to a hypothetical
   full-time role.
3. ⛔ **Q9 is never answered.** The letter of instruction already marks it *"Not asked — a question
   about workplace arrangements."* **Hold that line.**
4. ⛔ **Consent.** The s 89 letter of 3 August expressly refused use of the clinical material in
   connection with any other proceeding. **Any crossover use is a use already refused in writing.**
