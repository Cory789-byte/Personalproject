# IF THE APPEAL IS ALLOWED — what it delivers, what it does not, and the clock nobody has started
> 14 Aug 2026. ⛔ **Every statutory reference below is FLAGGED FOR VERIFICATION.** Chapter 5 WCRA and
> the limitation position must be read to source before anything relies on them. This matter has
> already been damaged twice by provisions used from memory.

---

## PART 1 — ⭐⭐ WHAT ACCEPTANCE GENUINELY DELIVERS
1. **The review decision is set aside and the claim is accepted** for the psychological injury of
   18 June 2024.
2. **Weekly compensation for periods of certified incapacity** — backdated. Realistically the
   substantial period is **18 Jun 2024 → his return on 24 Feb 2025**, plus any later certified
   incapacity.
3. **Medical and rehabilitation expenses**, reimbursed — including costs he has self-funded.
4. ⭐⭐⭐ **The employer's statutory rehabilitation and suitable-duties obligations are engaged.**
   MSH's position of **15 July 2026** was that the WCRA rehabilitation obligation *"applies to
   workers with accepted workers' compensation claims, and does not extend to me."*
   ⇒ **Acceptance reverses that sentence.** It is the single most useful practical consequence.
5. **A permanent impairment assessment** and a Notice of Assessment.
6. ⭐⭐ **The gateway to common law damages.** Access is restricted to workers with an accepted
   claim who have been through assessment — so the appeal really is the door. ⚠ **Verify Ch 5.**

## PART 2 — ⛔⛔ WHAT IT DOES **NOT** DELIVER — "SAFE" IS THE WRONG WORD
| Assumption | Reality |
|---|---|
| *"They would have to take me back"* | ⛔ **No.** Suitable-duties obligations are real but slow and weakly enforced — and they sit awkwardly with a worker **certified fit**, who is not within the rehabilitation framework in the ordinary way |
| *"My job would be protected"* | ⛔ **No.** A capacity/ill-health process can still run. Acceptance makes a medical separation **more exposed**, not barred |
| ⭐⭐⭐ *"It pays me for being held out since July 2026"* | ⛔⛔ **NO — AND THIS IS THE BIG ONE.** Weekly compensation requires **certified incapacity**. He is certified **FIT with restrictions**. You cannot generally claim compensation for a period you are certified fit to work |

### ⭐⭐⭐ THE CONSEQUENCE THAT MUST BE UNDERSTOOD
**The 2026 loss is a WITHHELD WAGES problem, not a COMPENSATION problem.**
It is recovered — if at all — through the **employment track**: wages and the AVAC, the Award and
certified agreement, the grievance and dispute pathway, and potentially the **AD Act**.
⛔ **Winning the appeal does not pay for the exclusion.** Two different losses, two different
forums, two different remedies. **They must never be run as one.**

## PART 3 — ⛔⛔⛔ THE CIVIL HALF: TWO THINGS THAT DESTROY CASES
### 3.1 THE ELECTION ON THE NOTICE OF ASSESSMENT — IRREVERSIBLE
On assessment he will be offered a **lump sum** for the degree of permanent impairment. For a
non-terminal injury below the statutory threshold, **accepting the lump sum generally EXTINGUISHES
the right to seek common law damages.**
⛔⛔ **This is a one-way door, and it is where unrepresented workers lose everything they have
built.** The offer arrives looking like a win and is frequently worth a small fraction of a damages
claim.
⭐ **RULE, ABSOLUTE: do not sign, accept, or respond to a Notice of Assessment without legal advice.**
Not the union — the union's demarcation excludes the appeal. A personal injuries solicitor, who will
act on a **no-win-no-fee** basis on a claim of this kind. ⚠ **Verify the threshold and election
mechanics under Ch 5 when the notice arrives.**

### 3.2 ⭐⭐⭐ THE LIMITATION CLOCK IS ALREADY RUNNING — AND IT IS NOT WAITING FOR THE APPEAL
**Personal injury limitation: three years from the date of injury. Injury 18 June 2024 ⇒ ~18 JUNE
2027.**
⛔⛔ **That is under two years away, and the appeal has not been heard.** WCRA pre-court procedures
(Notice of Claim for Damages, compliance steps) take **many months** on their own, and they cannot
ordinarily start before the claim is accepted and assessed.
⚠⚠ **VERIFY IMMEDIATELY how the limitation period interacts with a pending s 549 appeal and with
Ch 5 compliance — there are provisions dealing with this, and the answer changes the entire
timetable.** ⭐ **If the appeal is not resolved well before mid-2027, the civil claim can be
prejudiced by the delay alone.**
⇒ ⭐⭐ **This is a reason to force resolution of the appeal, not to let it drift.** It is the
strongest non-financial argument for moving now.

## PART 4 — ⭐ THE HONEST SUMMARY
**Acceptance is the right target and the correct gateway. It is not safety.**
- ✅ It pays the 2024–25 period, the medicals, and it engages the rehabilitation obligation.
- ✅ It opens the common law door — which is where the substantial money is.
- ⛔ It does not compel a return, does not protect the job, and **does not pay for the 2026 exclusion**.
- ⛔⛔ And it starts a sequence — assessment, election, pre-court procedures — that is **more
  dangerous to run unrepresented than the appeal itself**, against a limitation date in mid-2027.

⭐⭐⭐ **Which reorders the priorities one further step:**
**money → the report → a renewed offer → and, before the Notice of Assessment ever arrives, a
personal injuries solicitor engaged on a no-win-no-fee basis.**
The appeal is winnable self-represented. **The election is not a place to be self-represented.**
