# The outline against the Form 9A — what changed, why, and what it costs them
> 13 September 2026. Compares the served outline of 9 September 2026 with the Second Amended
> Form 9A as built 13 September 2026.

## 1 · They are not versions of each other. They do different work.

| | **Second Amended Form 9A** | **Outline of evidence** |
|---|---|---|
| Length | 14 pages | **one A4 page** (direction 2 cap) |
| Organised by | ⭐ **legal category** — three stressors, mapped to s 32(5)(a) | ⭐ **narrative topic** — role, sleep, roster, pay, database, hours, complaints, health, onset, after |
| What it asserts | facts **and contentions** — what is alleged and why it matters in law | **topics on which oral evidence will be given**. It says so: *"It is not a statement of evidence and is not verified."* |
| Proved by | documents, each proposition carrying its admitted paragraph inline | ⭐ **a person in the box** |
| Answered by | their statement of facts and contentions | ⭐ **their witnesses, under direction 4** |

⇒ **The 9A is built for the legal question. The outline is built for the witness box.** The same
events, arranged for two different audiences and two different moments.

## 2 · ⭐⭐ What the outline carries that the 9A cannot

A pleading can only carry what a document proves. The outline carries what only a person can say —
and much of it is what makes the admitted record **intelligible**:

- ⭐ **what the codes are**: *"a Code Blue, a cardiac or respiratory arrest; a MET call, a patient
  deteriorating on a ward; a Code Grey, an unarmed threat; a neonatal MET call, a newborn"*.
  ⇒ Admitted fact 58 — *"MET call team called x5290 asking where MET call was located 'VHUB'"* —
  **means nothing to a reader who does not know what a MET call is.** The outline supplies that.
- **the volume**: *"Between 15 and 50 emergency codes go out in 24 hours; some 200 to 300 calls a
  shift, many from people distressed, frightened or in crisis."*
- **the lived detail**: sleeping in the car before driving home; driving to work in May and June
  2024, being unable to enter the building, and calling in sick from the car park.
- **the practical obstacle**: his Queensland Health email was restricted, *"so I cannot produce my
  work emails myself."*
- ⭐⭐ **the mechanism, in his own voice**, closing topic 1:
  > *"from the moment I logged onto the board I was held responsible for what went wrong anywhere in
  > that system, while the means of correcting it, the directory, the on-call roster, my own roster
  > and my pay, were held by others."*

  The 9A pleads that as **contention 4**. The outline puts it **in the witness box**, where it is
  evidence rather than argument.
- ⭐ **the causation bridge**, topic 9:
  > *"the matters at paragraphs 2 to 7 above, and their effect on me as each occurred and as they
  > accumulated, **are the matters I reported to each doctor, as their records show; the events those
  > records name are the facts admitted on 8 September 2026**."*
- **the credit answer**, topic 7: *"I never said anything hostile about Ms Taylor or any other
  member of staff."* ⇒ A statement about himself. Right in evidence; wrong in a pleading.

## 3 · ⭐⭐ Why the combination is structurally harder for them

**1. The two documents have to be answered by different means, and they cannot borrow from each
other.** The 9A is answered in a statement of facts and contentions. **The outline can only be
answered by a witness.** Documents cannot contradict what a person saw.

**2. Their outlines are drafted to his agenda.** Direction 4 obliges them to outline each lay
witness's evidence by 30 September. His outline, and his two lay outlines, define what those
witnesses must address. He has set the topics.

**3. The arithmetic is against them.** He served **three outlines plus a 43-page medical schedule**.
They must answer with **one A4 page per lay witness** — and, on the mapping at
`WHAT-THE-SERVED-OUTLINES-FORCE.md`, the only people who can speak to any of it are **Taylor and
Reese**. Two pages against three outlines, five witnesses and the admitted record.

**4. The admissions removed the cheap answer.** With 298 paragraphs admitted, their witnesses cannot
dispute that the emails were sent, when, by whom, or in what terms. ⇒ **Their outlines can only go
to why** — and *why* is the one thing no document in the case records.

⚠ **Their available answer:** an independent medical examination. **WCRA s 556** lets the appeal body
order the claimant to submit to examination by a specified registered person, at any time before or
after the hearing starts. ⛔ **Nothing in the repo shows whether the Regulator has commissioned one.
Expect it, and expect it to be the centre of their causation case.**

## 5 · ⚠⚠ Two costs this list creates, neither of which is priced anywhere

1. **Conduct money for four witnesses.** Guide 10.1 and **IR(T) r 62** — the party calling a witness
   pays reasonable travel expenses and conduct money **before** the day of attendance, and
   *"if you do not pay… the witness will have a lawful reason for not attending."*
   ⭐ **"Expert witnesses are entitled to a higher rate of conduct money. The longer the expert is
   required, the more conduct money they will be entitled to."** ⇒ **Two treating doctors at expert
   rates is a real and unbudgeted number. Establish it before the hearing is listed.**
2. **Costs follow the event** (Guide Part 10) — if the appeal fails he may be ordered to pay the
   Respondent's costs of the hearing, including counsel and expert outlays.
   ⛔ Self-representation does not protect against this.
