The notice of 4 July 2025 to Mind and Memory sought:

> *"Full copy of notes, reports, memorandums, letter, and other correspondence… in relation to
> Mr Cory Shepherd… **concerning work related issues**."*

⭐ **"Concerning work related issues"** is a limit. Correspondence about a relationship breakdown is
not, on its face, within it.

⚠ **But whether the practice confined its production to that scope is unknown.** Practices commonly
produce the whole file. `AMENDED-LOD-14AUG2026-ANALYSIS.md` already flagged this as open:

## 4 · The substantive issue: competing stressors

Whatever is or is not in her hands, **non-work stressors will be explored** — through the GP records
(item 11, which she holds) and in cross-examination of Dr Krishnaiah. *Tuesley* [2020] QIRC 027 is
the warning: that appeal turned in part on admissions of *"significant non-work related stressors"*.

⭐⭐ **The answer is already a standing discipline of this matter, and it is the right one.**

**WCRA s 32(1) requires employment to be *a* significant contributing factor — not the major one,
and not the only one.** The "major significant" test for psychiatric injury was repealed by the 2019
amendments, long before 18 June 2024. **Competing causes need not be outweighed. They need only
leave employment as *a* significant contributor.**

And on the record as it stands:
- the Regulator's **own review officer** found the injury *"arose out of employment, to the extent
  that it arose out of factors 2, 3 and 4, where employment was **a significant contributing
  factor**"*;
- the 13 February 2025 report records the origin as *"workplace stress stemming from issues with
  management and rostering"*;
- the contemporaneous GP notes of 28 June and 1 July 2024 record *"stress at work"*, *"work
  stress"*, *"they withhold pay at times… manipulate his roster"*, and *"upset by people not
  following rules"*;
- ⭐ and Part B.1 of the Form 9A pleads the **baseline** — never diagnosed with or treated for
  depression before 18 June 2024, and no antidepressant, anxiolytic or other psychotropic medication
  as at 16 May 2024.

⇒ **Do not fight the existence of other stressors. Fight only the proposition that employment was
not a significant contributor — which their own officer has already rejected once.**

## 5 · The question to the psychiatrist about the diagnosis

⚠ Asking a treating doctor to explain where a diagnosis came from is **an ordinary thing for a
patient to do**, and any clinician would say so. Out of context in cross-examination it can be made
to look like uncertainty, or like shaping the evidence.

⭐ **The answer is preparation, not concealment.** He should be able to say, plainly and without
elaboration, why he asked and when. Dr Krishnaiah is being called and can confirm it in a sentence.

⛔ Do not attempt to keep it out. If it is in the file, it comes in.
