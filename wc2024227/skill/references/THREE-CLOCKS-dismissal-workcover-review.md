> ⭐⭐⭐ **25 business days expired Tuesday 22 October 2024.**
> ⭐⭐⭐ **The review decision is dated Tuesday 22 October 2024.**
> **She decided on the last available day. The reasons followed two days later.**

**What this does and does not establish:**

| | |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐ **DOES** | ⭐⭐ **It is a complete, innocent, verifiable explanation for the shape of the reasons** — 28 pages, no authority later than 2009, a "two out of three" formulation that miscounts, and recited shift times that do not produce her own conclusion. **A decision written against a hard statutory deadline looks exactly like that** |
| ⭐⭐ **DOES** | **It is neutral. It attributes nothing to anybody.** It is the kind of finding that survives cross-examination because it is arithmetic |
| ⛔ **DOES NOT** | **It is not a breach.** She decided **within** time |
| ⛔ **DOES NOT** | **It is not a ground of appeal.** s 550 is a hearing **de novo** — the reasons' quality is irrelevant to the Commission's task |
| ⛔ **DOES NOT** | **It is not evidence of bias.** ⭐ It is the opposite: it supplies a pressure-of-time explanation for errors that would otherwise need one |

anything to her** — and it is the answer that should sit at the front of
