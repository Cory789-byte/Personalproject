## 6. The conference (Appeal Guide, verified)

- **What the Guide says.**
  - "**No commercial settlement is possible at the conference.** Conferences … are not intended to facilitate
    conciliations".
  - "**Following the conference**, the Respondent may decide to review its position or consider conceding".
  - The conference can establish "whether any elements of s 32 of the Act can be agreed".
- **The Member.** "Usually the same Member … will also chair the second conference". The chairing Member "will not
  be the Member who hears the appeal".
- **Private conference.** The Member may hold one "if the Member believes that it may assist the parties … not
  recorded or transcribed".
- **Mentions.** As after 13 Mar, contested procedure is disposed of at a **mention with directions**.
- **Why the Regulator wants everything there:**
  - no written positions;
  - the §5.1 exit on "new information";
  - an unrecorded private session;
  - the Member explaining hearing risk to a self-represented appellant;
  - timing on counsel's dates;
  - a possible trade of consent to leave for narrowing the 9A.
