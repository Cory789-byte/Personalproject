## 2. ⭐⭐⭐ WHAT THE DEED DID TAKE WAS ANNUAL LEAVE — CLAUSE 2
> **2(a)** *"Credit leave balances paid out to the Applicant on 23 October 2024 as follows;
> (i) annual leave: 54.16 hours. (ii) leave loading: 54.16 hours"*
> **2(c)** *"Pay to the Applicant: (i) The amount the applicant would have been paid in wages for
> the period of 20 September 2024 to 13 December 2024. (ii) An amount of $5,000 by way of legal
> expenses. (the Settlement Sum)"*
> ⭐⭐⭐ **2(d)** *"**Deduct from the Settlement Sum the gross amount of the credited leave
> balances**, as calculated by the Health Service (inclusive of any taxable deductions paid by the
> Health Service, including but not limited to income tax withheld)."*

⭐⭐ **So the settlement was paid net of his own annual leave and leave loading** — 54.16 hours of
each was handed back as a balance and then subtracted, gross, from what he was paid. That is the
thing being half-remembered. ⛔ **It is annual leave, not long service leave.**

## 3. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE REAL LSL CONNECTION IS CLAUSE 4 — AND IT IS WORSE
> ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Clause 4:** *"The period from 13 December 2024 to 23 February 2025 **will be treated as
> leave without pay**."*

⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The second of the two periods now costing him his long service leave date was created by
MSH's own deed, executed by the Chief Executive.** It is not a payroll slip. Payroll's 4 September
exclusion list — 18 May–10 Jun 2022, **21 Jun–20 Sep 2024**, **13 Dec 2024–23 Feb 2025** — takes its
third line straight from clause 4.

- **Period 1 (21 Jun – 20 Sep 2024)** ends on the reinstatement effective date and is *outside* the
  deed. That one is the coding question raised with Payroll on 7 September 2026.
- **Period 2 (13 Dec 2024 – 23 Feb 2025)** is the deed's own period.

## 4. ⭐⭐⭐ THE RECODE DOES NOT CUT ACROSS CLAUSE 4
⭐⭐⭐ **Sick leave without pay IS leave without pay.** Clause 4 fixes the genus, not the reason
code. Recoding the period to SNP leaves it unpaid and leaves clause 4 satisfied on its own words;
all that changes is that the absence is recorded as what it was. ⭐⭐ **And it is Payroll's own
position, put in writing on 8 September 2026, that both periods "should have been booked as Sick
No Pay (SNP) rather than Leave Without Pay (LWOP)".**
