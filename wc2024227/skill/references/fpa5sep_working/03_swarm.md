## SKEPTICAL COMMISSIONER — unpersuaded, looking for where to land
What makes me uncomfortable: a self-represented appellant with a 308-fact notice, a pleading that
characterises everyone, and no expert. I will not find causation on a QSuper letter alone if the
respondent runs the bereavement hard. But I have my own Review Unit's finding that employment WAS a
significant contributing factor, and the respondent admits its contents; I will want to know why I
should depart from it. On the exclusion I am genuinely torn: the break is admitted, the standard is
admitted, the absence of any framework is in the employer's own letter — that is more than a
blemish — but the appellant has pleaded it as part of a composite hostile course, which invites me
to weigh it globally, and globally most of what he complains of looks like ordinary management.
Where I split the difference: I would likely find the 17–18 March rostering unreasonable and
contributory, and then ask whether the injury "arose out of" that or out of the whole. If he gives
me *Mahaffey* cleanly and stops arguing the composite, I can find for him. If he argues everything,
I weigh everything, and he loses on the weighing. I want a report — even a short treating-doctor
letter with a diagnosis date and a clinical sequence. Without one, I am 55/45 against on C.
