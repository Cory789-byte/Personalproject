# 9. ⭐⭐⭐ THE RIVAL ASSESSMENT'S *MAHAFFEY* POINT — RIGHT CITATION, BACKWARDS INFERENCE
**Their claim:** *"Mahaffey… requires weighing the accepted causal factors rather than simply
counting reasonable and unreasonable incidents. Mahaffey, especially [50]–[57]."*

## 9.1 ⭐⭐⭐ WHAT [57] ACTUALLY SAYS — VERBATIM
> *"The difficulties in construing s 32(5) support the conclusion that more than one interpretation
> of s 32 is available and that, therefore, **the beneficial interpretation approach should be
> applied**. In the cases decided in this Court **any attempt to provide some type of formula or
> application of dominant cause has been rejected**… If, after considering all the relevant evidence
> and weighing up the factors which were accepted as having given rise to the personal injury, the
> Commission forms the conclusion that **any of the conduct referred to in s 32(5) does not, on
> balance, DISPLACE the evidence in favour of the worker then a finding in the worker's favour MUST
> follow.**"*

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **[57] is a beneficial-construction paragraph with a MUST in it, and the burden it frames
runs the Respondent's way: the s 32(5) conduct must DISPLACE the evidence in favour of the worker.**

## 9.2 ⭐⭐⭐ AND [50] AND [54] BOTH CUT FOR HIM
- **[50], Hall P in *Rowe*:** ⭐⭐ *"I cannot recollect an occasion on which… Q-COMP has submitted
  that '…even if one stressor is caught by reasonable management action that will be sufficient to
  remove the psychiatric/psychological disorder from the definition'… **Neither have I any
  recollection of accepting such a proposition.**"* ⭐⭐⭐ **And: in *Delaney* "the claimant succeeded
  though the sixth stressor… was found to be but blemished management action."**
- **[54]:** ⭐⭐⭐ *"The construction favoured by this Court has been that **a worker can suffer a
  compensable injury even if reasonable management action has had some causative effect**."*
- **[51], Hochen [15]:** ⭐ *"**The presence of unreasonable management action with a sufficient
  causal connection to the injury may require that a negative answer be given to the question posed
  by s 32(5)(a).**"*
- **[53]:** dominant-cause enquiry is ⭐ **"an erroneous approach"**.

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **VERDICT: the citation is correct and the inference is backwards.** ⭐ ***Mahaffey* is not a
hurdle in this case. It is the paragraph that forecloses the Respondent's best remaining argument.**
