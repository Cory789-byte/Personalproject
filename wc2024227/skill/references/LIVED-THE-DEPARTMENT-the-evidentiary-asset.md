# "I LIVED THE DEPARTMENT" — WHY THAT IS THE ANSWER, AND WHY IT IS ALSO THE CASE
> 28 August 2026. ⛔ Internal.

## THE THREE CLAIMS, SEPARATED

| | What it answers |
|---|---|
| **I know them** | the structure — numbering, headings, the status column, the Form 29 cross-reference |
| **I read them** | the content — he could answer *about* the material, not just point at it |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **I lived the department** | **the operational reality underneath the documents.** This is the one that cannot be faked, borrowed, generated or briefed |

---

## 1. ⭐⭐⭐ THE PROOF IS ONE ANSWER, AND IT IS NOT A LEGAL ANSWER

> **[23:40]** *"When you go into, let's say, **eHealth**, for instance, or let's say **Outlook**, and
> you put a word in Outlook and **it filters all the words**. So **my filters were 'effective
> immediately'**."*

⭐⭐ **That is not a lawyer's explanation and it is not a generated one.** It is a switchboard operator
describing **a rule he built in his own mailbox to catch the phrase "effective immediately"** — so
that the directives could be counted.

⇒ **No language model produces that, because it is not in any document. And no lawyer produces it,
because it comes from sitting at the console.**

⭐ It also explains why *"filters"* was in the index at all — the category exists because **the
method existed first.** The document is downstream of the work.

## 2. ⭐⭐ THE SAME QUALITY IS THE EVIDENTIARY ASSET AT THE HEARING

The whole case is about **a system**: MET calls, code procedures, on-call numbers, paging, call
queues, the communication book, the roster, the seven-hour break.

| Who can speak to the system | |
|---|---|
| The Regulator | **One unparticularised sentence** at ¶27. No act, no date, no actor |
| Ms Taylor | A **manager's** account — what was directed, not what it did at the console |
| A psychiatrist | The **clinical** consequence, on assumed facts |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **The Appellant** | **What actually happened at the desk, at 2am, on four hours' sleep, when the on-call numbers were wrong** |

⇒ ⭐⭐ **That is the only evidence in the proceeding nobody else can give**, and it is the evidence
the s 32(1) question actually turns on.

### ⭐ And it maps onto Dwyer's own method
In **McCool** he reasoned about **why a person would remember a thing**: *"that was his purpose in
calling… the information would have been an important fact and **one that he is likely to have
remembered**."*

⇒ **Operational detail recalled with specificity is exactly the class of evidence this Commission
credits** — because the *reason* for the memory is visible in the detail itself.

## 3. ⭐ IT IS ALSO WHAT MAKES THE PSYCHIATRIC REPORT WORK
Question **2.1** asks the psychiatrist whether conditions of that kind — demand, control, support —
can produce the condition. **The mechanism is the operational reality**: responsibility imposed, and
the means to discharge it withheld.

⇒ **A report answering 2.1 on the role description alone is abstract. Answered against what the work
actually involved, it is concrete.** ⚠ Which is why the lived detail belongs in the **clinical
history**, and the admitted facts belong in the **reasoning**.

## 4. ⚠ THE DISCIPLINE — IT CUTS BOTH WAYS IN THE BOX

⛔ **The same knowledge that makes him unanswerable makes him vulnerable to volunteering.**

> **[29:18]** He went to the operational reality unprompted — direct calls to an emergency number,
> doctors sent to the wrong side of the room, *"not sent to someone having a cardiac arrest"*.
> **It was the strongest content of the hour.**
> **[29:40]** Dwyer: *"**I'm just going to move on for a moment**"* — and cut it off.

⇒ ⭐⭐ **The material was right. The moment was wrong.** It was volunteered rather than asked for, and
it was shut down mid-flight.

### THE RULE FOR THE HEARING
1. ⭐ **Release the lived detail on question, never volunteer it.** In the box, counsel's question is
   the licence. Unprompted, the same detail reads as grievance; prompted, it reads as knowledge.
2. ⭐ **Answer the question asked, then stop.** The detail's power is that it is *available on
   demand* — which is only visible if there is a demand.
3. ⚠ **And it is the one part of the case that improves under cross-examination**, because
   cross-examination is a series of demands. ⭐ **The Regulator cannot cross-examine him out of what
   he did for five years.**
