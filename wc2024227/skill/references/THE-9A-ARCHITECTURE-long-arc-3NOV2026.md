## The six layers

1. **Admitted fact as the unit.** Every material sentence carries its paragraph number. 288 facts are used.
2. **Particulars classed by role.** Causal: 1(b), 1(d), 1(f)–(h), 1(k), 1(l); 2(a)–(i), 2(l), 2(m); 3(b)–(f). Context: 1(a),
   1(c), 1(e), 1(i), 1(j), 2(j), 3(a). Record: 2(k), 2(n), 2(o), 3(g), 3(h). The tag is in each heading.
3. **Each stressor self-contained.** The same fact is re-pleaded where it plays a different part, so any stressor can be
   decided without reading another.
4. **The cross-ties.** 81 facts are cited in two or more stressors; 11 in all three (28, 37, 38, 266, 267, 272, 289,
   300–303).
5. **The arithmetic and the negatives.** B.4 turns admitted times into intervals (7 h; 5 d 18 h 14 min; 23 days); B.5
   lists what the SOFC and LOD do not contain, by admitted fact.
6. **The Respondent's own case folded in.** C 5A quotes the outlines; C 10 and facts 300–303 fix ¶27; C 8 states the
   limit of the admissions; C 9 accepts de novo.
