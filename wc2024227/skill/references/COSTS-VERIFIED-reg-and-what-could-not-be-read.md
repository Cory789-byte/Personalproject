# 1. ⭐⭐⭐ THE GUIDE'S CITATION IS OUT OF DATE — THE 2014 REGULATION HAS EXPIRED
The Guide (v2.10, 11 March 2025) says costs orders *"are based on **regulation 132 of the Workers'
Compensation and Rehabilitation Regulation 2014**."*
⇒ ⛔ **The 2014 Regulation expired and was remade as the *Workers' Compensation and Rehabilitation
Regulation 2025* (SL 2025 No. 116).** ⭐ **The costs provisions are now Part 9, and the relevant
section is s 191, not reg 132.**

# 2. ⭐⭐⭐ s 191 — VERBATIM, FROM THE AUTHORISED PDF
> **Part 9 Costs · Division 1 Hearing before industrial magistrate or industrial commission**
> **191 Costs of hearing before industrial magistrate or industrial commission**
> **(1) A decision to award costs of a hearing before an industrial magistrate or the industrial
> commission is AT THE DISCRETION of the magistrate or commission.**
> (2) If the magistrate or commission awards costs—
>  (a) costs in relation to **counsel's or solicitor's fees** are the costs stated in the **Uniform
>  Civil Procedure Rules 1999, schedule 2, part 2, scale C**; and
>  (b) costs in relation to **witnesses** are the allowances stated in the **Uniform Civil Procedure
>  (Fees) Regulation 2019, part 3**; and
>  (c) costs in relation to bailiffs are the fees stated in the **UCP (Fees) Regulation 2019,
>  schedule 2A**.
> **(3) However, the magistrate or commission may award costs up to 1.5 times the amounts under
> subsection (2)(a)**, in total or in relation to any item, if satisfied those amounts are inadequate
> having regard to— (a) the work involved; or (b) the importance, difficulty or complexity of the
> matter…

## 2.1 ⭐⭐⭐ WHAT THIS CORRECTS — THE DOWNSIDE IS DISCRETIONARY AND CAPPED
| What I told him earlier | ⭐ The provision |
|---|---|
| ⛔ *"no costs recovery either way"* | **wrong** — costs are available |
| ⛔ *"costs follow the event"*, an open-ended tail *"he cannot absorb"* | ⚠ **overstated.** *"Follow the event"* is the **Guide's description of usual practice**. ⭐⭐⭐ **The rule is a DISCRETION**, and any award is **capped to UCPR schedule 2, part 2, SCALE C**, with a **1.5× ceiling** |

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **The adverse exposure is bounded and scale-based, not indemnity and not open-ended.** ⚠ For a
multi-day hearing it is still real money, but it is a known and limited quantity, and **the Commission
may decline to award at all.**
⛔ **Still to do: obtain the actual UCPR schedule 2, part 2, scale C figures and cost a 3–5 day
hearing.** Only then is the number real.

## 2.2 ⭐⭐⭐ AND s 192 IS DIRECTLY FAVOURABLE — READ IT TWICE
> **192 Payment of additional amount for costs**
> **(1)** This section applies if— (a) **the Regulator or an insurer is required to pay costs** in a
> proceeding in relation to a witness who— **(i) is a medical practitioner; or (ii) gives evidence of
> a professional nature**; and (b) the **fees and expenses payable in relation to the witness by the
> party that called the witness is MORE than the amount of costs awarded**…
> **(2) The Regulator or the insurer MAY, on the application of the party that called the witness,
> pay an additional amount for costs** that it is satisfied are reasonable, having regard to the
> subject matter of the proceeding.

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **If he succeeds and the Regulator is ordered to pay costs, and Dr Krishnaiah's and
Dr Hawes's fees exceed the scale allowance, he may APPLY TO THE REGULATOR for an additional amount.**
⭐⭐ **That is the direct answer to the conduct-money problem, and it applies to exactly his two
expert witnesses.** ⛔ **Note it now; make the application if he wins.**

## 2.3 ⚠ AND WHAT HE CAN RECOVER, SELF-REPRESENTED
⚠ **s 191(2)(a) is counsel's or solicitor's fees — he has none.** ⭐ **But (2)(b) is witness
allowances, which is where his actual outlay sits**, and **s 192 tops that up for the doctors.**
⇒ ⭐ **A costs award in his favour would be modest but would substantially cover the thing he is
actually paying for.**
