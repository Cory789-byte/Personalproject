## 7. THE VIEW — first conclusion in this file

**1. The instrument is as strong as the file says, and the mention was answered point for point.**
Proof, materiality, legibility and the route to documents were all supplied, and the legibility was
demonstrated on the opponent rather than asserted. Categorisation is now the only battlefield, which
is where the bench twice predicted it would be.

**2. But the case is stronger than the pleading, and that gap is the exposure.** The served schedule
of 9 September is candid: it discloses the 2022 anxiety and ADHD entries and records the Regulator's
denial. The live pleading of 8 April does the opposite: it pleads a clean psychiatric baseline and
tags it as admitted, against the one row where they expressly denied. **An appeals officer assessing
hearing risk reads the pleading first.** That single mismatch is the best argument available to the
other side, it is self-inflicted, and the correction is already written.

**3. So "298 admitted, 0 denied" must stop being used as a whole-of-matter statement.** It is exact
about the August notice. About the matter it is not: they denied in February, and they denied on
causation baseline. That is their case, it is seven months old, and it has not changed.

**4. The highest-leverage act available is not further analysis.** It is serving the Second Amended
Form 9A before 30 September, so that their witness list and outlines are written against the amended
case rather than against five overstated tags and a baseline plea their own row 32 contradicts.
⚠ Confirm the mechanism first — whether amendment at this stage is by service with notice or
requires leave — and do not let it collide with the quiet period before the 25th.

**5. The second highest is the causation bridge, which is still open.** Krishnaiah declined. The
2022 history was disclosed but nothing has yet absorbed it in expert form. Element (d) currently
rests on treating records plus oral evidence, and their one cheap route to a medical case is
cross-examining a treating doctor on a history he was never formally asked to address.

**6. Where this ends, on everything above, is the second s 552A conference.** Dwyer would chair it;
he will not hear the appeal; Guide 5.1 makes the conference the concession venue; regulators concede
at conferences and not at hearings; and direction 5 puts the election in the Appellant's hands after
he has seen their 30 September list. The two tells remain the names and whether any expert report
is served at all.

**7. ⛔ What I got wrong in this session, stated plainly.** I read the mention in fragments and
built findings on whichever passage was in front of me, and I repeated "298 admitted, none denied"
as though it described the matter. It describes one notice. The February denial and the unserved
pleading correction were both in the file the whole time and neither reached any answer I gave until
this pass.
