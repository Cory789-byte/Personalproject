# ⭐⭐⭐ WHO IS WHO — THE REGULATOR, THE COMMISSION, AND THE REGISTRY
**10 September 2026 · WC/2024/227 · foundational reference**
> Corrects a live misconception: that the Regulator is employed by the QIRC, or the Commission by
> the Regulator. ⛔ **Neither is true, and the separation is load-bearing in this appeal.**

---

# 1. ⛔⛔ THE MISCONCEPTION, AND WHY IT IS AN UNDERSTANDABLE ONE
⛔ **The Workers' Compensation Regulator is NOT employed by, part of, or answerable to the QIRC.**
⛔ **The Commission's members are NOT employed by the Regulator, by OIR, or by any department.**

⭐ **Three real reasons the confusion arises:**
1. ⭐ **Both carry "Industrial Relations" in the name** — the Office of Industrial Relations, and the
   Queensland Industrial Relations Commission.
2. ⭐ **Both letterheads carry the same Queensland Government crest** (verified in `RD69983-OBJECT-
   LEVEL-FORENSICS.md` §1 — the crest PNG above *"Office of Industrial Relations"*).
3. ⭐ **Both decision-makers sound judicial** — a *"Review Officer"* and a *"Commissioner"*.

⇒ ⭐⭐ **Different buildings, different domains, different statutes:**
| | Address | Domain | Established by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Regulator / OIR** | **150 Mary Street**, Brisbane | `@oir.qld.gov.au` | **WCRA 2003** |
| **The QIRC** | **Level 21–22, 66 Eagle Street** | `@qirc.qld.gov.au` | **Industrial Relations Act 2016** |

# 4. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ WHY THE SEPARATION IS LOAD-BEARING IN THIS APPEAL

## 4.1 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ IT IS WHAT MAKES s 550 A **DE NOVO** APPEAL
⭐⭐⭐ **The Commission does not review the Regulator's decision for error. It decides the question
again, itself, on the evidence put before it.**
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **That is only possible because the decider is not part of the body that decided.**
⇒ ⭐⭐ **And it is why irregularities in the review process are worth nothing here** — the review is
superseded, not appealed. (`HOPGOODGANIM-QUESTION.md` §6.8.)

## 4.2 ⭐⭐⭐ IT IS WHAT MAKES THE MODEL LITIGANT POINT MEAN ANYTHING
> [59:41] *"They have responsibilities as a **model litigant**… **So they can't hide stuff from you.
> They can't be sneaky or tricky.**"*

⭐⭐⭐ **The model litigant principles bind the State and its agencies — so they bind the
REGULATOR, as a party.** ⛔ **They do not bind the Commission, because the Commission is not a
party.**
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **That sentence is a tribunal member describing an obligation owed by a litigant appearing
before him. It only makes sense because they are separate — and he staked his own credibility on it.**

## 4.3 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ AND IT IS WHY THE RESPONDENT IS IN A STRUCTURALLY WEAK POSITION
⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The Regulator must defend the reasonableness of management action taken by people it does
not employ, recorded in documents it did not create, in a workplace it has never seen — and it
cannot compel any of them to give evidence for it.**
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **That is not collusion and not a conspiracy. It is the design: the statute makes the
Regulator the respondent to an appeal about a PRIVATE EMPLOYER'S conduct.**
⇒ ⭐⭐ **The employer whose conduct is the subject matter is a stranger to the proceeding — which is
exactly what the 7 August mention demonstrated.**

# 5. ⭐⭐⭐ "DOES THE COMMISSION JUST SIT THERE?" — YES AS TO EVIDENCE, NO AS TO PROCEDURE

| | |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **As to EVIDENCE — effectively yes, and it is the mechanism he is relying on** | ⭐⭐ The proceeding is **adversarial**. The Commission decides on what the parties put in front of it. It does not investigate, gather documents, or call witnesses of its own motion |
| ⭐⭐ **As to PROCEDURE — no** | It directs, lists, times, narrows, orders disclosure and decides objections. **The whole of 7 August was procedure** |

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AND THAT PASSIVITY IS THE WHOLE ENGINE OF THE CASE:**
> ⭐⭐⭐ [15:40] *"the regulator does have to contradict things that you say… **if you say something
> and it's not contradicted… it will be accepted**"*
> ⭐⭐⭐ [28:56] *"if you give evidence… and the regulator doesn't contradict it, doesn't
> cross-examine you, doesn't produce documents… **that's a problem for them, not for you**"*

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **A tribunal that will not go looking for evidence is a tribunal that must accept
uncontradicted evidence. The Regulator's silence becomes a FINDING — but only because the
Commission is separate from it and passive as to proof.**

---

# 6. ⭐⭐⭐ THE ONE LINE
⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The Regulator is a public service office and a party. The Commission is an independent
tribunal and the decider. The Registry is the Commission's own office, not the Regulator's.**
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **They are not the same machine — and the entire strategy since 28 August depends on that
being true: 298 admissions extracted from a PARTY, to be weighed by a DECIDER who did not make them
and is not bound to look behind them.**

⛔⛔ **AND THE DISCIPLINE THAT FOLLOWS:** ⛔ **never suggest, in any forum, that the Commission and
the Regulator are connected.** ⭐ It is wrong on the facts, it is the shape of a bias allegation, and
bias is already quarantined off this track (`REVIEW-DECISION-RED-TEAM.md` §202: OIR Issue 4712 /
ESU 4822). ⭐⭐ **The asymmetry to use is §4.3 — the Regulator defending someone else's conduct — and
it is stated as structure, never as motive.**
