# 1. ⭐⭐⭐ THEY LOOK AT COSTS FIRST, AND THE ANSWER IS BETTER THAN IT USUALLY IS
> ⭐⭐⭐ **Guide Part 10:** *"In workers' compensation appeals **costs generally 'follow the event'**…
> the unsuccessful party will usually be required to pay the costs of the successful party"* —
> **reg 132, Workers' Compensation and Rehabilitation Regulation 2014.**

| | |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Why this matters commercially** | ⭐⭐⭐ **A win is COST-RECOVERABLE. That makes the matter fundable on a deferred or conditional basis, which a matter with no costs rule is not** |
| ⚠ **And it cuts both ways** | ⚠ lose and he is exposed to their **hearing** costs |
| ⚠ **The limit** | *"limited to the **costs of the hearing**"* ⇒ ⛔ **the two years of pre-hearing work is largely outside it** |

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **A plaintiff firm can take this. That is the first gate and it is passed.**

# 2. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ THEN THE 298 ADMISSIONS — AND THE REACTION IS IN TWO PARTS

## 2.1 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ FIRST REACTION: *"WHO DID THIS?"*
⭐⭐⭐ **Rule 49 notices to admit are UNDERUSED in this jurisdiction.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **303 facts served with a
deemed-admission clock, answered 298/303, is not something a represented appellant usually has
either.**

⭐⭐⭐ **And the commercial consequence is immediate and large:**
| ⭐ | |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **No tender fights. No proof of service. No authenticity arguments on 25 of 39 tabs** | ⭐⭐⭐ **that is typically a third of hearing preparation and a substantial part of hearing time** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **The listing estimate falls** | ⭐⭐ which changes the brief fee, the funding decision, and whether counsel is available |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **The file is HANDOVER-READY** | ⭐⭐ indexed, hashed, sourced. **That is 20+ hours a firm does not have to bill** |

## 2.2 ⚠⚠ SECOND REACTION, AND IT IS THE PROFESSIONAL ONE: *"WHAT DO THEY PROVE?"*
⛔⛔ **The admissions establish that named documents were sent, published or recorded on stated
dates and say what they say. THAT IS ALL.**
⛔⛔⛔ **They do NOT establish that the management action was unreasonable. They do NOT establish
causation.**

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **What a practitioner sees is a SUPERB EVIDENTIARY PLATFORM UNDER AN ARGUMENT THAT IS NOT
YET FINISHED.** ⭐⭐ **And they see that as good news — because the platform is the part that cannot
be built late, and the argument is the part they are actually for.**

# 4. ⛔⛔⛔ AND THEN THE TWO THINGS THEY WOULD FLAG IN THE FIRST TEN MINUTES

## 4.1 ⛔⛔⛔ NOBODY HAS ASKED EITHER DOCTOR THE STATUTORY QUESTION
⛔⛔ **The schedule says so on its own face: *"No report has been prepared for the purposes of this
proceeding."***
⭐⭐ **Treating records are contemporaneous and credible — but they are addressed to TREATMENT, not
to s 32.** ⛔⛔⛔ **No one has asked Dr Hawes or Dr Krishnaiah, in terms: WAS EMPLOYMENT *A
SIGNIFICANT CONTRIBUTING FACTOR* TO THE INJURY?**
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **This is the single biggest gap in the file, it is entirely fixable, and it is the first
thing a legal team would do.**

## 4.2 ⛔⛔ THE 5 SEPTEMBER EMAIL, AND HOW A LAWYER READS IT
> ⛔⛔ **Dr Krishnaiah, 5 Sep 2026:** *"I truly do not have much time to do medico-legal work…
> **I can't provide preformed answers as you have asked.**"*

⛔ **Two problems, and they are different:**
| | |
|---|---|
| ⛔⛔ **Attendance risk** | ⭐ a treating psychiatrist reluctant to engage with the proceeding at all. ⇒ **Guide 7.6.5: a report without its author is worth nothing. His attendance is not optional** |
