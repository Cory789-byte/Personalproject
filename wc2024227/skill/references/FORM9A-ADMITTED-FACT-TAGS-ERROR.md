## ⛔⛔⛔ AND THE BIGGEST SINGLE RISK FOUND IN THE FILE

**This is the Regulator's competing-cause case, and unlike everything else in its pleading it has a

It is also the assertion behind SOFC ¶8 — *"medical records identify a past medical history of

26 October 2022.**

### ⛔ WHAT MUST BE DONE
1. **Locate the 26 October 2022 record.** ⚠ They say it is *"missing from Exhibit A5"* — i.e. **not
   in the medical records bundle the Appellant has**. It must be obtained and read before the
   hearing.

   deal with it expressly**, and it cannot do that if the treating psychiatrist has not seen it.
3. ⛔ **Delete or qualify the "clean psychiatric baseline" plea.** As drafted it is contradicted by
   a document the Respondent says exists. **Pleading a clean baseline against a known contrary
   record is the single most damaging thing in the Form 9A.**
