# HOW OFTEN MUST A PSYCHOSOCIAL ASSESSMENT BE DONE?
> Asked 1 August 2026. **Verified against ATT24 (WHS Regulation 2011 (Qld), current 29 March 2026),
> ATT05 (Managing the risk of psychosocial hazards at work Code of Practice 2022) and ATT02 (EB12).**

---

# PART 1 — ⭐⭐⭐ THE ANSWER: THERE IS NO INTERVAL. THE DUTY IS TRIGGER-BASED.
**Nothing in the WHS Act, the Regulation or the Code says "annually", "every two years", or any
other period.** ⭐ **That is better for him than a calendar would be** — a department can keep a
tidy annual schedule and still be in breach, because the duty attaches to **events**, not dates.

## 1.1 The duty is continuous
| Provision | Words |
|---|---|
| **reg 55C(1)** | *"A person conducting a business or undertaking **must manage psychosocial risks** under part 3.1"* — an ongoing duty, not an occasional one |
| **reg 37** | control measures must be **maintained** so they remain effective |
| ⭐ **reg 38(1)** | *"A duty holder **must review and, as necessary, revise** control measures… so as to **maintain**, so far as is reasonably practicable, a work environment that is without risks to health or safety"* |

## 1.2 ⭐⭐ AND FIVE SPECIFIC TRIGGERS — reg 38(2)
> *"the duty holder **must** review and, as necessary, revise a control measure in the following
> circumstances—*
> *(a) the control measure does not control the risk it was implemented to control…;*
> *(b) **before a change at the workplace** that is likely to give rise to a new or different risk…;*
> *(c) **a new relevant hazard or risk is identified**;*
> *(d) the results of **consultation by the duty holder** … indicate that a review is necessary;*
> *(e) **a health and safety representative requests the review** under subsection (4)."*

⭐ **reg 38(3)** — a "change at the workplace" **includes** *"a change to a system of work, a process
or a procedure."*

# PART 3 — ⭐⭐⭐ AND HIS DESCRIPTION IS LITERALLY THE CODE'S OWN CHECKLIST
> *"so much leave… so many complaints… so many disagreements"*

**The Code's table of data sources for identifying psychosocial hazards names each of them:**

| Data source | ⭐ What the Code says to look for |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **Records of leave** | *"**increasing rates of unplanned leave for the work unit**, region or department **compared to the whole organisation**"*; *"patterns of use such as frequently taking unplanned leave **which could be associated with unpleasant working conditions and work-related stress**"* |
| ⭐ **Grievance information** | *"records in relation to grievances at work that indicate **conflict, organisational injustice, lack of role clarity, harassment, discrimination**, work-related violence and aggression, and **work-related bullying**"* |
| ⭐ **Industrial relations records** | *"records of **industrial relations disputes** which could be associated with psychosocial hazards that are **not managed or controlled effectively**"* |
| **Workers' compensation claims** | *"**increasing rates of psychological injury claims** and/or increased severity"*; *"return to work data, and the actions taken in the workplace to prevent further injury"* |
| **Issue resolution records** | WHS issues involving *"work-related bullying, harassment, incidents, and stress reports"* |
| **Employee assistance program** | usage and referral-reason trends |
| **Minutes of meetings** | *"**unresolved and reappearing worker issues over time**"* |
| **Turnover data / exit surveys** | *"higher than normal turnover"*; reasons citing *"workload, lack of support or the mention of any bullying, conflict or organisational injustice"* |
| **Hours of work** | fatigue, excessive overtime, *"shifts that are not well managed, or not taking rest breaks during shifts"* |

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **He has not described a general grievance. He has described the Code's own hazard
indicators — three of them by name.** **A unit with elevated unplanned leave, a pattern of
grievances and live industrial disputes is, on the Code's own terms, a unit where the data
indicates hazards and a review is required.**

⚠️ **BUT — the honest limit.** ⛔ **He does not hold the unit-level data.** Leave rates, turnover,
EAP usage, grievance counts and WC claim patterns for Switchboard Services are **all in MSH's
systems**. ⇒ **He can point at the framework. He cannot yet prove the numbers.**

# PART 4 — ⭐⭐ THE TWO ROUTES THAT DO NOT DEPEND ON HIM AT ALL
**Both are underused, and both are collective rather than individual — which makes them much harder
to characterise as one employee's dispute.**

## 4.1 ⭐ THE HSR — reg 38(2)(e) and 38(4)
> **reg 38(4)** *"A health and safety representative for workers at a workplace **may request a
> review of a control measure** if the representative reasonably believes that— (a) a circumstance
> mentioned in subsection (2)(a), (b), (c) or (d) affects or may affect the health and safety of a
> member of the work group…; and (b) **the duty holder has not adequately reviewed the control
> measure in response to the circumstance**."*

⇒ ⭐⭐ **That is exactly this situation:** a hazard identified on 7 July under (c), and no adequate
review since. **An HSR can require the review, and it is not Cory asking.**
**EB12 cl 7.1.7** — Queensland Health *"will promote the role of Health and Safety Committees and
the important role of health and safety representatives"*, and PCBUs *"will support requests for the
establishment of Health and Safety Committees"*.
**EB12 cl 7.1.8** — ⭐ *"**Workplace bullying will be a standing agenda item for safety committees**."*
- [ ] ⭐ **Find out whether Switchboard Services / Logan has an HSR, and who.** If there is one, this
      is the cheapest and most powerful lever in the file.
- [ ] If there is none, **cl 7.1.7 supports a request to establish a committee.**

## 4.2 ⭐⭐ THE UNION, THROUGH THE HCF — EB12 cl 7.2.1
> *"The parties to this Agreement are committed to support **workplace audits and risk assessments
> of workplaces as requested through a HCF either by an employer or a union party**."*

**HCF = Health Consultative Forum** (EB12 definitions; cl 3.5).
⇒ ⭐⭐ **Together Queensland can request a psychosocial risk assessment of Switchboard Services
through the HCF.** It is a **collective** request under the agreement, it does not turn on his
individual matter, and **cl 7.2.2** requires it be done using the 2022 Code *"in consultation with
workers and Health and Safety Representatives"*.
- [ ] ⭐ **Put this to Petering.** It is squarely inside the union's demarcation and it is the one
      route that produces the dated assessment he wants to hold.

# PART 5 — HOW TO PUT IT, AND HOW NOT TO
✅ **What is already asked** — Stage 1 notice **§2.5** and **§4(h)**: a cl 7.2 assessment, with
consultation under cl 7.2.2, quoting MSH's own 7 July identification.

⛔ **Do not say "you should have done one every year."** **There is no such rule**, and asserting it
invites a correct correction that costs credibility on the rest.

> *"A review of control measures is required where a new relevant hazard or risk is identified. The
> Health Service recorded on 7 July 2026 that there may be psychosocial hazards involving line
> management, and that it did not have sufficient information to assess them. I ask what review has
> been undertaken since."*

⇒ **That is reg 38(2)(c) put as a question, using their own words, with no adjective.**
