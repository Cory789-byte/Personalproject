# Case Theory Synthesis — what WC/2024/227 is structurally becoming, and how to plead it

**Status:** hearing/strategy capstone. Pulls together the 9A, the admissions,
the MSH letter, the disclosure, and the metadata. The lodged 64G is unaffected.
Verified facts cited to source; inference flagged.

---

## 1. What MSH gave away by answering item-by-item

MSH addressed all 20 Notice items individually (Items 1–2 together, 3–20 each).
That granularity is a gift: each answer is a fixed position. The load-bearing
ones:
- **Items 4, 5, 7 (CONCEDED in writing):** no mandatory FRMS training applied to
  Switchboard ("non-clinical staff"); FRMS assessment implemented only **after
  30 June 2024**. Onset was 18 June 2024. → **the employer admits it ran the
  Switchboard with no fatigue-management framework at the time of the injury.**
- **Items 1–2:** SPOK records "do not exist" (system upgrade) — but the MET-call
  spreadsheet for 17–18 March is available.
- **Item 20:** "does not exist" on a reference-number technicality — contradicted
  by MSH's OWN July 2025 production (the routing correspondence was already
  disclosed to the Regulator).
- **Items 8, 9:** objected on expense/particularity — the records exist (the
  differential tell).

## 2. The 9A as filed — a three-stressor GRIEVANCE catalogue

The Amended 9A (8 Apr 2026) pleads 13 particulars across 3 stressors:
- **Stressor 1 (hostile course):** (a) Dereliction of Clinical Governance,
  (b) Unilateral Destruction of WHS Records, (c) Refusal to Investigate WHS &
  Fatigue Complaints, (d) Disparate Leave Treatment, **(e) PID**, **(f) Immediate
  Reprisal**, **(g) Suppression of Industrial Representation**.
- **Stressor 2 (remuneration):** (a) Persistent Payroll Failures, (b) Unreasonable Delay.
- **Stressor 3 (fatigue):** (a) Mathematical Reality of the WHS Breach,
  (b) Flagrant Bypass of the Neville Mandate, (c) Unjustified Administrative
  Detriment, (d) Admitted Unreasonableness.
- Plus the legal argument already pleaded: Exclusion Fails Entirely; Global
  Evaluation (Delaney) vs **Single Unreasonable Stressor (Mahaffey)**.

This is how self-reps plead: everything that happened, equally weighted. It is
comprehensive but structurally diffuse.

## 3. What it is STRUCTURALLY TURNING INTO — a governance-vacuum case

The evidence has concentrated the case around a new centre of gravity. The
emerging core proposition:

> *The employer operated a life-safety clinical-coordination function (Code Blue
> / MET) entirely OUTSIDE the fatigue-management framework — by its own
> admission no FRMS applied to the Switchboard until after the injury — rostered
> an admitted, award-breaching, already-found-unreasonable 7-hour break within
> that vacuum, and when the worker raised the safety concern through every
> available channel, closed each one.*

Why this structure is far stronger than the catalogue:
1. **Anchored in admitted + conceded facts** — the 7-hour break (Form 24 ¶1),
   the award minimum (¶3), the Review Officer's unreasonableness finding, and
   the FRMS vacuum (MSH letter Items 4/5/7). Little of the spine needs to be
   *proved*; it is already on the record.
2. **Defeats Delaney dilution** — it is not "nine things, one bad"; it is ONE
   systemic governance failure with manifestations. Global evaluation has
   nothing to dilute against a single coherent failure.
3. **Converts the "non-clinical" defence into the negligence** — you cannot
   exempt the people who coordinate clinical emergencies from fatigue management
   on a clerical label and then call the resulting breach "reasonable."
4. **It is CLEAN** — about systems and classifications, not personalities. No
   villain, no fraud, no reprisal required to win it.

## 4. The structural problem — the case has two personalities

The 9A welds two incompatible cases together:
- **The CLEAN case (wins):** governance vacuum + admitted unreasonable roster +
  the safety concern raised and dismissed. Stressor 3; the fatigue/safety angle
  of 1(a) and 1(c); the manner limb of 2. Pure WHS/governance.
- **The GRIEVANCE/ADVERSARIAL case (vulnerable):** the PID (1(e)), the reprisal
  (1(f)), the suppression (1(g)), the fraud allegation embedded in the PID, the
  "destruction of WHS records" (1(b)). Personal, accusatory — vexatious-framing
  bait.

**The danger is contamination:** the Regulator (via WHS-specialist Willson) uses
the fraud/reprisal material to frame the WHOLE matter as a personal campaign, so
Dwyer discounts even the strong fatigue case. The reprisal complaint against
Matheson (Issue 4712) already handed them this lever once.

## 5. HOW TO PLEAD IT — the structural move (keystone, not catalogue)

Re-weight around the spine. Do not abandon strands — re-rank them:
- **PRIMARY (the spine):** Stressor 3 — the 7-hour break within the FRMS vacuum.
  Admitted + conceded + Mahaffey-sufficient. This wins the appeal by itself.
- **MANNER + SEVERITY (supporting):** the institutional-response strand — pleaded
  as *"every channel through which I raised the SAFETY concern was closed"* —
  WITHOUT the fraud allegation and WITHOUT the reprisal characterisation. This is
  1(a) clinical governance and 1(c) refusal to investigate fatigue, stripped to
  their safety/governance core.
- **HELD BACK (parallel track):** the PID-content (1(e)), reprisal (1(f)),
  suppression (1(g)). Keep them out of the WC pleading's load-bearing structure.
  The fraud word never appears. Issue 4712 runs on its own track behind the WC
  settlement (discipline rules 1, 2, 8).

**Practical reality on amendment:** the 9A is filed (with leave, 8 Apr); pleadings
are closing. The keystone IS already pleaded (Stressor 3 + the Mahaffey argument
are in it). So the RE-WEIGHTING is deployed not by re-pleading but in:
1. **Evidence-in-chief** — lead with the load profile (Code Blue/MET volume) and
   the fatigue arithmetic; establish the work was life-safety-clinical.
2. **The medical case** — the psychiatrist report (commissioned AFTER production)
   structured to tie the injury to the fatigue/governance failure AND the
   dismissive institutional response — cleanly, without the reprisal frame.
3. **Closing** — frame the whole as the governance vacuum, not the grievance
   catalogue. The "the only fatigue assessment ever done is the one the injured
   worker did himself, for this appeal" line lands here.
4. **If production justifies it,** seek leave for a tightened further-amended 9A
   that elevates Stressor 3 — but only if the records materially add; otherwise
   the re-weighting lives in evidence + closing.

## 6. Stress-test of the pleading structure

| Pleading approach | Upside | Risk | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Keep catalogue, all stressors equal (current weighting) | preserves everything | Delaney dilution; vexatious framing; strong point spread thin | **Avoid as the hearing posture** |
| Lead with keystone (vacuum + admitted roster), demote grievance to context, excise fraud | concentrates fire on admitted/conceded ground; defeats global dilution; hard to call vexatious when the lead is admitted | requires discipline to NOT lean on the strongest-feeling but most dangerous (reprisal/PID) material | **RECOMMENDED** |
| Drop the grievance strands entirely | maximally clean | loses severity/cumulative evidence that supports causation | Over-correction — keep them as *context*, not spine |

## 7. The one structural tension to resolve — the medical pathway

The strongest LIABILITY structure is the clean fatigue-vacuum. But the medical
evidence so far (Hawes: mechanism = "ongoing breaking of workplace rules by
bosses, victimizing him") points at the **cumulative-victimisation** pathway —
which pulls toward the grievance case. **These must be reconciled in the
psychiatrist report:** the injury should be opined as caused by the fatigue/
governance failure AND the institutional response to raising it — framed as
*systemic failure and dismissal*, not *personal reprisal*. If the medical case
leans only on "victimisation," it imports the grievance frame and its
vulnerability. Commission the report (post-production) on instructed assumptions
that foreground the governance vacuum and the safety-concern dismissal.

**Bottom line:** the case is structurally becoming a **clean governance-vacuum
appeal** — admitted unreasonable roster inside an admitted fatigue-management
vacuum, with a dismissive institutional response as the manner/severity. Plead
it as keystone-not-catalogue; hold the reprisal/fraud on the parallel track;
reconcile the medical pathway to the governance frame. That structure is hard to
dilute, hard to call vexatious, and largely already admitted.
