# 1. ⭐⭐⭐ THE FRAMEWORK, AS IT NOW STANDS
- ⭐ **Queensland Privacy Principles (QPPs) replaced the IPPs and, for health agencies, the NPPs,
  from 1 July 2025** (IPOLA Act 2023). ⇒ ⚠ **Metro South was under the NPPs before that date and
  the QPPs after. The May 2024 events and the 2026 events fall under DIFFERENT principle sets.**
- ⭐⭐⭐ **The route:** complaint to the agency → **45 business days** → if no response or an
  unsatisfactory one, refer to the **Office of the Information Commissioner** for mediation → if
  mediation fails, **referral to QCAT**.
- ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **QCAT's powers on a substantiated complaint:** order the act not be repeated or
  continued · order an apology · order amendment of documents · order action to compensate ·
  ⭐⭐ **compensation up to $100,000 for loss or damage, EXPRESSLY INCLUDING INJURED FEELINGS OR
  HUMILIATION.**
- ⛔⛔ **TIME LIMIT: the complaint must be made within 12 MONTHS of finding out about the breach.**

   documents, recipients.
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **It establishes NOTICE.** From 10 September 2026 Metro South cannot say it did not know.

5. ⭐⭐ **It preserves the loss without pleading it** — *"I am not asking LBH HR to assess a claim or
   to put a figure on any of it."* ⭐ **Preserved, not waived.**
