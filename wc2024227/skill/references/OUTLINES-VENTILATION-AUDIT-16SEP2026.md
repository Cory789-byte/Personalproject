# The three served outlines — forensic audit for departmental ventilation

**WC/2024/227 · 16 September 2026 · ⛔ INTERNAL ONLY. Not sent, not quoted to anyone.**

**Question put:** does any section of the served witness outlines have a *depth* that would amount
to ventilating Metro South as a whole, and publicly?

**Short answer:** ⭐ **Yes — three places, and one of them is not in the Appellant's own outline.**
But the finding is narrower than it first looks. The problem is **not depth**. Most of the depth is
load-bearing. The problem is that inside three passages the outline quietly changes subject —
**from what the Appellant was answerable for, to what happened to patients** — and it is the second
of those that ventilates a public hospital.

## 1. The test applied

Two limbs, because the question has two.

**Limb A — widening.** Does the passage prove *the Appellant's* working conditions, or does it
prove something about the department that is true whether or not he existed? The pleaded mechanism
is **one thing**: *responsibility imposed, and the means to discharge it withheld.* Anything that
serves that is not ventilation however long it is. Anything that does not, is.

**Limb B — publication.** ⭐⭐ **There is no non-publication order in this matter.** The s 580 order
was **draft order 6** of the February 2026 Form 4, and **that Form 4 was recalled at the 13 March
conference**. Nothing is sealed. The outlines themselves are served, not filed — they say so on
their face — so *the outlines are not public documents.* But **the evidence given on them is given
in open hearing, and a published decision follows.** ⇒ The exposure is not the paper. It is what he
says aloud and what a decision-writer would reproduce.

## 3. 🔴 FINDING 1 — the single sentence that states patient risk as a proposition

**Topic 1:**
> *"in a Code Blue or MET call it is the Switchboard that activates and pages the response team, and
> **an error or delay at the console is an error or delay in the emergency response**."*

⛔ This is not evidence about his work. It is **a proposition about what happens to patients when the
system fails** — offered by a witness who then says, correctly, *"I offer no clinical or diagnostic
opinion."* It is the one sentence in 1,909 words that a decision-writer would lift, and it is the
sentence that most invites the reading Dwyer already formed: *"a broader grievance … that
systemically, the place in which you worked had a number of failings."*

⚠ **And it is not needed.** Everything it is doing is done better by the sentence eight lines below
it, which is the pleaded mechanism stated exactly:

> ⭐ *"from the moment I logged onto the board I was held responsible for what went wrong anywhere in
> that system, while the means of correcting it, the directory, the on-call roster, my own roster and
> my pay, were held by others."*

⇒ **Responsibility framing proves the case. Consequence framing ventilates the hospital.** The
second sentence carries the first's work without carrying its risk.

⚠ **The surrounding detail is a different matter and should stay.** *What a Code Blue is; 15–50
codes in 24 hours; 200–300 calls a shift.* That is the **weight of the responsibility**, and the
weight is the case. ⛔ Do not cut it to look restrained — it is the reason the mechanism bites.

## 4. 🔴 FINDING 2 — topic 5 is the most departmental passage in the document

**272 words. 14.2% of the outline. 1.5 first-person references per 100 words — the lowest of any
topic.** For most of it the Appellant is not the subject of his own evidence.

The quoted material:
> *"nine calls reaching the wrong team, including the **MET call team** ringing because 'switchboard
> could not tell them where VHUB was'"* · *"'many ongoing issues' about calls 'transferred to the
> wrong medical teams'"* · *"the Integrated Respiratory Service writing … that **'we can not help
> patients or other clinical staff'**"*

**In its favour, and it is substantial:**
- ⭐ Every word of it is **the employer's own**, quoted — the only strong language on the page.
- ⭐ It is **admitted** (Notice ¶¶ 39–113). Nobody has to prove it.
- ⭐ It is the documentary spine of the mechanism: the complaints arrived at his console and the fix
  was held by someone else.

**Against it:**
- ⛔ Cumulatively it reads as *a public hospital's emergency communications were failing*, which is a
  proposition about Metro South, not about him — **Limb A fails**.
- ⛔ ⚠ Under **Limb B** this is the highest publication exposure in the matter. A named hospital,
  named services, misdirected emergency calls.
- ⛔ It runs against the standing discipline that **patient-safety-as-descriptor stays off WC paper**
  and that **patient harm is never alleged or hinted**.

⇒ **The fix is not deletion.** It is to close each item on *him*. The passage already ends that way
once — *"on each occasion the delay built up while the correction waited on someone else"* — and
that clause is the only part of the 272 words that is about the pleaded mechanism. ⭐ **Make it the
frame rather than the footer**, and the clinical specifics become the reason the complaints were
angry rather than a case about patient safety.

## 5. 🔴 FINDING 3 — ⚠ topic 7 names the PID, and infers reprisal without pleading it

Two separate problems in eleven words each.

**(a)** > *"my complaint of 13 May 2024, **later determined to be a public interest disclosure**
(content not set out)"*

⛔ **The PID is named on WC paper.** The standing discipline keeps the PID/reprisal track separate,
and this half-states it: the *fact* of a PID is asserted while the content is withheld. That is the
worst of both — it raises the question without the material to answer it, and it invites the
Regulator to say the real complaint is a reprisal case dressed as a s 32 appeal.

**(b)** > *"my interest in becoming the Switchboard union delegate **and what followed**"*

⛔ *"And what followed"* states no fact. It **invites an inference of reprisal** without pleading it,
which is the one thing the disciplines forbid in this track. ⚠ And it is already known to be thin:
at the mention the bench took him only as far as *"I can only infer the union"*, and on the
"actively suppressed" limb he answered honestly **"I don't know if I can prove it as much."**
⇒ **An unstated inference he has already conceded he cannot prove raises his own *Briginshaw* bar
for nothing.** Either state the dated sequence and stop, or drop the words.

**(c) And the universal negative:**
> *"no safety assessment, investigation or change of procedure followed **any complaint I made**
> about misdirected calls, directory accuracy or fatigue before 30 June 2024"*

⭐ **This one survives, and it should.** It reads as systemic, but it is (i) tied to *his* complaints,
(ii) on **Metro South's own statements**, and (iii) the direct documentary answer to Dr Hawes's
contemporaneous note *"they don't listen to his complaints"* — which is causation evidence, not
grievance. ⇒ **Keep it.** It is a negative about the response to him, not an audit of the hospital.

## 6. 🔴 FINDING 4 — ⭐⭐ the clearest widening is in **Ms Conaghan's** outline, not his

This is the finding I would not have predicted and it is the sharpest one.

| ¶ | What she will say | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| **10** | she was **not consulted** about the after-hours change and **not asked to vote** | ⛔ **her grievance, not his** |
| **11** | she was **not consulted about, and not balloted in relation to**, roster changes | ⛔ **her grievance, not his** |

⛔⛔ **Paragraphs 10 and 11 prove a consultation failure affecting the workforce. They prove nothing
about the Appellant's injury.** They are the only passages across all three outlines whose subject is
a *third party's* industrial entitlement. That converts a personal-injury appeal into a departmental
consultation dispute — and it is the precise shape of the "broader grievance" reading.

⚠ **And she is a serving Metro South employee** — "Administration Officer, Switchboard Services,
Logan Hospital". A current officer of the organisation giving evidence about the organisation's
failure to consult **its staff**, in a public hearing, with no non-publication order. ⭐ Her being
inside the department is exactly what makes her valuable on the facts and exactly what makes ¶¶ 10–11
exposed.

⚠ **¶¶ 5 and 7 add a second problem — hearsay carrying the clinical risk:**
> ¶ 5 *"what she was told at the console by those calling back **about results that had not reached
> the treating team**"* · ¶ 7 *"what she was told … by callers reporting that calls had reached the
> wrong medical team, **including calls concerning emergency responses**"*

⇒ These are **patient-safety propositions proved by what somebody told her**. They carry the full
publication exposure of Finding 2 with none of its documentary strength.

⭐ **What to keep, and it is most of her outline.** ¶¶ 2, 3, 4 (the abuse, the threats, the
complaints landing on the operator), ¶ 6, ¶ 8, ¶ 9 and ¶ 13 are all first-hand, all about **the
Appellant's working conditions**, and ¶ 12 is properly bounded (*"She offers no opinion as to
diagnosis or cause"*). ⇒ **The outline is 80% clean. Four paragraphs carry the whole risk.**

## 8. ⚠ The countervailing fact, so this is not read as a warning it is not

⭐ **The bench expressly permitted the patient-safety frame** — unprompted, two minutes before the
long pause at the mention:

> **DWYER IC:** *"If you think it's in a patient safety environment, **you can raise that**. Anyway,
> let's not get into the case."*

⇒ ⛔ **So none of this is inadmissible and none of it is forbidden.** What he stopped was
*re-arguing the case orally at a mention*, not the concept. The risk identified in this audit is
**not** that the material will be excluded. It is four things, in order of seriousness:

1. ⭐⭐ **The "broader grievance" diagnosis hardens.** It is already formed, it is quotable, and
   Findings 1, 2 and 4 feed it.
2. ⛔ **s 32(5)(b).** Material about the *perception* of systemic failure is the doorway to the
   exclusion for injuries connected to a worker's expectation or perception of management action.
   The Regulator has not pleaded it. ⛔ **Nothing should open it.**
3. ⚠ ***Briginshaw*.** Every proposition that reads as an allegation against Metro South raises
   **his own** standard of persuasion. Finding 3(b) does that for a point already conceded as
   unprovable.
4. ⚠ **Publication.** No order is in place; a decision will be published.
