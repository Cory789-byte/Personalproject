## 4 · ⚠ RISK FLAG — Conaghan item 11 is unqualified in time
Item 11 as served: *"That she was not consulted about, and was not balloted in relation to, changes
to the Switchboard roster."* No date limit. **Facts 176–177 are admitted:** a consultation paper
released 14 Nov 2024, feedback to 8 Dec 2024, **seventeen affected employees invited to vote,
seventeen votes received, fourteen Yes, three No**, effective 20 Jan 2025. If Ms Conaghan was one
of the seventeen — she is an AO in Switchboard Services — item 11 as written is contradicted by an
admitted document, and it is the Appellant's own Tab 23.

**Fix (no rebuild of the served outline — served originals stand):** confine the evidence in chief
to the period before 30 June 2024 and to the roster-line changes of 2023–24; ask her directly
before the hearing whether she voted in December 2024; if she did, lead it yourself ("the first
ballot she was invited to was December 2024"). Item 10 (the 15 Apr 2024 on-call change) is safe —
¶181 admits no ballot is alleged for it.

## 5 · The answer

**To rebut the outlines as a whole they need, at minimum:**
1. **Taylor** — indispensable. She is the only person who can speak to the reasons for every
   directive, the consultation, the hours, the reprimand, the comm book, the roster, and the
   observation evidence of two lay witnesses. Without her, topics 1, 3, 5, 6, 7 and both lay
   outlines stand unanswered.
2. **Reese** — for "not told" (topic 3), the 15 May reply (topic 6), the 7 Aug 2023 handling
   (topic 7) and every "I will follow up" absence. Without her, her admitted words stand unqualified.
3. **Stibbard** — the witness nobody has priced. Topic 5, Harrison-Jones 4–5, Conaghan 6 and 8
   are about *her* email and *her* access regime. Taylor can only give it second-hand. If the
   30 Sep list has a third MSH name, expect it to be Stibbard.
4. **An IME under s 556** — topics 8 and 9 have no lay rebuttal at all.
5. **The Emergency Code Register (Tab 31)** — the only way to touch the codes handled on
   17–18 March; a document, and one MSH has already said exists (¶268).
6. Optionally an HR or Payroll officer for the mechanics of topic 4 and the 2020 agreement — but
   Forrest's email is admitted (¶¶224–225) and adds nothing she has not already written.

**What no witness can rebut:** every admitted act; the ~40 negatives; the review officer's own
finding (¶260); MSH's statements to the Commission (¶¶263–268); the interval arithmetic; and his
account of the effect on him, which is met only through the medical evidence and his credit.

**So "who do they need to call" has a short answer: three MSH officers and a psychiatrist, and
even then the rebuttal is confined to reasons, observations and causation.** The direction 4 list
on 30 September is scored against this: Taylor + Reese = the reasons defence; + Stibbard = they
mean to meet topic 5 head-on; an expert listed = the s 556 route is already chosen; none of them =
the onus defence.

## 6 · The clinicians — Kwok, Dr Wong, Marriott (Cory, 14 Sep)
The one witness group **neither side needs and the defence cannot afford.**
- **He does not need them.** Their evidence is already in the record in its best possible form:
  their own contemporaneous emails, wording admitted (¶¶56–62, 89–104), and for the Kwok
