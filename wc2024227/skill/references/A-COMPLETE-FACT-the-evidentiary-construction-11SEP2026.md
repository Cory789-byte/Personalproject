# 1. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ WHAT "COMPLETE" MEANS — FOUR TESTS
A proposition is **completely proved** when:
1. ⭐⭐ **Every element has a source.**
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **Each source is admissible**, or **proof of it is dispensed with**.
3. ⭐⭐⭐ **No step requires an inference the sources do not support.**
4. ⭐⭐⭐ **It is not displaced** by a contrary case.

⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The 298 admissions satisfy 1–4 on the conduct. They satisfy none of them on injury or
causation. Keeping those two apart is the whole discipline.**

---

# 2. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE FIVE MECHANISMS THAT DO THE WORK

| # | Mechanism | What it removes |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **1. FORMAL ADMISSION — r 49** | An admitted fact is not proved by evidence. **Proof is dispensed with.** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Admissibility, tender AND weight all fall away at once. There is nothing to object to and nothing to weigh.** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **2. THE OPERATIVE DOCUMENT** | Where the act is done **in** the words — a direction, instruction, refusal, approval — the document is not tendered for the truth of any assertion | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The hearsay question never arises. Content = act.** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **3. ADMISSION BY A PARTY'S OWN OFFICER** | Statements by MSH's officers, in the course of their duties, about MSH's own conduct, are receivable **against MSH** | ⭐⭐⭐ **Receivable for their TRUTH, not merely as acts — a second, independent route to the same documents** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **4. COMPUTATION** | An interval between two proved dates | ⭐⭐⭐ **Not evidence at all. The tribunal performs it.** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **5. THE ADVERSARY'S OWN FORMAL DOCUMENT** | Their amended **List of Documents** is their formal statement of what they hold | ⭐⭐⭐ **An absence proved out of their own mouth, not by his assertion** |

⭐⭐⭐⭐ **MECHANISMS 2 AND 3 ARE THE INSURANCE.** ⇒ **Even if every admission were withdrawn
tomorrow, each document still enters by two independent routes.** ⭐⭐ **The admissions are a
shortcut, not a crutch.**

# 4. ⛔⛔ WHY A COMPLETE FACT IS NOT A COMPLETE CASE

⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The fact in issue under s 32 is NOT "did this happen".** It is whether the injury arose
out of or in the course of the employment, with the employment **a significant** contributing
factor, and is not excluded by **s 32(5)(a)**.

| Element of the case | Proved by the 298? | What actually proves it |
|---|---|---|
| **The conduct** — what was done, when, by whom, how long it took | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **YES, completely** | the admissions |
| **The manner** — s 32(5)(a) | ⭐⭐⭐ **Largely** — the documents are the manner | the admissions + the Review Decision |
| ⛔⛔ **The injury** | ⛔⛔ **NO** | expert opinion |
| ⛔⛔ **Causation — "a significant contributing factor"** | ⛔⛔ **NO** | expert opinion |

⛔⛔ **Nothing in 298 admitted facts touches injury or causation. They are opinion, and opinion
requires a qualified expert whose opinion is built on facts PROVED.**

# 6. ⭐⭐⭐ HOW IT IS PUT BEFORE THE COMMISSION — IT IS NOT TENDERED
⭐⭐⭐⭐ **An admitted fact is not tendered. It is recited.** In the outline: the stressor heading,
the dated facts in order with their numbers, then one line — *"Each of the facts in this section was
admitted by the Respondent on 8 September 2026."*
⭐⭐ The documents themselves go into the tender bundle for context and continuity, **not to prove
the admitted facts**. ⭐⭐ The expert reports **annex the schedule**.
⛔⛔ **No adjective attaches to any of it. The moment a closed fact is explained it becomes a
characterisation, and characterisation is the only ground the Respondent has left.**
