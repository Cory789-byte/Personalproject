## PART 3 — WHERE THIS CAN AND CANNOT BE USED
### ⛔⛔ NOT IN THE WC APPEAL
Causation stops **24 Oct 2024**. The post-return material is 2025–26. Population-level literature
about how reinstated employees fare adds nothing to **s 32(1)** and edges toward **s 32(5)(b)**,
which the Regulator has **not pleaded**. **Keep it out.**

### ⭐⭐ USE 1 — WHS / PSYCHOSOCIAL TRACK (the strongest use)
If post-reinstatement returns in hierarchical health settings are known to carry elevated
psychosocial risk, then an employer reinstating a worker after a dismissal dispute has a
**heightened** duty under **WHS Act ss 17 and 19** to identify and control that risk on return.
⇒ **No reintegration plan is recorded. No psychosocial risk assessment. No change of manager or
location. The underlying disclosure unresolved.** ⭐ That is a foreseeability argument built on
published literature plus MSH's own documents, and it belongs in the psychosocial hazard package
already built (`Psychosocial_Hazard_Identification_MSH-INJ-5795.pdf` and companions).

### ⭐ USE 2 — REPORT B / REPORT A, ON PROGNOSIS ONLY
A psychiatrist may properly refer to literature on post-return trajectories when addressing
**prognosis** and **the consequences of exclusion** — both already within the letter's scope.
⛔ **Not for causation.** Causation runs on the assumed facts, not on population data.
⚠ **Supply the literature only if verified.** An unverified citation in a medico-legal report is
worse than none.

### ⭐⭐ USE 3 — HIS OWN DECISION, AND THIS IS THE HONEST ONE
**The literature says the outcome he is fighting hardest for — a sustained return to Logan
Switchboard — is the one least likely to be sustainable.**
⚠⚠ **And his own treating psychiatrist said the same thing before any of this**: A4, 13 Feb 2025,
p. 5 — *"premature exposure to the workplace is more likely result in significant deterioration in
his mental health and increased risks of suicide."*
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **The independent literature and his own treating specialist point the same way, from
different directions.** That convergence is worth sitting with. **It is his decision and nobody
else's** — but it should be made knowing that *return* and *resolution* are not the same outcome,
and that the system settles far more often than it restores.

## PART 4 — ⛔ WHAT THE RESEARCH DOES **NOT** SUPPORT
1. ⛔ It says **nothing** about whether MSH acted badly toward **him**. It is population-level.
   Using it to characterise their conduct is precisely the over-reach the discipline rules bar.
2. ⛔ It must **never** be used to predict that a return would be made unbearable. That is
   prediction and perception — **the s 32(5)(b) doorway**.
3. ⚠ Evidence quality is **modest**: no Australian longitudinal post-reinstatement dataset,
   especially in health. Comparative Japanese/Canadian data and qualitative Australian work are
   **not** a substitute. **State the limitation whenever the material is used.**
