# WHO ACTUALLY INVESTIGATES WHAT — routing map (verified 4 Aug 2026)
> ⛔ Strategy layer. Corrects a common and costly assumption: NO single body investigates
> "the detriment and harm caused to me". Each has a narrow jurisdiction; most will decline
> while QIRC proceedings are on foot. The tribunal — through HIS OWN proceedings — is the
> body that makes findings about detriment. He is the investigator; QIRC is the fact-finder.

| Body | What it actually does | Reality here |
|---|---|---|
| **RTI / IP access** | NOT an investigation. A document-access process run by MSH's own RTI decision-maker; external review by the Office of the Information Commissioner. | Exactly right for the **nil return**. Produces documents, never findings. 25 business days + extensions. **DO IT (Mon 10).** |
| **QPS (police)** | Criminal offences only. PID s 41 reprisal is indictable, so jurisdiction technically exists. | In practice QPS treats workplace reprisal as civil/employment and refers on. Low return, high credibility cost, and never to be threatened. **NOT the pathway.** |
| **⭐ Workplace Health and Safety Queensland (OIR)** | The WHS regulator: inspects, issues improvement/prohibition notices, investigates Part 6 discriminatory conduct, prosecutes s 104 offences. | **The one external body whose jurisdiction actually fits** — unmanaged psychosocial risk + WHS-based detriment. An improvement notice would compel the assessment. Timing: hold until Stage 2 has run or an adverse act occurs. |
| **QIRC under WHS s 112** | An "eligible person" (incl. the person affected) may apply directly for **injunction, compensation, or reinstatement** for discriminatory conduct. | ⭐ A remedy HE can bring himself, not dependent on any regulator. **s 113(2): the prohibited reason is PRESUMED to be a substantial reason unless the employer disproves it on the balance of probabilities — a legal burden.** ⚠ **s 113(1): 1 YEAR from when he knew or ought to have known the cause of action accrued.** ⚠ **s 115: cannot run this and another proceeding about the SAME MATTER simultaneously — but workers' compensation proceedings are expressly carved out.** |
| **Queensland Ombudsman** | Maladministration in public-agency administrative action. | Usually declines where another remedy (QIRC) exists; makes systemic findings, awards no compensation. Secondary at best. |
| **CCC** | Corrupt conduct. Reprisal for a PID can qualify. | Typically devolves the complaint back to the agency (MSH investigates itself). Banked; not now. |
| **QHRC** | Impairment discrimination, victimisation; conciliation, referral to QIRC. | Real, paired with the AD Act limb. **Limitation ~8 Sep.** |
| **Royal Commission** | Established by executive government into matters of major public importance. | **Cannot be invoked by an individual.** Not a pathway — no application exists to make. |

## THE THREE THINGS THIS CHANGES
1. **The investigation of his harm is his own proceeding.** The 64G/Form 29 machinery, the
   disclosure, the sworn alternative — that IS the investigation, and Dwyer is the finder of
   fact. No external body will do it for him, and waiting for one costs time he does not have.
2. **WHS s 112 is a genuinely underused remedy** — his own application, compensation and
   reinstatement available, and a reverse legal burden under s 113(2) that is stronger than
   most protective provisions. Two limits to diarise: **1 year** (s 113(1)) and the
   **multiple-actions bar** (s 115) — WC proceedings excepted.
3. **WHSQ is the only external escalation with real teeth**, and it is a one-way door.
   Posture unchanged: hold until Stage 2 has run or an adverse act occurs.
