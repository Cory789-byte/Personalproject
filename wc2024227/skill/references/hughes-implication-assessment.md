## 6. ⛔ WHAT MUST NOT BE RUN

1. ❌ **"He adopted Chloe's attitude toward me."** No document. It is a claim about a state of mind
   in a man who sent 26 emails in 18 months, none about the work. **It cannot be proved and it makes
   everything beside it look like grievance.**
2. ❌ **"He never talks to me."** As a complaint about manners it is fatal to the register.
   ⭐ **As "there is no record of consultation by the decision-maker" it is a WHS ss 47–49 point and
   it is proved by the corpus.** Use the second; never the first.
3. ❌ **"He and Chloe acted together."** Discipline rule 1 — **conspiracy framing never reaches
   paper.** The **asymmetry** is held in reserve. What is provable is the **sequence** and the
   **roles**, not the agreement.
4. ❌ **"He deflected *all* my concerns."** Overstated on the record — he answered leave and roster
   traffic promptly through March 2025. **Say what is true: the DV disclosure got "Noted thanks", and
   the complaint about him got nothing.**
5. ⚠️ **The 14 typographical errors** stay in the union brief. Raising them reads as petty.

**In eighteen months he sent twenty-six emails, none about the work, and none at all in the year
surrounding the two decisions that mattered most.**

⇒ **That is not a personality problem. It is a person exercising decision-making power over an
employee he has never engaged with, in a matter in which he is the subject of the complaint.**
