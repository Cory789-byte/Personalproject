# 2. ⭐⭐⭐ HOW THEY GOT OUT OF IT — AND IT WAS NOT BY WINNING AN ARGUMENT

## 2.1 ⭐⭐⭐ THE IMPOSITION FRAMING PUT THE AFFIDAVIT OUT OF REACH BEFORE IT WAS ARGUED
> **[18:31]** *"making an order for a third party to disclose documents is a step **sort of beyond
> what normally happens in disclosure. It's quite an imposition** because the health service is not a
> party to these proceedings."*
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **If PRODUCING DOCUMENTS is *"quite an imposition"*, requiring a non-party to SWEAR TO ITS
SEARCHES is further still.** ⛔ **The verification affidavit was gone before anyone addressed it.**

## 2.2 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ AND THE SWORN-ABSENCE REQUEST WAS ANSWERED CORRECTLY, AND FATALLY
> **[47:29]** *"Do you know they did anything with it?"* → **the Appellant:** *"I'm asking for evidence
> or **a sworn statement** to say… they didn't do anything with it."*
> ⭐⭐⭐ **DWYER: *"Well, what are you asking for if that's the case?"***
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **A NEGATIVE CANNOT BE DISCLOSED. That answer was right, and it ended that route.**

## 2.3 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ AND THE OBJECTION WAS NEVER ADJUDICATED — IT WAS ROUTED AROUND
⭐⭐⭐ **By making the Regulator the conduit and never ruling on K-LM26/729, the question whether MSH's
non-existence answers were TRUE never arose.**
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **It was not decided against him. IT WAS NEVER REACHED.**
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **That is how they got what they wanted: not by winning, but by never having the argument.**

## 2.4 ⛔⛔ AND THEN HE CLOSED IT HIMSELF
⛔ **The Form 29 withdrawn 28 August 2026.** ⇒ ⛔⛔ **The route is now shut permanently. MSH will never
swear to those searches in this proceeding.**
