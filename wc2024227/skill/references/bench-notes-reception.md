# Bench Notes — how the Commission would annotate the Form 29, the MSH response, and the 64G

**Purpose:** a realistic judicial-reception model. Written as Commissioner Dwyer's
working annotations — neutral, close-reading, noting BOTH sides' problems. This is
how the materials likely LAND on the decision-maker. Reserve/strategy; not a filing.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
## I. BENCH NOTE — on the Notice (Form 29 + Continuation Schedule)

- *Overall:* unusually well-particularised for a self-represented appellant. The
  Continuation Schedule states the matter in issue and cross-refers each category
  to the pleaded Form 9A — **r 64C(1) satisfied, and conspicuously so.** Note the
  phrase-lexicon (Item 3): a server-side filter bounded by named phrase-families —
  this is a *structured search*, not a trawl. Marks against any "fishing"
  characterisation before MSH even raises it.
- *Caution to self:* the Schedule is dense and the pleaded conduct includes
  serious personal allegations (PID, "fraud", reprisal). **Watch for collateral
  purpose** — but note the Notice itself is framed around records of operational
  fact, not the accusations. Keep the two separate when assessing relevance.
- *Breadth:* some windows are wide (Items 8/9, 1 Dec 2023–30 Jun 2024). Query
  whether the full window is necessary or whether a floor (the admitted 17–18 Mar
  shifts) suffices. A candidate for *variation*, not refusal.

## II. BENCH NOTES — on MSH's response, item by item

**Items 1–2 (SPOK):** non-existence conceded (system upgrade); MET spreadsheet
offered. → *No contest. Produce the MET spreadsheet by consent. Note the search
was DONE — "do not exist" is an answer on oath waiting to happen.*

**Item 3(a)/(b) (complaints / mgmt discussion):** objection — expense (64E(4)(a)).
→ *MSH's own answer ("all such complaints made directly to the Line Manager by
email") IDENTIFIES the custodian and the medium. That supports a confined,
single-custodian search, not the seven-month multi-account exercise the objection
describes. Expense met by confinement + r 64I. Particularity already cured by the
phrase-filter. Inclined to OVERRULE, on the narrowed terms.*

**Item 3(c) (consequential SOP changes):** "do not exist — no consequential
changes." → *Note: the ABSENCE of any procedural change in the face of repeated
operational-error complaints is itself relevant to the manner of management

**Items 4, 5, 7 (FRMS):** cannot comply — "Switchboard non-clinical, no mandatory
FRMS"; assessment "implemented after 30 June 2024." → *This is a significant
ADMISSION. The Respondent's defence is reasonable management action; the
non-party concedes no fatigue-management framework governed the Switchboard at
the material time. Whether that classification was itself reasonable is a hearing
question — but the concession is now fixed. Verify by affidavit.*

**Item 6:** produced. → *No contest.*

**Item 8 (sign-in records):** objection — expense + particularity. → *Particularity

seven items but NOT for 8/9 — the records plainly exist and are identified.

**Item 9 (access/swipe):** objection — relevance, particularity, effect (b)(c)(f).

**Items 11–16:** largely produced. Item 14 (texts): explained — personal device,

**Item 20 (response to PID disclosures):** "do not exist — PID ref commenced Nov

## III. BENCH NOTE — on the 64G application and disposition

- *Posture:* the application is restrained and procedural. The Appellant has
  CONCEDED or not pressed Items 12(bal), 17, 18 — and adopted MSH's own narrowing
  on 19. **The concessions certify the necessity of what remains.** This is an
  applicant asking for less than the Notice, not more. Disposition is
  straightforward.
- *The contested core (3(a), 3(b), 8, 9, 10, 19, 20):* records that exist or whose
  existence is the very thing to be verified; held only by the non-party; directly
  relevant to pleaded, contested facts (erratic presence; the manner of management
  action; the response to operational-error complaints). Expense met by
  confinement + r 64I. Privacy met by withdrawal + the Items 8/9 regime.
- *Natural disposition — a Bunney-style PARTIAL PRODUCTION ORDER:* overrule the
  objections to the confined items; order production in 14 days; impose the
  confidentiality regime on 8/9; Item 19 in MSH's proposed terms with TQ notice;
  and — *the engine* — a **verification affidavit by an officer with direct
  knowledge** for the non-existence answers (1–2, 3(c), 4, 5, 7, 14, 20). Each
  party its own costs (r 64G(3)).
- *Why the affidavit:* the "do not exist" answers are presently statements in
  correspondence. They bear on the appeal; some (Items 4/5/7) are concessions the
  Appellant will rely on. They should be on oath. This is conventional and
  proportionate — and the non-party can hardly object to verifying its own answers.
- *Caution preserved:* the personal/fraud/reprisal material is NOT engaged by the
  application. Were it pressed, different considerations (collateral purpose) would
  arise. It is not. Note and move on.
- *Likely outcome:* substantially GRANT, in or near the terms of the draft order.
  The Regulator is likely to abide. MSH concedes the core or fights only the
  windows. The draft order is in a form the Commission can adopt with minimal
  amendment — which is itself why it will be.

**One-line bench summary:** *A well-particularised, self-narrowed notice; objections
that are partly concessions, partly privacy (accommodated), partly expense (met by
confinement); records that exist or must be verified; disposition = partial
production order plus verification affidavit, own costs. Grantable as drafted.*

## IV. THE MANAGER'S PRIVACY — how it is cured, and where retribution is foreclosed

The Line Manager is (a) the person whose Items 8/9/3/10 records are sought, AND
(b) the person the PID named. So the Commission is acutely alert to whether the
disclosure is retribution-by-other-means. The application cures it FOUR ways, all
concrete and on the face of the documents:

1. **Field-confinement (the core cure) — draft order ¶4(d):** records produced in
   answer to Items 8/9 are "confined to credential, date and time-stamp fields,
   with non-responsive personal information redacted." → This **de-personalises**
   her record into an objective presence/absence time-series. The application does
   not want anything *private* about her — not content, not reasons, not conduct —
   only "did the credential register, when." Her attendance becomes data points,
   not a dossier.
2. **The protective regime — order ¶4(a)–(c):** Registry-marked "Confidential";
   inspected only by parties/representatives; used only for this proceeding. → No
   publication, no leverage, no life beyond the appeal. The records cannot be
   weaponised against her elsewhere.
3. **Anchoring to a PLEADED FACT, not the person (Annexure B / Section D5):**
   Items 8/9 go ONLY to the pleaded "erratic physical presence" (Stressor 1(a))
   and the "roster was equitable" denial (Form 24 ¶39). The application states
   **"No broader use is made of these Items."** The records measure a SYSTEM fact
   (was the supervisor present) against a contested pleading — not her character,
   integrity, or the fraud suspicion.
4. **The withdrawals (the anti-retribution SIGNAL):** the Appellant does NOT press
   the items that would expose the manager/comparators personally — Item 17
   (comparator payroll), Item 12-balance (comparator leave evidence). A retributive
   applicant presses those hardest; the Appellant drops them.

**Where retribution is foreclosed — the structural proof:**
- The 64G never mentions the fraud, never characterises her conduct, never seeks a
  record to prove she did anything *wrong* personally. It seeks PRESENCE and the
  OPERATIONAL response. The instrument does not pursue her.
- The MINIMALISM is itself the proof: the narrower and more de-personalised the
  request, the more plainly it seeks the fact, not the person. You do not field-
  confine and redact and seal records you intend to use as retribution — you
  field-confine precisely to show you want only the metadata of presence.
- **Critical corollary:** the cure stays robust ONLY because the fraud is kept
  OUT of the 64G. The moment the application touched the fraud/conduct, the
  "retribution-by-disclosure" framing would revive and the privacy cure would look
  pretextual. Discipline rules 1–2 are what make the privacy cure credible.

**Double function:** curing her privacy and proving the Appellant is not
retributive are THE SAME ACT. Field-confinement protects her AND demonstrates the
seeker wants a system fact, not revenge. The cure is the character reference.
