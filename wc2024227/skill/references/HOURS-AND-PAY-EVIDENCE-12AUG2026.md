## 4. ⭐ THE CLOSED FORK (the argument these documents complete)

Either:
**(a) the reduced arrangement continued** — as the ECC of 3 July records ("continuation of
existing arrangement… worked and tolerated without deterioration") — in which case wages were
payable at 40 hours per fortnight from 29 June and nothing was paid; **or**
**(b) the reduced arrangement expired on 28 June** and the employee reverted to the substantive
76-hour contract — the very basis Hughes asserts in the RFMI — in which case wages were payable
at 76 hours per fortnight and nothing was paid, and a full-time employee certified fit with
restrictions was sent home without instrument on 26 June.
**There is no third branch.** On the employer's own documents, wages were payable on either
construction. The only mechanism by which neither was paid is the unilateral coding of the
period as unpaid sick / income-protection leave — a medical characterisation with no medical
foundation.
