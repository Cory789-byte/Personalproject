# LONGITUDINAL ANALYSIS — how this is actually happening
> 14 August 2026. Not what happened (see MASTER-ALIGNMENT) but **how it is unfolding**: the
> phases, the mechanisms driving events, the feedback loops, the asymmetries that persist, the
> slow drifts nobody decided, and where the system goes if nothing changes.
> ⛔ Rule 1 governs: **mechanisms, not motives.** A mechanism explains without accusing.

---

## PART 1 — THE TEN PHASES

**P0 · 2020 – mid 2023 · FUNCTIONING.** He works, raises things occasionally, gets answers.
Grievances are answered same-day. The full-time conversion goes through. Nothing reaches paper as
a dispute because the system absorbs issues at the point they arise.
⭐ *This phase is the control experiment. Same man, same register, no friction.*

**P1 · Jun 2023 – Feb 2024 · EROSION OF THE INFORMAL LAYER.** Comms book pages removed (6 Jun);
database access removed and the paper changes-book taken from the room (18 Jul); grievance
dismissed the same day it was made (7 Aug). Yet the **formal** system still delivers — the
full-time appointment is approved (27 Sep).
⭐ *The critical distinction of this phase: formal processes still worked; the informal layer —
consultation, availability, consistency — degraded. Everything after 2024 is a consequence of
having only formal channels left.*

**P2 · Feb – May 2024 · SIMULTANEOUS SYSTEM FAILURE.** COVID leave declined on a false premise
(20–27 Feb); the clinic directory modified without notice (22 Feb); a 7-hour break rostered
(17–18 Mar); pay errors accumulate; MASPER errors accumulate; the respiratory directory failure
runs. Five domains failing in fourteen weeks.
⭐ *Mechanism, not malice: a department with one escalation route and an unreachable manager
cannot self-correct. Errors do not get caught because there is nowhere for them to be caught.*

**P3 · May – Jun 2024 · COLLISION.** He escalates formally for the first time (PID, 13 May). The
response arrives in **two days** and is adverse (retraction demanded, 15 May). The respiratory
failure peaks (20 May). Injury onset (18 Jun).
⭐ *This is where the velocity rule first shows: the moment he used a formal instrument, response
time collapsed from weeks to hours — but the content was punitive.*

**P4 · Jul – Dec 2024 · ADJUDICATION, AND A PARADOX.** Claim rejected (13 Sep); employment ended
(9 Oct); Review Decision 69983 (24 Oct) records findings **in his favour** — unreasonable
management action, work events the sole cause, no pre-existing factor — yet the rejection stands
and he must appeal (26 Nov).
⚠ **OPEN QUESTION TO RESOLVE PRECISELY:** how a decision containing those three findings
nevertheless confirmed the rejection. The answer determines what the appeal actually has to
overcome. **Read Review Decision 69983 in full and identify the operative reasoning, not just the
quotable findings.** Do not assume; the answer may be narrower or wider than the extracts suggest.

**P5 · Dec 2024 – Jun 2025 · RESTORATION AND DORMANCY.** Deed; return to work 7 Apr 2025; working
at roughly 29% of full-time, 269–444 calls per shift. The appeal sits quiet.
⭐ *This phase manufactures the fact that later destroys MSH's 2026 position: he returned and
sustained reduced hours over time, without deterioration.*

**P6 · Jul 2025 · THE REGULATOR'S EVIDENCE WEEK.** NNPDs filed 4 Jul → Reese conferenced 8 Jul →
Taylor conferenced 10 Jul → payroll produces 11 Jul → disclosed 14 Jul.
⭐ *One concentrated week, thirteen months ago. **Their case was built then and has not been
rebuilt since.** Everything they have done in 2026 is reactive.*

**P7 · Sep 2025 – Feb 2026 · THE CONDUCT FRAME, TRIED AND ABANDONED.** The 34-occasions attendance
letter treats his absences as **conduct** — no medical inquiry, no G3, the psychological injury
unmentioned. Nothing follows from it.
⭐ *MSH selected a frame, and then dropped it. That matters: the medical frame adopted in 2026 is
the **second** frame, not the only one available.*

**P8 · Feb – Jun 2026 · FORMALISATION OF THE ACCOMMODATION.** Three "Change to Working Conditions"
instruments — 56, 56, 40 hours, each recording **Part Time**, each endorsed and each approved by
the **same senior delegate** (27 Feb · 17 Apr · 9 Jun). On 5 May he tells his manager he is
struggling and distressed and provides a certificate; three weeks later the deepest reduction is
formalised.
⭐⭐ *This is the phase that makes 2026 indefensible for them. They did not merely tolerate reduced
hours — they **instrumentalised** them, three times, at delegate level, with knowledge of his
health. The accommodation was the employer's own construct.*

**P9 · Jun – Jul 2026 · THE BREAK.** Excluded 26 Jun (two days **before** the arrangement expires);
certified fit with restrictions 3 Jul; wages cease 13 Jul; the absence coded to income protection
and then to unpaid sick leave, on his behalf, 19–20 Jul; payroll enquiry lodged 22 Jul; the medical
inquiry issued 31 Jul against a 76-hour baseline the same delegate had replaced.
⭐ *Five weeks in which every mechanism that could have resolved this was either not used, or used
against him.*

**P10 · Aug 2026 → now · CONTEST.** He escalates formally across every front. Within 48 hours of
12 August, both institutions move: MSH twice (one email recalled), the Regulator clearing a
three-week backlog with an apology.
⭐ *Confirmation of the velocity rule, third time in two years.*

## PART 2 — THE MECHANISMS DRIVING IT (how, not why)

**M1 · CHANNEL COLLAPSE.** From mid-2023 every route ran through one person who was structurally
unreachable, with the paper fallback removed and system changes bottlenecked into two days a
fortnight. **A system with one channel and no redundancy cannot absorb error.** Every 2024 failure
is downstream of that design.

**M2 · DEFAULT-TO-ADVERSE.** ⭐⭐ The most important mechanism for understanding 2026. Where nobody
decides, defaults decide. Leave defaults to sick leave; sick leave without balance defaults to
**no pay**. HR's own word for it is *"automatically"*. So harm accumulates **without anyone
choosing it** — which is why no instrument, no notice and no decision-maker can be identified. It
is not that the decision is hidden. It may be that **there was no decision**.
⚠ This does not make it lawful. An employer cannot discharge its obligation to pay by letting a
default run. But it explains the evidence better than design does, and it is the more defensible
thing to say out loud.

**M3 · VELOCITY ASYMMETRY.** Requests take weeks or never. Challenges to authority take hours.
Two years, no exception: 2+ weeks on the roster · 25 days on the AVAC · 3+ weeks on enquiry
4438861 · **versus** 2 days from PID to retraction demand, 5 hours from the hours question to the
retraction demand, 1 day from the served bundle to two HR emails.

**M4 · FORMALISATION IS THE ONLY LEVER THAT HAS EVER WORKED.** Every movement in this matter has
followed a formal instrument — a PID, a Form 29, a sealed bundle, a dated deadline. **No request
has ever produced movement.** This is now a rule he can rely on operationally.

**M5 · DOCUMENTATION GRAVITY.** He records; they do not. Over two years this has produced an
asymmetric record — his account dated and continuous, theirs fragmentary and, on their own
disclosure, partly unproduced. **The gap widens with time by itself**, which means delay quietly
favours him on the record even while it damages him financially.

**M6 · INSTITUTIONAL NON-COMMUNICATION.** Two institutions investigated the same events
independently and neither appears to know what the other found. The Regulator was checking whether
payslips had been amended while the employer was approving movement forms; the Regulator sought
the myHR audit trail on the COVID leave in April 2026 while the employer was formalising his
reduced hours. **No single actor holds the whole picture — except him.**

**M7 · MEDICALISATION AS THE LAST AVAILABLE CATEGORY.** ⭐ A more honest framing than "coordinated
exit": the conduct frame was tried in Sep 2025 and abandoned; consultation had already failed;
performance was never in issue. **Medical is the only remaining category that produces an outcome
without anyone having to justify a decision.** That explains the 2026 conduct at least as well as
design does — and it is the version that survives scrutiny.

## PART 3 — THE FEEDBACK LOOPS

**L1 · THE DOCUMENTATION SPIRAL (reinforcing).** He documents → they become more careful → less is
put in writing → more must be inferred → he documents more. Visible in the 13 Aug recall: an
officer wrote naturally, saw it, and withdrew it.

**L2 · THE ABSENT DAMPER.** A functioning consultation channel would have absorbed each issue at
source. It has not existed since mid-2023. **Nothing in this system dissipates a problem; every
problem must escalate to be resolved.**

**L3 · THE ESCALATION RATCHET.** request → silence → escalation → adverse response → grievance →
refusal → formal instrument → movement. Each cycle raises formality and stakes, and **cannot run
backwards.** There is no path back to informal resolution.

**L4 · ⭐⭐ THE FINANCIAL LOOP (new in 2026, and the dangerous one).** No pay → hardship → pressure
to concede → but every available concession (leave consent, resignation, cheap settlement)
destroys a claim → so he must hold → hardship deepens. **This loop has no internal exit.** It can
only be broken by money entering from outside: the insurer, wages recovered, hardship support, or
a firm funding the matter.
⭐ *Every other dynamic in this file favours him slowly. This one works against him quickly. That
mismatch — slow advantage against fast damage — is the strategic problem in one sentence.*

## PART 5 — THE DRIFTS (slow changes nobody decided)
**D1 · STATUS.** Employee → claimant → case. His matter is now administered from an injury
management mailbox by an injury management consultant with a rehabilitation provider copied and
an insurer's claim number in the subject line. **No one decided this; it accreted.**
**D2 · HOURS.** 1.0 → 0.7 → 0.5 → 0. Each step formalised **except the last**.
**D3 · THEIR CASE.** Causation (Jul 2025) → the exclusion and the perception limb (Apr 2026).
**D4 · HIS REGISTER.** Participatory ("I'd like to help fix this", with solutions and off-ramps)
→ forensic ("please confirm in writing"). Adaptive and necessary — and a real loss, because the
participatory register is what the 2023 record shows working.
**D5 · CUSTODY OF HIS INFORMATION.** MSH · WorkCover · the Regulator · ART/QSuper · Solv · the
union · two medical practices · the Commission. Eight holders, each with a partial view.
⭐ *D5 is why his own documentation is decisive, and why the alignment work is itself an asset:
**he is the only party who holds the whole record.***

## PART 6 — WHERE IT GOES IF NOTHING CHANGES
The default trajectory, absent intervention:
RFMI unanswered (the basis question never answered) → a decision "on the available information" →
a medical separation process → contested → the appeal continues slowly without causation evidence
→ **the financial loop forces a decision before either process matures.**
⭐ **The default is bad — and note precisely why. Not because the legal position is weak. It is
strong and getting stronger. It is bad because L4 runs faster than everything else.**

## PART 7 — THE THREE THINGS THAT BREAK THE TRAJECTORY
Each breaks a different constraint; any one changes the shape.
1. **Money entering** (ART item 4 · wages recovered · hardship support) → breaks L4, buys time.
2. **A causation opinion** → fills the vacuum before theirs does; worth ~20–25 points on the appeal.
3. **Representation** → solves funding, evidence and signalling simultaneously, and removes the
   asymmetry of him running four tracks alone while unpaid.
⭐ *All three are attempted by phone calls and letters that cost nothing. That is the whole of this
week's work.*

## PART 8 — THE SINGLE SENTENCE
**Every slow dynamic in this matter favours him, and every fast one does not — so the entire
strategic problem is buying enough time for the slow dynamics to arrive.**
