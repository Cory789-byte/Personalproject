# B. ⭐⭐⭐ THE ONLY LOAD-BEARING STEP — AND IT HAS FOUR INDEPENDENT DEFECTS
**The step, verbatim:**
> *"In the decision of **Delaney v Q-COMP** the Industrial Court held that… a **global evaluation** of
> management actions must be made. In Delaney, it was held that **when management actions are joined
> by subject matter, time and personality**, the actions are entitled to be evaluated on a global
> basis. I note that **since two out of the three causative factors amounted to reasonable management
> action… this means your psychological injury MAINLY arose out of such management action**. Further,
> factor 4 was **initially only substantiated to the extent of one incident**… Therefore, I find
> overall your psychological injury arose out of reasonable management action taken in a reasonable
> way and section 32(5) is consequently enlivened."*

| | The defect |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐ **1. "Mainly" is the dominant-cause test, and it is rejected** | s 32(5)(a) asks whether the injury arose out of reasonable management action, **not whether it *mainly* did**. ***Adams v Blackwood* [2015] ICQ 1 [23], adopting *Hochen*, rejects a dominant-cause approach.** ⛔ **Read the ratio before it is put.** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **2. The *Delaney* threshold was never found** | *Delaney* permits global evaluation **only where the actions are joined by subject matter, time and personality.** ⭐ **The reviewer recited the threshold and then did not apply it** — no finding that factors 2, 3 and 4 were so joined. |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **3. *Delaney*'s threshold is the WORKER'S protection, and it was inverted** | It exists to stop unrelated actions being lumped together so a bad one is diluted. ⭐ **Here it was used to do exactly that.** |
| ⭐⭐ **4. Counting is not evaluating** | *"two out of the three"* is arithmetic on a tally, not a global evaluation of anything. ⚠ **And factor 4 is discounted as "only… one incident" — which is a reason to weigh it, not to erase it.** |

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **On the reviewer's own findings, correctly applied, the claim was acceptable. The Regulator's
whole position rests on the one paragraph most vulnerable to legal attack.**
