## 3. Findings

Each finding states the proposition, its grade, its source, and its limit. None of them is a
finding a tribunal has made.

**F1 · The March 2026 position is now documented, and it is an indication, not an order. [D]**
Cory's 20 Mar 2026 email records "the Commissioner's indication on the record that the Medical
Exhibit will be sealed if it comes on the record", accepts it under s 580, withdraws the reprisal /
referral / costs / removal / strike-out limbs, and proposes a timetable. Matheson replied the same
afternoon without contradicting a word. *Limit:* the s 580 order itself is not in the file; the
sealing is conditional on the exhibit coming on the record; the withdrawal was of the *orders
sought*, expressly "not ... an abandonment of the factual matters". Nothing in this finding re-opens
the closed February Form 4.

20 March. [D]** "If the documents are in the possession of the Regulator, you can email me a
request for those documents and I will disclose them to you without the need for a Notice to
Produce." The 9 Sep request letter (documents not admitted; ask-first; 18 Sep) sits inside that

documents "in the possession of the Regulator"; the disputed tabs are Cory's copies of documents
whose contents are admitted and whose independent holders are named — the letter asks for
production or concession, not for her to find them.

**F3 · MSH's first act in the appeal was addressed to the Registrar and sent to the Regulator.
[D]** Ruttan's 30 Apr r 64E extension request opens "Dear Registrar" and was sent to OIR-Appeals and
Matheson, Cory copied; Griffin (Director, Employment Relations) forwarded it to the Registry an hour
