# Review of the 17 Oct 2026 "Pre-Conference Resolution Package" (draft letter to Matheson + 41-entry schedule)

Internal. The draft is at `drafts/inbound/2026-10-17_PreConference_Resolution_Letter_and_41_Document_Schedule_DRAFT_AS_RECEIVED.pdf` (6 pp). It was produced without the repo: its own closing note says the Regulator's disclosure index and the July 2025 payroll bundle were not available to it. Do not serve it as drafted.

## Verdict
The idea is sound: narrow the remaining document items and confirm that the Regulator's reservation does not withdraw its 298 admissions, before the conference. The execution is too long and has errors. Send a one-page version.
