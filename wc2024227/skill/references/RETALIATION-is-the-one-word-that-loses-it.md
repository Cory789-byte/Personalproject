# 1. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE TEST DOES NOT ASK FOR A MOTIVE
⭐⭐⭐ **s 32(5)(a) asks whether the management action was REASONABLE and TAKEN IN A REASONABLE WAY.**
⛔⛔ **It does not ask whether it was malicious, vindictive or retaliatory.**
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Conduct can be entirely well-intentioned and still be unreasonable. That is the whole point
of the provision, and it is why he is winning.**
