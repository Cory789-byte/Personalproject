# THE ROOM — WHAT 298 ADMITTED FACTS LEAVE AN MSH WITNESS TO SAY
> 10 September 2026. ⛔ Internal. ⭐ Built from facts verified against `build_form24_second.py`,
> Form 9C, and the served lay outlines.

# 1. ⭐⭐⭐ WHAT AN ADMITTED FACT TAKES AWAY
⭐ **Every one of the 298 is of the form: "document X, dated Y, says Z."**
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **A witness cannot say the document does not exist · was not sent on that date · does not
say that · was not written by them where authorship is admitted.**
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE ENTIRE FACTUAL LAYER IS GONE. A witness is no longer there to ESTABLISH anything.
They are there only to INTERPRET.**

# 2. ⭐⭐⭐ THE FOUR ESCAPES LEFT, AND WHY THREE ARE CLOSED
| Escape | Status |
|---|---|
| **"The context was different"** | ⚠ **Context must itself be proved.** ⭐ Any helpful context document would be in **their own amended List of Documents**. If it is not disclosed, it does not exist for this purpose |
| ⭐ **"It was reasonable because…"** | ⭐⭐ **THE ONLY REAL ROOM — and see §3** |
| **"I don't recall"** | ⭐⭐⭐ **Available, and fatal to THEM.** A witness who does not recall **cannot supply the reasonableness finding they need.** Non-recollection leaves the documents standing alone |
| **"That is not what it meant"** | ⭐⭐⭐ **The weakest evidence in litigation** — the author of a contemporaneous plain-language email to colleagues, saying it does not mean what it says |

# 3. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE FIVE-WAY INTERSECTION — AND IT IS NEARLY EMPTY
**To help them, a witness must say something that is ALL of:**
1. not contradicted by an admitted fact;
2. not contradicted by **their own review decision, RD 69983**;
3. not contradicted by **their own pleading, Form 9C**;
4. capable of establishing **reasonableness**; and
5. **within their own knowledge.**
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Almost nothing survives all five.**

# 4. ⭐⭐⭐ WORKED — STRESSOR 3, THE SEVEN-HOUR BREAK
| Could a witness say… | Closed by |
|---|---|
| "the break was longer" | ⛔ **fact 258** — *"equated to 7 hours"*, their own decision |
| "he could have left early" | ⛔ **fact 259** — *"even if you were allowed to leave early… you still did not receive a minimum 8-hour break"* — **their decision already considered and rejected it** |
| "it complied with the award" | ⛔ **fact 260** — *"in direct contradiction to the award and the 8-hour agreement"* |
| "we did not know it was a risk" | ⛔ **fact 219** — *"Chloe and I have run thought this and we say at best there would be a rating of 11 which is moderate"* |
| ⚠ **"it was a one-off mistake"** | ⭐ **OPEN — 9C ¶40, *"human error… not intentional or repeated"*** |
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE ROOM ON STRESSOR 3 IS ONE SENTENCE WIDE.**
⚠ **And it is answered by Conaghan ¶11 (no consultation, no ballot on ANY roster change before
30 June 2024) and by *Delaney* — a departure their own decision calls a "direct contradiction" is
not a scheme reasonably implemented that miscarried.**

# 5. ⭐⭐⭐ WORKED — STRESSOR 2, THE PAY LOOP
⭐ **Facts 185–203 and 210 admit the whole loop. 9C ¶¶20–22 pleads *"investigated in a timely
manner"* and *"remedied"*.**
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **ROOM: one sentence — "it was investigated and remedied."**
⚠ **Against a documented loop: 8 Apr request → 9 Apr referred to myHR → 3 May Payroll to the
manager *"submit an AVAC"* → 13 May Payroll *"speak to your Line Manager"* → 21 May *"waiting
payroll confirmation"* → 28 May asked to validate claims older than three months.**
⇒ ⭐⭐ **The witness must explain why *"timely"* describes seven weeks of referral, and why only the
manager could submit the AVAC.**

# 6. ⭐⭐ WORKED — THE DATABASE RESTRICTIONS
⭐ **ROOM: "the restriction was for data integrity."** ⭐⭐ **That is their best available answer and
it is respectable.**
⚠⚠ **But it does not answer the DELAYS** — Harrison-Jones ¶2 (the monthly task undone across
~3 months), Conaghan ¶6 (*"left unactioned over extended periods"*), Conaghan ¶8 (2–8 May, **no
means of self-correction**).
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **Restriction is defensible. Restriction PLUS non-action is not.**

# 7. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE TOTAL ROOM — THREE SENTENCES
1. *"The break was a one-off human error."* — **9C ¶40**
2. *"The pay issues were investigated and remedied."* — **9C ¶¶20–22**
3. *"The database restriction was for data integrity."* — **unpleaded, but available**

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **ALL THREE ARE ALREADY ON THEIR PLEADING OR AVAILABLE AS SUBMISSION. NONE REQUIRES A
WITNESS TO SAY ANYTHING NEW.**
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THAT IS THE WHOLE POINT: calling a witness gains them three sentences they already have,
and exposes that witness to everything else.** ⭐⭐ **It is a bad trade, and it is why the
30 September list may be thin.**

# 8. ⛔⛔ SO HOW HE CROSS-EXAMINES — CLOSE THE THREE SENTENCES AND STOP
⛔ **Do NOT cross-examine to extract an admission of wrongdoing. They cannot and will not give one,
and the attempt is what makes an appellant look like *Kerr*.**
⭐⭐⭐ **Cross-examine to CLOSE each of the three sentences. Two to four questions each. Then sit
down.**

## 8.1 CLOSING "A ONE-OFF HUMAN ERROR"
> *"The Award provides a minimum ten-hour break unless there is a mutual agreement about regular
> rosters?"* → *"You and Ms Reese ran the Roster Risk Assessment Matrix?"* → *"You rated it at 11,
> moderate?"* → *"The roster was published in that form?"*
⛔ **STOP. Do not ask why.**

## 8.2 CLOSING "INVESTIGATED AND REMEDIED"
> *"On 3 May 2024 Payroll wrote to you, copying Mr Shepherd, asking you to submit an AVAC to correct
> these shifts?"* → *"On 13 May Payroll told him to speak to his line manager?"* → *"On 21 May you
> told him you were waiting on payroll confirmation?"* → *"Under the payroll process, only the
> manager could submit the AVAC?"*
⛔ **STOP.**

## 8.3 CLOSING "DATA INTEGRITY"
> *"From 18 July 2023 operators could not correct an entry themselves?"* → *"A correction had to
> wait on you or Ms Stibbard?"* → *"After hours it waited until one of you was back?"*
⛔ **STOP.**

# 9. ⭐⭐⭐ THE RULE FOR EVERY QUESTION
⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Never ask a question whose answer is not already on the admitted record.** ⭐⭐ **Never ask
"why".** ⭐ **Every question is a proposition from a document, put as a question, to which the only
available answer is yes.**
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **The witness is not a source of evidence. The witness is a person being walked past the
evidence in front of the Commissioner.**
