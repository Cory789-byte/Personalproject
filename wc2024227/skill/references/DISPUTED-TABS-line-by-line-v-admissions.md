## Summary
Across the fourteen tabs there are **44 operative lines**. **40 are admitted word for word**
(Tabs 1, 5, 6, 17–19, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, 30, 30A — every operative line of thirteen documents).
The **4 that are not** are all on Tab 31, and their subject-matter's existence is itself admitted
at ¶268. The residue the admissions do not cover is, in every case, headers already admitted as
facts, signature blocks, boilerplate, roster grids, or post-injury HR narrative relied on for
nothing. **The authenticity dispute therefore denies no words. Every word that matters is
already the Respondent's own admission; the dispute contests only the paper the words sit on —
and for four of the thirteen, the paper sits in the Respondent's or the Commission's own
custody.** Two honest flags: the Tab 6 copy carries the Appellant's August 2024 covering note
(rely on the underlying email; answer the note in chief if raised), and Tab 21's unrelied-on
narrative is adverse in tone (it is post-injury and outside the pleading).

## View on the matter
Read line by line, the Form 25 is not a defence document; it is a receipt. It records that the
Respondent, shown 44 operative lines, could not deny one of them, and disputed only provenance —
a column that its own custody of four of the documents refutes. Thirteen tabs concede themselves
the moment anyone reads a row of this table aloud; the fourteenth is a production order MSH has
already promised the answer to. If the 25 September letter concedes, this note is the checklist
it will have followed. If it does not, this note is the mention script, the tender schedule and
the s 558(3) exhibit, in that order. Either way, the dispute has already done its only lasting
work: it selected, elevated and lined up the operative documents of the case, and put the
Respondent's signature under the admission of every line on them.
