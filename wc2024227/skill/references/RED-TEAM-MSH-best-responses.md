# ⭐⭐⭐ THE ONE THAT MATTERS MOST — PS Act ch 3 pt 10 APPEALS
**Found while red-teaming this. It changes what "their best move" means.**

| Provision | Effect |
|---|---|
| **s 131(1)(d)** + **s 130 def** | Appeal lies against a **fair treatment decision** — *"a decision a public sector employee believes is **unfair and unreasonable**"* |
| **s 133(d)** | Who may appeal: *"a public sector employee **aggrieved by the decision**"* |
| **s 134** | Heard and decided **by the IRC** under IR Act ch 11 |
| **s 131(1)(f), (g)** | **Suspension without pay** and **transfer** decisions are separately appealable |
| **s 131(2)** | If appealable other than under (1)(d), it **cannot** be brought under (1)(d) |

⇒ **A fair treatment appeal is the broadest and cheapest route he has**, and it is available on a
decision he simply *believes* is unfair and unreasonable.

## ⛔⛔ BUT — TWO EXCLUSIONS, AND MSH CAN TRIGGER BOTH
> **s 132(4)** *"A person can not appeal against a fair treatment decision—*
> ***(a) made under chapter 3, part 8, division 5**; or …*
> *(e) relating to the resolution of a grievance under an industrial instrument, **other than a
> decision about the outcome of the grievance**…"*

**⚠️ s 132(4)(a) — division 5 IS ss 103–109, mental or physical incapacity.**
⇒ **If MSH regularises under div 5, the fair treatment appeal closes for those decisions.** Their
best legal move is also the one that shuts his cheapest door.
⭐ **But not every door:** a **s 107 transfer** remains appealable as a **transfer decision** under
**s 131(1)(g)** — s 131(2) simply routes it there instead of to (d).

**⚠️ s 132(4)(e) — running the cl 1.12 grievance narrows the fair treatment route** on those matters
to *the outcome of the grievance* only.
⇒ ⭐ **This is a second, independent, verified reason to hold the grievance.** The advice given on
1 August was right for different reasons; this one is stronger.

- [ ] ⏳ **VERIFY: the appeal time limit.** It sits in the Appeals directive, **which is not held.**
      Commonly 21 days from the decision. ⛔ **Do not act on 21 days until the directive is read.**
      **Obtain the directive this week.**
