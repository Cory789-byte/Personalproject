## 4.1 THE RULES FOR HIS CROSS

⛔ **Never ask "why."** · **Never ask a question that invites an explanation.** · **Never put
motive.** · **Never argue with an answer — take it and move on.** · **Never accuse.** An accused
witness attracts sympathy; a witness taken through her own documents does not.
⭐ **Short. Closed. Document-anchored. Every question answerable yes or no.**

# PART 5 — CLOSING (Guide 7.7)

**Address the elements of s 32, in this order:**

1. **Injury** — Krishnaiah.
2. **s 32(1) — a *significant* contributing factor.** ⛔ Never "major": the 2013 test was repealed
   in 2019. Competing causes need not be outweighed (*Adams* [2015] ICQ 1 [23], adopting *Hochen*).
3. **s 32(5)(a) — in the alternative, and this is the fight:**
   - ⭐ **(i) It is not management action.** A failure to assess a reported hazard is an **omission**.
     s 32(5)(a) excludes injury arising out of management action *taken in a reasonable way*.
     **There is no action to characterise** (*Read* [2017] QIRC 72; *Allwood* [2017] QIRC 88).
   - ⭐⭐ **(ii) If it is, it was not taken in a reasonable way:** no consultation · a 2020 casual-era
     instrument applied to a 2024 full-time roster without review · a safety question answered with
     a payroll answer · escalation to HR producing no assessment · **and no framework in existence
     to assess it with, on the employer's own written statement.**
4. ⭐ **A single unreasonable stressor suffices** — *Mahaffey* [2016] ICQ 10 [51], [54]–[57]. Not
   arithmetical, not restored by outnumbering. **Pleaded stressors define the enquiry** ([19]–[20]).
5. ⭐⭐ **And the Regulator's own delegate determined factor 4 amounted to unreasonable management
   action** — against a pleading that all management action was reasonable (SOFC ¶27).
   ⚠ **Persuasive, not binding** — the appeal is de novo.

⛔ **Do not present the composite thirteen-limb course.** That is *Delaney*, which is **their**
authority. **One mechanism, one admitted instance, five steps.**
