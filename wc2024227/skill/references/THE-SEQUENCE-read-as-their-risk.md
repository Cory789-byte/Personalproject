## 1. ⭐⭐⭐ The document that makes the chain a collision, not a complaint

**`documents/instruments/ATT14_AO3_Switchboard_Role_Description.pdf`** — the AO3 Administration
Officer, Switchboard Services role description. ⭐ **The contact named on it is Chloe Taylor.**

**What it requires of the occupant, verbatim:**

> ▪ *"**Collate information and maintain Omnivista database and SharePoint** to ensure information
> held within Switchboard Services is **accurate and appropriate**."*
> ▪ *"…**maintaining database and registers with accurate and current information**."*
> ▪ *"**Participate in the Emergency Response process** by receiving emergency response
> notifications and distributing them to the appropriate response groups… **strictly adhering to
> protocols and timeframes**."*
> ▪ *"Ability to communicate at all levels and effectively establish and foster networks throughout
> the hospital **to maintain accurate and current records for effective switchboard operations**."*
> ▪ *"Maintain discretion and **exercise judgement where necessary to resolve problems within the
> scope of your role**; in situations where precedence have not been set and procedures not
> defined."*
> ▪ *"**Follow defined service quality standards, occupational health and safety policies and
> procedures** relating to the work being undertaken to ensure **high quality, safe services and
> workplaces**."*

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **Three things are role requirements, in the employer's own instrument:**
1. **keeping the directory accurate**
2. **participating in the emergency response to protocol and timeframe**
3. **following the OH&S procedures to ensure safe services**

⇒ ⭐⭐ **So the patient-safety consequence is not a grievance he imported into a compensation appeal.
It is the stated purpose of the position they employed him in.** And **keeping a record** is not a
"burn book" — it is *"maintain accurate and current records"*, in the same document.

## 3. ⭐⭐⭐ Their risk, in order of seriousness

### 3.1 The chain inverts the causal order their defence needs

Their case requires: **he became difficult → management managed him → he perceived it badly.**

The admitted chronology runs the other way. **The first act is hers, it is admitted, and it is
eleven months before the character email.** ⇒ **Read chronologically, the May 2024 conduct they rely
on is the end of a sequence of admitted employer acts, not the start of anything.**

⇒ ⭐⭐ **Their best material stops being a cause and becomes an effect.** That is the single largest
risk in the sequence, and **it costs him nothing to run, because every link but the last is theirs.**

### 3.2 The role description contradicts their own characterisation — on their own paper

| Their document says | Their other document says |
|---|---|
| *"used as a **'burn book**' more than a professional tool"* (6 Jun 2023) | *"**maintain accurate and current records** for effective switchboard operations"* (RD) |
| the 15 May email was *"not… in the first instance"* and breached the Code | *"**Follow defined service quality standards, occupational health and safety policies and procedures**… to ensure high quality, **safe services**"* (RD) |
| *"behaviour and **malice** towards me"* (17 May 2024) | *"**exercise judgement where necessary to resolve problems within the scope of your role**"* (RD) |

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **Every characterisation of his record-keeping and his raising of problems is answered by the
duty statement for the position they put him in — a document on which their own manager is the named
contact.** ⚠ They must explain that collision with a witness, and **the only witnesses who can are
Taylor and Reese.**

### 3.3 ⭐⭐ Duty retained, means removed — and it is now an instrument point, not an adjective

The RD imposed the duty to keep the database accurate. **On 18 July 2023 the employer removed the
access required to perform it and left the duty where it was.**

⇒ **That is the pleaded mechanism — responsibility imposed, the means to discharge it withheld —
proved by two employer documents and an admitted fact, with no characterisation anywhere in it.**
⭐ It is also **far harder to answer than a stressor**, because there is no version of it that depends
on him.

### 3.4 ⭐⭐ It makes s 32(5)(a) expensive rather than easy

For the exclusion, they must show the action was reasonable **and taken in a reasonable way.**
The questions the sequence forces are all about their conduct:
- why was the duty left on the operator when the access was removed?
- what was done when the MASPER Registrar reported nine calls to the wrong team?
- what was done when a clinical service wrote *"we can not help patients or other clinical staff"*?
- ⭐ and on their own statements, **no safety assessment, investigation or change of procedure
  followed any complaint before 30 June 2024**.

⇒ ⚠ **Restricting database access is easy to defend. Leaving the accountability and the emergency-
response obligation on the operator while removing the means, and then treating his record of the
consequences as hostility, is not.** **That is a *manner* case, and manner is the limb they have to
meet.**

### 3.5 ⭐ The logging is role performance, and third parties corroborate it

*"What followed"* is him recording missed routes. ⇒ **The RD requires exactly that.** And the record
does not depend on his own notes: **the MASPER Registrar and the Integrated Respiratory Service wrote
it themselves**, to his manager, and those emails are admitted.

⇒ ⭐⭐ **Their risk: the log is corroborated by clinicians with no stake in this appeal, in documents
the Respondent has already admitted.**

### 3.6 ⚠ The ambiguity is a preparation problem for them

Topic 7 does not particularise: *"the concerns I raised on 7 August 2023 **and the responses**"*,
*"my complaint of 13 May 2024 **(content not set out)**"*, *"my interest in becoming the Switchboard
union delegate **and what followed**"*.

⇒ **They must outline responsive evidence by 4 pm 30 September against a case whose edges they cannot
see.** ⚠ **The same ambiguity carries his own cost** — *"and what followed"* invites a motive
inquiry, and motive is the doorway to s 32(5)(b), which they have not pleaded. **Net: a preparation
gain, a framing risk. Both real.**
