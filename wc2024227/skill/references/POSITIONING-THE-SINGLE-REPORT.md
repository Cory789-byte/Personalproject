# POSITIONING THE SINGLE REPORT — one document, two recipients, two purposes, both declared
> 15 August 2026. How the report is described, to whom it goes, in what order, and with what
> covering note. ⚠ It cannot be redacted: if it goes to the employer, all of it goes.

---

## PART 1 — ⭐⭐⭐ WHAT THE DOCUMENT IS
**A treating psychiatrist's report, obtained by the patient, answering the matters in issue in his
proceeding, and provided to his employer in answer to its request for medical information.**

⛔ **Not** *"an expert report for the appeal that also answers the RFMI."*
⛔ **Not** *"a capacity report that also does causation."*
⭐ **One report, one attendance, two recipients, two purposes — and both declared.**

## PART 2 — ⭐⭐⭐ THE POSITIONING RULE THAT MATTERS MOST
**Each recipient is told, in the covering note, that the other holds the identical document.**

⇒ It forecloses, before anyone thinks of it: two versions · selective provision · a tailored copy ·
a suggestion that the employer's copy differs from the one in the proceeding.
⭐⭐ **It costs one sentence and it removes an entire line of attack.**

## PART 3 — ORDER: SIMULTANEOUS
| Option | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Employer first | ⚠ A non-party holds a causation opinion about the employer's own rostering before the party to the proceeding does. Invites *"why them first?"* |
| Regulator first | ⚠ Proper, but delays the answer that restores wages — and the wages are the survival issue |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **Simultaneous** | **Correct.** The report was commissioned on one attendance to answer both. Sending it to both at once is simply what the document is. **No sequencing inference is available to anyone** |

## PART 4 — ⭐⭐ TWO COVERING NOTES, DELIBERATELY ASYMMETRIC
**Same document. Different function. Both true.**

### 4.1 To the Regulator — ONE LINE, NO ASK
> *"I enclose, by way of continuing disclosure, the report of Dr R B Krishnaiah dated [date]. The
> same report has been provided to Metro South Hospital and Health Service."*

⛔ **No argument. No commentary. No request. No explanation of what it establishes.**
⭐ **A report is evidence. It argues for itself, and anything added to it subtracts from it.**
⭐⭐ It is also **disclosure, not a request** — the one category of communication that carries no ask
and spends none of the standing the withdrawal bought.

### 4.2 To the employer — AN ANSWER THAT REQUIRES ACTION
Four elements, in this order:
1. **It answers the request of 31 July 2026.**
2. ⭐⭐ **The scope line** — the report addresses the matters raised; questions going to workplace
   arrangements rather than to medical capacity are identified as such in the report; if any
   clinical matter remains outstanding it can be put. *(This is what stops "you did not answer
   question 6 or 9" — see the crosswalk.)*
3. ⭐⭐⭐ **Their own policy, quoted** — QH-POL-210 / HR Policy G3, which they invoked on 31 July:
   unjustifiable hardship *"is tested against the whole organisation, not a division or unit"*, and
   *"the onus is on Queensland Health, as the employer, to prove an adjustment is unreasonable."*
4. **The ask:** that the adjustments identified be implemented, and that pay be restored.
   ⭐ Plus, folded in and not as a separate instrument: that the questions of **3 August 2026**
   remain unanswered and the decisions have not been allocated outside Corporate Services.
5. **And the same sentence:** the report has also been provided to the Regulator in the appeal.

## PART 5 — ⚠⚠ THE RISK, NAMED
**The employer will hold a causation opinion about its own rostering.**
⭐ On balance that favours him — the working record already puts it well: *"a causation opinion
sitting in MSH's hands, on MSH's own request form, is exactly what makes MSH want the appeal
resolved."*
⚠ **But the opposite is possible.** An employer told that its own management contributed to a
psychiatric injury may become defensive rather than accommodating. ⛔ **The alternative — withholding
it — leaves the exclusion running and the pay stopped.** The risk is accepted, deliberately, and it
is the price of the single-report decision made on 6 August.

## PART 6 — WHAT IT DOES TO THE APPEAL'S POSTURE
⭐⭐ It moves the matter from **unproved on causation** to **proved on the medical, contested on the
characterisation** — which is the only contest the Regulator has left, and the one where the CE
letter hurts her.
⚠ **Do not attach a settlement offer to it.** Let it land. A renewed Calderbank is a separate
instrument at a separate time, and the highest-leverage window is **after the report and before she
commissions an IME.**

## PART 7 — ⛔ THE FOUR THINGS THAT MUST NOT ACCOMPANY IT
1. ⛔ Any explanation of what the report establishes — to either recipient.
2. ⛔ Any reference to the parallel tracks, the disclosure, or the conflict letter as such.
3. ⛔ Any characterisation of anyone's conduct.
4. ⛔ Any settlement proposal, to either recipient, in the same communication.

## 8.1 ⭐⭐ THE NOTES ARE NOT IDENTICAL — AND THAT IS FUNCTION, NOT CONCEALMENT
**Disclosure carries no ask**, so the Regulator's note asks for nothing. **A medical answer to a
capacity request is useless unless it is acted on**, so the employer's note asks for implementation
and pay. ⇒ **Neither recipient is told anything the other is not.** The difference is entirely in
what is *requested*, which is the one thing that legitimately differs between them.

## 8.2 ⭐⭐⭐ THE LETTER OF INSTRUCTION AND THE SCHEDULE GO TO BOTH
That is the forthcoming posture, and **it is also the strongest available move.**
| | |
|---|---|
| The instruction shows on its face that the assumed facts are **the Regulator's own accepted facts and the employer's own documents** — not his account | ⇒ it **proves** the answer to their question 1(c) rather than asserting it |
| It shows what he **declined to ask** — no view on reasonableness, no view on accommodation, no view on any reporting line or person | ⇒ **restraint is visible**, and restraint is what makes an expert credible |
| It shows the schedule's **section B** — the matters the Regulator does *not* accept | ⇒ **nothing was curated** |

⭐⭐⭐ **An expert report whose instruction is disclosed is worth far more than one whose
instruction is withheld and then demanded.** ⇒ Disclosing it voluntarily, at the same moment as the
report, converts the single most attackable feature of any expert opinion into its strongest.
