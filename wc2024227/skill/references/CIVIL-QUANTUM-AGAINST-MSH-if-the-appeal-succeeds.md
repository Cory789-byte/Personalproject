## PART 0 — ⛔⛔ THE PREMISE HAS TO BE SPLIT BEFORE IT CAN BE COSTED

The question assumes one thing: *the appeal is overturned, and I am paid for the time off and the
part work.* **That is two different losses, paid by two different payers, under two different
mechanisms — and only one of them is delivered by the appeal.**

| The loss | What actually pays it | Does the appeal deliver it? |
|---|---|---|
| **The time off** — certified incapacity, substantially 18 Jun 2024 → 24 Feb 2025 | Weekly compensation from WorkCover | ✅ **Yes.** This is exactly what acceptance delivers |
| **The part work** — the reduced-hours period, 1 Mar 2026 → 28 Jun 2026 (0.7 then 0.5 FTE) | Depends entirely on whether the reduction is treated as certified partial incapacity or as an agreed variation he signed | ⚠ **Partly, at best.** He signed each movement form. That is the problem |
| **The exclusion** — no pay at all from 3 Jul 2026, 76.00 hrs coded "NP Sick Leave", zero gross | Wages. The employment track | ⛔⛔ **No.** Weekly compensation requires **certified incapacity**. He is certified **fit with restrictions**. You cannot claim compensation for a period you are certified fit to work |

⭐⭐⭐ **The single most important sentence in this file, carried forward from
`WHAT-ACCEPTANCE-ACTUALLY-DELIVERS.md`: the 2026 loss is a WITHHELD WAGES problem, not a
COMPENSATION problem.** It is recovered, if at all, through wages, the Award and certified
agreement, the grievance pathway, the Anti-Discrimination Act and the adverse-action provisions.
**Winning the appeal does not pay for it.** The two must never be run as one claim.

### ⛔⛔ AND THE SECOND PREMISE FAILURE — THE STATUTORY MONEY IS NOT ADDITIONAL

The instinct behind the question is *statutory payment, then civil damages on top.* **It does not
work that way.**

Where damages are recovered for the same injury, the insurer is repaid the compensation it has
paid out of those damages. **Statutory compensation functions as an advance against the damages
claim, not as a separate pot that stacks on it.** ⚠ **VERIFY the refund mechanism and its exact
scope in Chapter 5 WCRA before relying on this** — but plan on the assumption that it is true,
because it is the ordinary position and planning the other way inflates every number below.

⇒ **The correct question is not "statutory plus civil". It is: what is the total recovery, of
which the statutory payment is the first instalment?**

**Why the breach case is strong.** Responsibility imposed and the means to discharge it withheld,

and secure it; it is the foreseeability case.

## PART 3 — THE NETTING. WHY THE HEADS DO NOT ADD UP

Adding the bands gives a number in the high hundreds of thousands to over a million. **That number
is wrong, and anyone who quotes it to him is selling something.** Four deductions apply:

1. ⭐⭐⭐ **The compensation refund.** Statutory benefits paid on the accepted claim are repaid out
   of any damages for the same injury. The **$55–150k** the appeal delivers is an **advance**, not
   an addition. ⚠ Verify the mechanism.
2. ⭐⭐ **No double recovery on the injury.** Heads A, C, D and E all compensate substantially the
   same psychological injury and the same earning loss. **They are alternative routes to one sum,
   not four sums.** The clean separations are: Head B (wages for a period he was certified fit —
   a different loss entirely) and exemplary damages under Head E.
3. **The ART / income protection position.** The overpayment window 7 Apr 2025 – 31 May 2026 runs
   to roughly **$51–80.5k** depending on which scenario governs. ⭐ **The appeal is the instrument
   that pays that debt off** — WC arrears of ~$55–80k against an ART debt of ~$50–80k. Net cash to
   him after tax is nearer **$25–45k** than the gross figure, with the debt extinguished and the
   leave banks restored.
4. **Litigation risk and vicissitudes** — already inside the Head A bands, but they compound with
   the causation attack described above.

## PART 5 — WHAT MOVES THE NUMBER, IN ORDER OF LEVERAGE

1. ⭐⭐⭐ **The updated psychiatric report.** It sets the future economic loss figure, which is
   **~70% of the entire claim**. It is the single biggest value lever in the matter and nothing
   else comes close. ⛔ **Never suggest anything to the doctors and never pursue a re-diagnosis.**
   The report is worth what it is worth because it was not shaped.
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **Getting the election right.** The difference between a correctly-advised election and an
   accidental one is the difference between the central case and zero.
3. ⭐⭐ **The 10 May 2024 internal fatigue record.** Foreseeability is the element most likely to
   decide breach. Obtain it and secure it.
4. ⭐⭐ **Reading *Koehler* before anyone commits.** It may reduce the number. Better to know.
5. ⭐ **Speed on the appeal.** Not because of the general limitation period — Schedule 5 relieves
   that — but because the pre-court procedures cannot begin until acceptance, and they take many
   months. **This is a reason to force resolution, not to let it drift.**
6. ⭐ **Fixing the "clean psychiatric baseline" plea.** It is contradicted by his own records and it
   damages both tracks. It sits unserved and fixable.

## PART 6 — ⛔⛔ THE FOUR WAYS THIS BECOMES NOTHING

1. **Signing the Notice of Assessment.** One signature, claim gone.
2. **Lodging a notice of claim now.** **s 303** makes a premature notice affirmatively harmful, and
   **s 275(8)(d)** makes a complying one impossible before assessment anyway. ⛔ **Do not.**
3. **Missing the adverse-action limitation.** It is the shortest clock in the file and it has not
   been dated.
4. **Running the tracks as one claim.** The moment the 2026 wages loss is pleaded as compensation,
   or reprisal language enters the workers' compensation track, **s 32(5)(b)** opens from his own
   side. The Regulator has not pleaded it and does not need to if we hand it over.

## PART 8 — THE ANSWER IN FIVE SENTENCES

Winning the appeal delivers roughly **$55–150k** of statutory benefit, most of which discharges the
income-protection debt rather than reaching his pocket, and it does **not** pay for the exclusion
since July 2026. Its real value is that it opens **s 237** — and until it does, the damages claim
against Metro South does not merely fail, it does not legally exist. Once open, the realistic total
recovery is **$300–500k**, running to **$750k** on a strong psychiatric prognosis and past **$1M**
only if career loss is established on the *Robinson* model. The withheld wages since 3 July 2026 sit
outside all of that, are genuinely additive, and are currently around **$19k** and growing by
**$3.3k a fortnight**. **Everything above depends on two decisions — the election on the Notice of
Assessment, and the limitation date on the adverse-action head — and neither of them is a place to
be self-represented.**
