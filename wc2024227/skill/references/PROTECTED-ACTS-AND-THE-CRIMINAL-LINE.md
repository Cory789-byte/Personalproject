## PART B — THE LADDER: WHERE CIVIL BECOMES CRIMINAL (verified text)
**Rung 1 — invalid/unauthorised act.** Exclusion, pay cessation without an identified
instrument. Reviewable, remediable; not criminal.

**Rung 2 — civil contraventions.** IR Act s 285 adverse action (s 306 reverse onus);
WHS Act Pt 6 Div 3 civil proceedings; PID Act **s 42** (reprisal is a **tort**, damages
incl. exemplary damages); AD Act. Compensation, penalties, reinstatement.

**Rung 3 — OFFENCES. Three separate ones are already in range:**
- **WCRA s 232B** — "Within 12 months after a worker sustains an injury, the employer must
  not dismiss the worker solely or mainly because the worker is not fit for employment in a
  position because of the injury. **Maximum penalty—500 penalty units.**"
  ⚠ Note the limit: the injury is 18 Jun 2024, so the 12-month window has passed on the
  original injury date. It bites only if a fresh injury/aggravation date within 12 months
  is established. NOT presently available — flagged for accuracy, not for use.
- **WHS Act s 104** — "A person must not engage in discriminatory conduct for a prohibited
  reason. **Maximum penalty—1,000 penalty units.**" s 105 discriminatory conduct includes
  dismissal and "puts a worker to his or her detriment"; s 106(h)/(j) supply the prohibited
  reason (raising WHS concerns; seeking compliance). **s 104(2): only an offence if the
  prohibited reason was the DOMINANT reason.** s 107 extends it to anyone who requests,
  instructs, induces, encourages, authorises or assists it.
- **PID Act s 41** — "A person must not take a reprisal. **Maximum penalty—167 penalty
  units or 2 years imprisonment.** An offence against subsection (1) is an indictable
  offence." s 40: causing, **attempting or conspiring to cause** detriment because of a PID
  or involvement in a proceeding.

**Rung 4 — Criminal Code (Qld) s 92A misconduct in relation to public office** — a public
officer who, with intent to gain a benefit or cause a detriment, deals with information or
performs/fails to perform a function in abuse of the authority of the office. Reserved for
egregious cases; named for completeness only.

## PART C — WHAT ACTUALLY CONVERTS RUNG 2 INTO RUNG 3
Not severity. **The mental element.**
- WHS s 104: the prohibited reason must be the **dominant** reason.
- PID s 41: the detriment must be caused **because of** the disclosure/proceeding — and
  attempt and conspiracy are expressly included.
