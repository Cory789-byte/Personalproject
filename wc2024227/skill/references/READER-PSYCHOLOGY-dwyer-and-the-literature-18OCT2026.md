## The literature, mapped to Stressor 1
| Finding | Source | In the 9A |
|---|---|---|
| Decision-makers build a story; evidence in story order persuades more than issue or witness order | Pennington & Hastie, story model (1986, 1988, 1992) | Duty, then means removed, then repair path, then consequences at the console |

| Easy-to-process material is judged more credible; hard material disengages | Reber & Schwarz (1999); Alter & Oppenheimer (2009) | "My brain doesn't process it" → a three-line summary, short headings, citations at sentence ends |
