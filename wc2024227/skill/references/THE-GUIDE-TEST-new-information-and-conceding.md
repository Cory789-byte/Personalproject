## 2. The Guide's trigger, read closely

Three things the sentence does, and one it does not.
1. **The operative trigger is "new information ... not yet considered" — not the conference.** The
   conference is the narrative occasion ("following the conference"); the condition is the
   information. The Guide is permissive throughout ("may decide"), and §7.5 confirms settlement
   by agreement "at any time." ⇒ E25.2 holds on the Guide's own text: script, not mechanism.
2. **The Guide names element-by-element agreement as a conference purpose** ("whether any elements
   of s 32 ... can be agreed"). That is the narrowing note's home, and it is the Member's own
   described function ("review, refine or narrow down the issues").
3. **The de novo caveat cuts one way only.** "Previously accepted ... does not mean ... accepted at
   the appeal stage" protects the Respondent from its prior acceptances (Findings A/B/C are not
   binding — house rule). It does not oblige the Respondent to contest an element it has no
   evidence against.
4. What it does not do: it does not require a conference before concession, and it does not
   require the Respondent to wait for the Appellant to request one.

agreement."

## 7. The assessment, in one paragraph

The Guide's concession sentence is conditioned on new information the Respondent "may not have yet
considered." That condition was satisfied between 28 August and 9 September by material none of
which existed at the review or the pleading — including the Respondent's own 298 express
admissions, which r 49 fixes for the proceeding and gives no path to withdraw. Read against s 32
element by element, the Respondent now holds no evidence on the injury or on the employment
connection, a causation contention contradicted by its own produced record and its own delegate's
admitted findings with no expert served, and an exclusion plea whose witnesses it has not named
and cannot field uncaged. The Member narrowed the contest to causation on 7 August, fixed the
injury as a dated event, sealed the medical in March, and set a direction that obliges the
Respondent to write its case down on 30 September and the Appellant to convene the conference at
which the Guide says elements are agreed and positions reviewed. The rules permit concession by
consent at any time; the Guide describes it following the conference; the record leaves nothing
for the conference to teach. The 25th shows the register; the 30th shows the names and whether
any expert exists; the conference is where the Guide's own words say the rest happens if it has
not happened by letter first.

motive; "a significant" never "major"; nothing suggested to doctors; nothing outbound before the
25th; the 1 Oct letter is not optional.
