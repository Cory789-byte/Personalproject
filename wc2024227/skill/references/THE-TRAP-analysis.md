# 2. THE STRUCTURE OF THE BIND

## 2.1 What they have already said in writing
> **Forrest, 7 July 2026:** "the Health Service **does not have sufficient information to understand
> the nature of these matters or assess whether they present any psychosocial hazards or risks**
> that may affect your proposed return to work."

## 2.2 What the law says about that
> **WHS Regulation 2011 s 55C(1):** "A person conducting a business or undertaking **must manage
> psychosocial risks under part 3.1**."
> **s 55A:** a psychosocial hazard includes one arising from **"the design or management of work"**
> or **"workplace interactions or behaviours"**.
> **WHS Act s 47(1):** the PCBU **must consult** workers directly affected. **s 48(1)(b)(ii):** they
> must have a reasonable opportunity **"to contribute to the decision-making process"**.

## 2.3 ⭐ THE FOUR-WAY BIND — every branch costs them
| If MSH… | Consequence |
|---|---|
| **Produces a current assessment** | It is dated, disclosable and testable — **and a specialist can evaluate it clinically**, which is precisely what their own Q1(d) invites. If it identifies management of work as a hazard, the exclusion inverts. |
| **Produces one with no worker input** | **s 47/48 breach on the face of the document.** Consultation is mandatory and carries a penalty. |
| **Produces one that never considered s 55D(2)(d) or (h)** | The determination of controls did not have regard to matters the Regulation **requires** — systems of work and workplace interactions. |
| **Cannot identify when the last worker-input assessment occurred** | ⭐⭐ **The strongest branch.** *"We do not have sufficient information to assess"* is answered: **the information does not exist because the assessment was never performed** — and performing it is mandatory. |
| **Declines to answer at all** | A refusal to state whether a mandatory assessment exists, **to a worker excluded for five weeks on the basis of an unassessed risk.** Goes directly to reasonableness and to s 48(1)(d). |

## 2.4 ⭐ AND THE REQUEST ITSELF TRIGGERS A REVIEW OBLIGATION
> **Reg 38(2):** controls must be reviewed where **"(c) a new relevant hazard or risk is identified"**
> or **"(d) the results of consultation by the duty holder under the Act or this regulation indicate
> that a review is necessary"**.

⇒ **Raising the hazards in writing is consultation output.** It engages reg 38(2)(d) whether they
answer or not. **The obligation attaches on receipt.**

# 3. ⚠️ THE HARD PART — CAN THE ASSESSMENT BE DEPLOYED AT THE APPEAL?

**Less than hoped. Say so now rather than discover it later.**

## 3.1 The mismatch
- **WC/2024/227 concerns the injury of 18 June 2024**, and was rejected on the **s 32(5)** exclusion.
- **A 2026 psychosocial assessment describes the workplace in 2026.**
- ⚠️ **It does not prove what the workplace was in 2023–24.** A Commissioner will see that immediately.

## 3.2 What it *can* do in the appeal — indirectly but genuinely
1. ⭐ **Corroborates that the pleaded stressors are recognised workplace hazards, not perception.**
   The Regulator's SOFC does not admit the stressors. An assessment identifying complaint handling,
   rostering and line-management matters as **psychosocial hazards under a Code MSH must apply**
   makes them features of the work.
2. ⭐ **Feeds s 32(5)'s second limb** — management action must be reasonable **and taken in a
   reasonable way**. Evidence that the employer had no assessment, and no consultation, over a
   sustained period bears on *the way* it acted.
3. **Answers the pre-existing-anxiety line at SOFC ¶8** by locating the cause in work design rather
   than the person.
4. **Continuity of the same hazards across 2024–2026** is itself probative.

## 3.3 ⛔ WHERE IT MUST NOT GO
- **Not into the 64G**, which is about production of records and is filed.
- **Not raised at the 7 August mention.** Ordinary procedural machinery; volunteer nothing.
- **Not as a substitute for the medical causation case**, which is Report B and Dr Krishnaiah.

⇒ **Its real home is the employment track.** Treat any appeal benefit as a bonus, not the plan.
