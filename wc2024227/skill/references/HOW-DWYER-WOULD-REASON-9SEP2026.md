## ⭐⭐⭐ B.1 The aggregation runs on a defective set — four faults, all internal

1. ⛔ **Factor 1 was never substantiated**, yet the sequence proceeds as though three causative
   factors stood.
2. ⛔⛔ **Factor 2 was substantiated AGAINST him.** Verbatim, p 13: *"I find this factor is able to
   be substantiated **to the extent that you lodged a complaint, and some action was taken by
   management to review it, but NOT to the extent that there was no response to it**."* His
   complaint was that no action was taken. She found action **was** taken. **That is a rejection
   worded as a substantiation — and it was then counted in the "three causative factors."**
3. ⛔⛔⛔ **Factor 2's reasonableness rests on conduct AFTER the injury** — meeting attempts of
   **12 and 16 July 2024**, being **+24 and +28 days** after the pleaded onset of **18 June 2024**.
   ⇒ **s 32(5)(a) asks what the injury *arose out of*. An injury of 18 June cannot have arisen out
   of a meeting in July.**
4. ⛔ **The counting formula.** *"two out of the three… **mainly** arose out of such management
   action."* ⭐ *"Mainly"* is a **dominant-cause test**, which ***Adams*** [2015] ICQ 1 at [23],
   adopting ***Hochen***, rejected. The statute is *"**a** significant contributing factor"*.

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **Strip out the factor never substantiated, the factor found against him, and the post-injury
conduct that rescued it, and the arithmetic is: one factor made out adversely to the employer —
factor 4 — and she found it unreasonable.**

## B.2 And the global evaluation was never earned
Global evaluation is permitted **only** where actions are joined by **subject matter, time and
personality** — the threshold that protects the worker. ⛔ **It was recited and skipped.** Factors 2,
3 and 4 differ on all three: complaint handling vs directory access vs rostering; different actors;
separated in time.

## B.3 The authority set stops in 2009
The decision uses ***Bowers*** [2002], ***Delaney*** [2005], ***Prizeman*** [2005] and ***Rowe***
[2009]. ⭐ **Nothing later than 2009. *Adams* [2015] and *Mahaffey* do not appear** — the two cases
that answer the dominant-cause formula and the aggregation.
⛔ **Run this as an observation about the reasoning, never about who prepared it. Motive never
reaches paper.**
