# PART 1 — THE RESPONDENT'S CASE, PLAYED PROPERLY

Assume competent counsel. They do not fight the facts — they have already said so in writing.

## 1.1 Open by conceding, and re-framing
*"The Respondent admits the events. The question is not what happened. It is whether the employment
was a significant contributing factor, and whether the exclusion applies."* Everything then runs on
**characterisation**, exactly as the covering letter of 8 September reserved.

## 1.2 Attack s 32(1) — through the Appellant's own medical
Their strongest material is in **his** documents:
- ⭐⭐ The report of 13 February 2025 (Tab M4): *"**perceived** discrimination and unfair treatment in
  the workplace have eroded self-esteem"*; *"multiple life stressors, including relationship
  breakdown, job loss, and bereavement"*; *"treatment resistant depressive state"*; *"collateral
  information from his mother"*.
- ⭐ **"Perceived" is the single best word available to them, and it is written by his own treating
  psychiatrist.**
- The referral letter of 16 May 2024 (Tab M1) **renewing** a psychiatrist referral, with
  *"26/10/2022 ADHD"* and *"26/10/2022 Anxiety"* in the history.
- Submission: by early 2025 the illness is driven by perception, litigation and non-work events.

# PART 2 — THE COUNTER

## 2.1 Do not fight s 32(1) on their ground. Use their own decision.
Review Decision 69983, contents **admitted**: *"a personal injury of a psychological nature"*;
*"no pre-existing factor or condition"*; employment *"was a significant contributing factor"*.
**They rejected under s 32(5), not s 32(1).** They may run de novo, but they must explain why their
own reviewer, on the same medical, was wrong — **with no medical evidence of their own**, and a
records-only reviewer contradicting admitted facts 185–203 and 211–221 would be discredited.

## 2.2 Kill "perceived" with the admitted contemporaneous record
The word appears in a report of **13 February 2025**, written for QSuper. Against it, admitted:
- **28 June 2024** (Dr Slawinski): *"stress at work"*, *"upset by people not following rules"*
- **1 July 2024** (Dr Hawes): *"work stress"*, *"**they withhold pay at times, no overtime- not
  processed, manipulate his roster- so he works lates then earlies**"*

⇒ ⭐⭐ **Those are events, recorded before any claim decision, dismissal or proceeding — and the
Respondent has admitted they were recorded.** *Prizeman* is reality versus perception. **The reality
is admitted.**

## 2.3 Turn "human error" against them
*"Human error"* is an admission that it was **error**. s 32(5)(a) requires the action to be
reasonable **and taken in a reasonable way**. A breach of the employer's own mandatory minimum is
neither.

And *"not repeated"* is answered **without the arc**: the FRMS guideline went to Human Resources on
**20 May 2024** (facts 218–223) — after the March break — and Metro South has told the Commission
that fatigue risk management at the Switchboard was implemented only **after 30 June 2024**
(fact 264, admitted). ⭐⭐ **Nothing was in place before, and nothing changed until after the injury.**

## 2.4 The payroll counter — the real fight
Their answer is decent and must be met precisely:
- The email of 3 May named **four** defective fortnights (05.02, 19.02, 18.03, 01.04). **One**
  retrospective claim effective 30 March does not answer four.
- That one claim is *"**Part Completed**"* (fact 203) while **every other** AVAC in the period is
  *"Completed"* (fact 204).
- ⭐ **On 13 May — ten days after — their own payroll unit wrote: *"I cannot see that any of the
  issues below have been corrected"*** (fact 193).
- Facts 200, 201: every AVAC initiated by the manager; **none by him**.

⚠ **Honest limit: this proves delay and incompleteness, not that nothing whatever was done.** Plead
it that way and it holds. Overstate it and fact 203 breaks it.

## 2.5 ⭐ The distinction that may decide the case
The removal of database access and of the contact book are **conditions of work**, not management
action *directed at the worker* (**Granato**). If they are not management action, **there is nothing
for s 32(5)(a) to exclude**, and s 32(1) — already answered by their own reviewer — decides it.

⇒ This is the route on which **the appeal succeeds whoever bears the onus**.
