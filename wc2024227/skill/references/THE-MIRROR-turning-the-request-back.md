## The comprehensive strategic framing

# PART 1 — THE MASTER FRAME, IN ONE LINE

> **They asked: is he safe to be returned to the workplace?**
> **Every instrument asks: is the workplace safe to return him to?**

**That is the whole reversal.** It requires no allegation, no motive, and no adjective — only the
statute.

| Their question | The question the law asks | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| Is **he** medically fit to return? | Has the **PCBU** ensured, so far as reasonably practicable, the health and safety of workers? | **WHS s 19(1)** |
| Can **he** follow a lawful and reasonable direction? | Have **lawful and reasonable directions** been given — and under what instrument? | implied power; **six shifting bases, none identified** |
| Is **he** fit under the **existing reporting arrangements**? | Have the **existing reporting arrangements** been assessed as a psychosocial hazard? | **Reg 55A(a)(i), (iv)** — the ECC's stressor (4) is line management |
| Can **he** perform the role **without restrictions or modifications**? | What are the **genuine occupational requirements**, and what adjustment enables them? | **G03**; **AD Act s 34** |
| If **we can't accommodate**, can **he** safely return? | What accommodation was **assessed and found unreasonable**, against the **whole organisation**, on **whose onus**? | **G03 cl 2** — *"the onus is on Queensland Health"* |
| What **controls** are medically necessary? | What controls has the **PCBU** identified and implemented, in the **prescribed order**? | **Regs 34–36** |

# PART 2 — THE FOUR CONCERNS, EACH TURNED BACK ONTO ITS PROVISION

## 2.1 "Directives without consultation"
**Their frame:** can he follow lawful directions? *(Q2)*
**The mirror:** **consultation is not optional and it is not an adjustment. It is the precondition of
a direction being reasonable.**

> **WHS s 47(1):** the PCBU "**must**… **consult**… with workers… **directly affected by a matter
> relating to work health or safety**."
> **s 48(1)(b)(ii):** workers must have a reasonable opportunity ⭐ **"to contribute to the
> decision-making process"**.
> **Reg 38(2)(b):** controls must be reviewed **"before a change at the workplace that is likely to
> give rise to a new or different risk"**.
> **Code 2022 — POOR ORGANISATIONAL CHANGE MANAGEMENT:** *"**not consulting workers on changes in the
> workplace that affect them**… or genuinely considering their views."*
> **EB12 cl 7.1.11** — feedback on WHS matters. **cl 7.2** — psychosocial risk assessment.

⇒ **Evidenced:** Taylor, 15 Apr 2024 — *"Ellen and myself have added Afterhours on call…
**This new process is effective from today.**"* Against MSH's own 2022 roster change, circulated
repeatedly *"for Consultation"* over four months. **They know how. They chose not to.**

## 2.2 "Risks to the department"
**Their frame:** is *he* a risk? *(the exclusion)*
**The mirror:** **the risk the regulation is concerned with is a feature of the work.**

> **Reg 55A:** a psychosocial hazard arises from **"the design or management of work"** or
> **"workplace interactions or behaviours"**.
> **Reg 36(3)(b):** the hierarchy of control requires **"isolating the hazard from any person exposed
> to it"**. ⭐ **Removing the exposed person appears nowhere at any level.**
> **Reg 55D(2)(d):** controls must have regard to **"the systems of work, including how work is
> managed, organised and supported"**.

⇒ **The Code's hazard list contains no category called "the worker".** Every named hazard is
organisational. **So a control directed at the worker cannot be a response to any of them.**

## 2.3 "Patient safety is the core function"
**Their frame:** a medical question about one employee.
**The mirror:** **a s 19 question about the safety of workers *and other persons*.**

> **WHS s 19(1)–(2):** the PCBU must ensure the health and safety of **workers** and **"the health
> and safety of other persons is not put at risk from work carried out"**.

⇒ The unit routes **emergency and after-hours calls**, on a **24/7 continuous shift roster** — from
**their own Role Description**. Changing call routing without consulting the operators who action it
is a **clinical** risk, not merely an industrial one.
⇒ ⭐ **And this is his consistent position since 2024, not a litigation position:**
*"**Failure to consult putting patients at risk**"* (30 Aug 2024) · *"most critically, **patient
safety**"* (15 May 2024).
⚠️ **State as concern, never as leverage. Never attribute current risk to his own absence.**

## 2.4 ⭐⭐ "Not being able to speak up" — THE NEWEST AND SHARPEST ROUTE
**Their frame:** why has he raised so much? *(Q2's premise: "not aware of any concerns being raised")*
**The mirror:** **raising a work health and safety concern is a protected act, and detriment for it
is an offence.**

> **WHS s 105(1)(a):** discriminatory conduct includes where a person **"(iv) alters the position of a
> worker to the worker's detriment"** or **"(iii) puts a worker to his or her detriment in the
> engagement of the worker"**.
> **WHS s 106(h):** it is a **prohibited reason** if the worker *"**raises or has raised or proposes
> to raise an issue or concern about work health and safety** with— (i) **the person conducting a
> business or undertaking**… (iv) a health and safety representative… (vii) **another worker**…"*
> **s 104(1):** *"A person must not engage in discriminatory conduct for a prohibited reason."*
> **Maximum penalty — 1,000 penalty units.**
> **s 107:** the same penalty for anyone who **"request[s], instruct[s], induce[s], encourage[s],
> authorise[s] or assist[s]"** it.

⭐ **Note s 106(h)(vii): raising a concern with *another worker* is protected.** The 15 May 2024
all-staff email — the one Reese asked him to recall — is on its face the raising of a WHS concern
with other workers.
⚠️ **s 104(2): the prohibited reason must be the DOMINANT reason** — a higher bar than IR Act s 305
(*"include that reason"*). **The IR Act route remains the easier one. Record this; lead with s 285.**

# PART 4 — WHY THIS FRAMING IS THE RIGHT ONE

| It never | It always |
|---|---|
| Alleges bad faith | Cites a provision |
| Names a motive | States a duty |
| Says "bullying", "reprisal", "conspiracy" | Says **hazard, risk, control, consultation** |
| Asks them to admit anything | Asks them to **produce a document** |
| Depends on anything unproved | Rests on **their own words and the in-force text** |

he is.** He complied with the 5 May request. He booked the appointment. He produced the certificate.
He asked *"confirm I'm right to attend"*. He has been raising consultation as a **patient safety**
issue since 2024. **He is asking to participate in the work, not to be excused from it.**
