**11 September 2026 · worked from the served Form 24, admitted 8 September 2026. ⛔ INTERNAL.**

---

# 1. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ AN EMAIL CAN PLAY FIVE DIFFERENT ROLES, AND THEY ARE NOT THE SAME THING

| Role | What it is | What it proves | Can a witness weaken it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **1. THE ACT** | the thing is *done in the words* — a direction, instruction, refusal, approval, removal | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **that the act occurred, and its terms** | ⛔ **No.** There is nothing behind it to contradict |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **2. THE SELF-SET STANDARD** | the actor publishes the rule they will be measured by | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **the yardstick — in their own words, to an audience** | ⛔ **No.** They chose it |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **3. THE CONTEMPORANEOUS CONFIRMATION** | the actor, or a neutral unit, records the state of affairs at the time, for its own purposes | ⭐⭐⭐ **what was known, by whom, on that date** | ⚠ Only by explanation, never by denial |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **4. THE FINISHED ACT** | the event that closes the sequence | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **the date the thing was finally done** | ⛔ **No** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **5. THE ABSENCE** | no document — and **the adversary's own list says so** | ⭐⭐⭐ **that no other explanation is on the record** | ⚠ Only by producing the document, which is then late |

⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE ANSWER TO THE QUESTION: they are not "confirmations by people" in the sense of
witnesses confirming something afterwards. They are the people DOING and RECORDING, at the time,
for their own purposes, with no idea it would ever matter.** ⇒ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **A witness confirms in
retrospect and can be tested. These cannot be tested, because they are not testimony — they are
conduct, and the contemporaneous record of conduct.**

# 4. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE DIVISION OF LABOUR — WHAT EACH KIND OF PART ESTABLISHES

| The part | Establishes | s 32(5)(a) element it goes to |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The acts** | **what was done, and when** | that there *was* management action |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The confirmations** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **what was known, by whom, at each point** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **the manner — and knowledge is the whole of manner** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **The intervals** | **how long** | the manner |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **The absences** | **that no other explanation is on the record** | forecloses the answer |

⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Conduct, knowledge, duration, and the exclusion of alternatives — and not one witness is
needed for any of it.**

## 4.1 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ SO WHAT IS LEFT FOR A WITNESS?
⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Only WHY.** ⇒ **That is the only ground the Respondent has, it is the ground it reserved
in terms on 8 September (characterisation and inference), and it is the ground on which
cross-examination is conducted.**
⭐⭐⭐ **A witness who must explain why cannot start by disputing what — because what is admitted.**
⇒ **They begin the hearing having already conceded the facts, in a forum where the appellant bears
the onus.**

# 5. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE THIRD SHAPE — WHERE AN EMAIL IS THE ACT *AND* THE CONFIRMATION AT ONCE
**Fact 221, Ms Reese to Mr Mackenzie Pritchard of Human Resources, 10 May 2024:**
> ⭐⭐⭐⭐ *"**Cory's roster will not be considered**, however **Cory is not yet aware of this** as we
> cannot advise what is the roster we are planning on going with before we send out the
> implementation plan for this business case for change."*

**And immediately before it, fact 220:**
> ⭐⭐⭐⭐ *"**I acknowledge there has been a few rostering errors made by Chloe** with regards to
> Cory's line in past rosters"*

⭐⭐⭐⭐ **In one email: a decision (the act), an admission of error (the confirmation), and a
statement that he has not been told (the confirmation of the state of his knowledge).** ⇒ **Three
roles in two sentences, internal, to HR, written for their own purposes, and admitted.**
⛔⛔ **It is never characterised. It is quoted, dated, and left alone.**

# 6. ⛔⛔ THE RULE THAT MAKES ALL OF IT WORK
⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Every part is written as a primary fact: an actor, a date, a document, and what it says.**
⛔⛔ **The moment a part is written as what it MEANS, it becomes an evaluative proposition, and it
is refused — as paragraphs 48 and 49 of the first notice were refused.**
⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The loops close because nothing in them was ever asked to be admitted except what the
documents literally say.**
