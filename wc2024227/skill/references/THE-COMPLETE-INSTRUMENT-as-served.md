# The complete instrument, as served — de-hedged (14 Sep 2026)

**The register of this file.** Everything in §§1–7 is verified against the served PDFs in
`documents/served-2026-09-09/` and the Form 24/25 responses. It is stated as fact because it is
fact — the documents exist, they are metadata-clean, they were served on 9 September 2026, and
their contents are fixed. The ONE inference in the whole picture is the Regulator's internal
decision (defend vs concede), isolated in §8. Nothing else here is a guess.

Two legal-accuracy notes, stated once and not repeated as hedges: (a) a tribunal has not yet made
any finding — "the case is built" means undismissable on the served record, not that the result is
entered; (b) concession is the *rational* move engineered by this instrument, not a decision
observed. Neither qualifies a single fact below.

These four instruments (2–5) are one instrument in four parts. Read together they leave a

## 2. The admissions — the foundation

The Form 24 response of 8 September admitted **298 of 303 facts and denied none**. The Form 25
disputed the authenticity of 14 annexure tabs by a provenance column ("copy from the Appellant's
own records") and admitted every Respondent-produced tab. That split is not a judgment about any
document; it is a mechanical rule. **You cannot admit a document's contents word-for-word and
believe the document is fabricated** — the 298 admissions are the Regulator's own certificate that
the record is genuine. The disputes die at one question ("does the Regulator allege fabrication?"),
whose only truthful answer is no.

## 3. The acts — verified, and what the admission does with them

The letter of 9 September (§2) fixes the footing on the record, in terms:

> "Where the document is itself the step taken — a roster as published, a directive as issued by
> email, a payroll instruction or claim as recorded, a request as made and the reply as given, each
> on the date recorded — the Appellant relies on the admission as establishing that the step was
> taken on that date in those terms."

The facts relied on **as acts** are named: **49, 66, 74–77, 81** (the directives and replies);
**182–205, 210** (the payroll thread and the myHR report); **211–223** (the rostering
correspondence); **263–268** (Metro South Health's own statements to the Commission); and **56–68,
89–104** (the MASPER Registrar's reports of 3 and 8 May 2024 and the Integrated Respiratory
Service's of 15 and 20 May 2024 — each report that calls reached the wrong team or that "we can not
help patients or other clinical staff", made on the date and in the terms recorded, with the
replies given on their dates).

An **act does not need its author on the stand.** The admission establishes the step was taken. So
these facts are proved now, on paper, without a witness — the roster was published, the directive
was issued, the report was made, on the dates and in the words admitted.

To rebut the outline the Respondent must call the actors behind the admitted acts — Taylor, Reese,

is an act conceded; every name they call is a caged witness.

## 6. The medical schedule — element (d) closed both ways

Element (d) — causation, s 32(1), employment "**a significant contributing factor**" (never
"major", never outweighing) — was the Respondent's only live element after the conduct limb closed
on the admissions. The schedule closes it:

- **Affirmative build:** clean pre-morbid baseline (no prior psychological injury — no competing
  pre-existing condition); work-timed onset at 18 June 2024 with the shift/console conditions as
  mechanism; physiological corroboration (the melatonin/sleep thread — contemporaneous treatment,
  not testimony); an unbroken referral-and-certificate spine; the treating records at schedule rows
  1, 4 and 7 keyed to admitted facts; and the treating opinion behind it. The letter states **"No
  report has been prepared for the purposes of this proceeding"** — element (d) runs on the treating
  records plus the oral evidence of Krishnaiah and Hawes, both called.
- **Foreclosed counter:** the only respondent move on (d) is the competing-cause argument. The one
  candidate (the relationship breakdown) is defeated by chronology if it post-dates the 18 June
  onset — sequela, not cause — and by the statutory test regardless, since a competing stressor does
  not defeat the claim unless it displaces employment as significant. ⛔ It stays **outside the
  case**: never led, never put in issue; the prepared answer if opened is minimal and chronological.
  The DFV material (AttA–AttD) is not opened and is not needed.

## 7. The Dwyer contact — immediate pressure, not the foundation

The request letter invokes the 7 August mention, in terms:

> "At the mention on 7 August 2026 the Commissioner indicated that, for a document held by Metro
> South Health, the starting point is to ask the Regulator to make inquiries and produce it, and that
> non-party disclosure follows only if the Regulator does not. This letter is that step, put as a
> choice so that the matter can be closed without an application, and before the Respondent's
> material is due on 30 September 2026."

This is compounding pressure, not the case. It puts maintaining the disputes squarely against the
case-managing Member's own prescribed process, sets an 18 September deadline, and reserves NNPD and
costs — so the cheap institutional move is to concede the tabs rather than defend a provenance rule
before the Member who set ask-first. The foundation is the admissions and the schedule; the Dwyer
step just makes the clock immediate.

## 8. The whole instrument, read together — and the one thing not known

A respondent reading the 9 September package faces this, all of it fact:
- the conduct limb closed on 298 admissions + the negatives + the CE letter;
- the acts proved on paper without witnesses;
- the disputed tabs inert, each holdable independently, dying at one question;
- element (d) built on a clean baseline and the treating doctors, its only counter foreclosed;
- their entire defence roster caged and named back to them as the cross list;
- the Member's own process invoked with an immediate clock.

There is no element left to defend and a dignified frame (conditions of work) in which to concede.
Constructing any defence outline runs through MSH — instructions, the war-game of who survives the
cage — and the war-game's answer is the caged roster itself. That is why the rational reading is
"we will likely lose."

**The one inference, isolated:** whether the Regulator has *decided* to concede. That is not on the
record and cannot be until the 25 September letter (the register) and the 30 September filed list
(the decision — MSH officers named = defending; absent = not). Everything above is settled. Only
that is pending.
