# 1. ⭐⭐⭐ THE ORDINARY MANNER CONSIDERATIONS
⚠ **Described, not cited. ⛔ Pull and read the Queensland authorities before any of this is put on
paper.** Whether the person was **told** of a decision affecting them · whether they were
**consulted** before it · whether they had an **opportunity to respond** · the **timeliness** of
the response to what triggered it · whether the response was **proportionate** to what was raised ·
the **channel and audience** · whether the employer followed **its own stated standard** · whether
the **stated reason** matches the documented one · whether the decision was taken by the person the
employer's own instrument **authorised**.

# 3. ⛔⛔ THE SEVEN FEATURES — FOR HIM ONLY, NEVER FOR THE PAGE
⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Across the admitted record the same seven manner features recur, each proved by documents
and none requiring motive:**
1. **Departure from the employer's own written standard** — 8, 4, 7, 15
2. **A decision taken, and the person not told** — 14, 13
