# 1. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ THREE DIFFERENT INSTRUMENTS — DO NOT CONFUSE THEM

| | What it is | How it binds | How hard to escape |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐ **r 49 admission** *(what he has)* | his notice, their answer | admitted **"for this proceeding only"** | ⭐⭐ **leave required to withdraw** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Statement of agreed facts** *(what he would propose)* | a **joint** instrument, signed by both, tendered as one document | the Commission works from it | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **practically much harder — a considered joint act, not a response to a notice** |
| ⭐⭐ **Statement of issues in dispute** | a case-management document | **not evidence at all** | ⭐⭐⭐ **but it defines the hearing** |

## 1.1 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ WHY CONVERTING THE FIRST INTO THE SECOND IS WORTH DOING
1. ⭐⭐⭐ **One instrument replaces 303 numbered paragraphs and a 44-paragraph response.**
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **It forecloses any later argument about the scope or effect of the 8 September response.**
3. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AND IT CANNOT COHERENTLY RESERVE "TRUTH".** Their cover letter reserved *truth,
   relevance, admissibility, weight, characterisation and inference*. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **A party cannot agree
   a fact and reserve its truth.** ⇒ **Proposing an agreed statement forces that word out — and
   costs him nothing, because relevance, weight, characterisation and inference are matters for the
   Commission in any event.**

# 2. ⚠⚠ THE RISK, STATED HONESTLY
⛔⛔ **A proposal that ENUMERATES what is closed teaches them their own position.** ⚠ They may not
yet have worked out that the admissions close seventeen matters, or that four February denials are
contradicted by September admissions. ⇒ **Enumeration invites an application to amend the SOFC or
to withdraw admissions.**
⭐⭐⭐ **Leave to withdraw an admission gets harder the later it is sought and the more the other
party has relied on it.** ⇒ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **DELAY FAVOURS HIM. Every week the admissions stand unremarked
is a week of reliance.**

## 2.1 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE DRAFTING ANSWER — PROPOSE THE MECHANISM, NOT THE SCHEDULE
⭐⭐⭐⭐ **He proposes the two documents and asks the RESPONDENT to state the issues that remain.**
⇒ **The labour flips.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Whatever they state becomes the hearing: state it narrowly and the
narrowing is won; state it broadly and the breadth sits against their own admissions — visibly,
without him pointing at it.**

## 2.2 ⭐⭐⭐ AND OFFERING TO DRAFT IS SAFE
⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The teaching risk is the ANALYSIS, not the facts.** The facts were served on 28 August;
they have had them for a fortnight. ⭐⭐⭐ **A statement of agreed facts contains facts only, in
date order, with no loops, no contradictions identified and no argument.** ⇒ **Offer to prepare the
first draft — whoever drafts, frames.** ⛔ **Chronological, never grouped by stressor.**
