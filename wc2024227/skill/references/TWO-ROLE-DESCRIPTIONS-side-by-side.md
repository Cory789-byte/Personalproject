## What the pairing does
1. **The audit duty meets Tab 31.** Her role required monitoring and auditing of *all emergency
   code groups and responses*, with regular reports to committees. The Emergency Code Register
   is exactly the artefact that duty produces — which is why MSH could say it "is available."
   And the misdirected MET calls of May 2024 (¶¶56–68) are squarely inside the duty to monitor
   responses. The List discloses no committee report; the SOFC alleges none; the CE says
   complaints were handled "solely via email or verbally" (¶267). **Either the audit reports
   exist and are unlisted, or the audit duty was not performed. Both are theirs to explain.**
2. **The sole-channel chain gets its second document.** ¶267 said the whole complaints system
   was the line manager. Her RD confirms the design: she is "the central point of
   communication." The design is theirs; the absence of hours, backup and escalation around
   that central point is the pleaded condition.
3. **The rostering system was hers to develop and monitor** — read with ¶260 (the reviewer's
   unreasonableness finding), ¶220/¶287 (Reese's "errors" emails), and ¶¶167–168 (the Award's
   own consultation mechanism). The pleading never needs to say "she failed"; the documents
   sit next to each other.
4. **The identical WHS sentence** in both role descriptions removes any suggestion that safety
   duties sat only below: the same sentence bound the Manager.

document in this proceeding, so refusing authentication is impossible — but that is exactly why
it buys nothing: the tender path is already unimpeachable (produced by MSH, Item 6;

discover. And it would break the quiet period (nothing to them before the 25th).
