# ORDERING THE TRANSCRIPT AND AUDIO OF EVERY LISTING
> 8 August 2026. Verified against the **QIRC Workers' Compensation Appeal Guide v2.10** (Parts 5.2,
> 7.4, 11), **r 91 Industrial Relations (Tribunals) Rules 2011** (ATT27, p 61), and the Queensland
> Courts / QTranscripts published material.

---

## 1. ⭐ AUDIO IS AVAILABLE — not only the transcript

**QTranscripts is the platform for requesting transcripts *and* audio recordings** of Queensland
court and tribunal proceedings, including the QIRC. **Parties and their representatives may order
as of right.** Cory is a party.

⛔ **He may not record a proceeding himself.**
- **r 91 IR (Tribunals) Rules 2011:** *"A person who is not a recorder under the **Recording of
  Evidence Act 1962** must not use a recording device in a proceeding unless the court, commission
  or registrar allows it."*
- **Appeal Guide, Part 11:** *"Do not record proceedings (audio or visual). If applicable, a
  transcript of proceedings will be supplied to the parties."*

⇒ **QTranscripts is the only lawful route.**

## 2. HOW TO ORDER

**Online (preferred)**
1. **qtranscripts.justice.qld.gov.au**
2. **Log In → "Continue with Digital Identity."** A **Digital ID / QGov** account is required —
   register at identity.qld.gov.au if he does not have one.
3. Place the order; track it on the same platform.
4. On completion a **secure download link is emailed. It expires after 30 days** — download
   immediately.

**Offline (if the Digital ID cannot be created)**
- **Request for Transcript** form — from courts.qld.gov.au, or by phoning **1800 842 122**
  (Mon–Fri, 8:30am–4:00pm).
- Lodge at a courthouse, or post to: **Recording and Transcription Services, Department of Justice,
  PO Box 15167, City East QLD 4002.**

**What to have ready:** matter number **WC/2024/227**; the **date and time** of each listing; the
Commission and the Member (Commissioner Dwyer); the parties; whether it was in person or by
telephone; and whether the whole listing or only part is wanted.

**Turnaround options:** **same-day, 1, 3, 5, 10 or 20 days.**
⚠ **Order early.** The QIRC has published a notice that turnaround times are longer than usual
following the **voluntary administration of VIQ Solutions Pty Ltd**.

## 3. ⚠ COST — TWO SOURCES CONFLICT. CONFIRM WITH THE REGISTRY BEFORE ORDERING

| Source | What it says |
|---|---|
| **Appeal Guide 7.4 and 5.2** | *"Parties to matters before the Commission may obtain copies of the transcript **free of charge**."* |
| **Queensland Courts fees schedule** | Transcript already in existence — **Industrial Relations Commission: $5.40 per page**; additional copies $1.20/page. **Audio recording: $45.25 per hour (or part thereof)** |

⛔ **Do not assume it is free.** At $5.40 a page a single substantial mention can run into the
hundreds, and five listings of audio at $45.25 an hour is real money for someone with no income
since 13 July 2026. **Ask the Industrial Registry, in writing, what a party is charged in a
workers' compensation appeal — before placing any order.**

⭐ **AND APPLY FOR THE HARDSHIP WAIVER.** The published position is that a **party experiencing
financial hardship may obtain a full or partial fee waiver** on an existing transcript or
recording. **He plainly qualifies to apply.** Do this in the same enquiry.

### 4.1 ⭐⭐⭐ Why 7 August is the priority

It is the only record of:
- the **unprompted pay-limb concession** — a credibility act, made against his own interest,
  before being pushed, in front of the employer;
- the **item-by-item necessity walk**;
- the **cross-examination mechanic** and where the burden of contradiction falls;
- *"that's just management action"* and the answer to it.

⭐ **It converts `MENTION-7AUG2026-RECOUNT.md` from `[R] recall only` into a citable record** — and
until it does, ⛔ **nothing about 7 August may be asserted in any external document** (FULL-PICTURE
§17.8).

### 4.2 ⚠ THE 13 MARCH CONFERENCE — order it, but handle it differently

**Guide 5.2:** conferences *"will usually be recorded and transcribed… and **only the parties
involved are able to request a copy**."* So it is available.

⛔ **But two limits:**
1. *"The Member may also decide to **'go off the record'** and go into 'private conference'…
   **this is not recorded or transcribed**."* **Any private session simply does not exist on the
   record.**
2. A s 552A conference is a **settlement conference**. Obtaining it for his own record is one
   thing; **deploying its contents is another and must not be done without care.** And the
   conference Member is not the hearing Member (Guide 7.1) — see
   `PROCEDURAL-ROADMAP-APPEAL-GUIDE.md` §1.1.

## 5. ⭐ TRANSCRIPT OR AUDIO? — order the transcript first

| | Use |
|---|---|
| **Transcript** | ⭐ **The citable record.** This is what goes into submissions, a costs argument, or a chronology |
| **Audio** | Tone and emphasis — **neither of which is evidence and neither of which can be cited.** Its one real use: **proving the transcript is wrong** on a point that matters |

⇒ **If money is tight: transcript only, 7 August only, longest turnaround.** Add the other
listings and any audio afterwards, and only if the hardship waiver comes through.
