# The Harrison "discretion" letter (15 July 2026) — why the logic is misconceived (research note)

> STATUS: research-grounded working analysis (web-verified 15 Jul 2026). For the UNION BRIEF and
> strategy only. NOTHING here goes into correspondence with Harrison/MSH (no reply-war) and nothing
> touches the WC/64G track. Sources listed at foot; pinpoint-verify any provision before external use.

## Her syllogism, unpacked
1. WCRA rehab/suitable-duties obligations attach only to ACCEPTED claims (narrowly TRUE).
2. Therefore MSH owes Cory no return-to-work obligations (FALSE — non sequitur).
3. Therefore his return is "at the discretion of the Employer" (FALSE — the discretion is
   structured/constrained on at least five fronts).
4. Pay sits with QSuper, not MSH (FALSE — category error).
5. His condition may be a "psychosocial hazard" justifying exclusion "in any capacity" pending
   clarification (INVERTS the WHS framework it borrows its vocabulary from).

## Front 1 — Anti-Discrimination Act 1991 (Qld) + the 1 July 2025 POSITIVE DUTY
- Impairment is a protected attribute; work is a covered area. Blanket exclusion of a worker with a
  treating doctor's fit-with-restrictions certificate, based on UNPARTICULARISED assumed risk, with
  no individualised assessment and no timeframe, is the classic less-favourable-treatment /
  failure-to-adjust pattern. Workplace-health defences require EVIDENCE, not speculation.
- Respect at Work and Other Matters Amendment Act 2024 (Qld): from **1 JULY 2025** duty holders owe a
  POSITIVE DUTY to take reasonable and proportionate measures to ELIMINATE discrimination as far as
  possible (broader than the federal SDA duty; QHRC enforcement powers). MSH's "discretion" is being
  exercised INSIDE a period where the positive duty is live. (Note: the separately-proposed express
  reasonable-adjustments duty was deferred — the adjustment analysis still runs through direct/
  indirect discrimination + QH's own policy, below.)
- Human Rights Act 2019 (Qld) s 58: MSH is a public entity — decisions must be compatible with human
  rights and give proper consideration to them (equality/non-discrimination, privacy). "Discretion"
  exercised without that consideration is unlawful in the public-law sense.

## Front 2 — QH'S OWN POLICIES structure the "discretion" (the strongest practical point)
- **QH-IMP-401-5 Workplace rehabilitation standard** (under QH-POL-401 Health, Safety & Wellbeing
  policy): rehabilitation is provided as a priority to accepted-claim workers, **"may also [be
  provided] to employees without a current accepted workers' compensation claim... where this is
  operationally reasonable"** — graduated RTW plans, temporary adjustments, suitable duties;
  managers responsible for identifying suitable duties "where operationally reasonable."
- **QH-POL-210 Reasonable adjustment (HR Policy G03)**: a standing QH policy on adjustments for
  employees incl. disability/health conditions.
- So the space Harrison calls bare "discretion" is occupied by HER OWN EMPLOYER'S published
  instruments, which require a considered, individualised, operational-reasonableness assessment —
  not "no duties in any capacity, no timeframe." A public authority that fails to apply its own
  policy without reason acts unlawfully/unreasonably in the administrative-law sense; industrially,
  the certified agreement + these policies are the union's first exhibit.

## Front 3 — the "Health Management processes" track is NOT free-form
- PS Act 2022 **ch 3 pt 8 div 5 (mental or physical incapacity)** applies ONLY when the s 103
  gateway is met: (a) employee ABSENT from duty (or CE reasonably satisfied duties not performed
  satisfactorily) AND (b) CE REASONABLY SUSPECTS the absence/performance is CAUSED by mental or
  physical illness/disability. Governed by **Directive 04/24 (Independent medical examinations)** +
  **QH-POL-170 (IMEs of employees)**: statutory-power exercise, natural justice, and (per the
  directive framework) redeployment/retirement decisions must align with the AD Act and HRA.
- THE GATEWAY PROBLEM (their problem): Cory is not absent by choice or incapacity — he is absent
  because MSH DIRECTED him out while certified fit. A precondition MANUFACTURED by the employer's
  own exclusion decision is a fragile foundation for the track, and "reasonable suspicion" that
  illness prevents duties must contend with the treating doctor's contrary certificate.
- Commitment stated in the framework: supporting employees to MAINTAIN employment incl. reasonable
  adjustment. The track's stated purpose is retention-first, not exit-first.

## Front 4 — "pay sits with QSuper" is a CATEGORY ERROR
- An income-protection insurance benefit is not wages. The wage obligation arises from the contract,
  Award, and certified agreement and is untouched by the existence (or here, CLOSURE) of an IP claim.
- No general right to suspend without pay: where the employee is READY, WILLING AND ABLE and the
  EMPLOYER prevents performance without a lawful stand-down/suspension basis, the wage obligation
  continues (Automatic Fire Sprinklers v Watson [1946] HCA 25 line: "no work no pay" answers employee
  non-performance, not employer prevention on a subsisting contract). Public-sector suspension is
  ordinarily ON remuneration. No stand-down power has been invoked or identified by MSH.
- Charging SICK LEAVE to an employee who is certified FIT and being involuntarily held out is
  contrary to the leave's purpose (sick leave compensates incapacity, which on their own file he
  does not have).
- Certificate-rejection law cuts against them: an employer may question a certificate that is VAGUE
  (Gadzikwa v DHS line) — the ECC is the opposite of vague: a structured capability instrument
  completed by the treating doctor. Seeking clarification may be reasonable; doing so with no
  timeframe while unpaid exclusion continues is where it fails.

## Front 5 — the psychosocial-hazard vocabulary INVERTS the framework it borrows
- WHS Act 2011 (Qld) + WHS Regulation psychosocial provisions + the 2022 Code: the duty is to
  MANAGE psychosocial risks arising from WORK DESIGN AND MANAGEMENT SYSTEMS via risk assessment,
  the hierarchy of controls, and MANDATORY CONSULTATION with affected workers (WHS Act ss 47–49).
- Blanket exclusion "in any capacity," decided BEFORE the information said to be needed is sought,
  with no risk assessment, no consultation, and no consideration of controls short of total
  exclusion, is not risk management — it is risk AVOIDANCE performed on the worker instead of the
  work. And the indefinite unpaid exclusion of a recovering worker is ITSELF a psychosocial hazard
  (loss of job control/security) under the same Code.
- THE INVERSION (dated): 2023–24 Cory raised the actual psychosocial hazards (fatigue, pay failure,
  information withheld from the Switchboard) → duties unperformed (grievance dismissed same-day
  7 Aug 2023; comm-book removal 6 Jun 2023) → injury (18 Jun 2024) → Jul 2026: first energetic use
  of the framework's vocabulary is AGAINST the injured worker.

## Front 6 (reserve) — general protections, IR Act 2016 (Qld)
- Adverse action (incl. injuring the employee in employment / altering position to their prejudice)
  because the employee HAS or EXERCISES a workplace right (WC claim + appeal, complaints, PID) is
  prohibited; **REVERSE ONUS** — the employer must disprove the prohibited reason; remedies include
  uncapped compensation, injunctions, penalties. The July sequence (fit certificate → 58-minute
  hold-out → leave-docking → indefinite exclusion, mid-appeal) is the fact pattern the jurisdiction
  exists for. RESERVE track — union first; nothing filed without advice; sequencing rule 8 applies.

## What she has RIGHT (credibility requires conceding it)
- WCRA suitable-duties/rehab obligations do attach to accepted claims (that is why WINNING/SETTLING
  the appeal dissolves this whole letter — their own dividing line).
- An employer may seek genuine clarification of restrictions before restarting a safety-critical
  role; the p7 authorisation does permit fund-initiated information requests. The failures are the
  NO-TIMEFRAME, NO-PAY, NO-CONSULTATION, EXCLUSION-FIRST way it is being done — and the
  direction-of-flow gap on the 13 Jul push (banked).
