# 4. ⭐⭐⭐ HOW THE THREE INTERLOCK — THE ORDER OF PROOF
⭐ **This is the sequence, and *Delaney* fixes it:**
1. **The event** — an admitted fact about a document.
2. **The instrument it breached** — award, guideline, manual, delegation instrument.
3. **His exposure to it** — his own evidence, plainly.
4. ⭐ **Corroboration where it exists** — Conaghan, Harrison-Jones.
5. **What he reported, and when** — the treating records, tied to the same admitted facts.
6. ⭐⭐⭐ **The doctors' opinion** — K8, in the statutory words.
7. **Then, and only then, s 32(5)** — and *Stretton* [84]: one significant unreasonable aspect
   suffices; **fact 260 already supplies it.**

⛔⛔ **Never invert this.** *Delaney*: it is **wrong** to *"start at the other end, eliminate all…
reasonable management action reasonably taken and inquire whether any remaining causes might be
characterised as significant"*.
