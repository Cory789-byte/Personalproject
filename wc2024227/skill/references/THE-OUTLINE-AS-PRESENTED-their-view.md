# 5. ⚠⚠ THE ONE THING TO WATCH — TOPIC 7 AND THE PID
Topic 7 names *"my complaint of 13 May 2024, **later determined to be a public interest disclosure
(content not set out)**"*.

## 5.1 Why it is, on balance, correctly handled
- ⭐ It is used as an **identifier for a complaint in the chronology**, with the content expressly
  withheld.
- ⭐ Its status is a **fact stated by the employer**, not a characterisation by him.
- ⭐⭐⭐ **And the proposition of topic 7 is the opposite of a reprisal narrative**: *"no safety
  assessment, investigation or change of procedure **followed** any complaint I made"* — **inaction,
  not retaliation** — anchored to Dr Hawes's contemporaneous *"they don't listen to his complaints"*.
- ⭐ The closing sentence is a shield against the s 32(5)(a) counter: *"I never said anything hostile
  about Ms Taylor or any other member of staff."*
⇒ ⭐⭐ **Nothing in it uses reprisal, punishment, hostility or motive. The s 32(5)(b) door stays
shut.**

## 5.2 ⛔⛔ AND WHERE THE DANGER SITS
⚠⚠ **"and what followed"**, twice. **It is the only phrase in the outline that gestures at a
consequence without naming one** — and it sits in the same topic as the PID and the union delegate.
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **If particulars are sought on it, the answer is what he was going to say anyway and nothing
more: the dates, the documents, and that nothing followed.** ⛔ **Never a consequence, never a
