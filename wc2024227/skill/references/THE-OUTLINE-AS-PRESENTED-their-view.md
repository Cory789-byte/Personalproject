# 1. WHAT THE PRESENTATION ACTUALLY IS
Two devices, and they are the whole design:

1. ⭐⭐⭐ **The header:** *"The events at topics 2 to 7 **are established by the facts admitted on
   8 September 2026**, except the emergency codes of 17 and 18 March 2024, which are not admitted and
   which I give from my own knowledge."*
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **Every topic closes with a bracketed range of admitted-fact numbers** — [¶¶ 1–16, 26–48,
   182–223], [¶¶ 17–25, 211–227, 232–261, 263–271, 285], and so on for all ten.

⇒ **The outline states, on its face, that his evidence-in-chief rides on their own answer sheet, and
identifies the single place where it does not.**

# 2. ⭐⭐⭐ NO — THEY DO NOT WANT IT PRESENTED THIS WAY

## 2.1 Because it removes both of the answers they would otherwise have
| If the outline had been… | Their answer |
|---|---|
| a narrative with no fact references | *"assertions — he will have to prove each one"* ⇒ **a long fight, topic by topic** |
| argued, drawing conclusions | ⭐ **object: it is a submission, not an outline** ⇒ have it narrowed or struck |

⭐⭐⭐ **This one is neither.** It asserts nothing beyond the admitted facts except in one place it
names itself, and the ranges are verifiable against the response they wrote eight days earlier.
⇒ **A Commissioner can check every topic without opening a document.**

## 2.2 ⭐⭐⭐ It converts their answer sheet into his evidence-in-chief, visibly, on one page
This is the §17 mechanism made legible. **Their letter of 8 September limited the *effect* of the
admissions. The outline does not argue with that limitation — it simply shows what the admissions
already establish, topic by topic.**

## 2.3 ⭐⭐ And naming his own gap buys credibility they cannot take back
By flagging the emergency codes of 17–18 March as the one unadmitted item, he demonstrates the
completeness of everything else. ⭐ **A party who names his own gap is believed about the rest.**
⚠ It also tells them exactly where to cross-examine — **an acceptable trade, and the honest one.**

# 5. ⚠⚠ THE ONE THING TO WATCH — TOPIC 7 AND THE PID
Topic 7 names *"my complaint of 13 May 2024, **later determined to be a public interest disclosure
(content not set out)**"*.

## 5.1 Why it is, on balance, correctly handled
- ⭐ It is used as an **identifier for a complaint in the chronology**, with the content expressly
  withheld.
- ⭐ Its status is a **fact stated by the employer**, not a characterisation by him.
- ⭐⭐⭐ **And the proposition of topic 7 is the opposite of a reprisal narrative**: *"no safety
  assessment, investigation or change of procedure **followed** any complaint I made"* — **inaction,
  not retaliation** — anchored to Dr Hawes's contemporaneous *"they don't listen to his complaints"*.
- ⭐ The closing sentence is a shield against the s 32(5)(a) counter: *"I never said anything hostile
  about Ms Taylor or any other member of staff."*
⇒ ⭐⭐ **Nothing in it uses reprisal, punishment, hostility or motive. The s 32(5)(b) door stays
shut.**

## 5.2 ⛔⛔ AND WHERE THE DANGER SITS
⚠⚠ **"and what followed"**, twice. **It is the only phrase in the outline that gestures at a
consequence without naming one** — and it sits in the same topic as the PID and the union delegate.
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **If particulars are sought on it, the answer is what he was going to say anyway and nothing
more: the dates, the documents, and that nothing followed.** ⛔ **Never a consequence, never a
motive, never the PID's content.** (See `THE-PATIENT-SAFETY-SPINE-and-the-boundary.md` §4.)

# 6. ⭐⭐⭐ HAVE THEY REALISED THEY ARE NOT LET OFF EASILY?
**Yes — but they realised it on 7–8 September, not today.** The evidence is their own letter: two
internal review cycles, a two-page framing memorandum for a two-line enclosure, and a reservation in
the **past tense** against a conclusion already drawn. ⇒ **They read their own completed answer sheet
and saw what it carried.**

⭐⭐⭐ **What 9 September added is worse for them, and different in kind: it showed them that he knows
what they conceded, knows how to use it, and will not over-reach.**
- A self-represented appellant who over-reaches is manageable — **you answer the over-reach.**
- ⭐ One who concedes every limitation in your own words and then closes on the admitted facts alone
  leaves nothing to answer.

⭐⭐ **And topic 9's closing sentence is the one that will land hardest:** *"The matters at paragraphs
2 to 7 above, and their effect on me as each occurred and as they accumulated, are the matters I
reported to each doctor, as their records show; **the events those records name are the facts
admitted on 8 September 2026**."*
⇒ **Their heading 3 said the medical records do not prove their contents. He has answered that the
events those records name are separately admitted** — so the notes corroborate the reporting, and the
events stand on their own footing.

## 6.1 ⛔ THE HONEST LIMIT
⚠⚠ **None of that proves causation.** Doctors recording what he told them is not an opinion that the
employment was a significant contributing factor. ⭐⭐⭐ **That gap is exactly what they read in topic
9, and it is why the report is now worth more than any further procedural gain.**
