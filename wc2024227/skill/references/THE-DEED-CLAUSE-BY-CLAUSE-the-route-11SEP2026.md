# 2. ⭐⭐⭐ THE THREE GATES, AND HOW EACH IS PASSED

## 2.1 ⭐⭐⭐ GATE — CLAUSE 8 (the release)
⛔ Blocks anything *arising from the Dismissal*.
⭐⭐⭐⭐ **ROUTE: never plead the termination as the detriment.** The detriment pleaded is
pre-dismissal and independently sufficient — the retraction direction **15 May 2024**, the pay
obstruction and COVID-leave declines **May–Jul 2024**, the meetings while certified **12 and 16 Jul
2024**, and ⭐⭐⭐ **the injury itself, 18 June 2024**. The termination of 8 Oct 2024 is one item and
the last; it is **dropped, not argued**.
⭐⭐ **Second route, if the first is ever strained:** the carve-outs. *"excluding any common law
personal injury claim, statutory Workers' Compensation claim, or any claim that cannot be excluded
at law."*

## 2.2 ⭐⭐ GATE — CLAUSES 9 + 17 + 18 (the bar, the deed poll, the class)
⛔ Any individual officer or employee can plead clause 8 in their own right.
⭐⭐⭐ **ROUTE: identical to 2.1.** The bar is bounded by the same five words. Suing an individual
is not the problem; pleading the Dismissal is.

## 2.3 ⭐⭐⭐ GATE — CLAUSE 4 (13 Dec 2024 – 23 Feb 2025 is leave without pay)
⛔ This is the **only clause that costs him anything he now wants**, and it is the only real
argument MSH has against the second application. **Four answers, in order of strength:**

1. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Clause 5 is the answer, and it sits directly underneath clause 4.** *"If the Applicant
   is not fit to return to the workplace on executing this deed, he will apply for leave using the
   usual application process."* He was not fit, the certificates prove it, and clause 5 provided
   for exactly this application. **Clause 4 set the default; clause 5 supplied the mechanism.**
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **Sick leave without pay IS leave without pay.** Clause 4 fixes the payment status, not
   the reason code. Recoding leaves the period unpaid and leaves clause 4 satisfied word for word.
3. ⭐⭐⭐ **SCK_NP is not a novel code — it is all over his own record** (see §4). Nothing
   exceptional is being asked for.
4. ⭐⭐⭐ **It is MSH's own position.** Payroll, 8 Sep 2026: the periods *"should have been booked
   as Sick No Pay (SNP) rather than Leave Without Pay (LWOP)"*.

⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AND NOTE WHAT CLAUSE 4 DOES NOT SAY.** It is one sentence. It does not say the period is
not continuous service and it does not mention long service leave. **The LSL exclusion is a
consequence of the LWOP code, not of anything the parties agreed.**

⛔⛔ **PERIOD 1 (21 Jun – 20 Sep 2024) HAS NO GATE AT ALL.** It ends on the reinstatement effective
date and is outside the Deed entirely. ⇒ **Never argue the two periods together. Period 1 needs no
