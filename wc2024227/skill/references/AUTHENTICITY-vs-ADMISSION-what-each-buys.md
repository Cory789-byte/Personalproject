# AUTHENTICITY, ADMISSION OF FACT, AND HOW FAR EACH TRAVELS
> 9 September 2026. ⛔ Internal. Answering: *"authentication of a document is what it says it is, the
> admit deny is something different, and if it authenticates it I can carry that wherever I like."*
> **The first two are right. The third is right inside this proceeding and wrong outside it.**

---

# 1. ✅ THE DISTINCTION IS CORRECT, AND THE NOTICE WAS BUILT ON IT
| | |
|---|---|
| **Form 25 — authenticity (r 49)** | that the document **is what it purports to be**: this copy is a true copy of the document it says it is. ⛔ **Says nothing about whether anything in it is true.** |
| **Form 24 — facts** | that a **stated fact is true**, including a fact about **what a document says** |

⭐⭐ **The notice was deliberately split that way**: the *contents* went into the Form 24 as facts
(*"that email states: '…'"*), and *authenticity* went into the Form 25.

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **Which is what makes the 8 September response incoherent on its face: they admitted the
CONTENTS of 38 of 39 tabs through the Form 24, while disputing the AUTHENTICITY of 14 of those same
tabs.** ⭐ **They admitted what the documents say while disputing that the copies are what they say
they are.**

---

# 2. ⛔⛔ BUT IT DOES NOT TRAVEL OUTSIDE THIS PROCEEDING — AND THAT IS THE RULE, NOT THEIR SPIN
> **r 49(1), verbatim:** *"…ask the other party to admit, **for the proceeding only**, the facts or
> documents stated in the notice."*

⭐⭐⭐ **"For the proceeding only" is in the rule itself.** It is also in **his own service email of
28 August** (*"taken to be admitted for this proceeding only"*), in **their letter**, and conceded in
**his §1**. ⇒ **Four times over. It is not arguable.**

⛔ **An admission — of a fact or of authenticity — cannot be carried into:**
the employment / grievance track · an AD Act complaint · a QHRC complaint · the PID or CCC track ·
any later common law proceeding · any other QIRC matter.

⚠⚠ **Practical consequence:** ⭐ **any document he needs in the employment track must be obtained
there separately** — by RTI/IP Act application or by disclosure in that forum. ⛔ **Do not assume the
WC bundle transfers.** ⭐ And **rule 9: the deed releases the compensation claim only.**

# 3. ⭐⭐⭐ INSIDE THIS PROCEEDING, THOUGH, HIS INSTINCT IS RIGHT — AND BETTER THAN HE PUT IT

## 3.1 ⭐⭐⭐ AUTHENTICITY IS NOT FACT-SPECIFIC. IT ATTACHES TO THE WHOLE DOCUMENT.
An admission that a tab is authentic authenticates **the entire document at that tab** — not only the
passages quoted in the Form 24.

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **He is not confined to the facts he pleaded it for.** Live examples, all with authenticity
**ADMITTED**:
| Tab | Pleaded facts | ⭐ What is actually authenticated |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐ **25 — Review Decision 69983** | facts 241–261 | **the whole 28-page decision** — including the **Conclusion** (*"employment was a significant contributing factor"*; *"factor 4 amounted to unreasonable management action"*; the ***Delaney* global-evaluation paragraph**), the **Factor 3 payroll passage**, and the **12 July 2024 statement** |
| **26–27 — his 18 Feb 2026 response** | facts 285, 289 | the whole response, every admission in it |
| **8B, 11, 9, 16, 2–4, 7, 8A** etc. | selected facts | the whole of each document |

⭐⭐ **That is a substantial holding, and it is available now.** ⛔ **But every passage still has to be
verified against the source before it is quoted** — the cardinal habit does not relax because a tab
is authenticated.

## 3.2 WHAT AUTHENTICITY BUYS, PRECISELY
1. ⭐⭐ **No custodian or maker need be called to prove the copy.** Fewer witnesses, shorter hearing.
2. ⭐ **The document can be put to a witness and tendered**, subject only to relevance and
   admissibility.
3. ⭐ **For every purpose in this proceeding**, not just the pleaded one.
