# 2. ⛔⛔ WHAT IS **NOT** ESTABLISHED — AND HE MUST NOT ASSERT IT
⛔ **That Ms Taylor knew of the PID, or that she "pointed the complaint at him" because of it.**
- ⭐ **Harrison routed it to three HR officers. NOT to Taylor. NOT to Reese.** ⇒ **No
  Harrison→Taylor connection exists on the record.**
- ⭐⭐⭐ **And the chronology runs the wrong way for the inference:**
  **Ms Reese directed the retraction at 15 May 6:23 pm.** **McGinley's escalation attaching the form
  reached her at 16 May 11:43 am — SEVENTEEN HOURS LATER.** ⇒ ⭐⭐ **Reese directed the retraction
  BEFORE she received the PID.**
- ⚠ **Ms Taylor's HR email of 17 May 1:20 pm postdates Reese receiving the form. A Reese→Taylor
  route is POSSIBLE. It is not evidenced.**

⛔⛔ **The repo's own standing discipline (working-notes §5099) is: DO NOT FIGHT "REESE-UNAWARE".
Our own verified record supports it.** ⭐⭐ **And it costs nothing, because the retraction direction
is unreasonable in its MANNER regardless of what anyone knew: a direction to retract a written
concern, the email removed from the server in their own words, no process, in the same week as
their own rating of 11.** ⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **Chronology, manner, system. Never motive.**
