# Has the mention's method turned the case into admissions?

**WC/2024/227 · 17 September 2026 · ⛔ INTERNAL ONLY.**

## ⭐ The answer in one line

**On what happened — yes, essentially completely. On whether it caused the injury — no, and it never
could.** The method closed the half of the case that is normally the expensive half. The half that
remains is the half that was always going to be decided by the doctors and by the Commission's own
evaluation.

## 2. ⭐⭐ What that closed, and it is a great deal

- **No witness is needed to prove any act.** The acts are established on the Respondent's own
  response, under **r 49(1)**, *"for the proceeding"*.
- ⭐ **The duty is admitted** — the role description's requirement to maintain the database and to
  participate in the emergency response *"strictly adhering to protocols and timeframes"* (§ A).
- ⭐ **The removal of the means is admitted** — Stibbard's withdrawal of everyone's access,
  recorded in the Regulator's own Review Decision (§ D).
- ⭐ **The consequences are admitted** — the MASPER emails, the MET call team that could not be told
  where the MET call was, the Respiratory Service (§ D).
- ⭐ **The seven-hour break is admitted**, with their own *"human error"* characterisation (§ L).
- ⭐⭐ **Their own delegate's finding of unreasonable management action is admitted** (§ N).
- ⭐ **And the negatives are admitted** — what their pleading does **not** allege (§ S), and what was
  never done or does not exist (§ P). Those are the hardest facts to prove and they are conceded.

⇒ ⭐⭐ **The ordinary contest in a case of this kind — whose account of the workplace is right —
is over.** Dwyer's method was *"if you say something and it's not contradicted … there's a likelihood
that it'll be accepted."* The instrument built on it went further: it did not rely on
non-contradiction, **it obtained the concession**.

## 3. ⛔ What it did not close, and what no admission ever could

### 3.1 ⛔⛔ Element (d) — causation. Untouched.

**s 32(1) requires that employment be *a significant contributing factor* to the injury.** Not one
of the 303 facts goes to it, and none could: they are facts about documents and events, not about
the aetiology of a psychiatric condition.

⚠ **And they said so expressly.** Their covering letter of 8 September:
> *"any admission that is made is an admission '**for the proceeding only**' and has no effect beyond
> these proceedings and… **does not of itself determine relevance, admissibility or weight**"*

⇒ ⛔ **The bridge from the admitted acts to the diagnosed condition is medical, and it is open.**
Krishnaiah declined to provide a report on 5 September; **no expert report has been served by either
side**; element (d) runs on the treating records plus the oral evidence of Krishnaiah and Hawes.

### 3.2 ⛔ s 32(5)(a) — the exclusion. Theirs to run, and untouched.

Whether those admitted acts were **reasonable management action taken in a reasonable way** is an
evaluative question for the Commission. ⚠ **Admissions do not answer it — and they cut both ways**,
because the Respondent needs the acts established too in order to characterise them.

⇒ ⚠ **The admissions may have made this contest sharper rather than softer.** With the facts fixed,
the Respondent cannot blur them; it must argue that these precise acts were reasonable. **That is
better for him — but it makes the *why* and *how* evidence more important, not less**, which is
exactly why the covering letter's transfer of Taylor and Reese matters.

### 3.3 ⚠ The two substantive refusals

- **Facts 228–231** — the Emergency Code Register for 17–19 March 2024: **the load carried across the
  seven-hour break.** ⭐ Curable: fact **268** admits that *"a spreadsheet of recorded MET calls is
  available for the period 17-18 March 2024."*
- **Fact 154** — their amended List of Documents does not list the Communication Book or any page
  from it.

### 3.4 ⚠ And the admissions are not unconditional

**r 49(1)** — admitted *"for the proceeding only"*. **r 49(3)** — an admission taken to be made under
r 49(2) may be withdrawn **with leave**. ⇒ Leave would need a reason and these were **express**
admissions, not deemed ones, but **"admitted" is not the same as "beyond recall"** and should not be
described that way.

---

## 4. ⚠ Three honest qualifications on the number itself

1. **"298 admitted" is not 298 operative propositions.** Roughly a third of the facts are of the form
   *"that email states: …"* — they prove **the content of a document**, not the event. Another
   ~one in nine are negative-universe facts (*"as presently constituted, does not allege…"*).
   ⇒ **The count is a measure of coverage, not of weight.**
2. **Admitted facts are in evidence; they are not argument.** They establish the steps. The inference
   from the steps to the mechanism still has to be made, and made by him.
3. ⛔ **The live Form 9A still pleads *"Reprisal"*, *"actively suppressed"* and *"composite, hostile
   course"*.** That is not an admissions gap — it is a self-inflicted one, and it is the only part of
   the case that can lose ground that the admissions have already won.

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **So: not "all of it", and the distance left is not small — but what remains is exactly what
was always going to be contested, and nothing else is.** He converted a thirteen-grievance factual
dispute into a two-issue hearing, and did it **without calling a witness, and without a document
fight.**

⚠ ⛔ **And the thing to hold on to:** *"undismissable on the served record" is not a result.* Nothing
has been found by anybody. **298 admissions prove that steps were taken on dates in terms. They do
not prove the injury.**
