### 6.1 ⭐⭐⭐ The single-stressor route (Contention 2(b), *Mahaffey*) — the outlines leave it whole

1. The break was seven hours (226, 284). The floor was ten, or eight by written agreement (285, 257).
2. The agreement applies only to staff-initiated swaps (225); no swap is alleged (234); the employer
   confirmed his contract and hours had changed since 2020 (20); nobody re-executed it (21); the
   Regulator's own reviewer found *"uncertainty"* (254).
3. It was not the first error on his line: Aug 2023 (28, 156–157, 287), Apr 2024 (211–213), and
   *"a few rostering errors … with regards to Cory's line"* (220).

   rating and the FRMS guideline (218–223); no assessment, training or register existed before

   no interim response (250); the reason has now changed to "not overtime" (W4) while payroll's own
   email says a shift that fortnight needed to be overtime (188). His own sick leave paid for 19 Mar (235).
6. The Regulator's reviewer found the rostering *"unreasonable management action"* (260).
7. The role required him to work the roster (2, 13); the "could refuse" answer is contradicted by
   their own payroll witness.
**Their evidence on all seven: "human error", "not repeated", "paid sick leave", and payroll
