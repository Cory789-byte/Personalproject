# Will reprisal be a finding in WC/2024/227?

**16 September 2026 · ⛔ INTERNAL ONLY.**

## ⭐ The answer: **No.** And while it stays on the pleading it is a cost, not an asset.

---

## 1. It is not a question the Commission is deciding

This is an appeal against a review decision on whether he sustained an **injury** under **WCRA s 32**.
The questions are: a personal injury; arising out of or in the course of employment; employment **a
significant contributing factor**; and whether s 32(5) excludes it.

⇒ ⛔ **Reprisal is not an element of any of them.** A reprisal finding is made under the PID Act, or
on an adverse action application under the IR Act — **different proceedings, different jurisdiction.**
The Commission sitting on a s 32 appeal does not reach it, is not asked to, and will not make it.

⇒ **So the limb cannot produce a favourable finding. There is no finding for it to produce.**

## 2. They have already met it, and on the one thing he cannot prove

The Regulator's SOFC of 13 May 2026, ¶ 16(a):

> *"says that **Ms Reese was unaware that the appellant had lodged a PID**"*

⇒ ⭐⭐ **Reprisal requires that the decision-maker acted *because of* the disclosure.** If Ms Reese did
not know of it, the limb fails at its first step. ⛔ **And her state of knowledge is precisely what he
cannot prove from the outside.** They have chosen the cheapest possible answer and it is a complete
one.

## 3. He has already conceded the neighbouring limb, on the record

At the mention, on *"actively suppressed… for 13 months"*:
> **DWYER IC:** *"So actively means they deliberately. Am I right about that?"*
> **MR SHEPHERD:** *"**I don't know if I can prove it as much.**"*
> **DWYER IC:** *"Well, that answers the question."*

⇒ ⭐ **The concession was right and it was honest.** It also shows the bench testing exactly these
words — **and they are still in the live pleading.**

The live pleading is the **amended Form 9A filed 8 April 2026**. Verified from the filed PDF:

⚠ **And note what 1(f) actually pleads underneath its own heading:** *within 48 hours he was directed

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **That is admitted differential treatment — two dated documentary facts.** It does its whole
job on **manner** under s 32(5)(a) **without any allegation about why anyone acted.**
**The heading is doing damage the facts do not need.**

## 5. ⚠ It cannot win. Here is how it loses.

| | Cost |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐ ***Briginshaw*** | *reprisal*, *deliberate*, *actively suppressed*, *hostile course*, *corrupt* are serious allegations against named individuals. **They raise HIS OWN standard of proof** — on limbs he does not need to win |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **s 32(5)(b)** | the exclusion for injury connected with the worker's **expectation or perception** of management action. ⛔ **The Regulator has not pleaded it.** A reprisal case is a standing invitation to |
| ⭐⭐ ***Delaney* v *Mahaffey*** | the pleading says the events *"form a **composite, hostile course**"*. ⛔ **"Composite course" is *Delaney*, and *Delaney* is the Respondent's authority.** His case is ***Mahaffey*** — one mechanism, one proven instance. **The pleading currently argues their case for them** |
| ⚠ **Credit** | an unproved allegation of deliberate reprisal against named people costs him on everything else — including the material he has already pre-empted with three "never hostile" paragraphs |

---

## 6. ⭐⭐⭐ And the sequence replaces it completely

The chain established on admitted facts — **the duty imposed, the means removed "while she fixed
it", the failures accumulating, the record of them treated as hostility** — proves that employment
was a significant contributing factor, and that the **manner** of management action was not
reasonable.

⇒ ⭐⭐ **It does that without one word about motive, and it is proved on their documents.**

⇒ **That is the finding available here. Reprisal is not, and the sequence does not need it.**

⭐ **What the Commission can find**, all of it adjacent and all of it enough:
- the acts occurred (admitted);
- the manner in which management action was taken (s 32(5)(a));
- ⭐⭐ and it already has the Regulator's **own delegate's** finding that the rostering of the two
  shifts was **unreasonable management action**.

⛔ **What it will not find:** that anybody did anything **because** of the disclosure.
