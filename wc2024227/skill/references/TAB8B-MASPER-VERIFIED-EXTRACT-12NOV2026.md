## 5. What this changes

1. **1(h) is a condition of the work, not a delay case.** Misdirected calls and clinicians' complaints are not
   management action, so the exclusion has nothing to attach to. That is how the 9A pleads it (C 4), and it is the
   whole of the item's value.
2. **Do not put "was five days reasonable?"** The Regulator answers it with the calendar and the reply: a Friday
   afternoon, a weekend, a public holiday, a reply the morning after the second report, courteous, team-wide,
   and saying "its not only switch". A Member reads that as reasonable.
3. **"5 days 18 hours 14 minutes" is true and admitted, and it includes a weekend and a public holiday.** In any
   completed 9A, say so first: "3 May 2024 was a Friday and 6 May 2024 was a public holiday." Conceding it keeps the
   strong intervals credible (23 days for the fatigue enquiry; eleven months for the database; the pay chronology).
4. **In any completed 9A, describe the 9:20 am reply as a whole,** not only the business-hours request.
5. **"Like I was doing my job incorrectly."** The documents say the opposite, and that helps: the Manager told the
   Registrar "its not only switch", and told staff the step was to "determine whether they are coming from
   switchboard or other departments". No operator is named. No disciplinary process, performance management or
   warning is alleged (facts 300–302).
6. **The mechanism is the role description against the database.** Fact 6: a key responsibility was to "maintain
   Omnivista database and SharePoint to ensure information held within Switchboard Services is accurate and
   appropriate". Fact 40: "I will be removing everyone's access to the database." Fact 55: restoration not alleged
   before 18 June 2024.
7. ⛔ **Never:** cardiac arrest, a patient not reached, or any outcome, on this item. It is not in the document, it
   is the only version the Regulator can deny, and it invites "who took those calls?". The Code Blue in the record
   is 17 March 2024 (Tab 31, facts 228–231, not yet admitted): calls the console handled before the seven-hour
   break. That is load carried, not a call that went wrong. Keep the two apart.
8. **Correction to the reply of 12 Nov (earlier the same day).** It said that if the Regulator calls the three
   items management action it "has to say the intervals were reasonable: five days, five days and eleven months".
   For the calls and the respiratory entry that overstated the bind, for the reasons at 2 and 3 above. It holds for
   the database.
