# PART B — THE LAW OF THE ADMISSIONS

The form itself cites **Industrial Relations Act 2016, s 989** and **Industrial Relations
(Tribunals) Rules 2011, rr 41, 49, 108 and 113.**

⛔ **THE RULES ARE NOT IN THIS REPO.** `documents/instruments/` holds ATT01–ATT26 and none of
them is the Tribunals Rules. **Obtain sl-2011-0237 and verify rr 41, 49, 108, 113 before any
of Part B is put in a filing.** What follows is the framework as it appears on the form's face
and from the Response's own drafting, not a verified rule-by-rule statement.

## B1 — What an admission does

**Deemed admission by silence.** On the face of the form: if the recipient does not serve a
disputing notice **within 14 days**, they "are taken to admit … the facts specified in this
notice". Silence is admission.

**⭐ "FOR THIS PROCEEDING ONLY."** The form says so expressly. This is the most important
limitation and it is easy to forget:
⛔ **These admissions do not operate in the employment track, in any Anti-Discrimination Act
complaint, in a WHS proceeding, or before the Chief Executive.** They bind the Regulator in
WC/2024/227 and nowhere else. Do not deploy them elsewhere as admissions — the underlying
documents can be used, the admissions cannot.

**A formal admission dispenses with proof.** It does not become evidence to be weighed against
other evidence; it removes the fact from contest. That is why ¶8 matters so much — the
patient-safety criticality is simply established.

**Withdrawal requires leave** (⚠ verify the rule). A party seeking to withdraw generally must
show inadvertence, or that the admission is contrary to fact, and the tribunal weighs
prejudice. **Practical effect: the Regulator cannot quietly resile from ¶1, ¶3, ¶8, ¶20 or
¶25.** If they attempt it, require a formal application.

## B2 — ⭐ "DOES NOT ADMIT" IS NOT "DENIES". THE DIFFERENCE IS FORENSIC

| Formulation | Legal effect | What it costs them |
|---|---|---|
| **"admits the facts contained therein"** | Fact established | Everything |
| **"does not admit"** | Puts the Appellant to proof. **No positive contrary case is asserted** | Nothing — but they cannot lead a positive case they never pleaded |
| **"denies as untrue"** | ⭐ **A positive assertion of the contrary, which they must support** | They now carry a case to run |

**Every "denies as untrue" in the Response is a commitment.** They appear at ¶4 (the asserted
June 2020 8-hour agreement), ¶9, ¶12, ¶19, ¶22, ¶23, ¶25 (the r 64E limb) and ¶26.

⭐ **¶4 is the one to press.** They have positively asserted that "in June 2020 the Appellant
signed an agreement allowing an 8 hour break between shifts". That is not a non-admission —
it is an affirmative case, and **they must produce the agreement.** If it cannot be produced,
a denial with nothing behind it is worse for them than a non-admission would have been.

## B3 — THE THREE TIERS OF ADMISSION IN THIS RESPONSE

**TYPE A — the fact itself is admitted. Removed from contest.**
¶1, ¶3, ¶5, ¶6 (removal only), **¶8**, ¶14, ¶17, **¶20**, ¶25, ¶40, ¶41, ¶46.

**TYPE B — the DOCUMENT's CONTENT is admitted, its TRUTH is not.** Proves only that the
document says what it says. **You still have to prove the underlying fact.**
- **¶33/34** — "admits the entry is listed in the record, but does not admit the accuracy"
- **¶36** — admits the certificate records the mechanism, **"denies that as fact"**
- **¶37** — Review Decision **contents** admitted, relevance reserved
- **¶38/39** — admits the reports say it; does not admit accuracy
- **¶50** — Review Unit decision contents admitted; does not admit a misleading statement

⚠ **This is where the clean-baseline and causation propositions actually sit.** ¶34 does not
establish that he had no psychological illness in November 2023. It establishes only that a
doctor wrote that sentence. **You must still prove the fact through evidence.**

**TYPE C — admitted, but with a qualification that changes the effect.**
- **¶6** — removal admitted; **the handwriting is not**
- **¶21** — the retraction direction admitted, **but "no correlation between the two events"**;
  event admitted, **causation contested**
- **¶25** — no service admitted, **but no requirement under s 64D**; the fact is yours, the
  legal consequence is not
- **¶41** — the 25-day delay admitted, **justification contested**
- **¶18** — the text was sent, **but "not a formal notification"**

## B4 — THE DE NOVO OVERLAY (unique to this matter)

The appeal is a **hearing de novo** under **s 550(4) WCRA**. The Respondent uses this
throughout to reserve relevance rather than contest fact — ¶2 "does not admit … for the
Commission"; ¶37 contents admitted, relevance reserved.

**What that means:** an admitted **fact** still binds. But admitting the **contents** of the
Review Decision does not make the Review Officer's **findings** binding, because the Commission
determines the matter afresh. ⚠ **Do not present Review Decision 69983 as a finding that binds
Commissioner Dwyer.** It is a document, admitted, and persuasive.
