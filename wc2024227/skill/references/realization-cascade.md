## THE "NO CHANGES" BIND (Item 3(c) sworn vs Item 10 produced) — the trap

Order ¶6(a) forces MSH to SWEAR that Item 3(c) — "consequential changes to
operating procedures" — does NOT exist ("no consequential changes" over the
period). Order ¶¶1–2 force MSH to PRODUCE Item 10 — the Line Manager's directives
(incl. the "effective immediately" sub-set). **These two collide:**

- **Branch A — the directives WERE procedural changes** → the sworn "no
  consequential changes" is FALSE, contradicted by MSH's own production. False
  verification exposure on the deponent.
- **Branch B — the directives were NOT procedural changes** → then the Line
  Manager was issuing unilateral disruptive directives (Item 10) while management
  made NO consequential procedural changes (3(c)) to FIX the operational-error
  complaints (Item 3(a)). i.e. **active, aware management that REMEDIATED NOTHING**
  — the manner/governance failure, proven on oath.

**Either branch hurts them.** Swearing "no changes" is a FIXED position that, set
against the produced directives AND the produced complaints, proves EITHER a false
statement OR a failure to remediate. And note: the "no changes" answer is ALREADY
damaging standing alone — it proves nothing was fixed despite the complaints (the
"complaints in / nothing out" black hole). The affidavit merely locks it onto oath
and the directives production adds the second blade.

**DISCIPLINE:** this is sprung by the RECORD (the sworn answer next to the produced
directives), discovered by the Commission — NEVER argued as "they are lying."
Discovered contradiction = evidence; alleged perjury = discounted + vexatious. The
affidavit (order 6) is the mechanism; the contradiction reveals itself.
