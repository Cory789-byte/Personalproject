# WHEN THE APPROACH COMES — protocol. Banked 5 Aug 2026.
> For the moment MSH (or the Regulator) opens resolution. Written in advance so nothing
> is improvised under relief.

## 1. RECOGNISE IT
It will not say "settlement". It looks like: a new senior signatory; "a discussion about
arrangements going forward"; "without prejudice"; an invitation to meet with someone from
HR you have not dealt with; or a quiet payroll correction followed by a call. Treat ANY of
these as the approach.

## 2. FIRST 24 HOURS — three rules
(a) DO NOT respond substantively on the spot, by phone or in a meeting. "Thank you — I'd
    like to consider that and respond in writing." Then send a same-day written record of
    what was said (meeting-record protocol).
(b) GET IT IN WRITING. No term exists until it is written.
(c) TELL THE UNION (Petering) before responding. If a delegate/collective limb is running,
    they need to know the personal matter is moving.

## 3. CHECK THE FORM (this decides everything)
- ACCEPTANCE of the claim (Regulator concedes) = statutory entitlements flow; best form.
- DEED = negotiated; every clause must be read against §4.
- "Informal arrangement" / verbal assurance = NOT a resolution. Consent orders or written
  terms only. (Same rule as the mention: dates on paper.)

## 4. ⛔ DEED RED LINES (rule 9 and its companions)
1. Releases the COMPENSATION CLAIM ONLY. No employment releases. No release of anything
   arising after 26 June 2026.
2. NO confidentiality/non-disparagement clause that would gag the WHS material, the
   assessment request, the tabled package, or his ability to speak to a regulator, the
   union, or a health and safety representative.
3. Nothing restraining future work, training, or industry participation.
4. Nothing that withdraws or nullifies the PID, the s 89 disclosure, or protected acts.
5. Nothing that requires him to withdraw the assessment request (see §6).
6. Tax/super treatment specified; medical costs to date reimbursed; leave RE-CREDITED not
   "paid out" (re-credit preserves the entitlement, payout extinguishes it).

## 5. THE RECONCILIATION LEDGER (have it ready BEFORE negotiating)
Wages withheld from 13 Jul (and the 26 Jun-13 Jul characterisation issue) · every leave
hour debited, with dates · superannuation on unpaid wages · self-funded medical costs ·
the ART overpayment position (do not let MSH's settlement wash into ART's recovery) ·
any interest/penalty entitlements. A number you can state in one line beats a number you
have to construct in the room.

## 6. ⭐ PROTECT THE ASSESSMENT
His resolution and the workplace assessment are in tension: a deed that resolves him lets
the assessment die. Before/while negotiating —
  (a) transfer the request to the UNION as a collective work-group matter; and/or
  (b) support an HSR election for the work group.
Either gives the assessment an owner who survives his settlement. Do this EARLY — after an
offer arrives it looks like a bargaining chip.

## 7. TIMING DISCIPLINE
Never accept the first number. Never negotiate while acutely distressed. Take 48 hours on
any substantive term. Keep every clock running while negotiating (Stage 2, cl 10.3.6,
RTI, mention timetable) — withdrawing machinery mid-negotiation is the classic error and
it always reduces the final number.
