# 4. ⛔⛔ THE FINDING HE EXPECTS IS NOT AVAILABLE — AND EXPECTING IT IS THE RISK
> Cory, 10 Sep 2026: *"the only finding would be a stern warning from a Commissioner that they find
> it difficult to rationalise how a documented case so clearly could be considered not to have
> settled earlier."*

## 4.1 ⛔ THERE IS NO SUCH ORDER
⭐ **A s 550(4) appeal produces s 558(1) orders: confirm, vary, or SET ASIDE AND SUBSTITUTE.**
⛔ **There is no rebuke, no declaration about conduct, no finding about MSH (not a party), and
nothing about the PID, the dismissal or the reinstatement.** ⭐ **Comment on a party's conduct
appears, if at all, in COSTS reasons under s 558(3) — and only if costs are sought and argued.**

## 4.2 ⚠⚠ AND THEIR DECISION TO DEFEND IS NOT DERISORY — WHICH HE MUST ACCEPT
⭐ **Asked "why did this not settle?", they would say: causation was contested; the appellant had no
medical evidence in admissible form; Guide §7.7 required the doctors to be called and they were not
secured.** ⚠ **That is a respectable answer. It may be wrong. It is not absurd.**
⚠ **They also hold the GP and psychiatrist files via the NNPDs of 4 July 2025.**

## 4.3 ⛔⛔ THE REAL DANGER — IT INVERTS THE BURDEN IN HIS OWN MIND
⭐⭐⭐ **If he expects the Commission to be indignant on his behalf, he will under-prepare the parts
that require him to PROVE things, and he will be tempted to argue indignation instead of evidence.**
⛔⛔ **That is exactly what Dwyer warned against, in terms:**
> *"It's **not a broad-brush criticism** of what you regard as the systemic failings of the
> organisation… **You don't need to prove all of that.** You need to prove the matters that you've
> identified in the stressors as being causative of your injury."*
⇒ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **A Commissioner who hears an appellant inviting him to be outraged marks the APPELLANT
down.** ⛔ **The outrage, if any, is his to arrive at unassisted. Never solicited.**
