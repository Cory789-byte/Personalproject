## Discipline rules (govern every sub-skill — non-negotiable)

1. **Conspiracy/reprisal framing never reaches paper or the bar table.** Coordination between MSH and the Regulator is real and lawful (joint defence). Use the *asymmetry* (same documents, same custodians, two postures by requester), held in reserve, deployed only on trigger — never the collusion narrative.
2. **The word "fraud" never appears in the WC track.** Cory's PID alleged timesheet fraud against a named manager — the collateral-purpose grenade. It belongs to the PID/reprisal track and evidence-in-chief only. The PID *complaint form* stays out of the 64G; only the clean *response/routing* documents go in.
3. **Items 8–9 (manager's attendance records) anchor ONLY to pleaded Stressor 1(a)** (erratic presence — a contested live fact the records measure) and the "roster was equitable" denial (Form 24 ¶39). NEVER to the reprisal (admitted, so the records prove nothing about it) or the fraud allegation (the collateral purpose).
4. **File first, ask second.** The 64G compels; the index email requests. Never lead with the request when holding the compeller. Communications to the Regulator wait until after filing + service, in writing only — never by phone.
5. **Tone is neutral, procedural, proportionate.** Dwyer (close reader, ran the failed conference) must discover MSH's problems himself. A discovered pattern is evidence; a stated pattern is discounted argument. Strip adjectives.
6. **Restraint as posture.** Concede what's honestly available; withdraw what the record doesn't need — the concessions certify that what remains is necessary and make it grantable.
7. **Severity = the whole course of conduct** (cumulative institutional response to raising concerns), never a single event. Lives in evidence-in-chief, the medical case, closing — not the 64G.
8. **Parallel tracks stay sequenced BEHIND the WC settlement.** Maximum accountability risks the WC matter; restraint manufactures the accountability later.
9. **At settlement, release the compensation claim ONLY.** Any deed reaching MSH, the reprisal tort, or "all claims arising from employment" buys their liability-shield immunity with Cory's compensation money. The whole strategy is tested here.
