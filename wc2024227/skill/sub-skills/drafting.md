# Sub-skill: Drafting (64G, orders, submissions, emails)

Load with the parent SKILL.md and `references/documents-and-64g.md`. Apply ALL parent discipline rules — especially tone (neutral/procedural), the fraud-word ban, and Items 8–9 anchoring to 1(a) only.

## How Cory's documents are built (his voice)
- **Concession-first architecture** — lead with what is NOT being fought. Concessions certify the necessity of what remains.
- **Precision as aggression** — exact figures, timestamps, PRNs, device dates do the accusing; no adjectives.
- **Keystone, not perimeter** — the 7-hour break is the anchor, not the boundary; the inquiry is the whole course.
- **Discovered, not stated** — place findings for a close reader to find; never point. "The answer cannot stand against the document" beats a paragraph of argument.
- **Commit, don't hedge** — state the law and the position flatly. (The onus hedge was removed in v5 for this reason.)
- **Their records convict them** — build on Form 24 admissions, MSH's own answers, the Regulator's own disclosure.

## No case law (Cory's instruction)
v5 strips ALL case citations. "Directly relevant = tends to prove or disprove an allegation in issue" is stated as rule-text, no Robson. Statute/rules only (rr 64B/64C/64E/64G/64I, s 32(5)(a) WCRA, s 65 PID Act). Bunney [2020] QIRC 210 is held in reserve as the order template, not cited.

## Onus — corrected (was wrong pre-v5)
Appellant bears the onus of establishing the injury incl. excluding s 32(5)(a). The corrected onus is the STRONGER necessity argument: a party who must prove the character of conduct cannot be left without the records of it, held solely by the author. Severity goes to BOTH limbs.

## Email discipline
- Registry filing email: factual, references the objection by identifiers, attaches the application + draft order.
- MSH/Regulator service email: "substantive correspondence in writing" shield; no merits.
- Index email (post-service): the Form 29 / 64D questions; neutral; two-to-four lines.
- Never phone the Regulator. Anything by phone → "please put that in an email."
