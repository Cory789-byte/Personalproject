## Known problems in the older documents

These are recorded in full at Part C of the assessment. In short:

1. The 25 February strike-out is treated as an answer to the "order in force" element. It probably is not — the conduct alleged is 23 February and the court dealt with the application on the 25th. The conditions argument stands on its own and does not need it.
2. "There was no condition capable of being breached" is too strong. The prosecution case must be that taking the dog was itself domestic violence under the limb covering animals. That needs a different answer.
3. The December 2024 transfer is treated as voidable almost as a given. It is arguable, not given. In the criminal matter it does not matter — honest belief does not require the transfer to be invalid.
4. "Alexia went to prison for 18 months in America" appears in several places. The Miami-Dade record shows a 2018 misdemeanor charge disposed of by nolle prosequi about seven weeks after arrest. No custodial sentence. Do not repeat it.
5. The forensic-linguistic video material (z-scores, tone-shift, spatial impossibility) should not be led.
6. Some instructions are recorded as though they were evidence.
