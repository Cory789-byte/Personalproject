The Commissioner, Queensland Human Rights Commission. By email: enquiries@qhrc.qld.gov.au

Dear Commissioner

**Complaint under the Anti-Discrimination Act 1991 and the Human Rights Act 2019: Cory Lea Shepherd against the State of Queensland (Queensland Police Service)**

1. I make this complaint under section 134 of the Anti-Discrimination Act 1991. It is in writing, sets out the alleged contraventions and gives my address for service (s 136).

2. **What is attached.** My answers to the Commission's complaint form (Parts A to G), the statement of complaint, and Annexure D (my reasons on time). To keep within the Commission's limit of 20 pages including attachments, I have not attached Annexure A (a chronology giving the source of each entry), Annexure B (a treatment and comparison matrix), Annexure C (a schedule of documents) or Annexure E (particulars of each contravention). Numbers in square brackets in the statement are rows of Annexure A. I will send any of them, and any document listed in them, on request.

3. **In short.** In 2026 police gave me, a person their own records describe as having "a mental illness", until the close of business the next day to particularise a complaint and three business days to send anything else. They then closed the complaint without contacting me again, and without mentioning the report I sent on 14 July 2026. That part is within time. Between December 2024 and April 2025 police dealt with the same dispute about the same dog twice and reached opposite answers, depending on which of us held the dog. That part is out of time, and Annexure D sets out good cause.

4. **What I ask.** I ask the Commissioner to:
   - accept the complaint (s 141), including the earlier events for good cause (s 138(2)(a)); in the alternative, accept the part within time and defer the decision about the rest until conciliation has been tried (s 141A);
   - not stay the complaint (s 140(1)(a)). Charges arising from 24 February 2025 are before the Southport Magistrates Court (CO2500030466). I deny them. This complaint is about what police did. If a stay is thought necessary, I ask that it be limited to the events of 24 February 2025;
   - investigate the complaint (s 154A) and direct the respondent to give the Commissioner (s 156(1)) the records listed in Annexure C, Part 2, in particular: police's records of 1 and 2 December 2024 (occurrence QP2402065947), including the protective assessment; the record of the release of the dog seized on 22 March 2025 (property receipt 137237) and of any application under s 694 of the Police Powers and Responsibilities Act 2000; and, for complaint COM-26-01249, who set the deadline of 18 June 2026 and on what instruction, the Complaint Resolution Guidelines then in force, and the Complaint Assessment Committee's record of its "decision, rationale for decision";
   - consider the human rights part together with the discrimination complaint. I seek relief for it under s 59 of the Human Rights Act 2019. I complained to the Service about its decision on 29 October 2026. If the human rights part must be dealt with as a separate complaint, I ask that it be accepted because of exceptional circumstances (s 65(2)), or from 6 January 2027.

5. **My earlier complaint (EDR19098).** The Commission accepted it on 15 April 2026 under the Human Rights Act only, as alleging a possible unreasonable limitation of the rights to property and to privacy and reputation, and reported it unresolved on 18 June 2026. No discrimination claim has been considered. I do not ask the Commissioner to deal again with property or privacy for the 2025 events.

6. **The criminal proceedings.** The respondent to this complaint is also my prosecutor. This complaint says nothing about what happened inside the unit on 23 February 2025, and nothing in it is an admission. I will give that account when the criminal proceedings end.

7. **Adjustments.** Because of my condition, I ask that the Commission write to me by email, allow me at least 10 business days to answer any request, hold any conciliation by teleconference with breaks, and allow me a support person or a lawyer.

8. **Address for service.** By email: coryshepherd1@hotmail.com. Telephone: 0417 400 227.

Yours faithfully

Cory Lea Shepherd
