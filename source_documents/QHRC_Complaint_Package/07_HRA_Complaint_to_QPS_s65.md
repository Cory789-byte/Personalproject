**Complaint under the Human Rights Act 2019 about the decision of 10 September 2026 (COM-26-01249)**

1. This is a complaint about an alleged contravention of section 58 of the Human Rights Act 2019 by the Queensland Police Service. I make it so that the requirement in section 65(1)(a) of that Act is met. My letters of 29 October 2026 and 5 November 2026 about the same decision were, and should be treated as, complaints under section 65 from those dates.

2. **The decision.** On 18 June 2026 Ethical Standards Command required particulars of my complaint by close of business the next day. On 10 September 2026 it finalised the complaint without contacting me.

3. **The contravention.** In making that decision the Service:
   - (a) failed to give proper consideration to my human rights relevant to the decision, including my right to equality and to equal and effective protection against discrimination (s 15), my property rights (s 24), my right not to have my privacy, home or family arbitrarily interfered with (s 25) and my right to liberty (s 29). Those are the rights my complaint was about. The outcome does not identify any of them (s 58(1)(b) and (5)); and
   - (b) acted in a way not compatible with those rights, by imposing a one-day deadline on a person whom police records describe as having "a mental illness and requires medication", and then closing the complaint without contact (s 58(1)(a)).

4. **What I ask.** That the decision be set aside and the complaint reassessed by an officer outside Ethical Standards Command and the Southport chain of command, with a reasonable time for me to respond, and that I be told in writing who made the decision of 10 September 2026 and what was considered.

5. I also ask that all records of COM-26-01249 be preserved.

If I do not receive an adequate response within 45 business days, I intend to take this complaint to the Queensland Human Rights Commission.
