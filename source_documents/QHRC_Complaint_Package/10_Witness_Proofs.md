# Witness outlines

For the complainant's use in preparing statements. Each witness gives only what they saw, heard or did, in their own words. These outlines list the topics; they are not scripts. No witness should be told what anyone else has said.

## 1. The building manager

**Why.** He confirmed the lease to police at 2:28 am, and was told at 5:23 am that the complainant was not the leaseholder.

**Topics.**

1. His role at 158 Scarborough Street and how long he has held it.
2. Who the tenants of Unit 104 were in February 2025, and what records he relied on.
3. The entry notice of 19 February 2025: who asked for it, how it was given.
4. What he was told by the leasing manager on 21 to 22 February 2025 about whether the tenancy had ended.
5. What he told the complainant about entering to collect his belongings.
6. The call on speaker at about 2:27 to 2:30 am on 24 February 2025: the question asked and his answer.
7. The call from police at about 5:23 am: what he was told, what he was asked to do, what he answered.
8. Whether anyone from police spoke to him again before 2026.
9. The originals of his CCTV files for 1 December 2024 and his texts with the leasing manager from 21 February to 8 March 2025.

**Caution.** His earlier declaration gives some times one hour late (it used a phone log in Sydney time). His statement should use the camera times, after he has checked them.

## 2. The complainant's friend (present on 23 February 2025)

**Why.** The only adult witness from outside the two households. Not approached by police for fifteen months.

**Topics.** Limit his statement to what police did, and to what he handed police, until the criminal case ends:

1. When police arrived on 24 February 2025 and what they asked him.
2. Whether he gave police any documents (the lease, the order, medical records), when, and to whom.
3. Whether police asked him for a statement that night or later, and when police first contacted him.
4. His video: the date, time and length, and where the original is kept. Do not describe its contents in this statement.

## 3. The complainant's sister's partner

**Why.** The calls of 3 and 4 March 2025, and the questioning of the complainant's sister.

**Topics.**

1. The visit to the Benowa unit: the date and time (police camera puts it on the evening of 24 February 2025 from about 10:17 pm; check before signing), who attended, what was said.
2. Each police call on 3 and 4 March 2025: the time, the caller, what was asked.
3. The call on which the complainant's sister, then 16, was questioned: what she was asked.
4. Whether the complainant lived at the Benowa unit, or stayed there from time to time.

## 4. The complainant's mother

**Why.** The visit of 24 February 2025 and the notices that followed.

**Topics.**

1. The visit on the evening of 24 February 2025: who came, what they said about the complainant and the dog, including the words "essentially terrorized a female person" if she recalls them.
2. The arrangement she described to police ("two to three weeks ... extended out to 10 weeks"): what it was.
3. Whether the complainant lived with her, or stayed for short periods.
4. The notices of 23 March, 12 April and 19 April 2025, and what she was told prompted them.

Her own losses are her claim, not the complainant's. If she wishes to complain about how she was treated, that is a separate complaint in her own name.

## 5. The leasing manager (not a friendly witness)

Do not approach her for a statement. Her police statement of 27 May 2026 is answered by her own emails and texts (full case, Annex F). If the matter goes to the tribunal, her documents can be obtained by a notice to produce.
