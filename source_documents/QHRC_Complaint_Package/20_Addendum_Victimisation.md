## Addendum to the statement of complaint: victimisation (ss 129 and 130), in the alternative

A1. On [date] the complainant gave [the Commission / the respondent] his written submission of 3 May 2026 in complaint EDR19098. In it he alleged in good faith that police had discriminated against him because of his psychiatric disability. That was an allegation that a person had committed an act that would amount to a contravention of the Act (s 130(1)(a)(ii)). In giving it to the Commission he was also supplying information to a person performing a function under the Act (s 130(1)(a)(iii) and (2)(c)): the Commission's direction to attend the conciliation was given under s 159 of the Act as well as s 81 of the Human Rights Act 2019.

A2. On 18 June 2026, the day the Commission reported that complaint unresolved, the respondent required particulars of his police complaint by close of business the next day. That was less time than the three business days its own acknowledgment gave. On 10 September 2026 it finalised that complaint without contacting him again. Those acts were to his detriment.

A3. The complainant says the shorter deadline was set for him, and his complaint finalised without further contact, because he had made that allegation or because the respondent believed he had (s 130(1)(a)(ii) and (b)). He relies on the written submission and the dates. He does not rely on anything said at the conciliation conference.

## Why this is not in the complaint as lodged

- **The fact is not confirmed.** Your saved records do not show that the submission reached the Commission or police. The Commission's letter of 15 April 2026 says the complaint was "made up of the complaint form".
- **Knowledge is not shown.** The claim needs proof that the officers who set the deadline knew of your allegation. Nothing shows that yet. The acknowledgment and the request of 18 June 2026 followed Ethical Standards registering the Crime and Corruption Commission's referral that morning.
- **It is a serious allegation.** Victimisation is also an offence (s 129). It should not be alleged on a fact that has not been checked.
- **Nothing is lost by waiting.** The acts were on 18 June and 10 September 2026. A victimisation complaint about them can be made until 18 June 2027 (s 138(1)).
