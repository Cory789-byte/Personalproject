**Correction to my Case Study of 14 July 2026** (CO-25-2722 / CO-26-1318; occurrence QP2500332406)

I correct the following statements. The rest of the Case Study stands.

1. I said calls were made from my telephone while I was in custody, including a call at 3:26 am on 24 February 2025. That is wrong and I withdraw it. My carrier's record is in Sydney time. In Queensland time that call was at 2:26 am, before my arrest. It is the speaker call to the building manager recorded on the attending officer's camera. I withdraw the same statement in my email to Policelink of 17 April 2025 (case 11518211).
2. I said I was arrested before the officers confirmed the tenancy, and that the building manager confirmed it at about 1:54 am. The camera shows the building manager confirming on speaker at about 2:28 am that both names were on the lease. I was arrested at 2:35 am.
3. Record D3 is the bond authority's acknowledgement of 24 February 2025, showing me as the only bond contributor. It is not a release. The refund reached me on 25 February 2025.
4. I said Romeo is registered solely in my name. The council registration is in my name. In December 2024, at my former partner's request, I signed a change-of-owner form for the microchip registry.
5. Police attended my mother's home on the evening of 24 February 2025, not 25 February. My sister's partner's account is in a draft affidavit that is not yet sworn.
6. On 3 and 4 March 2025 police telephoned my sister's partner four times and questioned my sister, then 16, on one of those calls.
7. The property receipt for the dog (137237) does not record where the dog was seized. I withdraw "a retail carpark at Ashmore".
8. The address exchange is not at 00:05:01 of my interview. It is on the body-worn camera at 00:17:49 to 00:18:15, and in the interview at 00:21:06 to 00:21:15 and 00:29:43.
9. [Keep only if you had not been served with the order of 21 March 2025 before 22 March.] A new temporary order was made on 21 March 2025. I withdraw the statement that the documents to be served on 22 March were ones I had already been served.
10. My application M14056/25 was dismissed in August 2025. No court is now dealing with ownership of the dog.
11. My mother received a breach notice on 23 March 2025, a notice to leave on 12 April 2025 and a second breach notice on 19 April 2025.
12. [Keep only if you said it.] I withdraw any statement that police placed a "duress" flag on my record. The remark on the officer's camera of 22 March 2025, at 04:00 to 04:32, is about the officers' own call sign.
