# Schedule X — Account of 23 and 24 February 2025 (confidential)

**For the complainant and his lawyers only. Do not lodge, send or show this to anyone until the criminal proceedings (CO2500030466) have ended and the defence lawyers have approved it.**

Why it is held back: a complaint is sent to the respondent. The respondent is the Queensland Police Service, which is also the prosecutor. Anything said here about the events inside the unit could be put to you in the criminal trial. The complaint (document 02, paragraph 23) says only that you deny the charges and will give this account when the criminal proceedings end. That protects you without weakening the complaint, which is about what police did.

This schedule records your account as you have given it. Bracketed items are for you to complete. Settle every line against your friend's video, your carrier log and the police camera before anyone relies on it.

## Before 23 February

1. I bought Romeo in December 2023 and paid for him. The council registration and the vet records are in my name.
2. In December 2024, while she had Romeo, she asked me to sign the microchip registry's change-of-owner form, and I did. When I put the registration in her name, she wrote "I won't restrict you from the animals" and "you can trust me". [Locate this message.]
3. She had said I could keep Romeo while she was away, until I ended the relationship. I told her that if we disagreed about ownership, it could be decided in court.
4. On about 12 February 2025 I wrote to her that when she left I would take my animals, and that we could discuss ownership terms when she was back.
5. The building manager told me I kept the right to enter the unit to collect my belongings. An entry notice was put under the door and emailed to her on 19 February 2025. I was still on the lease.
6. On 22 February 2025 I paid the fee and posted the papers to end our registered relationship.

## 23 February 2025, about 7:50 pm

7. I went to Unit 104 to collect my belongings. I had receipts for them. My friend came with me and filmed.
8. I rang the police line as I went in, at 7:51 pm, and stayed on the line for about 37 minutes.
9. Her friend was in the unit. I had seen her once before and did not know her name.
10. Romeo came to me. [Describe in your own words.]
11. There was no confrontation. [Describe what was said and done, in order.]
12. Her friend said I should wait until my former partner got back. I said she was saying she would keep all my things, and that I did not want to see the person I had just paid to separate from.
13. I said Romeo was my dog. I left with him.

## 24 February 2025, about 1:50 am

14. I came back to collect my mattress and my other belongings. [Describe how you entered, what you found at the door, and what was said.]
15. Police arrived at about 1:55 am. I told them this was my home and that I was on the lease. I told them I had rung police earlier.

## What I say about it

16. I went to my own home, while I was a tenant, to collect my own property. I took a dog I had bought and had told her in writing I would take. I believed then, and believe now, that I was entitled to do so.
