# Information requests to police

Reworded on 18 November 2026 after testing each item against the Act and the decided cases (document 13). Lodge with the Queensland Police Service Right to Information and Privacy Unit.

## How to lodge

- **Identification.** Attach a certified copy of your identification to each request (Right to Information Act 2009 s 24(2)(d)).
- **Address for notices.** Give an email address, and a postal address that is not your mother's home.
- **Fee.** None, because every document sought contains your personal information (s 24(5)). Do not add policy documents.
- **Order.** Keep no more than two requests with police at a time. Police may add your applications together when deciding whether the work is too much (s 41(1)).
  1. Now, before or with the letter of 20 November: Request A and Request A2.
  2. When one is decided: Requests B and C together.
  3. Then Request 1 (the address).
  4. Then Request E.
  5. Last, Request D.
- **Consents.** Attach signed consent and identification from your friend (Requests A2 and B) and your mother (Request B).
- **Blanks.** Fill in every blank. A request police cannot pin down can be refused (s 24(2)(b)).
- **Your criminal lawyers.** Show them Request A2 before it is lodged.
- **Copies.** Keep a copy of each request and the date you lodged it. They are part of the good cause for the out-of-time parts of the complaint.

## Standard paragraphs

Put paragraphs 1 to 7 in every request. Add paragraph 8 if you are content for police to know why you ask.

1. This application is for documents that contain my personal information. No application fee is payable (Right to Information Act 2009 s 24(5)).
2. Under section 28, this application expressly includes metadata about each document: the date each entry was created or changed, and the station or unit and registered number of the officer who made it. I do not ask for the activity report or for any record of who viewed my file.
3. Where information is held in a database and not in a written document, I ask for a written document created from the database (s 68(1)(e)).
4. I do not seek the names, addresses or contact details of other people, except those whose signed consent is attached. I ask that those details be deleted and the rest released (ss 74 and 75).
5. Complaint COM-26-01249 was finalised on 10 September 2026. [If you were told the outcome of the complaint referred on 18 August 2025 (CO-25-2722), add: That complaint was finalised on (date).] To the extent Ethical Standards Command obtained or used any document within this application for those complaints, the investigations have been finalised and the document consists of information about me. It is not exempt (schedule 3, section 10(6)).
6. If any part is refused under schedule 3, section 10(1), I ask that the documents themselves be examined for section 10(2)(a): Commissioner of the Police Service v Shelton [2020] QCA 96 at [47] to [49].
7. This application does not seek any document I sought in RTI/54169 or RTI/56733. [If an item does overlap, replace this with: Item (number) was sought in (reference). I ask again because (reason).]
8. I need these documents to decide whether to make, and to support, a complaint to the Queensland Human Rights Commission about how police treated me on these dates. That complaint has a time limit (schedule 4, part 2, items 7, 10, 11, 16 and 17).

For recordings and footage add: I ask for a copy, with other people's faces and voices obscured where needed; or, if a copy cannot be given, a transcript (s 68(1)(b) and (d)). I ask that the recordings and footage be kept until this application and any review are finished.

## If anything is refused or delayed

- **Notice that the work is too much.** Answer in writing within 10 business days, narrowing the request. If you do not answer, the application is treated as withdrawn (s 42(4) to (6)).
- **No decision in time.** Police have 25 business days, plus 10 if another person is consulted. No decision by then counts as a refusal (s 46).
- **Review.** Apply to the Information Commissioner within 20 business days of the decision (s 88(1)(d)). You do not need an internal review first. Police carry the onus (s 87(1)), and the Commissioner can require further searches (s 102).
- **"No document exists."** Keep the notice. Where police recorded nothing of your report, the notice is evidence of that.
