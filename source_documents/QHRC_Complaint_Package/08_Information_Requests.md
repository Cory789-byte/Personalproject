# Information requests to police

Lodge with the Queensland Police Service Right to Information and Privacy Unit, with certified identification. There is no application fee for your own personal information. Lodge each request separately, so one refusal does not hold up the others. Keep a copy and the date of lodgement: these requests are part of the good cause for the out-of-time parts of the complaint.

Request 1 (the address) is in the full case, Annex C, and should be lodged as it stands.

## If anything is refused

Ask for internal review within 20 business days, or apply straight to the Information Commissioner for external review. If a part is refused under schedule 3, section 10(1) of the Right to Information Act 2009, ask that the documents themselves be examined for section 10(2)(a): Commissioner of the Police Service v Shelton [2020] QCA 96 at [47] to [49].
