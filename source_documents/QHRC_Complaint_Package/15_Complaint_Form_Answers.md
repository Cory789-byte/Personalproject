## 3. What happened

In 2026 police gave me one business day to particularise a complaint, although their own records describe me as having "a mental illness", and then closed the complaint without contacting me.

Between December 2024 and March 2025 police dealt with the same dispute about the same dog twice. When I reported that people had entered my home, pushed me and taken the dog, police called it "a civil issue", told me to "let it go" and took no statement. When my former partner reported that I had taken the dog, police called it "stolen property", charged me, seized the dog from me "regardless of what paperwork he has", and gave it to her without the court application their own receipt said would be made. On 24 February 2025 officers said on camera that I was "technically ... allowed to be here", the building manager confirmed my tenancy to them, and police then told him I was "not the leaseholder".

The full account is in the attached statement of complaint and Annexure A.
