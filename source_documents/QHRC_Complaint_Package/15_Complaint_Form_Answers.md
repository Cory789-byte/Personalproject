## 3. What happened

In 2026 police gave me until the close of business the next day to particularise a complaint, and three business days to send anything else, although their own records describe me as having "a mental illness". I could not meet either limit. They then closed the complaint without contacting me again, and without mentioning the report I sent on 14 July 2026.

Between December 2024 and March 2025 police dealt with the same dispute about the same dog twice. When I reported that people had entered my home, pushed me and taken the dog, police called it "a civil issue", told me to "let it go" and took no statement. When my former partner reported that I had taken the dog, police called it "stolen property", charged me, went to my mother's home and my friend's home at night to find it, seized the dog from me "regardless of what paperwork he has" after I asked to call my solicitor first, and gave it to her without the court application their own receipt said would be made. On 24 February 2025 officers said on camera that I was "technically ... allowed to be here", the building manager confirmed my tenancy to them, and police then told him I was "not the leaseholder".
