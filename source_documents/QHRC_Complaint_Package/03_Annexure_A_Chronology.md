# Annexure A — Chronology

Complaint of Cory Lea Shepherd against the State of Queensland (Queensland Police Service). Times are Queensland time (AEST). Camera times are from the body-worn camera recordings. Words quoted from recordings are from machine-made transcripts; the recordings, which the respondent holds, are the primary record, and contested lines are to be checked against the burnt-in clock on the source video before any hearing.

**Basis column.** *Record* means a police, court, tribunal, bond-authority or agency document, or police camera. *Third party* means a document from a person outside the dispute. *Complainant* means the complainant's own account, which he will give in evidence.
