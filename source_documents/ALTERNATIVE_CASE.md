## The chronology read end-to-end — what it establishes

1. **The retaliation matrix begins in December 2023**, twelve months before the Negro relationship breakdown. Cory's first PID-protected disclosure (Service Delivery Complaint 8933558) was self-investigated by the OIC of the very station under complaint and dismissed as 'lawful and justified'. 2. **By February 2025, Cory was the named lessee on a joint co-tenancy** (AFF11 Constable; lease valid to 10 April 2025), Negro had gone on holiday to Perth (her own admission AFF05), and the property manager had issued Cory a Form 9 Entry Notice. 3. **On 23 February 2025 at 19:51, Cory called Police Link and entered his own leased premises with a key.** Phillips's text to Negro at 7:51 PM uses 'opened the door' — not 'burst' or 'broke'.
