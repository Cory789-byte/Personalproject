## The chronology read end-to-end

4 days before the alleged breach (Row 1) the property manager issued Cory a Form 9 Entry Notice. The same day (Row 2) the alleged-victim characterised the dispute in writing as a civil property matter. On the night of the alleged breach Cory called Police Link first (Row 3), entered with a master key while the call was active, with an independent witness filming. The alleged-victim's house-sitter texted the alleged-victim using the word 'opened' (Row 4) — not 'burst' or 'broke'. The house-sitter then called 000 (Row 5) and began filming for evidentiary protection — her own video records her self-asking 'Are we fucking scared?' and self-confirming 'you are safe to leave'. Cory left with property and the dog (Row 6) and waited at a known address for police to attend. Police did not arrive at the unit for ~6 hours.

At 1:35 AM (Row 7) police attended Cory + recorded the alleged-victim was on a flight 3,500km away. At 1:50 AM (Row 8) police forced entry to retrieve the house-sitter — the door broken at 1:50am was broken by police, not Cory. At 1:54 AM (Row 9) the property manager telephoned police explicitly stating Form 13 does not terminate the lease. At 2:00 AM (Row 10) the arriving officer's BWC briefing recorded Cory had key, lease, bond. At 3:26 AM (Row 11) police asked the property manager to reactivate Cory's fob — admitting Cory's continued security control. Cory was arrested anyway (Row 12), with phone deliberately omitted from the property receipt and a printed evidence packet from Tom Balsey discarded into the alleged-victim's friend's custody.

The night following the arrest (Row 13), the arresting officers themselves admitted on tape — at Cory's mother's house — that the prosecution rationale was deference to other officers' judgment, not facts; recited the dispositive defeating facts; acknowledged the alleged-victim's invitation messages, US DV criminal history, the absence of theft mens rea, and the substance of the 'stalking' framing. That tape was deliberately excluded from the original Filed Brief of Evidence. The next morning (Row 14) the Magistrate struck out the foundational order on substantive 'no evidence' grounds.
