## 3. The case in one paragraph

Cory bears the onus of establishing the injury under s 32 — that employment
significantly contributed AND that it did not arise out of reasonable
management action taken reasonably (s 32(5)(a)). The keystone admitted fact is
the 7-hour break between rostered shifts (Form 24 ¶1 admitted; 10-hour minimum
admitted ¶3; an asserted June-2020 8-hour agreement is the live contest, ¶4).
The records that prove the conduct sit only in MSH's systems; the Form 29 /
Rule 64G machinery compels them. MSH and the Regulator run a coordinated
defence; the seam is the Regulator's overriding duty to the Commission — which
is why it disclosed MSH-adverse documents on 11 June.
