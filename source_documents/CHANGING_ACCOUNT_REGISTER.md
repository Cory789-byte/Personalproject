# The changing account — register

**13 August 2026**

Every account on the other side that can be tested against a contemporaneous record has moved. One account has not moved at all. That contrast is the point, and it is now stronger because the prosecution has conceded a charge it maintained for eighteen months.

This register records each shift, its two sources, and what it is useful for.

---

## Why this matters, in three places

**1. Reliability.** A witness whose account changes when a contemporaneous record exists is a witness whose account was not fixed by the record in the first place. Each entry below is prior-inconsistent-statement material.

**2. The submission on the surviving charges.** The concession on Charge 2 was not made on new information. The material that produced it was on the investigating officer's own camera on the second day. So what changed was not the evidence — it was the review. That is a fair and powerful submission: *the same review, applied to what remains, produces the same result.*

**3. The human rights matter.** The quality of the decision-making is the procedural limb. An account that reshapes itself each time it is tested, alongside a brief from which the contradicting recordings were excluded, is evidence about how the decision was made.

---

## The discipline — how this must be framed

**Do not allege concert.** Do not say they coordinated, conspired, or agreed. It is unnecessary, it is not provable on this material, and the moment it is asserted the argument becomes about the allegation rather than about the documents.

**Say what is provable: the asymmetry.** Every account on one side has moved when tested. One account has not. That is a factual observation supported by dated documents, and it does not require anyone to have agreed with anyone.

The register is the evidence. It should be allowed to speak.

## The account that has not changed

Set against all of the above, one account has stayed the same throughout.

| When | Where | What was said |
|---|---|---|
| 24 Feb 2025, hours after the entry | Const. Harmer's camera, at the unit | On the lease; verified it with the RTA and building management over a week; bond still there; invited to stay two to three weeks; had never met Ms Phillips; the order required Ms Negro to leave, not him |
| 24 Feb 2025, in interview | Bickery and Davies interviews | Offered every message on a USB; identified the message from Ms Negro about walking the dog |
| 24 Feb 2025, evening | Davies's camera at his mother's house | Same account, corroborated by his mother |
| 22 Mar 2025, four weeks later | Five separate officers' cameras at the seizure | *"It wasn't a DV breach… She was in Perth. I was allowed to be in my place"*; *"that first temp order has been removed"*; *"I own the dog… The dog was in my name"* |
| Aug 2025 – Aug 2026 | Every submission since | Unchanged |

Thirteen months, at least eight officers, three separate recordings on the first night alone, and the account has not moved on any material point. That consistency is itself evidence, and it is the reason the register above lands.

---

## The submission this supports

Not *"they are lying."* This:

> The prosecution has accepted the submission on Charge 2 and will discontinue it. That charge was laid in February 2025 and maintained until August 2026. The material that has now produced the concession was not newly discovered — it was recorded by the investigating officer on his own body-worn camera on 24 February 2025, where he described the charge as a blanket charge whose substance was emails sent to the aggrieved and her family.
>
> What changed was the review, not the evidence.
>
> The same officer, on the same recording, said that the defendant had been told he could enter the premises, that his name was still on the lease, that his belongings were still there, that the aggrieved was two thousand kilometres away, and that it did not sound as though he had the intent of taking the dog. That recording was excluded from the original brief of evidence.
>
> The review that removed Charge 2 has not yet been applied to what remains.
