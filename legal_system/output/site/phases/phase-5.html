Phase 5 — 24 February 2025 early hours — the lockout and arrest

Police arrive at 1:50 AM. Officer briefing on arrival explicitly notes Cory's lawful tenancy. Officers nevertheless proceed to arrest after officers attend Cory's mother's house at 1:35 AM. Throughout: officers admit on tape that the situation is 'confusing' and 'we don't know who owns the place'. The night ends with Davies and Bickery at Cory's mother's house — the night before the strike-out — where Davies on tape lists the dispositive defeating facts AND admits the prosecution rationale is deference to other officers' judgment, not facts.
