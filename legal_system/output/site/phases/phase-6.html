Phase 6 — 25 February 2025 — the foundational order is struck out on substantive merits-based grounds

Less than 48 hours after the arrest, the Magistrates Court strikes out the TPO. The strike-out was on substantive grounds — there was no evidence that the incidents Negro had stated in support of the PPN actually happened. Yaun's email confirms 'no evidence'. This is not a technical / procedural strike-out — it is a finding that the underlying DV incidents Negro alleged did not occur on the available evidence. Negro had alleged physical injury (marks on her neck) but had no marks, and was with Cory at the address every day during the period she claimed to have been 'in refuge'.
