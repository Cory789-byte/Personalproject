CO-25-2722 — Domestic Violence Breach — alleged 23 February 2025 entry to Unit 104

What QPS alleges

- Cory entered Unit 104, 158 Scarborough Street Southport on 23 February 2025 - Entry was in breach of the Police Protection Notice issued 1 December 2024 (QP0899) - Phillips (a houseguest of Negro) was the witness present - Negro (the aggrieved) was in Perth and was contacted by Phillips

Elements the prosecution must prove

  1. (1) Domestic violence order in force at the relevant time
  2. (2) Respondent named on the order (the accused)
  3. (3) Conduct prohibited by the order
  4. (4) Conduct in fact occurred
  5. (5) Knowledge / recklessness as to the order
